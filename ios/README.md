# CivilCraft for iPad

**Planned.** Tablets first. Until then, use https://getcadcraft.com/try/civilcraft in Safari.

The iPad app will be the same browser edition in WKWebView (Tauri mobile, [getcadcraft/desktop-shell](https://github.com/getcadcraft/desktop-shell)), released through the App Store after a touch pass: long-press for right-click, larger targets, touch orbit in 3D.
