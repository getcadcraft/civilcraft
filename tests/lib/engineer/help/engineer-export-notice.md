:::note
**Export controls.** Engineer and its generators are general-purpose design tools built from published textbook engineering. We make no representation that any generator, design, data sheet or exported file is free of export-control or sanctions restrictions, or that it may lawfully be exported, re-exported, transferred or used in any country or by any person. You are responsible for classifying what you design and for following the export-control and sanctions laws that apply to you. This is a basic notice: we expect to change these controls to match the export-control rules of the country of origin.
:::

The same words are at the foot of every data sheet (in the panel, in the PDF and in the JSON file) and beside any generator that carries the notice, such as the nozzle.

Engineer is not an app of its own: it is a shared part of every CadCraft program that mounts it, and the notice is the same in each. See [Parts from physics: generators](topic:engineer-generators).
