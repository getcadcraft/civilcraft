It is a design aid, not a certificate, and nobody has compared a result with DIALux or another lighting program on the same room. Walls do not block light from each other, so a room that is not a box reads a little too bright in its bays; furniture you list blocks direct light only. Daylight has no sun, clear sky, ground reflection, buildings or trees outside, the same glass transmittance at every angle and no frames. The requirement numbers for rooms (EN 12464-1 style) and for emergency lighting (EN 1838 style) are typed from memory, unverified, and waiting for a lighting engineer; never read them as the standard's. Emergency lighting counts direct floor light only: no signs, duration or switch-over time. UGR is an estimate not compared with a published table. Not built: isolines, a 3D picture, a maker's catalogue, exterior and road lighting. The BimCraft window height is assumed 1.2 m and the room's ceiling height is the walls' usual height. The SuperCad Studio adapter is not built.
