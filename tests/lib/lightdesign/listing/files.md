Reads IES and EULUMDAT (.ies, .ldt) photometry files, one at a time or a whole folder into a catalogue kept in the add-on's storage on this device. Writes an HTML report, a CSV of the grid, drawing entities in CadCraft (layers LD-LUMINAIRE and LD-LUX), and in BimCraft IfcLightFixture elements (CadCraft_Electrical and CadCraft_LightDesign property sets) and drafting lines in the level's plan view, and in ModelCraft coloured bodies (luminaires and the 3D lux surface) in a tagged group of the model.
