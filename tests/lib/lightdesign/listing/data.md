It ships five synthetic luminaire types (not a maker's) and typical requirement numbers for 15 kinds of room and for emergency lighting. Those numbers were typed from memory of EN 12464-1 and EN 1838, are not checked against any edition and wait for a lighting engineer to confirm them; each can be typed over. It reads .ies and .ldt photometry files you load. The room comes from your drawing or model; nothing is sent anywhere.
