# Round 6 brief: 100 objects in each of 26 new categories (2026-11-03)

Same job, framework and rules as rounds 4 and 5. **Read `tools/library/detail/R4-BRIEF.md` and `R5-BRIEF.md` first** (API of `fam()`, `Acc`, `cab`, `tbl`, the kit namespaces, the rules that do not bend, privacy patterns, look-before-you-publish), then skim one finished file such as `r5-aviation.mjs` for the level of detail expected. Owner's only quality instruction: **high quality**. That means: real proportions, real parts drawn (not boxes with a label), details a viewer recognises from the front elevation and in 3D, plan entities that match the body, and real size variants.

## What differs from round 5
- You own TWO groups (named in your assignment). For each, write `tools/library/detail/r6-<group>.mjs` (default export: array of `fam()`), **exactly 100 variants per group** (at least 15 families per group; every family draws something real; variants differ in real size or configuration, not just the label).
- Commands take `--round 6`. Check: `node tools/library/detail/make-round4.mjs --round 6 --check --files r6-<g1>.mjs,r6-<g2>.mjs`. Write the CSV: `node tools/library/detail/make-round4.mjs --round 6 --files r6-<g1>.mjs,r6-<g2>.mjs --expect <g1>,<g2> --write --groups <g1>,<g2>` (writes `tools/library/blocks/<group>-7.csv`). Preview: `node tools/library/detail/r4-preview.mjs tools/library/detail/r6-<g>.mjs <scratch-dir> --all`, then LOOK at the sheets with Read and fix what looks wrong (floating parts, flat slabs standing in for a shape, parts outside the body box, objects that read as the wrong thing). Then `node tools/library/detail/detail.mjs --check` must pass. Do not touch other agents' files, `make-round4.mjs`, `fam.mjs`, kits or the PHP/JS catalogs.
- A group is the part of `cat` before the slash. Allowed `cat` keys per group are listed below; spread the 100 over them (about 25 each).
- Names start with "Generic " and are unique across ALL block CSVs (about 6,500 names). Grep `tools/library/blocks/*.csv` before choosing a title; add a setting word to stay distinct. No maker, model, brand, franchise, character name, sports team, military unit or standard. Generic means by type and size class.
- Heavy or large things use real size in inches (a rocket stage, a combine harvester, a boat hull are big); keep each variant under about 8,000 triangles; geometry stays inside the body box (the check warns).
- Use the commercial-quality helpers: `cab()`/`tbl()` where casework applies, tubes for rails and frames, cylinders for pipes, wheels with hubs, etc. Round-5 files (`r5-aviation.mjs`, `r5-earthmoving.mjs`, `r5-construction.mjs`) have good helpers to copy (prismX/prismY, wheels, rails, braces); copy them into your file, never import from another agent's file.
- Safety and taste: the Military group is **generic vehicles, shelters, field facilities, logistics and non-weapon field gear only** (no firearms, ordnance, weapon systems, or anything operational). Jewellery, toys, "Just for fun" and "Speedrun" are playful or small-scale: real sizes in inches (a ring is about 0.8 in across), still drawn with real parts (band, bezel, prongs, stone).
