# IRC electrical (chapter 39) and alarms — research notes, 2026-10-27
Read from ICC's free viewer (IRC2021P1, IRC2024V1.0 chapter 39 and chapter 3). Numbers and section numbers only.
- Wall receptacles: no point along the floor line more than 6 ft from a receptacle; wall spaces 2 ft or wider, unbroken by doorways, fireplaces, fixed cabinets — E3901.2.1 (2021, 2024); NEC 2023 210.52(A).
- Countertops: 24 in (E3901.4.1); islands: 2021 one per 9 sq ft + one per 18 sq ft; 2024 / NEC 2023 no minimum (provision for future). Not checked: countertops are not modelled.
- Bathroom: a receptacle within 36 in of each basin — E3901.6 (checked as "at least one outlet in each bathroom": basins are not placed by the electrical tools).
- Outdoor: front and back, ≤ 6 ft 6 in above grade — E3901.7 (checked as "at least two outlets outside").
- Laundry E3901.8, garage (one per bay) E3901.9, hallways 10 ft or longer E3901.10.
- Lighting: a switched lighting outlet in every habitable room, kitchen, bathroom, hallway, stairway, attached garage — E3903.2/E3903.3; stair switch at each level for 6+ risers E3903.3.1.
- GFCI: 2021 E3902.1–14; 2024 E3902.1–18 (all kitchen receptacles, laundry, within 6 ft of sinks/tubs, outdoors, garages, basements, crawl spaces, specific appliances). AFCI: 2021 E3902.17, 2024 E3902.21 (kitchens, family/dining/living rooms, bedrooms, closets, hallways, laundry…). Not checked: circuit protection is not in the model.
- Smoke alarms: each sleeping room, outside each sleeping area, each story — 2021 R314.3, 2024 R310.3; 3 ft from bathroom doors; cooking distances 2021 20/10/6 ft, 2024 10 ft. CO alarms: 2021 R315.3, 2024 R311.3 where fuel-fired appliances or an attached garage.
