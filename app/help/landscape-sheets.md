Landscape › More › {{ui:Landscape sheets…}} makes one PDF with the sheets you tick:

| Sheet | What is on it | Legend |
|---|---|---|
| **Planting plan** | Plants, beds, hedges and the hardscape over the ground | The plant schedule with each plant's symbol, botanical and common name, container and count |
| **Grading plan** | Contours, spot elevations, grading outlines, steep areas, flow arrows and ponds, hardscape levels | What each mark means |
| **Irrigation plan** | Heads with their coverage, pipes, valves, the supply, over the hardscape and bed outlines | Heads by kind and arc with their symbol, pipe by size, valves, each zone's flow and pressure |
| **Details** | Each wall's section at its tallest point (ground, footing, wall, coping, drainage layer), each paving's build-up, each flight of steps in profile | Each detail names its scale |

Choose the **paper**, the **scale** (the one that fits is chosen for you) and **colour** or **black only**. Each plan is the site plan with the other trades' layers left out, so what you see on screen is what prints.

Sections along roads and alignments are in [Sections](topic:sections); the plan sheet of the whole site is File › PDF.
