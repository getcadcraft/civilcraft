{{ui:Areas}} oo ku jirta kooxda Beerta wuxuu sawiraa **cawl**, **beer geedka lagu beeray**, **buur** ama **dhulka faafa**: guji geesaha, oo riix {{key:Enter}}. Beer ama dhulka faafa wuxuu ku bilaabmaa geedka lagu doortay qaybta Geedaha; cawl ma leh geedo.

## Waxa uu haysta

- **Dhismaha**: qoto dheer ciidda sare, qoto dheer mulch, membrane maro-cawska, iyo xad (bir, alwaax, dhagax, kaanaad, plastik ama midna). Qotooyinka caadiga ah ayaa la buuxiyay; dejinta kaaga.
- **Isku-dar**: noocyada (oo leh cultivar iyo cabbir) iyo qaybahooda, oo loo buuxiyay sida beer masaafada ama kacaanka.
- **Heerka dhammaystiran**: ka tag madhan, aagguna wuxuu raacaa dhulka; ku sii heer, aagguna wuxuu noqonayaa beddelaha dhulka (eeg [Beddelayaasha dhulka iyo heerarka](topic:ground-modifiers)).
- **Clip around paving, walls and steps** (waa shaqeeya caadi ahaan): meel kasta oo dhagaxa jiid, waddo, derbi, kerb, rampa ama tallaabo ay isku dhacaan aagga, qaybtaas waa la iska tagaa. Geed lagama dhigo hoostooda ama dhow-dhow masaafo nus ah cidhifkooda.

## Tirinta

**Properties** wuxuu muujinayaa **Bed** saafi ah (kadib gooyidda, si sax ah loo cabbiray), **wareegga**, **dherer-ka cidhifka** (oo keliya cidhifka aagga laftiisa, ma aha meelaha hardscape-ku jaro), mugga ciidda sare iyo mulch, aagga membrane iyo tirada geedka safka isku-darka kasta. Jadwalka geedku wuxuu tiriyaa geedaha aagga; faylka qadarka ee TakeoffCraft wuxuu helaa aagga, ciidda sare, mulch, membrane iyo xadka; IFC-ga beerta wuxuu qoraa IfcGeographicElement oo leh qaybta qadarka.
