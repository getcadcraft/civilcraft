Toolbar-ku wuxuu ku socdaa sare-ka app-ka, oo ku qeybsan toddobo kooxood. Badhan kasta wuxuu muujiyaa tooltip-kiisa iyo furahiisa markaad kursorka ku dul-dhigto.

## File

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:New}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu bilaabaa mashruuc madhan. Marka hore wuu weydiiyaa haddii isbeddel aan la kaydin jiro. |
| {{ui:Open}} | {{key:Mod+O}} | Wuxuu furaa fayl barta (CSV, TXT), LandXML ama mashruuca CivilCraft. |
| {{ui:Save}} | {{key:Mod+S}} | Wuxuu soo dejiyaa mashruuca sida `<name>.civilcraft.json`. |

## Dhoofi

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:LandXML}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu soo dejiyaa `<name>.xml`: barrooyinka, dusha, alignment-ka, profile-yada iyo shabakadda tuubooyinka sida LandXML 1.2. "Weli wax la dhoofin karo ma jiro." markuu mashruucu madhan yahay. |
| {{ui:DXF}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu soo dejiyaa `<name>.dxf`: xarriiqyada naqshadda sida R12 DXF. |
| {{ui:PDF}} | {{key:Mod+P}} | Wuxuu furaa **Qorso qorshaha PDF-ka**. "Weli wax la qorsan karo ma jiro." markuu mashruucu madhan yahay. |

## Dusha

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Breakline}} | {{key:B}} | Guji barrooyinka sahanka dalbadoodii, {{key:Enter}} si aad u dhammaysato. Breakline-ku wuxuu qaadaa heerarka barrooyinka. |
| {{ui:Boundary}} | {{key:O}} | Guji geesaha xadka dibadda, {{key:Enter}} si aad u dhammaysato. Wuxuu beddelaa xad kasta. |

Labadaba waxay leeyihiin "Weli dusha ma jiro: marka hore fur barrooyinka sahanka." markay dusha jirin.

## Naqshad

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Alignment}} | {{key:A}} | Guji PI-yada dalbadoodii, {{key:Enter}} si aad u dhammaysato. |
| {{ui:Pad}} | {{key:G}} | Guji geesaha padka, {{key:Enter}} si aad u dhammaysato. Sida qalabyada dusha, wuu digayaa marka dusha aan jirin; pad-ku wuxuu u dhaqmaa kaliya dusha dushiisa. |
| {{ui:Pipes}} | {{key:P}} | Guji qaababka laga bilaabo sare ilaa hoose (guji mid jira si aad ugu biirto), {{key:Enter}} si aad u dhammaysato. |
| {{ui:Profile}} | {{key:R}} | Muujinaya ama qarinaya muuqaalka dherer ee hoosta naqshadda. |

## Tafatir

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Undo}} | {{key:Mod+Z}} | Wuxuu ka noqdaa isbeddelka u dambeeya (ilaa 200). Waa la damiyaa marka wax la ka noqon karo ma jiro. |
| {{ui:Redo}} | {{key:Mod+Y}} | Wuxuu sameeyaa mar kale waxa laga noqday. Waa la damiyaa marka wax dib loo samayn karo ma jiro. |
| {{ui:Copy}} | {{key:Mod+C}} | Wuxuu nuqul ka sameeyaa barrooyinka sahanka la doortay, oo wuxuu ku dhigaa clipboard-ka sida safafka barta ee spreadsheet-ku aqbali karo. Waa la damiyaa ilaa barro la doorto. |
| {{ui:Paste}} | {{key:Mod+V}} | Wuxuu dhajiyaa barrooyinka: waxay raacaan kursorka, guji kasta wuxuu dhigaa iyagoo leh lambarro barro kale aanay lahayn. Safafka barta ee laga nuqulay spreadsheet-ka ayaa sidoo kale la dhajiyaa. |
| {{ui:More}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu furaa liiska hoose. |

Liiska {{ui:More}}:

| Waxa | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Cut}} | {{key:Mod+X}} | Wuxuu nuqulaa barrooyinka la doortay oo tirtiraa (hal tallaabo oo ka noqosho). |
| {{ui:Copy}} | {{key:Mod+C}} | Sida badhanka {{ui:Copy}}. |
| {{ui:Paste}} | {{key:Mod+V}} | Sida badhanka {{ui:Paste}}. |
| {{ui:Paste in place}} | {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} | Wuxuu dhajiyaa barrooyinka isku-xirkooda, lambarro barro kale aanay lahayn. |
| {{ui:Duplicate}} | {{key:Mod+D}} | Nuqul barrooyinka la doortay wuxuu raacaa kursorka; guji meesha ay tagayso. Clipboard-ka lama taaban. |
| {{ui:Delete}} | {{key:Delete}} | Wuxuu tirtiraa barrooyinka sahanka la doortay, ama alignment-ka, padka, qaabka (iyo tuubooyinkiisa), tuubada ama PVI-ga la doortay. |
| {{ui:Select all points}} | {{key:Mod+A}} | Wuxuu doortaa barrooyinka sahanka oo dhan. |
| {{ui:Select none}} | {{key:Esc}} | Wuxuu nadiifiyaa doorashada. |

Waxa aan wax ku qabsan karin hadda waa la damiyaa, tooltip-kiisuna wuxuu sheegaa sababta. Faahfaahin: [Dooro, nuqul iyo dhaji barrooyinka sahanka](topic:copy-paste-points).

Ka noqosho wuxuu daboolaa wax ka beddelka naqshadda, dejinta dusha iyo barrooyinka sahanka (dhajin, goyn, nuqul, tirtir). Ma daboolo dejinta muuqaalka, furidda ama soo dhoofinta fayl kasta ayaa tirtirta.

## GIS

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Coordinates}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu furaa daaqadda **Nidaamka isku-xirka**: sheeg nidaamka mashruucu ku jiro, ama isticmaal isku-xirka dhulka ama kan gudaha. Eeg [Deji nidaamka isku-xirka mashruuca](topic:coordinate-systems) iyo [Isku-xirka dhulka iyo gudaha](topic:ground-and-local-coordinates). |
| {{ui:Import}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu soo dhoofiyaa xogta GIS: shapefile, GeoJSON, GeoPackage, gridka korka iyo LiDAR. Eeg [Soo dhoofi xogta GIS](topic:gis-import). |
| {{ui:Export}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu dhoofiyaa xogta sida la dhisay waaxda GIS. Eeg [Dhoofi xogta sida la dhisay ee GIS](topic:gis-export). |
| {{ui:Analyze}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu furaa panelka falanqaynta GIS: buffer, overlay, spatial join, dooro sifo ahaan, cabbir. Eeg [Falanqee layer-yada GIS](topic:gis-analysis). |
| {{ui:Attribute table}} | ma jiro | Ku jirta Dusha › {{ui:More}}. Wuxuu furaa miiska sifooyinka layer-ka GIS: kala-sooc, shaandheyn, dooro, tafatir, ku dar goobo, xisaabi, kaydi sida CSV. Eeg [Ka shaqee miiska sifooyinka layer-ka](topic:gis-table). |
| {{ui:Map}} | ma jiro | Wuxuu furaa daaqadda **Layer-yada khariidadda**: sawir leh meel-geyn dhulka, ama server-ada khariidadda aad ku darto, oo hoosta qorshaha lagu muujiyo. Eeg [Muuji layer-yada khariidadda hoosta qorshaha](topic:map-layers). |

Qalabyada GIS waxay soo kordhaan markii ugu horreysay ee aad isticmaasho mid ka mid ah badhamadan, aad furto fayl GIS, ama aad furto mashruuc leh nidaam isku-xirka ama layer-yada GIS.

## Muuqaal

| Badhan | Furaha | Waxa uu sameeyo |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Select}} | {{key:V}} ama {{key:Esc}} | Guji si aad u doorato jid-hagaha, padka, qaababka, tuubooyinka iyo barrooyinka ({{key:Shift}} iyo guji ama jiid ayaa ku daraa barrooyin); jiid meelaha gacanta si aad u dhaqaajiso PI-yada, geesaha padka iyo qaababka. |
| {{ui:Fit}} | {{key:F}} | Wuxuu weyneeyaa qorshaha si uu u muujiyo dusha, barrooyinka, jid-hagaha iyo padka (muuqaalka 3D: wax walba ayuu muujiyaa). |
| {{ui:3D}} | {{key:D}} | Wuxuu muujiyaa goobta 3D oo ka sarreysa qorshaha, ama wuu ku noqdaa qorshaha. Bar-ka ka sarreeya muuqaalka 3D wuxuu dejiyaa muuqaalka, weynaynta, midabaynta iyo waxa muuqda, wuxuuna dhoofiyaa GLB ama OBJ. Eeg [Arag goobta 3D](topic:site-3d). |
