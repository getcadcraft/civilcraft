Dooro waddo oo guji **Warbixinta go'yaasha** oo ku jirta **Sifooyinkeeda**.

## Go'yaasha tiro-meerayaasha iyo fogaanta

1. **Ka** iyo **ilaa**: tiro-meerayaasha la go'ayo (1+50 ama 150).
2. **kasta**: farqiga. Go'yaasha waxay ku yaallaan meel kasta oo ah tiro lagu dhufto, labada dhamaadkaba, iyo (sanduuqa la calaamadiyey) meelaha ay curyaannada waddadu ka bilaabanto iyo ka dhammaanto.
3. **Fogaanta**: bidix iyo midig ee waddada, marka laga eego tiro-meerka kor: `12 L, 0, 12 R`, `L12 CL R12` ama `-12, 0, 12` (bidixdu waa minus). Waddo leh koridor ayaa ka bilaabmaysa geesaha dusha iyo garbaha.
4. **Tirinta ka bilow** iyo **hor-ku-xirka**: go'yaasha waxaa loo magacaabi karaa 1, 2, 3, ama S101, S102.

## Barrooyinka sahanka ee u dhow waddada

Dooro **barrooyinka sahanka ee u dhow waddada** iyo sida ay u dhow tahay. Barta kasta oo ku jirta masaafadaas waxay heshaa tiro-meerkeeda iyo fogaanteeda, naqshadda halkaas, iyo sare-kuhaysteeda oo dhul ah, taas oo ah hubin barrooyin la go'ay ama la sawiray sida la dhisay.

## Waxa warbixinta ku jira

| Tiirka | Waxa uu yahay |
|---|---|
| Barta | Magaca go'da, ama lambarka barta sahanka |
| Station, Offset | Halka ay ku taal waddada: L bidix, R midig |
| Northing, Easting | Isku-xirkeeda |
| Design elev. | Dusha koridorka la qaabeeyay halkaas (laga bilaabo khadka daylight ilaa khadka daylight), haddii kale naqshadda sare ee xarriiqda dhexe |
| Ground elev. | Dusha jirta halkaas, ama sare-kuhaysiga barta sahanka |
| Cut/fill | C markii dhulku naqshadda ka sarreeyo (goyn hoos), F markii ka hooseeyo (buuxin kor), ilaa boqolaad |

Go' aan sare-kuhaysi naqshad lahayn (ka baxsan koridorka, ama waddo aan lahayn naqshad meel ka fog xarriiqda dhexe) ma leh goyn ama buuxin; daaqaddu waxay sheegtaa inta ay tahay.

## Kaydi

- **CSV**: sadar cinwaan ah, kadibna tiirarka kor ku qoran, iyo goynta ama buuxinta oo ah tiro calaamad leh (dhulka ka dhig naqshadda), si loo isticmaalo spreadsheet-ka iyo ururiyeyaasha xogta.
- **PDF**: bogagga miiska ee warqadda aad doorato, go' safka kasta, goynta oo oranji ah, buuxinta oo cagaar ah, iyo xarig cinwaanka xaashiyada kale.

:::note
Go'yaasha waxaa laga xisaabiyaa naqshadda mashruuca. Ka hubi qorshayaasha la ansixiyay ka hor intaadan wax dhisin.
:::
