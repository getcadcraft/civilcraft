## Waxa aad baran doontid

Sida loo doorto barooyinka sahanka adigoo wareegaya, marka sanduuqu ka badan yahay barooyinka aadan doonayn.

## Samee lasso

1. Guji {{ui:Select}} (ama riix {{key:V}}).
2. Hay {{key:Shift}}, riix qorshaha oo jiid **wareeg** barooyinka aad rabto, adigoo marka hore u dhaqaqaya **midig**.
3. Sii deyso.

Dhibcaha ku jira hareeraha ayaa lagu daraa doorashada. Jiid aan ka fogaanaynin khad toosan wuxuu sawiraa sanduuq, sida hore; markii uu ka leexdo khadkaas (ka badan 12 pixel qiyaas ahaan) wuxuu noqdaa lasso.

## Daaqad, isgoys iyo xayn

| Waxa aad jiidda | Lasso | Doorta |
|---|---|---|
| Midig marka hore | Daaqad (hareer adag) | Barooyinka ku jira |
| Bidix marka hore | Isgoys (jajab, cagaar) | Barooyinka ku jira, iyo barooyinka ay hareerta taabanayso |
| Riix {{key:Space}} inta aad jiidayso | Daaqad, kadibna isgoys, kadibna xayn | Xayn (jid jajab, oranji ah, aan la xirin): barooyinka ay jidku marayo |

Status-khaddu wuxuu magacaabaa lasso-ga inta aad jiidayso. {{key:Esc}} inta aad jiidayso wuu joojiyaa, wixna ma doorto.

:::tip
Xayn waa habka degdega ah ee lagu doortaa barooyinka ku dhex jira kerb-ka ama khadka godka: jiid dhinacooda oo sii deyso.
:::
