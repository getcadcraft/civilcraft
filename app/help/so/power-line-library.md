Qaybta **Maktabad** ee Xariiqyada korontada waxay haysaa dhammaan alaabta xariiq ama kaabal kasta ay isticmaali karto. Waxba ku xidhan maaha dal ama soo saare gaar ah: alaabta aad shaqaysid ayaad samaysaa.

## Saddex nooc oo alaabta ah

| Nooca | Meesha ay ku nool tahay | Ma beddeli kartaa? |
|---|---|---|
| **Tusaalooyin** | Waxaa lagu soo daray CivilCraft, leh tirooyin guud oo aan ka xisaabinnay hantida alaabta | Maya: marka hore {{ui:Koobiyee maktabaddayda}} |
| **Mashruuca gudihiisa** | Waxay ku timid faylka mashruuca aad furtay | Maya: marka hore {{ui:Koobiyee maktabaddayda}} |
| **Anigu** | Alaabtaada, oo lagu kaydiyo browser-kan | Haa, isbeddelladuna isla markiiba way kaydsamaan |

Mashruuc kasta wuxuu hayaa nuqul alaab kasta oo uu isticmaalo, si mashruucu ugu furmo kombiyuutar kasta xadhigyadiisa iyo tiirarkiisa.

:::warning
Alaabta tusaalaha ah waa tusaalooyin: hubi tiro kasta xogta alaabta iibiyahaaga. Kaablaha dhulka hoostiisa ma leh cabbir korontada oo la isku hallayn karo, sababtoo ah waxay kuxirantahay sida loo dhigay kaabalka.
:::

## Samee alaab

1. Gudaha **Maktabad**, dooro **Nooca** (xadhigyada sare, kaablaha dhulka hoostiisa, tiirarka, qalabka ama noocyada jidka).
2. Riix {{ui:Shay cusub}}, ama dooro tusaale oo riix {{ui:Koobiyee maktabaddayda}} ama {{ui:Samee nuqul}}.
3. Buuxi goobahaaga. Goobaha leh `*` waa kuwa hubinta u baahan tahay; alaab ay ka maqan tahay mid waxay leedahay `!` waxayna ku jirtaa liiska hoos **Hubin** marka xariiq ay isticmaasho.
4. Siiso **Qaybta**, adigoo `/` u dhexeeya heerarka: `Overhead/LV/Bundled`, `Supports/Concrete/12 m`. Geedka waxaa raacaa qaybahaaga.

Waxa nooc kasta u baahan yahay:

| Nooca | Loo baahan yahay hubinta |
|---|---|
| Xadhig sare | qaybta cross-section, dhexroorka, miisaanka mitir kasta, xoogga jebinta, modulus-ka elastic-ka, ballaarinta kulaylka |
| Kaabal dhulka hoostiisa | xariiqyada, qaybta cross-section, korontada, dhexroorka, iska-caabbinta, cabbirka korontada |
| Tiir | dhererka guud, culeyska magacaabani (la qiyaaso barta ka hoosaysa sareeyaha), qoto-dheerka dhulka |
| Qalab | nooca; culeyska la qiimeeyay ee kaabalada, qalabka xidhka iyo jir-kaabbeyada; masaafada safafka iyo hoos-u-dhaca kaabalada dhidibka-tiirka; dherer kaabalada xidhka; xagasha jir-kaabbeyada |
| Nooca jidka | qoto-dheer, ballac, dabool ka sarreeya kaablaha |

## Wadaag

- {{ui:Kaydi maktabaddayda}} wuxuu kaydiyaa alaabtaada oo dhan hal fayl `.cclines.json` ah; {{ui:Fur maktabad…}} wuxuu mar kale akhrisaa (alaabta furaha isku mid ah ayaa la beddelaa).
- {{ui:Kaydi sida CSV}} wuxuu kaydiyaa nooca aad eegayso sida spreadsheet; {{ui:Fur maktabad…}} wuxuu akhrisaa CSV oo nooca ah mar kale. Tiirarka waxay iman karaan siday u kala horreeyaan; faylka leh kala-soocaha semicolon wuxuu isticmaali karaa comma tobanaad.
