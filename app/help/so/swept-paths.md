Jidka wareegga wuxuu gaari ku wadaa khad, wuxuuna muujinayaa dhulka uu jirkiisu mari doono. Isticmaal si aad u hubiso in baabuurku geesta wareegi karo, albaabka dhoofinta gaadhi karo, ama gaari-dambe dib u riixi karo oo ku dhigi karo meesha sax ah iyada oo aanu waddada ka bixin.

## Gaari ku wad khad

1. Sawir khadka ay gaarigu raacayo, jihada socodka: khad muuqaal (feature line) (qaansooyinka way fiican yihiin: taabo {{key:C}} markaad sawirayso, eeg [Khadadka muuqaalka](topic:feature-lines)) ama alignment.
2. Dooro oo guji {{ui:Swept path…}} **Sifooyinkeeda**.
3. Hoosta **Ka bilow**, dooro gaari, kadibna deji cabbirkiisa naqshadda gaarigaaga: ballaca, dheerka hore (front overhang), wheelbase, dheerka dambe (rear overhang), track iyo **Xidhka wareegga** (Steering lock). Ku dar ilaa saddex gaari-dambe, mid kasta oo leh meesha uu ku xirnaan, dherkiisa ilaa dhidibka (axle), dheerka hore iyo dambe, iyo **Isku-xirka ugu weyn** (Largest articulation).
4. Dooro **Wadista**: hore ama dib. Guji {{ui:Drive it}}.

:::warning
Gaadiyada laga bilaabayo waa meel-hayn (placeholder), ma aha caadi. Cabbirka ka dhig gaariga naqshadda ee mashruucaaga isticmaalo.
:::

## Hore iyo dib

| Wadista | Waxa raacaya khadka | Waxa CivilCraft xisaabiyo |
|---|---|---|
| **Hore** | Bartamaha dhidibka hore | Meesha dhidibka dambe iyo gaari-dambe kasta u gudbayaan, xagalka wareegga iyo isku-xirka |
| **Dib** | Bartamaha dhidibka ugu dambeeya (gadaasha gaari-dambe) | Meesha unug kasta oo hore u jira, iyo wareegga cabka ay gaari-dambe halkaas u gaadhayso |

Dib u socodka waa sida dib-u-riixidda loo hubiyo: sheeg meesha gaari-dambe ay u tagi lahayd, CivilCraft wuxuu kuu sheegaa cabka ma samayn karo.

## Akhri natiijada

Jidka wareegga waxaa lagu muujiyaa naqshadda midab orange ah: qaabkiisa iyo, qaab dashed ah, jidadka giraabta ugu dambeeya. Dooro (guji gudihiisa) si aad ugu aragto **Sifooyinkeeda**:

- **Dhererka la wado**, xagalka ugu weyn ee **Wareegga** iyo **Isku-xirka** marka loo barbar dhigo xadadkooda, iyo **Aagga wareega**.
- Dhibaato kasta oo khadka ku jirta, iyo masaafadeeda: wareegga oo dhaafay xadka, ama gaari-dambe oo wareegaya dhaafay xadka isku-xirka (wuu jackknife-ayaa).
- **Gaariga khadka dhinac**: dhaqaajinta slider-ka si aad u aragto gaariga meel kasta, oo leh wareegga iyo isku-xirkiisa halkaas.

Khadka marka aad beddesho, jidka wareegga wuu raacaa. {{ui:Edit the vehicle…}} wuxuu beddelaa cabbirrada; **Wadista** wuxuu u beddelaa hore iyo dib. Isbeddel kasta waa hal tallaabo: {{key:Mod+Z}} ayaa ka noqda. {{key:Delete}} wuxuu tirtiraa jidka wareegga la doortay (khadka ayaa sii haysa).

Dhibcaha iyo jidadka giraabta waxay u gudbaan PDF, DXF iyo DWG layer-ka C-ROAD-SWPT.

## Sida loo xisaabiyo

Gaari kasta wuxuu wareegaa xarriiqa dhidibka dambe, waxaana raacaya nuqta ka horeysa (dhidibka hore ama hitch-ka), oo ah habka xawaaraha-hooseeya ee aan lisin (low-speed, no-slip) ee ay jidadka wareegga hubiyaan. Aagga wareega waa dhammaan qaabka jirka mid kasta oo isku dhafan. Ma moodeelayo xawaaraha, lisidda taayirka, waqtiga ay wareegga u qaadato, gaari-dambe wareegga, iyo fogaanta hoosta jirka.
