Shaqada beerta waxay qalabka naqshadda beerta ku dhejisaa goobta CivilCraft ee aad haysato: isla barooyinka sahanka, isla dhulka jira, isla dusha la soo jeediyay iyo isla faylka mashruuca sida shaqada dhulka.

## Fur

Dooro {{ui:Surface}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Landscape workspace}}. Qalabka sare ayaa markaas muujinaya doorasho **Shaqada** iyo koox **Beerta**, Dhulka, Naqshadda iyo GIS-ka ayaa dhinac u durugaya si loogu bixiyo meel. Dooro **Dhulka** hoos Shaqada si aad dib ugu noqoto. CivilCraft wuxuu xusaa shaqada aad isticmaashay ugu dambeysay kombuutarkan.

Bidix-panelka wuxuu ku daraa saddex qayb sare:

- **Geedaha**: liiska geedaha, si aad u raadiso oo u doorato waxa aad dhigayso. Eeg [Liiska geedaha](topic:plant-list).
- **Hubinta sare-u-qaadista**: falanqaynta janjeerka, fallaadaha socodka iyo biyo-ururka. Eeg [Sare-u-qaadista](topic:landscape-grading).
- **Biyo-qulqulka**: madasha iyo tuubooyinka aad dhigayso xiga, dejinta isha biyaha. Eeg [Biyo-qulqulka](topic:irrigation).

## Waxa ku jira kooxda Beerta

| Qalab | Waxa uu sameeyo | Mawduuca |
|---|---|---|
| **Geed**, **Beer**, **Xaleen** | Geedo keligood, beero beerdhig ah oo lagu buuxiyo masaafada geedaha, xaleen khad dheer | [Beerdhigga](topic:planting) |
| **Sare-u-qaadis** | Madalo, berm-yo, swale-yo, aagagga sare-u-qaadista oo leh barooyinka meel-gaarka ah iyo konturro, hubinta janjeerka iyo socodka | [Sare-u-qaadista](topic:landscape-grading) |
| **Dhul adag** | Dhagax-dhigis, waddooyin, darbiyo, kerb-yo, jid-dhaafyo iyo tallaabooyin oo raacaya dhulka | [Dhul adag](topic:hardscape) |
| **Aagagga** | Aagagga beerta: cawska, beeraha, daaqyada iyo dhul-daboolka oo leh dahaadh, oo lagu jarayo hareeraha dhul adag | [Aagagga beerta](topic:planted-areas) |
| **Heerarka** | Dhulka oo ah shayada qaabeeya dhulka oo leh jarista iyo buuxinta, dabaqa, heerarka meel-gaar, fallaadaha janjeerka, qaybaha khadka | [Wax-beddelayaasha dhulka](topic:ground-modifiers), [Qaybaha](topic:landscape-sections) |

Wax kasta oo aad ku darto waa hal tallaabo oo ka noqod ah, waxaana lagu kaydiyaa faylka mashruuca. Waxaana lagu sawiraa qorshaha lakabyada loo magacaabay `L-PLNT-…`, `L-GRAD-…`, `L-HARD-…` iyo `L-IRRG-…`, si dhoofinta DXF, DWG iyo PDF ay u qaadaan sidoo kale.

## Hal dusha wax kasta

Dhagax-dhigista, waddooyinka, berm-yada, swale-yada, aagagga sare-u-qaadista iyo dhagax-dhigista waxaa loo sare-u-qaadaa **dusha la soo jeediyay**, oo ah isku mid kan ay ku shaqeeyaan jidadka iyo pad-yada. **Dhulka jira** weligiis ma bedelmo. Geedaha waxay saaran yihiin dhulka ku hoos jira, sidaas darteed way u guuraan marka sare-u-qaadistu isbeddesho. Shaqada dhulka, sare-u-qaadista beerta oo dhan ayaa lagu tiriyaa wixii kale.
