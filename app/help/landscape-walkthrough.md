This walkthrough uses the sample site, so you can follow it without a file of your own.

1. **Open the sample site** from the start panel, then {{ui:Surface}} › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Landscape workspace}}.
2. **A terrace.** {{ui:Hardscape}} › {{ui:Paving}}: click four corners on the flatter ground, {{key:Enter}}. In Properties set **Falls** to **To a drain** and click near one corner. The arrows show water running to it.
3. **A retaining wall.** {{ui:Hardscape}} › {{ui:Wall}} along the uphill edge of the terrace, {{key:Enter}}. Read its heights in Properties; type a **Top** to hold it level.
4. **Steps.** {{ui:Hardscape}} › {{ui:Steps}}: click below the wall, then above it, {{key:Enter}}. Properties say how many risers and whether they fit.
5. **A path.** {{ui:Hardscape}} › {{ui:Path}} from the steps down the slope. If it is steeper than its limit, Properties say so and the plan marks where.
6. **Trees.** In **Plants**, search `olive`, choose it, {{ui:Plant}} and click two or three places. Press {{key:Esc}}.
7. **A planting bed.** Search `lavender`, choose it, {{ui:Bed}}, click its corners, {{key:Enter}}. In Properties, choose rosemary on the left and **Add a plant**; set the shares.
8. **A hedge.** Search `box`, {{ui:Hedge}} along the boundary, {{key:Enter}}.
9. **Check the grading.** In **Grading checks**, tick **Slope analysis** and **Flow arrows and ponding**. If water ponds against the terrace, add a {{ui:Swale}} round it.
10. **Irrigation.** Place a **Point of connection**, a **Valve**, a few **Heads** over the lawn and a **Lateral pipe** from the valve through them, then **Check the system…**.
11. **The schedule and the sheets.** More › {{ui:Plant schedule…}} (save it as XLSX, or send it to TakeoffCraft), then More › {{ui:Landscape sheets…}} › **Make the PDF**.
12. **Save** the project: everything is in the one file. More › {{ui:Export landscape IFC}} gives BimCraft the plants and the hardscape.

See [The Landscape workspace](topic:landscape) for every tool.
