Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala ibeka amathuluzi okuklama isikhala sangaphandle esayithini le-CivilCraft osenayo: amaphoyinti okuhlola afanayo, umhlaba okhona ofanayo, umphezulu ohlelwe ofanayo nefayela leprojekthi elifanayo.

## Vula

Khetha {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala}}. Ibha yamathuluzi ibe isikhombisa ukukhetha **Indawo yokusebenza** neqembu le-**Isikhala sangaphandle**, futhi amaqembu e-**Umphezulu**, **Ukuklama** no-**GIS** ayancipha ukuze kwenze indawo. Khetha **Civil** ngaphansi kwe-Indawo yokusebenza ukuze ubuyele emuva. I-CivilCraft ikhumbula indawo yokusebenza osebenzise ekugcineni kulesi kompyutha.

Iphaneli yangakwesobunxele ithola izigaba ezintathu phezulu:

- **Izitshalo**: uhlu lwezitshalo, ukusesha nokukhetha okufakayo. Bona [Uhlu lwezitshalo](topic:plant-list).
- **Ukuhlola kokulinganisa umhlaba**: ukuhlolwa kwethambeka, imicibisholo yokugeleza nokuchitheka kwamanzi. Bona [Ukulinganisa umhlaba](topic:landscape-grading).
- **Ukunisela**: izinhloko nemipayipi ozibeka okulandelayo, izilungiselelo zomthombo wamanzi. Bona [Ukunisela](topic:irrigation).

## Umphezulu owodwa wakho konke

Amaplatifomu, amabheme, amasweyili, izindawo zokulinganisa, ukufaka ipavumenti nemizila kulinganiswa kube **umphezulu ohlelwe**, owufanayo onezindawo namapad nemigwaqo ayisebenzisayo. **Umhlaba okhona** awushintshi ngakanye. Izitshalo zihlala emhlabeni okuphansi kwazo, ngakho ziyanyakaza uma ukulinganisa kushintsha. Umsebenzi womhlaba ubala ukulinganisa kwesikhala sangaphandle kanye nakho konke okunye.

:::
