**Okuhlolwa** ohlwini lwe-Power lines lukhombisa zonke izinkinga, okubomvu okwehlulekayo nokuphuzi okulumbisayo. Chofoza eyodwa ukuze ukhethe umugudu, isekelo, ucingo noma impahla okuyiyo; amasekelo nezinhlu ezehlulekayo zibekwa umbambo obomvu epulanini uma indawo yokusebenza ivulekile.

:::warning
Isisetshenziswa sokuklama: lokhu kuhlola okuklame kwakho ngokwemithetho yakho, hhayi isitifiketi.
:::

## Amagebe

Iphoyinti eliphansi kakhulu lecingo lilandelwa ngayo yonke indawo ngayo yonke imithwalo. Igebe layo eliphansi kakhulu phezu komhlaba kumele lifinyelele **Umthetho wegebe** womugudu. Lapho inhlu idlula **isithiyo** (sidwetshwe nge-{{ui:Isithiyo}}: iphoyinti noma umugqa, nokuphakama kwaso ngaphezu komhlaba nomthetho wegebe waso), igebe ngaphezu kwesiphezulu sesithiyo lihlolwa ngendlela efanayo.

**Okusethwe indawo yokusebenza yeLandscape ngaphezu komhlaba nakho kubalwa.** Izitshalo ezodwa (umqhele wazo ngobubanzi nobude obuvuthiwe), izihlahla namabhedi okutshala (izinhlobo eziphakeme kakhulu kuzo) kanye nezindonga (iphezulu lazo) ziyizithiyo ngaphansi komugudu, ngaphandle kokudweba futhi. Zigcina imithetho esethwe ku-{{ui:Izimo zomthwalo nemikhawulo…}}: **Izitshalo ze-Landscape ngaphansi komugudu** (amasethi esibonelo asebenzisa *Izihlahla nezitshalo*, 3 m) kanye **Izindonga ze-Landscape ngaphansi komugudu** (*Isakhiwo*); khetha **Akuhlolwanga** ukuzishiya. Igebe lilinganiswa liqonde phezulu kusukela phezu kwesitshalo noma kudonga kuya kocingweni lapho inhlu idlula kuwo; ibanga eceleni kumqhele ongaseduze nomugudu alihlolwa. Ukuhambisa isihlahla noma ukuphakamisa udonga kushintsha okuhlolwa ngokushesha.

Imithetho yegebe ingeyakho: vula **Imithetho…** eceleni kwanoma yiliphi **Umthetho wegebe** (noma {{ui:Izimo zomthwalo nemikhawulo…}}) ukuze wengeze, uqambe kabusha, ushintshe noma ususe imithetho.

## Ukuhlukana nokugxumela

- **Ukuhlukana kwezigaba** okudingekayo: k·√(sag + ubude be-insulator) + U/150, kusetshenziswa i-sag yesimo esishisayo nevolovolo U ngo-kV (indlela ejwayelekile; k ingeyomthetho wakho). Iqhathaniswa nokuhlukana kwezigaba kwe-crossarm.
- **Ukugxumela kwe-insulator** emasekelweni okuncika, ngaphansi kwesimo somoya: atan(umthwalo ovundlile ÷ umthwalo oqondile), iqhathaniswe nokugxumela okukhulu kakhulu kohlelo lwemithetho.
- **Ukuphakanyiswa**: isekelo lokuncika elingaphansi kwamakhelwane alo lingahudulwa ngesikhathi sokubanda; liyaphawuleka.

## Imithwalo yamasekelo nosayizi

Ngalo lonke isekelo nesimo ngasinye somthwalo, nazo zonke izintambo zomugudu:

- **Ukuvundla**: umoya ngamitha × ubude bomoya (uhhafu wezinhlu ezimbili), kanye no-2·H·sin(ukugoqa ÷ 2) ekugoqeni.
- **Emugudini**: umehluko wamandla amagceke amabili ekugcineni noma engxenyeni; ezasekelweni lokuncika kuphela ngaphansi kweqhwa ohlangothini olulodwa (inhlu ngayinye isebenza yodwa, okuvikela kakhulu).
- **Phezulu nasezansi**: umthwalo wamitha yesisindo × ubude bokusinda (phakathi kwamaphoyinti aphansi kakhulu ngakolunye nakolunye uhlangothi) kanye nempahla.

Umthwalo ovundlile uyiswa kwiphoyinti elihlongozwayo lesekelo (umthwalo olinganayo = umthwalo × ukuphakama kokunamathela ÷ ukuphakama kwephoyinti elihlongozwayo) futhi uqhathaniswe **nomthwalo ohlongozwayo** × isici sohlelo lwemithetho. Uma uthambile, isekelo elincane kakhulu lohlobo olufanayo ocwaningweni lwakho elithwala lo mthwalo liyaphakanyiswa. Isekelo esinentambo yokubamba (stay) sidlulisela umthwalo ovundlile kuyo, ethwala umthwalo ÷ sin(i-engile yentambo) futhi ihlolwe ngesilinganiso sayo.

Izixhumi (izibambi, ama-insulator, ama-crossarm) zihlolwa ngomthwalo wazo ovunyelwe: ixhumi lokubamba lithwala ukuqina kocingo, ixhumi lokuncika lithwala ingxenye yalo yomthwalo ophezulu nezansi nowokuvundla. **Isisekelo** esiboniswayo ukujula kwesekelo emhlabeni nomgodi obanzi kancane kunonyawo lwalo.

## Idatha, ukuxhumeka nokugeleza kwamandla

- Into enensimu edingekayo engenalutho, noma ukhiye ongekho emtapweni.
- Ukuphela kohlelo lwekhebula, noma impahla, engaxhunywanga kutho.
- **Ukwehla kovolovolo** nokugeleza **kwamandla** kusuka kumthombo ngamunye (impahla ephawulwe njengomthombo) kuya emithwalweni (i-kW ne-power factor kwimpahla), kuwo amagebe okuhamba phezulu nakukhebula: √3·I·L·(R cos φ + X sin φ), ngo-R ku-20 °C. Okungaphezu kwesilinganiso sokugeleza kwamanje sekhebula, noma ukwehla okungaphezu kwalokho okuvunyelwe ngumthetho, kuyaphawuleka.
