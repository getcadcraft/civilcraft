I-CivilCraft ineskrini eyodwa. Ayinawo amathebhu futhi ayinawo amalayawudi. Amaphaneli asohlangothini angahamba, ahlale esikhundleni, noma aye kwiwindi lawo: bheka [Hlela amaphaneli usebenzise isikrini sesibili](common:arrange-panels). Kusuka phezulu kuya ezansi nakwesobunxele kuya kwesokudla:

- **ibha yohlelo** (ulogo ye-CadCraft, igama lohlelo, inguqulo, Mayelana, imodi yeskrini, Usizo, Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, Iphepha noModeli)
- **ibha yamathuluzi** enamaqembu ayisikhombisa wezinkinobho
- **iphaneli yesobunxele**: Indawo, Amaphoyinti, Umphezulu, Ukubonisa, kanye nemigqa yesakhiwo esiyengeza emva kokuthi uthenge amaphoyinti
- **ipulani** phakathi, nebuka lephrofayili ngaphansi kwalo uma livuliwe
- **iphaneli yesokudla**: **Izakhiwo** zento ekhethiwe
- **ibha yesimo** esiphansi

## Ibha yohlelo

Ibha ebanzi kakhulu phezulu ingeyesikhungo se-CadCraft, hhayi i-CivilCraft uqobo.

- Imenyu yenguqulo ibonisa okwakhiwe okusebenzayo.
- {{ui:About CivilCraft}} ivula ikhasi lalolu hlelo esiteshini.
- {{ui:Full screen}} ijikeleza phakathi kwemodi zeskrini: iskrini esigcwele, bese iskrini esigcwele namabha efihliwe, bese kubuyela ekujwayeleni. {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} yenza okufanayo kuzo zonke izinhlelo, kubandakanya i-Mac (kuleyo nhlelo kuyinkinobho yeControl, hhayi i-Command). Uma amabha efihliwe, hambisa isikhombisi emphethelweni ongaphezulu ukuze ubuyise.
- {{ui:Help}} (noma {{key:F1}}) ivula lo siza eyiphaneli ecaleni; {{key:F1}} noma {{key:Esc}} ivala.
- {{ui:Save for offline}} igcina i-CivilCraft kulesi siphequluli ukuze ivule ngaphandle kokuxhumeka; bese ibonisa {{ui:Offline ready}}. Bheka [Amafayela ongawavula, ongawalondoloza nongawathumela](topic:files#online-and-offline).
- {{ui:Paper}} ne-{{ui:Model}} zishintsha phakathi kwesihloko esikhanyayo nesimnyama. Ipulani nebuka lephrofayili zilandela isihloko; amaPDF njalo aphrintwa emhlophe.

## Ibha yamathuluzi

Iqembu ngalinye linengcazelo encane phezu kwezinkinobho zalo. Hambisa isikhombisi phezu kwenkinobho ukuze ubone isiqondiso sayo nokhiye.

| Iqembu | Izinkinobho | Okwenzayo |
|---|---|---|
| Ifayela | {{ui:New}}, {{ui:Open}}, {{ui:Save}} | Qala kabusha, vula ifayela lamaphoyinti, i-LandXML noma iphrojekthi, londoloza iphrojekthi njengokulandwa |
| Thumela | {{ui:LandXML}}, {{ui:DXF}}, {{ui:DWG}}, {{ui:PDF}} | Bhala i-LandXML 1.2, i-DXF ye-R12 noma i-DWG yepulani, noma uphrinte ipulani ibe yi-PDF |
| Umphezulu | {{ui:Breakline}}, {{ui:Boundary}} | Dweba umugqa wokwephula kudlula emaphoyinteni okuhlola; dweba umngcele wangaphandle |
| Ukuklama | {{ui:Alignment}}, {{ui:Pad}}, {{ui:Pipes}}, {{ui:Profile}} | Dweba umzila, ipedi, umugqa wamapayipi; bonisa noma ufihle ibuka lephrofayili |
| Hlela | {{ui:Undo}}, {{ui:Redo}}, {{ui:Copy}}, {{ui:Paste}}, {{ui:More}} | Hlehlisa, phinda wenze, kopisha namathisela amaphoyinti okuhlola; {{ui:More}} inezinketho zokusika, Namathisela endaweni, Phinda, Susa, Khetha wonke amaphoyinti nokuthi Ungakhethi lutho |
| GIS | {{ui:Coordinates}}, {{ui:Import}}, {{ui:Export}}, {{ui:Map}} | Setha uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi, ngenisa nethumele amafayela e-GIS, bonisa izendlalelo zemephu ngaphansi kwepulani |
| Ukubuka | {{ui:Select}}, {{ui:Fit}}, {{ui:3D}} | Khetha futhi uhambise izinto; sondeza ukuze kubonakale konke; bonisa indawo nge-3D ([Buka indawo nge-3D](topic:site-3d)) |

Ithuluzi lokudweba lihlala licindezelwe kuze kusebenze. Ukuchofoza kabusha, ukucindezela {{key:Esc}} noma ukucindezela {{key:V}} kubuyisela ku-{{ui:Select}}. {{ui:Undo}}, {{ui:Redo}}, {{ui:Copy}} nazo zonke izinto ku-{{ui:More}} ziyafiphala uma zingasebenzi; isiqondiso sithi kungani (isibonelo "Khetha amaphoyinti okuhlola kuqala"). Yonke inkinobho idwetshwe ku-[Ireferensi yebha yamathuluzi](topic:toolbar).

Esikrinini esincane ibha yamathuluzi iyaskrola ngakwesobunxele nakwesokudla.

## Iphaneli yesobunxele

### Indawo

- **Igama**: igama lephrojekthi. Liqamba wonke amafayela olondoloza noma othumela futhi liphrintwa esikhundleni sesihloko se-PDF.
- **Iyunithi**: {{ui:US feet}} noma {{ui:Metres}}. Lokhu kuchaza ukuthi izinombolo zisho ini. Ukushintsha lokhu **akuguquli** amakhoordinethi; bheka [Iyunithi, izitezi nefomethi yezinombolo](topic:units-and-stations).

### Amaphoyinti

Uma engenalutho, utshela ukuthi uvule kanjani ifayela lamaphoyinti. Uma kunamaphoyinti, ibonisa igama lefayela, inani lamaphoyinti nokuthi angaki anokuphakama, bese kuba ithebhula lama-**qembu wamaphoyinti**: umugqa owodwa ngegama lokuqala lencazelo (GRD, FL, TBS …), kanye nenani nokukhetha **Umphezulu**:

- {{ui:Points}}: amaphoyinti angena emphezulwini (okuzenzakalelayo kuwo wonke amaqembu).
- {{ui:Breakline}}: amaphoyinti eqembu ayahlanganiswa, ngokulandelana kwenombolo yephoyinti, abe umugqa wokwephula owodwa.
- {{ui:Left out}}: iqembu alithinti umphezulu.

### Umphezulu

Izinto mayelana nomhlaba okhona: amaphoyinti, onxantathu, ukuphakama okuphansi nokuphezulu, ukuphakama okuphakathi, izindawo ezi-2D nezi-3D, imigqa yokwephula nokuthi kukhona umngcele yini. Ngaphansi kwezinto kukhona ibhokisi elidwetsha noma yini umakhi womphezulu obelana ngokulungisa noma ayingenakukwazi ukuyenza. Bese kuba:

- **Umphetho wangaphandle omude kakhulu**: 0 igcina wonke onxantathu bangaphandle; inani lisika onxantathu abade nabamancane ngaphandle.
- Izixhumanisi ezivela uma zisebenza: {{ui:Remove the boundary}}, {{ui:Remove drawn breaklines}} kanye, kumphezulu ofundwe ku-LandXML, {{ui:Rebuild the surface from the points}}.

### Ukubonisa

- **Umphezulu**: {{ui:Existing ground}} noma {{ui:Proposed (graded)}}. Okwesibili kutholakala uma ipedi noma ikoridoro sekuqhubeke ngaphandle kwezinkinga.
- **Contours every**, **Major every** kanye **Bands** (inani lamabhande emibala).
- Amabhokisi okuhlola: {{ui:Contours}}, {{ui:Contour labels}}, {{ui:Triangles}}, {{ui:Elevation bands}}, {{ui:Slope arrows}}, {{ui:Points}}, {{ui:Point labels}}, {{ui:Breaklines}}, {{ui:Surface edge}}, {{ui:Pipes}}.
- Incazelo yokuphakama kwamabhande ivela uma amabhande evuliwe.

Izilungiselelo zokubonisa zilondolozwa nephrojekthi, kodwa azilona izinyathelo zokuhlehlisa: {{key:Mod+Z}} akuyi buyisi ibhokisi lokuhlola.

## Ipulani

Ipulani libonisa amaphoyinti, umphezulu nakho konke okuklama, ngombono wepulani nenorth phezulu. Phendula ivili ukuze usondeze endaweni yesikhombisi, hudula isikhala esingenalutho ukuze uhambe. Uhlu olugcwele lusenkombeni [Amakhiyi nembewu](topic:keyboard).

- Kuphrojekthi engenalutho ibhokisi elithi **Qala ngamaphoyinti okuhlola** lidwetsha izinyathelo zokuqala futhi linikeza {{ui:Open the sample site}}.
- Uma ithuluzi lokudweba lisebenza, isiqondiso phezulu kwepulani sitshela okufanele uchofoze kanye nokuthi uqede kanjani.
- Ukuhudula ifayela phezu kwepulani kubonisa ukuthi "Lahla ifayela lamaphoyinti, i-LandXML noma iphrojekthi".
- Izinto ezikhethiwe ziyakhanyiswa. I-alignment noma ipedi ekhethiwe ibonisa izibambisi ezinesikwele ezindaweni zayo ze-PI noma amakhona, kanti isakhiwo esikhethiwe sibonisa isibambisi esisodwa. Hudula isibambisi ukuze uhambise.
- Amaphoyinti okuhlola akhethiwe anendandatho. {{key:Shift}} nokuchofoza kwengeza noma kususe iphoyinti; {{key:Shift}} nokudonsa kudweba ibhokisi (noma, udonse uzungeze, i-lasso) elengeza amaphoyinti angaphakathi kwalo. Bheka [Khetha, kopisha namathisela amaphoyinti okuhlola](topic:copy-paste-points).

## Ibuka lephrofayili

Cindezela {{key:R}} noma chofoza {{ui:Profile}} ukuze uyivule ngaphansi kwepulani. Ibha yalo inalezi zinto:

- uhlu lwemizila (luyasebenza uma kunemizila emibili noma ngaphezulu)
- {{ui:Add PVIs}}: uma icindezelwe, ukuchofoza kuphrofayili kwengeza i-PVI ({{key:I}} iyishintsha)
- ukufunda kwesiteshi, umhlaba, ukuphakama okuklanyelwe, ibanga nokukhipha noma ukugcwalisa ngaphansi kwesikhombisi
- inkinobho yokuvala

Iphrofayili libonisa umhlaba okhona, iphrofayili yokuklama, amabanga ezindaweni ngayinye, izinombolo ze-PVI ngayinye nokuphakama kwayo, amajika aqondile ngamunye nawo L nawo K nawo BVC nawo EVC, amaphuzu aphezulu nalawo aphansi, nokuqina okuphakanyisiwe. Ihlala ilingana nomzila wonke: ayisondezi noma ayihambisi. Ukuhambisa isikhombisi kuyo kubeka ichashazi emzileni epulanini.

## Iphaneli yesokudla: Izakhiwo

Isihloko sayo sisho ukuthi ibonisani.

- **Iphrojekthi** (akukho okukhethiwe): imizila namapedi njengezixhumanisi, ibhlokhi ye-**Umsebenzi womhlaba**, nebhlokhi ye-**Inethiwekhi yamapayipi** nemithetho yayo nezinkinga zayo.
- **Umzila**: igama, isiteshi sokuqala, ubude, ithebhula le-PI, amajika, ithebhula le-PVI nezinto zephrofayili yokuklama, nezilungiselelo ze-**Ikoridoro**.
- **Ipedi**: igama, ukuphakama, amathambeka okukhipha nokugcwalisa, indawo, izinto zokukhanya kwemini nomsebenzi womhlaba.
- **Isakhiwo** noma **Ipayipi**: amasayizi, imiphetho, izinsimbi, ukuthamba, isendlalelo nanoma yiziphi izinkinga.
- **Iphoyinti** nenombolo yalo: amakhoordinethi, ukuphakama, incazelo nelungu leqembu (ukufunda kuphela).

Izinkambu zisebenza uma ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uphuma enkambeni. Inani elingamukelwanga liphawulwa njengelingasebenzi futhi akukho okushintshayo.

## Ibha yesimo

- **E** no-**N**: ukuya empumalanga nasenyakatho ngaphansi kwesikhombisi.
- Ukuphakama **komhlaba** lapho (no-**Graded** lapho ipedi noma ikoridoro sishintshe khona), noma "Ngaphandle komphezulu".
- Umzila osondele kakhulu (noma owukhethiwe) ne-**Sta** nokuphambuka (**L** noma **R**) ngaphansi kwesikhombisi.
- Ngakwesokudla, imiyalezo: okwenzeke ngokushesha, noma ukuthi kungani kwenqatshwe okuthile. Umyalezo uyanyamalala wodwa ngemva kwemizuzwana embalwa.

## Izingxoxo

- **Ngenisa amaphoyinti**: ivulwa ngamafayela wamaphoyinti wonke. Bheka [Ngenisa amaphoyinti okuhlola](topic:import-points).
- **Phrinta ipulani ibe yi-PDF**: bheka [Phrinta ipulani ibe yi-PDF](topic:plot-pdf).

## Amaskrini amancane

Kuze kube ngamapikseli ayi-760 ububanzi, njengefoni, ipulani ligcina iskrini: ibha yamathuluzi iba umugqa owodwa oskrola ngakwesobunxele nakwesokudla, neziphaneli zivula njengamashidi kusuka kubha ephansi. I-{{ui:Tools}} ifihla ibha yamathuluzi, ne-{{ui:Move}} iyibeka ezansi.
