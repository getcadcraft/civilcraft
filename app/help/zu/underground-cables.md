## Dweba umzila wentambo

1. Beka imishini kuqala uma intambo iphela ekhabhinethini, ku-transformer noma kwixhumanisi: {{ui:Imishini}}, bese uchofoza.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Ikhebuli}} bese uchofoza umzila; qala futhi uphele kusipoti noma kumshini ukuze uxhume kuwo. {{key:Enter}} iqeda.

Ku-**Izakhiwo**, khetha i-**Intambo**, i-**Uhlobo lomfucumbi**, izintambo ezingaki ngokuhlangana, kanye ne-**ukukhululeka** (%). Umzila ubonisa ubude bawo, lapho uqala khona nalapho uphela khona, nomsinga owuthwalayo.

## Yini ehlolwayo

- **Ukumbozwa**: uphahla lwentambo ngaphansi komhlaba emzileni, ngokuqhathaniswa nokumbozwa kohlobo lomfucumbi (noma ukumbozwa okuncane kwesethi yemithetho).
- **Ukuwela**: lapho umzila uwela ipayipi le-CivilCraft noma enye intambo, igebe phakathi kwawo liqhathaniswa nomthetho wokukhululeka: **Ukukhululeka kokuwela** komzila (ku-**Izakhiwo** zawo) uma ukhetha lowo, kungenjalo umthetho othi **Underground crossings** wesethi yemithetho (amasethi esibonelo asebenzisa *Underground crossing*, 0.3 m), kungenjalo igebe lohlobo lomfucumbi kwezinye izinsiza. Izintambo ezimbili eziwelana zigcina elikhulu kulawo mabili. Umugqa ophezulu owelwayo ubhalwa njengolwazi, futhi intambo ibonakala kuphrofayili yalowo mugqa.
- **Izixhumanisi**: eyodwa ngobude ngabunye bedramu yentambo (nokukhululeka), uma into yentambo inikeza ubude bedramu.

## Imishini

Imishini inelungu lomtapo, **umthwalo** (kW) nesilinganiso sawo samandla, futhi ingaba **umthombo**: inethiwekhi iphakelwa kuwo. Ivolthi yomthombo ivela kulungu lawo lomtapo, noma kulayini noma kwintambo oyiphakelayo.

## Izinani
