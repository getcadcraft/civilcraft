Ku-CivilCraft kunomphezulu omunye womhlaba okhona ngephrojekthi ngayinye. Uyi-TIN (triangulated irregular network) eakhiwe ngamaphoyinti akho, futhi uyakhiwa kabusha njalo lapho kushintsha okungenayo. Akukho umyalo we-"rebuild" futhi akukho umphezulu ophelelwe yisikhathi.

## Okungena emphezulwini

- Yonke iphoyinti elinokuphakama kweqembu elisethelwe ku-{{ui:Amaphoyinti}}.
- Yonke iqembu elisethelwe ku-{{ui:Umugqa wokuphula}}, lixhunyiwe ngokulandelana kwenombolo yephoyinti.
- Yonke imigqa yokwephula odwetshe nge-{{ui:Umugqa wokuphula}}.
- Umngcele wangaphandle, uma uwudwetshe.
- Isethingi ethi **Umugqa wangaphandle omude kakhulu**.

Ama-triangle awasoze adlule emgqeni wokwephula: umphezulu uphoqelelwa ukuthi uwulandele, bese wenziwa ulingane ngangokunokwenzeka uwuzungeze (Delaunay kwenye indawo yonke).

## Yenza imigqa ekhodiwe ibe yimigqa yokwephula

1. Ngakwesobunxele ngaphansi kwe-**Amaphoyinti**, thola iqembu (isibonelo FL).
2. Setha ukukhetha kwalo kwe-**Umphezulu** sibe {{ui:Umugqa wokuphula}}.
   Umphezulu uyakhiwa kabusha, umugqa udwetshwe ngewolintshi (ngokuvula {{ui:Imigqa yokwephula}} ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa**), futhi **Imigqa yokwephula** kuhlolo lomphezulu iyanyuka ngu-1.
3. Hlola umugqa kwiplani. Kufanele uhambe ephoyinteni ngephoyinti ngokulandelana okwadutshulwa ngakho. Uma ugoqa, iqembu linemigqa engaphezu kweyodwa, noma amaphoyinti awanalandelana ngenombolo: setha iqembu libuyele ku-{{ui:Amaphoyinti}} bese udweba imigqa ngesandla (isigaba esilandelayo).

## Dweba umugqa wokwephula udlula emaphoyinteni

1. Cindezela {{key:B}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Umugqa wokuphula}} kuqembu le-**Umphezulu**.
   Isiphakamiso sithi "Umugqa wokuphula: chofoza amaphoyinti okuhlola ngokulandelana (uthatha ukuphakama kwawo). Enter iyaqeda."
2. Hambisa isikhombisi eduze kwephoyinti. Isikwele esincane sewolintshi sibonisa iphoyinti ezolithatha.
3. Chofoza amaphoyinti omugqa ngamunye ngemuva kwelinye. Ukuchofoza ngakunye kuqoka iphoyinti nokuphakama kwalo kwibha yesimo.
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda.
   Ibha yesimo ithi "Umugqa wokuphula 1 wengeziwe; umphezulu wakhiwa kabusha."

Ukuchofoza kufanele kuwele phezu kwephoyinti lokuhlola elinokuphakama; ukuchofoza okunye kuyenqatshwa ngalo umyalezo othi "Chofoza iphoyinti lokuhlola elinokuphakama: umugqa wokuphula uthatha ukuphakama kwamaphoyinti." {{key:Backspace}} ukhipha iphoyinti lokugcina; {{key:Esc}} ukhansela umugqa wonke.

:::warning
Qeda ngo-{{key:Enter}}. Ukuchofoza kabili nakho kuyaqeda, kodwa kule bhilidi kukhipha iphoyinti owalichofoze kabili. Chofoza iphoyinti lokugcina kanye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
:::

Ukuze ususe yonke imigqa yokwephula edwetshiwe ngesikhathi esisodwa, chofoza {{ui:Susa imigqa yokuphula edwetshiwe}} esigabeni se-**Umphezulu**. Ukuze ususe owokugcina kuphela, sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}}. Imigqa yokwephula edwetshiwe ayikakwazi ukukhethwa noma ukuhlelwa ngayodwa.

## Dweba umngcele wangaphandle

Umngcele unquma umphezulu ube sendaweni oyethembayo, isibonelo imingcele yokuhlola, ukuze ama-triangle amade kumakhona angahlolwanga anyamalale.

1. Cindezela {{key:O}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Umngcele}}.
2. Chofoza amakhona ngokulandelana aphezu kwendawo. Umugqa oqoshiwe ubonisa isimo.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
   Ibha yesimo ithi "Umngcele usethiwe; ama-triangle angaphandle kuwo asusiwe." **Umngcele** kuhlolo lomphezulu uthi "Yebo" kanye nenani lamakhona.

Okufanele ukwazi:

- Kunomngcele owodwa. Ukudweba omunye uwushintsha. {{ui:Susa umngcele}} uwususa.
- Ungawunciphisa kuphela: umngcele odlula amaphoyinti uyancishwa ufike emgqeni wawo wangaphandle, ngesaziso "Umngcele udlula amaphoyinti; uncishisiwe ufike emgqeni wawo."
- Amakhona awo athatha ukuphakama kwawo emphezulwini, ngakho umphetho ulandela umhlaba ngokunembile.
- Imingcele efihliwe (yangaphakathi) ayikatholakali okwamanje.

## Qeda ama-triangle angaphandle amade

Ngaphandle komngcele, umphezulu ugcwalisa wonke umjikelezo wamaphoyinti, kubandakanya ama-triangle amade amancane emaphethelweni angaphandle.

1. Ku-**Umphezulu**, bhala ubude ku-**Umugqa wangaphandle omude kakhulu** bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Qala ngokuncamelayo ngamahlandla amabili noma amathathu ubude bokuhamba kwakho okujwayelekile.
2. Ama-triangle angaphandle amaphethelo angaphandle wawo emade kunalobo ayasuswa, ngokuqhubeka ngaphakathi kuze kube yilapho wonke amaphethelo angaphandle emfushane ngokwanele. Umphethelo oyimigqa yokwephula awukaze ususwe.
3. Bhala 0 ukuze ugcine zonke ama-triangle futhi.

## Funda umbiko womphezulu

Ngaphansi kwehlolo lomphezulu, ibhokisi liyakhombisa ukuthi umakhi wenzeni. Ibha ephuzi ngakwesobunxele isho ulwazi; ibha ebomvu iqukethe iphutha futhi akukho mphezulu.

| Umyalezo | Incazelo |
|---|---|
| "Amaphoyinti angu-3 ayesendaweni efanayo nelinye futhi ahlangana nalo." | Amaphoyinti aseduze kakhulu kunango-0.0001 ku-plani aba yiphoyinti eyodwa (okokuqala kugcinwa). |
| "Izindawo ezi-2 zinokuphakama okuhlukile …" | Amaphoyinti angaphezu kwelinye, noma imigqa yokwephula ehlanganayo, aphikisana ngokuphakama. Ukuphakama komugqa wokwephula kuyanqoba, bese kuthathwa okokuqala. |
| "Ucezu olulodwa lomugqa wokwephula alukwazanga ukuphoqwa emphezulwini." | Ucezu omfushane kakhulu noma oluhlanganayo kakhulu alungenele; hlola lowo ndawo. |
| "Umphezulu udinga okungenani amaphoyinti amathathu anokuphakama." | Amaphoyinti amancane kakhulu: hlola ukuhleleka kwekholomu namaqembu. |
| "Wonke amaphoyinti ku-mugqa owodwa: akukho ama-triangle." | Amaphoyinti alingana nomugqa owodwa, isibonelo umugqa oyodwa wokuhamba. |
| "Umngcele awuhlanganisi namaphoyinti." | Umngcele usendaweni ehlukile, ngokuvamile kuwumdidiyo wamayunithi noma wamakhoordinethi. |
| "Akukho ama-triangle asele ngaphakathi komngcele." | Umngcele mncane kakhulu noma ubekwe kabi. |

## Umphezulu ofundwe ku-LandXML

Uma uvula i-LandXML, umphezulu wayo wokuqala we-TIN usetshenziswa njengoba unjalo: ihedisi ethi **Umphezulu** ithi "Umphezulu (ovela ku-LandXML)" futhi **Umugqa wangaphandle omude kakhulu** uyafiphala. Ngenkathi lowo mphezulu usasetshenziswa, amaqembu emigqa yokwephula, imigqa yokwephula edwetshiwe nomngcele omusha akuwushintshi. Ukuze wakhe umphezulu kumaphoyinti efayela, chofoza {{ui:Akha kabusha indawo kumaphoyinti}}. {{key:Mod+Z}} ubuyisela umphezulu we-LandXML.
