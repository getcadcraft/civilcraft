## Okuzofunda

Okwenzeka eprojekthini uma abantu abaningi besebenza kuyo ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqala futhi ijoyine iseshini ye-Live TeamWork (Yabelana live, Joyina); leli khasi lichaza iprojekthi ngokwayo.

## Konke kusebenza ngesikhathi esisodwa

Amaphoyinti kanye nokuklama komphathi kufika kubo bonke abajoyina. Hlela amaphoyinti, udwebe ukuhambisana, amapheja, imigqa yezici namapaseli njengoba ujwayelekile. Ukushintsha kwakho kubonakala masinyane; ukushintsha kwabanye kuvela emzuzwini ngemva kokwenza kwabo.

- **Amaphoyinti** ahlanganiswa kanye kanye: abantu ababili abahambisa amaphoyinti amabili bayagcina kokubili izinguquko zabo.
- **Ukuhambisana, amapheja, imigqa yezici namapaseli** ahlanganiswa ngento nangesilungiselelo ngasinye; isilungiselelo esifanayo siyahamba ngoshintsho lwakamuva.
- **Umphezulu** wakhiwa kuwo wonke amawindi kusuka emaphoyinteni nasemigqeni yokwehlukanisa ofanayo, ngakho imigqa yokuma nevolumu ziyavumelana.

Akwabelwani eseshini: umphezulu olayishwe kusuka kufayela legridi, izici zezendlalelo ze-GIS, izithombe zemephu kanye namafayela okushintsha igridi. Zihlala kuwindi okuvule kuwo.

## Babonane

Wonke umuntu eseshini unombala wakhe. Epulanini ubona inkomba yomuntu ngamunye njengetshaza emibaleni yakhe negama lakhe eduze kwayo, kanye nebhokisi elinemigqa ezungeze amaphoyinti nento akhethe. Ngenkathi ibonakalo ye-3D ivulekile phezu kwepulani, izinkomba zilinda kuze kuvalwe.

Izinkomba, amagama nokukhethwa kuyizinto zeseshini kuphela: azisoze zigcinwe kufayela futhi azisoze zingene ku-undo yomuntu. Ziyashelela ziye lapho kukhona umuntu ngamunye, ngakho zihlala zishelelayo nasexhunyweni elibuthakathaka.

## Hlehlisa kwenza umsebenzi wakho

{{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa ushintsho lwakho lokugcina kuphela futhi ishiye oluntshintshe abanye. Uma omunye umuntu ekuguqule into efanayo kusukela ngaleso sikhathi, akukho ohlehliswayo futhi ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi ngubani.

## Joyina kusiphequluli

Isisebenzi esingenayo i-Studio singaskena isithombe ngaphansi kwekhodi yomphathi ngefoni, ithebulethi noma ikhompyutha ekunethiwekhi efanayo. I-CivilCraft ivula kusiphequluli futhi igcwaliswe yiprojekthi yomphathi uma umphathi evuma ukungena. Isixhumanisi sokumema sivumela umuntu ukuthi ajoyine kolunye uxhumano: umphathi udinga isihlalo seTeam Cloud Plan sohlelo, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi siphumelela kuphela uma iseshini yomphathi ivuliwe.

## Lapho iseshini iphela

Umphathi kuphela ogcina iprojekthi. Abanye bonke baguga ikhophi engagcinwanga: yigcine uma ufuna ukuyigcina.
