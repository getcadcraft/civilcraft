Konke lapha kusetshenziswa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Okuningi}} eqenjini le-Power lines.

| Okuphumayo | Okutholayo |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Amathebula okufaka umugqa…}} | Ngesigaba ngasinye, ukuqina okuthambile nokuhla kokufaka umugqa ngakunye ngakho konke ukushisa; londoloza njenge-CSV. {{ui:Londoloza ithebhula lezinsika (CSV)}}: indima yesekelo ngalinye, impahla, umhlaba, ukunamathela, umthwalo omkhulu kakhulu, ohlongozwayo, OK, isiphakamiso, isisekelo, ukugxumela, ukuphakanyiswa |
| {{ui:Amanani…}} | Ucingo (ubude be-catenary × imigudu × amasekethe + 2 % wama-jumper nezikhala ezifile), amasekelo nempahla ngokwento yomtapo, ikhebula, umsele, ukumba, ama-duct, amaxhuma. I-CSV, i-Excel, noma ifayela lezinani le-TakeoffCraft |
| {{ui:Amashidi nombiko…}} | I-PDF eyodwa: ipulani; ishidi lephrofayili lomugudu ngamunye ngocingo ezimweni zomthwalo ozikhethela zona nesiqephu sobude besigaba, izinhlu nokuhla kanye namagebe aphansi kakhulu; nombiko wocwaningo (uhlelo lwemithetho, izigaba, amathebula okufaka umugqa, amasekelo, nakho konke okuhlolwa) |
| {{ui:Hambisa ku-GIS…}} | Amasekelo, izigaba, imigudu yamakhebula, impahla nezithiyo njengezingqimba nedatha yazo (ukuphakama, imithwalo, ukuhla, amagebe, okwehlulekayo), njenge-GeoPackage, ama-shapefile, i-GeoJSON, i-KML noma i-KMZ |

I-GeoJSON ne-KML zidinga uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi lwephrojekthi ([Uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi](topic:coordinate-systems)); ngaphandle kwalo, i-GeoPackage ne-shapefile zithwala izinombolo zephrojekthi ngokwazo.
