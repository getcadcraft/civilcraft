| Ishidi | Okukuwo | Isimpawu |
|---|---|---|
| **Ipulani yokutshala** | Izitshalo, amabhedi, imiduli nezakhiwo zangaphandle phezu komhlaba | Uhlu lwezitshalo nophawu lwesitshalo ngasinye, igama lesayensi nelivamile, isitsha nenani |
| **Ipulani yokulinganisa umhlaba** | Amakhonthuwa, amazinga amaphoyinti, imigqa yokulinganisa, izindawo ezinamathambeka, imicibisholo yokugeleza namachibi, amazinga ezakhiwo zangaphandle | Ukuthi impawu ngayinye isho ini |
| **Ipulani yokunisela** | Izinhloko nokumbozwa kwazo, amapayipi, ama-valvu, umthombo, phezu kwezakhiwo zangaphandle nemigqa yamabhedi | Izinhloko ngohlobo nengxenye yesiyingi nophawu lwazo, ipayipi ngosayizi, ama-valvu, ukugeleza nokucindezeleka kwesifunda ngasinye |
| **Imininingwane** | Isigaba sendonga ngendawo yayo ephakeme kakhulu (umhlaba, isisekelo, udonga, ukugoqa, isendlalelo sokukhipha amanzi), isakhiwo ngasinye sokufaka ipavumenti, izinyathelo ngalinye ngokuphakama kwalo | Imininingwane ngayinye ibiza isikali sayo |

Khetha **iphepha**, **isikali** (elilingana likhethelwa wena) kanye **nombala** noma **okumnyama kuphela**. Ipulani ngayinye iyipulani yesayithi nezingqimba zamanye amakhono zishiywe ngaphandle, ngakho okubonakala esikrinini kuyafana nalokho okuphrintwayo.

Izigaba ezisemigwaqweni nasemizileni ziku-[Izigaba](topic:sections); ishidi lepulani lesayithi yonke lingu-Ifayela › PDF.
