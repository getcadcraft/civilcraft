Amashidi eplani neprofayili yindlela okuvame ukudwetshwa ngayo amaplani emigwaqo: ishidi ngalinye libonisa ingxenye yomgwaqo kabili, iplani phezulu nephrofayili ngaphansi, ngesikali esifanayo esiswini, ukuze isiteshi sihlale endaweni efanayo kokubili.

## Yenza amashidi

1. Khetha i-alignment. Ku-**Izakhiwo**, chofoza i-{{ui:Plan and profile sheets}}.
2. Khetha i-**Iphepha**, i-**Isikali seplani** kanye ne-**Ukukhulisa kweprofayili** (kangaki okuphakeme ngayo iprofayili kunendelelo yayo; 10× ukuqala). Ingxoxo itshela ukuthi amashidi angaki akhiwe nokuthi kangakanani kwe-alignment ngalinye elibonisa.
3. Ukuze uhlele ingxenye ye-alignment, shintsha i-**Kusukela esiteshini** kanye ne-**Kuya esiteshini**.
4. Chofoza i-{{ui:Download PDF}}. I-PDF inekhasi elilodwa ngeshidi ngalinye.

## Okusesidini

| Ingxenye | Okubonisayo |
|---|---|
| Iplani | Konke okusepulanini (amakhonthuwa, amaphoyinti okuhlola, i-alignment nesiteshi sayo, amakhoridoro, amapadi, imigqa yezakhiwo, amapayipi, imigqa yesampula) kujikelezwe ukuze lesi sikhala sihambe phambi kweshidi, kanye nomcibisholo wasenyakatho ojikelezwe ukuze uhambisane |
| Imigqa yokuhlangana | Umugqa wedash-dot ocaleni kwe-alignment kowo wonke umphetho oqhubeka kwelinye ishidi, ulebulwe ngokuthi "Umugqa wokuhlangana sta 5+50.00, bheka ishidi 2" |
| Iphrofayili | Umhlaba okhona (ohlanganisiwe), iphrofayili yomklamo (ebomvu), ukuthambeka kwangalunye lwe-tangent kanye ne-PVI, ukuphakama, i-L ne-K yawo wonke amajika ahlukile; amasiteshi aphansi, nokuphakama ohlangothini lwemibili |
| Ibhulokhi yesihloko | Igama lephrojekthi, i-alignment kanye nobubanzi besiteshi sawo, "Ishidi 1 kwangu-3", izikali ezithe xaxa nezithe ngqo kanye nokukhulisa |

Isikhala ngeshidi ngalinye sinde njengoba iwindi leplani livumela ngesikali, sijikelezwe sibe ngu-50 ft ophelele (20 m) emigwaqweni emide noma ngu-10 ft ophelele (5 m) emifushane.

:::note
Uma ukuphakama kweprofayili kungeke kulingane ngokukhulisa okukhethile, i-CivilCraft idweba wonke amashidi ngokukhulisa okukhulu kakhulu phakathi kwe-10×, 5×, 4×, 2× ne-1× okulingana, futhi ibha yesimo iyakutshela lokho. I-alignment ejikelezile iyajikeleza ngokuya kwendlela ukusuka esiqalweni kuye esiphethweni kwesikhala ngasinye, ngakho umgwaqo ungase uhambe kancane phezulu noma phansi eshidini.
:::
