Indawo ibonakala ngamakhonthuwa uma isivele ikhona. Konke okusesihlokweni lesi kusethwa ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa** ohlangothini lwesobunxele. Izilungiselelo zokubonisa zishintsha okubonakalayo kuphela (nokuthumela ngaphandle); azisona isinyathelo sokuhlehlisa.

## Setha ibanga lamakhonthuwa

1. Ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa**, khetha inani ku-**Amakhonthuwa njalo**.
   - Ngamafidi: 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 noma 20 ft.
   - Ngamamitha: 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 noma 10 m.
2. Khetha **Amakhonthuwa amakhulu njalo**: ikhonthuwa lesi-2, lesi-4, lesi-5 noma lesi-10.
   Amakhonthuwa amakhulu adwetshwa ngokujulile futhi aphawulwe ngokuphakama kwawo. Awela kumaphindaphindwe aphelele ebanga elikhulu (ngamakhonthuwa angu-1 ft nalo lonke lesi-5 elikhulu: 245, 250, 255 …).
3. Vala {{ui:Amalebula amajika}} uma amalebula ephindana esakhiweni esincane.

Iphrojekthi entsha iqala ngo-1 wesiyunithi nalo lonke ikhonthuwa lesi-5 libe likhulu. Amakhonthuwa agoba emigqeni yokwephula; uma ehlikihla ngqo phambili komugqa wokugeleza noma umqansa, lowo mugqa awusona umugqa wokwephula okwamanje (bona [Yakha futhi uthuthukise indawo](topic:build-surface)).

:::note
Amakhonthuwa adwetshwa kuphela uma kungekho amazinga angaphezu kuka-2,000 phakathi kwephoyinti eliphansi nelisezingeni eliphezulu. Esizeni esinokuhluka okukhulu kokuphakama nebanga elincane kakhulu, ipulani ayibonisi amakhonthuwa ngokuphelele: khetha ibanga elikhulu.
:::

## Bushelezela amakhonthuwa

Amakhonthuwa adwetshwa njengezingxenye ezigoqekile ezithile, kunxantathu ngamunye wendawo, ngakho aphenduka ikhona elincane ku-edge ngayinye ye-triangle. **Ukubushelezela amakhonthuwa** ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa** kuwuqeda lawo makhona:

- **Akukho (izingxenye eziqondile)**: imigqa eyadwetshwa njengoba injalo. Yileyo ebonakala kumdwebo omusha noma omdala.
- **Okukhanyayo**: amakhona agoqekile kancane. Umugqa awuze unyakaze ngaphezu kuka-10% ukuya kokhonthuwa olulandelayo, futhi awuze ukhashane nengxenye yawo eqondile ngaphezu kwengxenye yesithupha yobude balowo mucu.
- **Okuqinile**: ijika elipheshulayo. Umugqa awuze unyakaze ngaphezu kuka-25% ukuya kokhonthuwa olulandelayo, futhi awuze ukhashane nengxenye yawo eqondile ngaphezu kwengxenye yesithathu yobude balowo mucu.

Noma kukuphi okukhethiwe, ikhonthuwa elibushelezelwe lisadlula emphethweni ngamunye wonxantathu endaweni efanayo nomugqa oqondile, lihlale ngaphakathi konxantathu udlula kuwo ingxenye ngayinye, ngakho alikwazi ukudlula noma lithinte elinye ikhonthuwa. Lapho ikhonthuwa ledlula umugqa wokwephula noma umngcele, ligcina ijika layo elibukhali. Imigqa ebushelezelwe yimigqa ehlelwa futhi ethunyelwa ngaphandle (DXF, DWG, PDF, GIS nangu-3D), inamaphoyinti amaningi kunemigqa eqondile.

Ukubushelezela kushintsha imigqa edwetshiwe kuphela. Ukuphakama, imithamo, amaphrofayili nezigaba kusafundwa kusuka endaweni ngqo. Ikhonthuwa elidlula ngqo emaphointini okuhlola (iphoyinti elingu-250.00 kukhonthuwa lika-250) ligcina ikhona elincane kulelo phoyinti ngalinye.

## Indlela indawo eyisisekelo samakhonthuwa eyakhiwe ngayo

Amakhonthuwa alandela indawo, nendawo ilandela **Indlela yokudweba amakhonthuwa** ngaphansi kwe-**Umphezulu** ohlangothini lwesobunxele. Okuzenzakalelayo, **Ukuhlanganisa unxantathu ngokuhlanganisa okuqondile**, kuhlanganisa amaphoyinti akho kanxantathu futhi kugcina ilo phoyinti ngalinye nomugqa wokwephula ngokunembile. Ezinye izindlela zokuqala zifaka igridi emaphointini (i-kriging, ubuncane bokugoba, umakhelwane osedwaleni, ibanga elingaphambili nezinye), okunika amakhonthuwa ajikile phakathi kwamaphoyinti kodwa angadluli ngqo kuwo wonke amaphoyinti; umbiko womphezulu ngaphansi kwe-**Umphezulu** utshela ukuthi umphumela ukhuba kangakanani kumaphoyinti akho.

**ABOS (approximation based on smoothing)** yenye yezindlela ezinegridi. Inezilungiselelo zayo ezimbili: **Ukubushelezela** (0.5 ukuqala; okuphansi kubushelezela kakhulu) kanye **Ukunemba** (1 ukuqala: indawo ilethwa ngaphakathi kuka-1% wobubanzi bokuphakama kwesayithi emaphointini akho; bhala inani elincane ukuze ulingane kakhulu). Uma ingakwazi ukufinyelela ukunemba egridini eliliphethe, umbiko womphezulu uyasho lokho: bhala **Ibanga legridi** elincane.

## Funda ukuphakama noma kuphi

Hambisa isikhombisi phezu kwepulani. Ibha yesimo ibonisa:

- **E** no-**N** ngaphansi kwesikhombisi
- **Umhlaba** nokuphakama komhlaba okhona lapho, okulinganiselwe konxantathu ongaphansi kwesikhombisi
- **Ulingisiwe** nokuphakama okuhlongozwayo, lapho ipedi noma ikhoridoro sekushintshe umhlaba
- "Ngaphandle kwendawo" ngaphandle kwendawo

Ukuze ufunde iphoyinti lokuhlola ngokunembile, khetha {{ui:Khetha}} bese uchofoza iphoyinti: uhlangothi lwesokudla lubonisa inombolo yalo, i-easting, i-northing, ukuphakama, incazelo kanye neqembu.

## Bonisa amabhande okuphakama

1. Faka itiki ku-{{ui:Amabhande okuphakama}}.
   Indawo igcwaliswa ngombala kusukela ophansi (oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka-oluhlaza) kuya ophezulu (ophuzi obomvu). Inkomba ngaphansi kwamabhokisi okukhetha ibhala uhla lobubanzi bokuphakama kwebhande ngalinye.
2. Khetha inani lamabhande ku-**Amabhande**: 5, 8, 10 noma 12. Amabhande ahlukanisa ububanzi kusukela ekuphakameni okuphansi kuya ekuphakameni okuphezulu ngezinyathelo ezilinganayo.

Amabhande akhona ukubukwa kuphela. Awathunyelwa ngaphandle ku-DXF noma akhishwe ku-PDF.

## Bonisa izintolo zokuthambeka

Faka itiki ku-{{ui:Izintolo zokuthambeka}}. Intolo emfushane kunxantathu ngamunye ebanzi ngokwanele ukubamba eyodwa ibheke phansi. Onxantathu abalingani abathi intolo. Izintolo ziyindlela esheshayo yokuhlola ukuqondiswa kokukhipha amanzi; inani lokuthambeka ngokwalo alikabhalwa.

## Bonisa onxantathu, amaphoyinti namaphethelo

- {{ui:Onxantathu}} udweba amaphethelo we-TIN. Yivule ukuze uhlole ukuthi indawo ixhuma kanjani amaphoyinti, ikakhulukazi emigqeni yokwephula nangaphandle.
- {{ui:Amaphoyinti}} udweba iphoyinti ngalinye lokuhlola; {{ui:Amalebula amaphoyinti}} yengeza inombolo yalo, ukuphakama kanye nencazelo (noma okuncane: bona **Amalebula amaphoyinti** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings)).
- {{ui:Imigqa yokwephula}} udweba imigqa yokwephula (nomngcele) ngombala wolintshi.
- {{ui:Umphetho wendawo}} udweba uhlaka lwendawo.

## Okukhona noma okuhlongozwayo

Uma ipedi noma ikhoridoro selulinganise ngaphandle kwezinkinga, **Umphezulu** ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa** unikeza {{ui:Okuhlongozwayo (okuqhutshiwe)}}. Amakhonthuwa, amabhande nezintolo sekubonisa indawo elinganisiwe, ukuze ubone umhlaba omusha nemigqa yokukhanya kwemini ozungezile. Buyela ku-{{ui:Umhlaba okhona}} ukuze ubone ukuhlola.

## Okuthunyelwa ngaphandle

Okuphumayo kwe-DXF ne-PDF kulandela lezi zilungiselelo: okuvaliwe akubhalwa, futhi amakhonthuwa ayawo wendawo ebonisiwe. Amabhande okuphakama awakaze athunyelwe ngaphandle.
