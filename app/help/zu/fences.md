Yenza umduli ngomugqa wesici, i-alignment, ipaseli noma i-pad, nezinsika namasango kubekwe ngokuzenzakalelayo.

## Yenza umduli

1. Khetha umugqa, ipaseli noma i-pad.
2. Vula **Surface, More, Fences...** (**Indawo, Okwengeziwe, Imiduli...**).
3. Khetha isitayela (amaphaneli, izinti noma amapikethi), eqandekile noma enezinyathelo, ukuphakama, ukuhlukana kwezinsika nobubanzi besango, bese ukhetha **Umduli ngalokho okukhethiwe**, noma **Nesango** ukubeka isango phakathi kwesigaba eside kakhulu esiqondile.

Izinsika zibekwa emaphethelweni, emakhoneni ngamunye (indawo ephendukayo ngamadigri angu-20 noma ngaphezulu), ezinhlangothini zombili zesango ngalinye nasezigabeni ezilinganayo phakathi, kungabi khona ezide kunokuhlukana okukhulunywe ngakho. **Eqandekile** amaphaneli alandela umhlaba phakathi kwezinsika zawo. **Enezinyathelo** amaphaneli ahlala elinganile futhi ashintshela ezinsikeni; ukuvuleka ngaphansi kwephaneli kubanzi kakhulu ensikeni yalo ehlehla phansi, futhi ifasitela liyaxwayisa uma leso sikhala sidlula umkhawulo, noma uma umhlaba ukhuphuka ngaphakathi kwephaneli. Amasango angu-6 ft (1.8 m) nangaphezulu anamashidi amabili; ipulani liwabonisa evulekile nendlela avula ngayo.

## Ishidi nemiphumela

Umduli ngamunye ubonisa ubude bawo, izinsika ngohlobo, amaphaneli, amasango namanyathelo, kanye nesikhala se-**CSV** sezinsika, amaphaneli, izinti, amapikethi namasango. Ipulani linamashidi womduli (C-FENC, C-FENC-POST, C-FENC-GATE) we-PDF, DXF ne-DWG. Ukuthunyelwa kwe-IFC kubamba izinsika (IfcMember, post), amaphaneli (IfcRailing, user defined fence panel), izinti (IfcMember) namasango (IfcDoor, gate), uhlelo olulodwa kumduli ngamunye.

## Kumsizi

`fence_build` yenza umduli (amaphoyinti noma igama lomugqa, ipaseli noma i-pad, amasango ngesiteshi) futhi `fence_list` ibala imiduli nohlu lwayo.

## Okungakwenziwe

Ubukhulu bokuqala yizinketho ezijwayelekile, hhayi ikhodi. Izinhlobo ezivela ku-Umtapo zithwalwa njengegama kuphela. Amasango kumele abe kusigaba esisodwa esiqondile. I-BimCraft ayinalelo thuluzi okwamanje.
