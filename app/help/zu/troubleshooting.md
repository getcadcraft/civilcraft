Imilayezo ivela ku-status bar ngaphansi kwesobunxele, emabhokisini angaphansi kwezinhlangothi zesobunxele nesokudla, nakumadayalogi. Ikhotetshwe lapha njengoba uhlelo lukubonisa.

## Ukuvula amafayela

**"Leyo fayela ingaphezu kwe-200 MB; i-CivilCraft ayivuli amafayela amakhulu kangaka okwamanje."**
Hlukanisa ifayela lamaphoyinti, noma nciphisa idatha ngaphambi kokuyivula.

**"Akukwazanga ukuvula i-site.json: Le akuyona ifayela leprojekthi ye-CivilCraft."**
Ifayela liphela nge-`.json` noma liqala nge-`{` kodwa alisiyo iprojekthi ye-CivilCraft. Amafayela amaphoyinti akufanele aqale nge-`{`.

**"Akukwazanga ukuvula … Le fayela leprojekthi ivela ku-CivilCraft entsha (inguqulo 2)."**
Iprojekthi yalondolozwa yi-build entsha. Yivule khona, noma uyithumele njenge-LandXML.

**"Akukwazanga ukuvula … Lena yi-XML kodwa hhayi i-LandXML (i-root element yayo ngu-<…>)."**
Ifayela liwuhlobo olunye lwe-XML. Thumela i-LandXML ngohlelo olunye.

**"… alinawo amaphoyinti, indawo ye-TIN noma imizila i-CivilCraft engayifunda."**
Konke okusefayelini i-CivilCraft iyakushiya (isibonelo indawo zegridi kuphela, noma imizila ye-spiral kuphela). Okunye komlayezo kuchaza ukuthi kwakukhona yini. Bheka [LandXML: okufundwayo nokubhalwayo](topic:landxml-reference).

**"Isampula alikwazanga ukulayishwa: …"**
{{ui:Vula isayithi lesampula}} ifunda ifayela ku-CadCraft site. Ngaphandle kokuxhumana isebenza kuphela uma i-CivilCraft yalondolozelwa ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi (inkinobho ku-app bar ibonisa {{ui:Offline ready}}). Xhuma ku-inthanethi, chofoza {{ui:Save for offline}}, bese isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kusukela lapho. Bheka [Ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

**{{ui:Save for offline}} ibonisa {{ui:Could not save}}.**
Amanye amafayela awazange ayodwe. Hlola uxhumo bese uchofoza le nkinobho futhi.

**{{ui:Save for offline}} ibonisa {{ui:Offline not available}}.**
Leli windi lesiphequluli alikwazi ukugcina ikhophi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ngokuvamile ngoba liwindi eliyimfihlo noma ikhompyutha iphethwe. Vula i-CivilCraft ewindini ejwayelekile.

**Ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, uhlelo alivuli neze.**
Aligcinwanga ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kulesi siphequluli, noma isiphequluli sisule idatha yesayithi (i-Safari iyenza lokhu kumasayithi angavakashelwanga izinsuku eziningi). Xhuma ku-inthanethi, vula i-CivilCraft bese uchofoza {{ui:Save for offline}}.

**Ikhophi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi iyinguqulo endala.**
Chofoza {{ui:Offline ready}}, bese {{ui:Save again (get the newest version)}} ngenkathi uxhunyiwe.

**Ifayela libonakala lingatholakali ebhokisini lokuvula.**
Ibhokisi lilanda kuphela amafayela amaphoyinti, i-LandXML namafayela eprojekthi ngokuphela kwawo. Hudula ifayela uliphonse epulanini esikhundleni salo, noma uqamba igama libe ne-`.txt`.

## Ukungenisa amaphoyinti

**"Akukwazanga ukufunda amaphoyinti kulelifayela."**
Khetha i-column order ku-**Columns** ngesandla. Uma lutho lusebenza, vula ifayela kumhleli wombhalo bese uhlola ukuthi kukhona iphoyinti elilodwa ngomugqa, nezinombolo kulo.

**Isiza sibonakala sibukwe ngasemuva, sijikelezwe noma sisongelwe sibe umugqa.**
I-northing ne-easting ziguquke indawo. Isifinyezo sithi "kuqagelwe ngenani lamakholomu". Khetha PENZD esikhundleni se-PNEZD (noma okuphambene) ku-**Columns**, noma ngenisa futhi.

**Amaphoyinti akude kakhulu nalawo asele, nepulani isithombe sisondele kakhulu kangangokuthi sibe yichashazi.**
Umugqa othile unephutha ekuhlelweni kwe-coordinate, noma ifayela lixubanisa izinhlelo ezahlukene zokuhlela. Thola iphoyinti (lichofoze ukuze ubone inombolo yalo) bese ulungisa ifayela.

**Imigqa ebhalwe ngaphansi kwe-"Imigqa engafundwanga".**
Bheka ithebula kwi-[Ngenisa amaphoyinti okuhlola umhlaba](topic:import-points#rows-that-are-not-read).

**Amaphoyinti ami amadala anyamalele.**
Ukungenisa ifayela lamaphoyinti kuyashintsha wonke amaphoyinti, futhi kususe i-undo. Vula iprojekthi yakho elondoloziwe ukuze uwabuyisele.

## Ingaphezulu

**"Ingaphezulu: Alakakhiwa" ngebhokisi elibomvu.**
Ibhokisi lisho ukuthi kungani: "Ingaphezulu lidinga okungenani amaphoyinti amathathu anokuphakama.", "Amaphoyinti onke ayomugqa oyodwa: akukho onxantathu.", "Akukho maphoyinti anokuphakama.", "Umngcele awuhlanganisi namaphoyinti." noma "Akukho onxantathu abasele ngaphakathi komngcele." Hlola ukuhleleka kwamakholomu, amaqembu amaphoyinti (ingabe ashiywe ngaphandle, {{ui:Left out}}?), kanye nomngcele.

**Amakhonthuwa anqamula ngqo umsele noma umqansa.**
Lowo mugqa awuwona umugqa wokuphula. Setha iqembu lawo libe {{ui:Breakline}}, noma uwudwebe ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Breakline}}. Bheka [Akha uphinde ulungise ingaphezulu](topic:build-surface).

**Iqembu lomugqa wokuphula lidweba i-zig-zag.**
Iqembu linemigqa engaphezu kweyodwa, noma amaphoyinti alo awabalwanga ngomugqa. Setha iqembu libuyele ku-{{ui:Points}} bese udweba umugqa ngamunye ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Breakline}}.

**Onxantathu ende nabancane ngaphandle.**
Dweba umngcele ({{key:O}}) noma setha **Umphetho wangaphandle omude kakhulu**.

**Akukho makhonthuwa nhlobo, nakuba ingaphezulu selakhiwe.**
Noma i-{{ui:Contours}} ivaliwe ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubonisa**, noma isikhala phakathi kwamakhonthuwa siphansi kakhulu kangangokuthi kube namazinga okhonthuwa angaphezu kwe-2,000. Khetha isikhala esikhulu.

**Imigqa yokuphula noma umngcele awushintshi lutho.**
Ingaphezulu livela ku-LandXML (Ingaphezulu (livela ku-LandXML)). Lisetshenziswa njengoba lilili. Chofoza {{ui:Rebuild the surface from the points}} ukuze wakhe kusukela kumaphoyinti.

**"Akukho ngaphezulu okwamanje: vula amaphoyinti okuhlola umhlaba kuqala."**
Umugqa wokuphula, umngcele kanye ne-pad kudinga ingaphezulu lomhlaba elikhona. Ngenisa amaphoyinti kuqala.

**"Chofoza iphoyinti lokuhlola umhlaba elinokuphakama: umugqa wokuphula uthatha ukuphakama kwamaphoyinti."**
Ithuluzi le-{{ui:Breakline}} likhetha amaphoyinti okuhlola umhlaba kuphela. Sondeza bese uchofoza iphoyinti (isikwele esiwolintshi sibonisa elizothathwa).

## Ukudweba

**Iphoyinti lami lokugcina lanyamalala ngesikhathi ngiqeda ngokuchofoza kabili.**
Kule build, ukuchofoza kabili kuqeda umzila, i-pad, umugqa wokuphula noma umngcele kodwa kulahle iphoyinti ochofoze kabili kulo. Chofoza iphoyinti lokugcina kanye bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Sebenzisa {{key:Mod+Z}} uma sekwenzeke, bese udweba futhi.

**Angikwazi ukuqeda ukudweba ku-tablet.**
Ukuqeda kudinga {{key:Enter}}; akukho nkinobho esikrinini okwamanje. Xhuma ikhibhodi. Bheka [Imbewu, i-trackpad nokuthinta](topic:keyboard#mouse-trackpad-and-touch).

**Ukudonsa ukubeka iphoyinti kuhambisa ipulani kunalokho.**
Ukudonsa okudlula ipikseli ezimbalwa kuhambisa ipulani kuwo wonke amathuluzi. Chofoza ungahambisi.

**"Umzila udinga okungenani amaphoyinti amabili." / "I-pad idinga okungenani amakhona amathathu." / "Umugqa wepayipi udinga okungenani izakhiwo ezimbili."**
Ucindezele {{key:Enter}} kusenesikhathi. Ukudweba kuyalahlwa; qala kabusha.

**Okhiye abafana ne-A noma i-G abenzi lutho.**
Ibhokisi lombhalo noma uhlu esigabeni busagxile, ngakho izinhlamvu ziya kulo. Chofoza ipulani kanye bese ucindezela ukhiye. Ku-Mac, ukhiye wokususa uba yi-Backspace kwisiphequluli: sebenzisa Fn kanye nokhiye wokususa ukuze ususe ukukhethwa.

## Imizila namaphrofayili

**"Amajika ku-PI 2 ne-PI 3 ayangqubuzana: … Sebenzisa imisebe emincane."**
Amajika amabili adinga i-tangent engaphezu kwaleyo esele phakathi kwama-PI awo. Nciphisa imisebe yomunye noma yombili kwithebula le-PI, noma uhambise ama-PI ukuze ahlukane.

**"PI 3 ibuyela emuva ngqo; ayikwazi ukuthatha ijika."**
Umzila uphindela kuwona kuleyo PI. Hambisa noma susa i-PI.

**"Lo mzila awunawo umhlaba ngaphansi kwawo okuzoqala kuwo."**
Umzila ungaphandle kwengaphezulu. Uhambise uwufake engaphezulu, noma engeza ama-PVI ngokuchofoza ngama-{{ui:Engeza ama-PVI}} evuliwe.

**"Ijika elithi phezulu-phansi ku-PVI 3 (L = …) liqala ngaphambi kokuphela kwejika noma kwe-PVI engaphambili." / "… idlula i-PVI elandelayo."**
Fingqa ubude bejika noma hambisa ama-PVI ahlukane.

**"Sekukhona i-PVI ku-…"**
Sekukhona i-PVI kulelo siteshi. Chofoza kwenye indawo, noma hudula i-PVI ekhona.

**Umbono wephrofayili awunalutho: "Dweba umzila ukuze ubone iphrofayili yawo."**
Akukho mzila, noma umzila okhethiwe unamaphutha.

## Ama-corridor nama-pad

**"I-corridor idinga iphrofayili yedizayini engenamaphutha."**
Qala iphrofayili yedizayini ({{ui:Qala emhlabathini}}) bese ulungisa noma yiziphi izingqubuzano zamajika.

**"… ama-side slope agijima aphume engaphezulu ngaphambi kokuhlangana nomhlaba." / "… imigqa yokuthambeka igijima iphume engaphezulu ngaphambi kokuhlangana nawo …"**
Ukulinganisa umhlaba kufinyelela umphetho wengaphezulu ngaphambi kokuhlangana nomhlaba. Hudula idizayini ingene emphethweni, noma uyisondeze emhlabeni. I-pad entsha edwetshwe ngaphandle engaphezulu ingaqala ku-elevation 0: thayipha ukuphakama okuyiqiniso.

**"Imigqa yemini yekhoridoro iqamba …" / "Umugqa wemini uqamba wona ngokwawo …"**
Ukuthambeka okuvela ezinhlangothini ezahlukene kuyangqubuzana: amajika aqinile, ama-pad amancane, imigodi ejulile noma ukugcwaliswa okuphakeme. Sebenzisa umsebe omkhulu, umbumbo omncane, i-pad enkulu noma ukuthambeka okuqinile.

**"Azikho izivolumu okwamanje: i-pad idinga ukulinganiswa ngaphandle kwezinkinga."**
Lungisa izinkinga ezibhalwe ebhokisini elibomvu elingaphezulu kwalo.

**{{ui:Okuhlongozwayo (okuqhutshiwe)}} ibonakala ingatholakali.**
Akukho pad noma corridor eqhutshwe okwamanje.

## Amapayipi

**"I-P2 alinawo amazinga aphansi okwamanje."**
Chofoza {{ui:Setha amazinga aphansi ngezimiso}} noma uthayiphe amazinga aphansi.

**"P1: akukho mhlaba ku-S1 lokuqala."**
Isakhiwo esingaphezulu kwawo sesiphume engaphezulu futhi asinamphetho. Thayipha umphetho wakho.

**Wonke amapayipi abhekwe ngophawu ngemuva kokuthi ngihambise isakhiwo.**
Ukuhambisa isakhiwo akushintshi amazinga aphansi. Chofoza {{ui:Setha wonke amazinga aphansi ngezimiso}}.

Yonke imilayezo yenethiwekhi ichazwe ku-[Hlela inethiwekhi yamapayipi adonsela phansi](topic:pipe-network#read-and-fix-problems).

## Ukugcina nokuthumela

**"Akukho okuthunyelwe okwamanje." / "Akukho okuzokukhishwa okwamanje."**
Iprojekthi ayinalutho. Ngenisa amaphoyinti kuqala.

**Angikwazi ukuthola ifayela elilondoloziwe.**
Lisesikhwameni sokulanda sesiphequluli (ngokuvamile kwifolda ye-Downloads). Bheka [Amafayela ongawavula, ongawalondoloza nongawathumela](topic:files#where-the-files-go).

**Isilinganiso se-PDF asifani ephepheni.**
Phrinta ku-100% noma ngosayizi wangempela, hhayi "fit to page".

**Izimpawu zombuzo kumbhalo we-PDF.**
Amagama noma izincazelo zisebenzisa izinhlamvu ezingaphandle kwesethi yaseNtshonalanga Yurophu. Umlayezo wesimo uyazibala. Qamba ngezinhlamvu ezilula.

**Ipulani iqhekeziwe emaphethelweni.**
Isikali sikhulu kakhulu ephepheni. Khetha isikali esimakwe ngokuthi "(fits)", ishidi elikhulu, noma {{ui:Okusesikrinini}}.

**{{key:Mod+P}} ivule ingxoxo yokuphrinta yesiphequluli.**
Ikhasi alibanga ukugxila. Vala ingxoxo, chofoza ipulani, bese ucindezela {{key:Mod+P}} futhi, noma chofoza {{ui:PDF}}.

## Uhlelo ngokwalo

**Isikrini sishiya singenalutho noma ithuluzi alisebenzi.**
Sebenzisa inguqulo yamanje ye-Chrome, Edge, Firefox noma Safari, bese uvuselela. Bheka [Izisiphequluli](common:browsers).

**Uhlelo luhamba kancane ngefayela elikhulu lamaphoyinti.**
Vala {{ui:Onxantathu}}, {{ui:Amalebula amaphoyinti}} no-{{ui:Amabhande okuphakama}}, bese usebenzisa isikhala samakhonthuwa esikhulu. Izindawo ezikhulu kakhulu zithatha isikhathi ukuze zakhiwe kabusha ngemva kwalokho.

**Isiphequluli sibuza "Leave site?"**
Iprojekthi inezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga. Hlala, londoloza nge-{{key:Mod+S}}, bese uphuma.
