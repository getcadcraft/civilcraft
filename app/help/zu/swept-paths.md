Indlela yokushayela ishayela isithuthi ngomugqa futhi ibonise umhlaba umzimba waso edlula kuwo. Yisebenzise ukuhlola ukuthi ilori lingajika ikhona, lifinyelele indawo yokulayisha noma lihlehlele itrela endaweni ngaphandle kokuphuma emgwaqweni.

## Shayela isithuthi ngomugqa

1. Dweba umugqa isithuthi esilandela, ngokuqondisa kokuhamba: umugqa wesici (amajika ayamukelekile: cindezela {{key:C}} ngesikhathi udweba, bona [Imigqa yesici](topic:feature-lines)) noma umzila.
2. Khetha wona bese ucindezela {{ui:Indlela yokushayela…}} ku-**Izici** zawo.
3. Ngaphansi kwe-**Qala kusuka**, khetha isithuthi, bese usetha usayizi waso kusuka esithuthini sakho sokuklama: ububanzi, ukuphuma kwangaphambili, ibanga lamasondo, ukuphuma kwangemuva, ububanzi besondo kanye **nokukhiya kokuqondisa**. Engeza kuze kube amatrela amathathu, ngayinye nendawo ayixhunywe kuyo, ubude bayo kusuka ekuxhunyweni kuya ku-axle yayo, ukuphuma kwayo, kanye **nokugoba okukhulu kakhulu**.
4. Khetha **Ukushayela**: phambili noma emuva. Cindezela {{ui:Shayela}}.

:::warning
Izithuthi zokuqala ziyizibambi, hhayi izinga elijwayelekile. Setha usayizi kusuka esithuthini sokuklama esisetshenziswa ngiphrojekthi yakho.
:::

## Phambili nasemuva

| Ukushayela | Okulandela umugqa | Okubalwa yi-CivilCraft |
|---|---|---|
| **Phambili** | Insika ye-axle yangaphambili | Lapho i-axle yangemuva netrela ngalinye kuya khona, i-engeli yokuqondisa nokugoba |
| **Emuva** | Insika ye-axle yokugcina (ingemuva letrela) | Lapho ingxenye ngayinye phambili iya khona, nokuqondisa okudingwa yikhabhi ukuze ibeke itrela lapho |

Ukubuyela emuva kuhlolwa ngalendlela: tshela lapho itrela kufanele liye khona, bese i-CivilCraft ikutshela ukuthi ikhabhi lingakwenza yini.

## Funda umphumela

Indlela yokushayela idwetshwe epulanini ngewolintshi: umkhawulo wayo kanye, ngamachashaza, indlela yamasondo angaphandle kakhulu. Ikhethe (cindezela phakathi kwayo) ukuze ubone ku-**Izici**:

- **Ubude obushayelwe**, i-engeli enkulu kakhulu ye-**Ukuqondisa** kanye ne-**Ukugoba** ngokumelene nemikhawulo yazo, kanye ne-**Indawo esuhambile**.
- Inkinga yonke emugqeni, nobude bayo: ukuqondisa okudlula ukukhiya, noma itrela elijika ngaphezu komkhawulo wokugoba (liyagoba).
- **Isithuthi emugqeni**: hambisa isilayidi ukuze ubone isithuthi kunoma yiliphi iphoyinti, nokuqondisa kwaso nokugoba lapho.

Shintsha umugqa indlela yokushayela iyalandela. {{ui:Hlela isithuthi…}} ishintsha usayizi; **Ukushayela** kushintsha phakathi kokuya phambili nokuya emuva. Ushintsho ngalunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyahlehlisa. {{key:Delete}} isusa indlela yokushayela ekhethiwe (umugqa uyahlala).

Umkhawulo nezindlela zamasondo ziya ku-PDF, ku-DXF nasku-DWG kungqimba C-ROAD-SWPT.

## Ukuthi ibalwa kanjani

Ingxenye ngayinye ijikela ngomugqa we-axle yayo yangemuva futhi idonswa yiphoyinti eliphambili kuyo (i-axle yangaphambili, noma ukuxhunywa), imodeli yesivinini esiphansi, engenankinga yokudlidlizela, esetshenziswa ukuhlola indlela yokushayela. Indawo esuhambile iyonke imikhawulo yomzimba ngayinye ihlanganisiwe. Akumodeli isivinini, ukudlidlizela kwamasondo, isikhathi sokuqondisa, amatrela aqondisekayo noma ukukhululeka ngaphansi komzimba.
