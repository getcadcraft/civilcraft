I-CivilCraft ibala amaphrofayili womanzi azinzile, amamodeli amabili ngokuqondile, emfuleni: kwa-flow ngayinye oyinikezayo, izinga lamanzi kuwo wonke amasigaba ezinqubo, kusuka ebhalansini lamandla phakathi kwezigaba eziseduze (indlela yesinyathelo esijwayelekile). Isendaweni yelabhoratri ngenkathi ihlolwa.

**Isisekelo somklamo kuphela.** Amazinga amanzi adinga ukubukwa unjiniyela oyingcweti ngaphambi kokuthi noma ubani athembele kuwo: imodeli ye-1D ilingana kuphela nezigaba zayo, amanani okuqina, izinde zezindawo nemingcele yayo, futhi ayazi nhlobo ngamabhuloho, amakhulvati noma okugeleza okuphuma emfuleni.

## Dweba umfula

1. Yakha kuqala ubuso bomhlaba obukhona (amaphoyinti, imigqa yokuhlukanisa, noma i-TIN oyivulile).
2. Ngaphansi kwe-**Ubuso** › {{ui:More}}, khetha {{ui:River}}.
3. Chofoza eduze komgqa omaphakathi womfula **kusuka phezulu ukuya phansi**, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze kabili.

I-CivilCraft isika isigaba esingaphezu kwesikhungo somgqa njalo ngamamitha ayi-50 (ft ayi-200), amamitha ayi-100 (ft ayi-300) ohlangothini ngalunye, kusukela emhlabeni. Izinkomba zomfula zilinganiswa kusukela ekugcineni kwasezansi, ngakho zikhula ziya phezulu. Amabhange aqala njengokuqagela okungamamitha ayi-10 (ft ayi-30) ohlangothini ngalunye kwendawo ephansi kakhulu yesigaba ngasinye.

Ukuze ungeze isigaba lapho umfula uncipha noma lapho kukhona isakhiwo, khetha umfula, chofoza {{ui:Add a section}} bese udweba umugqa wokusika kuwo (noma kuluphi uhlangothi: uyaguqulwa ukuze ubalekele kwesokunxele ubheka ezansi komfula).

## Lungisa umfula

Khetha umfula ukuze uwubone ku-**Izakhiwo**:

- **Uhlobo lokugeleza**: subcritical (evame kakhulu: kubalwa ukusuka phezulu ukuya emngceleni wasezansi), supercritical (kubalwa ukusuka ezansi ukuya emngceleni wasephezulu) noma mixed (kokubili, bese kukhethwa lowo onamandla amakhulu ocingweni ngasinye; ukubuka kuqala).
- **Amaphrofayili okugeleza**: igama nokugeleza kwalo ngalinye (isibonelo ukugeleza kweminyaka eyi-10 nekweminyaka eyi-100), nomngcele ekugcineni kwasezansi (nasekugcineni kwasephezulu uma uhlobo lokugeleza lungeyona i-subcritical): **Normal depth** nethambeka lamandla (ithambeka lomgodi lingukuqagela kokuqala), **Known water surface** ukuphakama kwendawo yamanzi okwaziwayo, noma **Critical depth**.
- **Izigaba ezivela emhlabeni**: ukuhlukana, kuya kangakanani ngakwesokunxele nakwesokudla, ukuqagela kwamabhange kanye namanani amabili ka-Manning's n; **{{ui:Cut sections again}}** ishintsha izigaba (isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa).
- **Ithebula lezigaba**: ngesigaba ngasinye, izinkulumo zamabhange angakwesobunxele nakwesokudla, i-n yomsele kanye ne-n yasesikhathini, ubude bezindawo kuya esigabeni esilandelayo ezansi (izikhathi zesikhathi sangaphezu kwebhange lesobunxele, somsele, nebhange lesokudla; zilinganiswa kusuka kuplan ukuze kuqale) kanye nezinkomba zokuncishiswa nokunwebeka (0.1 no-0.3 ukuqala: izinguquko ezithambile). **{{ui:Edit}}** ivula umhleli wesigaba (okulandelayo); ichashazi elingemuva kwayo lisho ukuthi isigaba sinelevu noma indawo engasebenzi.

Konke ukushintsha kungesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa ({{key:Mod+Z}}).

## Hlela isigaba: amabhange, izilevu nezindawo ezingasebenzi

{{ui:Edit}} kwesigaba iyidweba umhlaba waso ikuvumele ukuthi uhudule okufundwa yisixazululo: **ibhange lesobunxele nelesokudla** (ahlukanisa isigaba ibe yisikhathi sangaphezu kwebhange lesobunxele, umsele omaphakathi nesikhathi sangaphezu kwebhange lesokudla), **ilevu** ohlangothini ngalunye kanye **nezindawo ezingasebenzi zokugeleza**. Zonke izibambo zingahanjiswa ngamakhiye omcibisholo, futhi zonke izinombolo zingabhalwa eduze kwepulothi. {{ui:Apply}} yisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

- **Ilevu** linendawo (inkulumo) nokuphakama kwesigqoko. Umhlabathi ongaphesheya kwayo, ukusuka emfuleni, uvaleka emfuleni lapho indawo yamanzi isengaphansi kwesigqoko: indawo yawo, umjikelezo wamanzi kanye nokudlula kwawo kukhishiwe. Ngaphezu kwesigqoko isigaba sonke siyabalwa futhi. Ilevu limi ngaphandle kwebhange evikelayo; eliphakathi kwamabhange liyabonakaliswa.
- **Indawo yokugeleza engasebenzi** ibamba amanzi kodwa ayiphathi ukugeleza (isikhala ngemva komfanekiso womhlabathi, amanzi afile ecaleni kwendlela yebhuloho). Nika izinkulumo zayo zombili kanye, uma uthanda, nezinga lamanzi okungaphansi kwalo okungasebenzi ({{ui:always}} uma kungabhalwe): ngaphezu kwalelo zinga ligeleza njengalo lonke. Eyodwa ehlanganisa ingxenye yomsele oyinhloko iyabonakaliswa.

Phakathi kwezigaba ezimbili, indawo ekhukhule ngayo amanzi ishiya yomile umhlabathi ngemva kwelevu okwabelwana ngalo izigaba zombili, uma indawo yamanzi isengaphansi kwesigqoko (umugqa phakathi kwezigqoko ezimbili uqondile).

## Bala futhi ufunde imiphumela

Chofoza {{ui:Water surface profiles}}. Ngephrofayili oyikhethayo uthola:

- **ipulothi** esuka emfuleni (ukugeleza kusuka kwesobunxele ukuya kwesokudla): ubuso bomsele, amabhange, indawo yamanzi, umugqa wamandla kanye nendawo yamanzi ebucayi; izigaba ezibanjwe ngokujula okubucayi ziyajikelezwa;
- **ithebula** ngesigaba ngasinye: inombolo yomfula, ukugeleza, ubuso bomsele, indawo yamanzi, indawo yamanzi ebucayi, umugqa wamandla, ukujula, ijubane emsele nasekugcineni, inombolo yeFroude yomsele, indawo yokugeleza, ububanzi phezulu, ukugeleza kwamabhange ngamabhange nomsele, ithambeka lamandla, kanye nezexwayiso;
- **izexwayiso** ngamagama alula, isibonelo isigaba esimiswe ekujuleni okubucayi, amanzi aphezu kwemiphetho yesigaba, noma ushintsho olukhulu kokuhamba phakathi kwezigaba ezimbili (engeza izigaba phakathi).

{{ui:Table (CSV)}}, {{ui:Report}} (umbhalo ophelele onendlela, imingcele nawo wonke amathebula) kanye {{ui:Plot (SVG)}} zigcinwa kuma-downloads akho. Yilelo ngalinye liphethe umugqa wokubuyekeza.

Thikha {{ui:Show the flood extent}} ku-**Izakhiwo** ukuze ushade indawo emanzi kuplan ngephrofayili okhethiwe, nomugqa wamanzi udwetshwe lapho ukujula kuyi-zero. Phakathi kwezigaba ubuso bamanzi buyalingana kulo lonke umfula futhi buguquka ngokulinganayo nesiteshi somfula. Umhlabathi ongemuva kwelevu uhlala womile uma amanzi esengaphansi kwesigqoko. Imihlaba ephansi kwenye engaphakathi kwekhoridoro yomfula engafinyelelwa yamanzi (ngemuva komfanekiso womgwaqo, isibonelo) isashadiwe: yihlole.

## Ku-drawing nakwiProblems

Imigqa yomfula iya ku-plan, ku-PDF nasku-DWG ngezingqimba zayo: **C-WATR-CNTR** (isikhungo somgqa), **C-WATR-SCTN** (umugqa ngamunye wokusika, nesiteshi somfula sawo) kanye **C-WATR-FLOD** (umugqa wamanzi wendawo ekhukhule ngayo amanzi, uma {{ui:Show the flood extent}} ithikiwe ngephrofayili). Izexwayiso zomfula (isigaba esibekwe ekujuleni okubucayi, amanzi aphezu kwemiphetho yezigaba, ushintsho lokuhamba olubiza izigaba eziningi, ibhalansi yamandla engavaleki, ilevu ngaphakathi kwamabhange) ziya nasesigabeni se-**Problems**, zibe mayelana nomfula futhi zibekwe esigabeni esizivela kuso.

## Kubalwa kanjani

- Amandla phakathi kwezigaba ezimbili: WS₂ + α₂V₂²/2g = WS₁ + α₁V₁²/2g + L·Sf + C·|α₂V₂²/2g − α₁V₁²/2g|.
- Ukuhamba ngokuhlukaniswa: ikhoyili ngayinye yebhange yahlukaniswa lapho in-n yayo ishintsha, K = Σ (1/n)·A·R^(2/3) (1.486/n ezinhlelweni zase-US); umsele uyingxenye eyodwa, nomndeni ohlanganisiwe lapho in-n yawo ishintsha. α iphuma ngokuthi ukuhamba kuhlukaniswa kanjani.
- Ithambeka lokungeneka ngokuhamba okulinganisiwe, Sf = ((Q₁ + Q₂)/(K₁ + K₂))², phezu kobude bomfula obulinganiswe ngokugeleza.
- Isilinganiso sokuncipha lapho ukugeleza kusheshisa ezansi, nokunwebeka lapho kuhamba kancane.
- Ukujula okubucayi = amandla ancane ocwecwe ngamunye. Ingaphakathi kwebhrakhethi, ngakho ayikaze igxumele ohlangothini olungafanele lwesibucayi; lapho ingekho impendulo, isethwa ekujuleni okubucayi futhi iyakhonjiswa.

Uhlelo luhlolwa ngokujula okujwayelekile kwe-Manning, ngokujula okubucayi emsele onesimo sikhonkolo, nangesibonelo sokubuyela emuva sikaChow (*Open-Channel Hydraulics*, 1959, isahluko 10) kuhlanganiswe ngokuzimele. Indlela efanayo isetshenziswa ngamaprogram ajwayelekile omfula ka-1D, ngakho ukubala ngesandla noma amanothi omfundi kuyadluliselwa.

## Amakhulvati namabhuloho

Ku-**Izakhiwo** zomfula, ngaphansi kwe-**Amakhulvati namabhuloho**, {{ui:Add a culvert}} noma {{ui:Add a bridge}} kubeka enye phakathi kwezigaba ezimbili (yonke indawo igcina okungenani izigaba ezimbili). Iphrofayili engaphansi kwayo ayishintshi; ubuso bamanzi obuphezulu kwayo buvela esakhiweni, futhi iphrofayili iqhubeka phezulu kusukela lapho. **Imiphumela** yengeza ithebula lesakhiwo ngasinye: i-tailwater, izinga le-headwater, ukulawula okubusayo, ukugeleza ngokuvuleka kanye nokugeleza ngaphezu komgwaqo noma idekhi. Ukugeleza okungaphansi kwe-subcritical kuphela.

- **Ikhulvati** (ibhokisi noma indilinga, eyodwa noma ngaphezulu yemigqa elinganayo). Ukulawula okungenayo kungukugeleza kwe-weir uma indawo yokungena ivulekile, bese kuba ukugeleza kwe-orifice uma sekucwiliswe, kuhlanganiswe phakathi kwe-headwater yokuthi u-1 nokuthi u-1.5 wokuphakama. Ukulawula okuphumayo kungukubalansa kwamandla: ukulahlekelwa kokungena, ukuhamba nekhanda lejubane lokuphuma, phezu kwe-tailwater engaphezulu nephakathi-nenduku yendawo ebucayi nophezulu, ngaphandle kwokwehla ngaphansi komzila. I-headwater iyinkulu kakhulu kwezimbili. Thikha **Amanzi angadlula emgwaqeni** nokugeleza kwe-weir yomgwaqo kuyabelana ngokugeleza nemigqa.
- **Ibhuloho.** Ukuvuleka kuyisigaba esingaphansi kwebhuloho, esinqunywe ezindaweni zokuxhasa, kanye nenqwaba ngayinye njengendawo engathwali ukugeleza. Ubuso bamanzi okuvulekile buvela esigabeni esingaphansi (isilinganiso sokwanda), kanti isigaba esingaphezulu sivela ekuvulekeni (isilinganiso sokuncipha). Uma ukuvuleka kungadlulisi ukugeleza ngale tailwater kwenziwe lokho kuhamba ekujuleni okubucayi futhi ibhuloho liyaphawulwa njengelivimbayo. Uma amanzi efinyelela ku-low chord kuba ukugeleza kwengcindezi (uhlobo lwesluice lapho i-tailwater ingaphansi kwe-low chord, icwiliswe ngaphezulu kwayo), namanzi aphezu kwedekhi engeza ukugeleza kwe-weir; izinga langaphezulu yilelo lapho zihlanganyela khona ukugeleza.

Izilinganiso (ukukhishwa, ukulahlekelwa kokungena, i-weir) zinamanani ajwayelekile anzima ngamazinga. Yishintshe ngokwedizayini yakho. Akuyona amachati afakiwe amabhuku omklamo wamakhulvati abhalwe, ngakho phatha i-headwater yekhulvati eseduze nomkhawulo waye njengesilinganiso. Amanzi aphezu komgwaqo noma idekhi, ukucwiliswa kokungena, ukuvuleka okuvimbiwe nokuhamba okusheshayo kumsele kuboniswa ku-**Problems**.

## Ukugeleza okungazinzile

**{{ui:Unsteady flow…}}** ku-**Izakhiwo** zomfula iqondisa i-hydrograph yesikhukhula ngezigaba. Bhala ukungena njengamahora nokugeleza, ingodwa ngomugqa ngamunye, ithambeka lasezansi (normal depth) nokuthi kuzothatha isikhathi kangakanani. Iqala kusukela endaweni yamanzi ezinzile ekugelezeni kokuqala bese ixazulula izibalo ezigcwele ezimbili zokuhamba (isikimu esine-point esiphelele, esilinganiswe ngo-0.6 esikhathini). Uthola izinga eliphezulu lamanzi nokugeleza kuwo wonke amasigaba, nehora ngalinye lapho lenzeka khona, ipulothi yokugeleza emaphethelweni amabili, nokuhlolwa kwevolumu (okungenayo, okuphumayo nokushintsha okugcinwe). Kuyahlolwa ukuthi ukugeleza okuzinzile kuhlala kuzinzile, ukuthi isinyathelo esingenayo sigcina ephrofayilini ezinzile yokugeleza okusha, ukuthi ivolumu iyavaleka, nejubane lamagagasi esikhukhula ngokuqhathaniswa negagasi lekinematic. Ukugeleza okungaphansi kwe-subcritical, umsele oyodwa, akukho ukungena okuvela ngaseceleni, futhi akukho makhulvati noma mabhuloho ngaphakathi kokusebenza.

## Okuhlanganayo

Imigqa ehlanganisa ukugeleza ibe yiphuma eyodwa: ukugeleza kuyanezelwa, nobuso bamanzi ekuqaleni komsele wasezansi bukhuphuka ngaphezu kwemigqa ehlanganayo nayo (ubuso bamanzi obufanayo ekuhlanganeni, indlela elula: akukho ngaphandle, akukho mpendulo, ngakho ifanele amanani aphansi e-Froude). Imodeli nokuhlolwa kukhona (`river-structures.js`); ipaneli yokuhlanganisa imifula ezimbili kwipulani aikakhiwa.

## Okungakakhiwa

Izivinjwa namasango njengezakhiwo zazo, ipaneli yokuhlanganisa, izakhiwo ngaphakathi kokusebenza okungazinzile, nokugeleza okungazinzile kwe-supercritical. Ukusebenza kwe-mixed kuveza ijubane lamanzi phakathi kwezigaba ezimbili kodwa ayilibekeli lapho.

**Lapho imifula ihlangana.** Ku-Izakhiwo zomfula, "Flows into" iqamba umfula ohlanganayo nawo. Imifula ehlanganayo ixazululwa ndawonye: umfula owenyela kuwo uthwala isamba sokugeleza kwayo, futhi ngayinye iphela ebuso bamanzi esigabeni sokuqala salowo mfula (indlela yobuso bamanzi obulinganayo; ayihlanganisi i-engile nomfutho wokugeleza). Ukuze uhlanganise umfula omkhulu nesifunda, dweba umfula omfushane ngaphansi kokuhlangana bese uvumela bobabili ukuthi bagele kuwo. Imiphumela ibonisa ithebula lokuhlanganisa. Izilinganiso kulezi zimiphumela (ukuncipha 0.1, ukunwebeka 0.3, umsele wesiqephu esisodwa nokuqina okuhlanganisiwe, uhlobo lokugeleza olumixed ngamandla ancane ocwecwe) zihlala zize kube yilapho unjiniyela ezibuyekeza, nemiphumela iyakutshela lokho.
