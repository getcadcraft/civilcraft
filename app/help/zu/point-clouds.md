Abantu abaningi abakudingi lokhu, ngakho kuyi-add-on. Vula {{ui:Amafu amaphoyinti}} ku-Izilungiselelo › Okwengeziwe; ithebhu ethi {{ui:Amafu amaphoyinti}} iyavela eceleni kwesokudla. Ifayela lifundwa kwisiphequluli sakho futhi lihlukaniswe libe amathayela agcinwa kwisitoreji sesiphequluli sakho (noma kwifolda oyikhethayo). **Akukho lutho olulayishwayo.**

:::warning
Konke okubikwa yithebhu le yinombolo evela kudatha yakho, hhayi isitifiketi. Ihlolwe kusayithi elenziwe, hhayi ekuhlolweni okwangempela nge-drone: funda imiphumela yokuqala ngokucophelela futhi usitshele ekutholayo. Unjiniyela obhalisiwe unquma ukuthi umhlaba, amakhonthuwa nama-volume banesisindo esingakanani.
:::

## Vula ifu

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Vula ifu}} khetha ifayela le-LAS noma le-LAZ. Ithebhu itshela ukuthi linamaphoyinti amangaki, uhlelo lwalo lwamakhoordinethi nezinyunithi, nokuthi kudingeka isikhala esingakanani sokwakha; cindezela {{ui:Akha ifu}}. Ukwakha kungamiswa futhi kuqhubeke nge-{{ui:Qhubeka}} (khetha lelo fayela ngokuphindile). Ifayela alishintshwa ngoke. Ifayela lamaphoyinti ayizigidi eziyikhulu liyakhiwa ngemizuzu embalwa esiteshini sokusebenza futhi lidinga isikhala esilingana nalo mfayela.

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Amafu agcinwe kule komputha}} uvula noma ususe ifu. {{ui:Gcina amafu kufolda…}} (Chrome, Edge kanye ne-app ye-Studio) igcina amathayela kufolda oyikhethayo, isibonelo ifolda ye-CadCraft yamafu e-Documents, ukuze ifu elingu-10 GB lingadingi ukuthi libe ngaphakathi kwesitoreji sesiphequluli; amafu akhiwe ngaphambili ahlala lapho akhiwe khona. Isiphequluli sicela imvume futhi ngemva kokuqala kabusha: cindezela {{ui:Sebenzisa ifolda … futhi}}.

## Bheka ifu

{{ui:Bheka ku-3D}} udwebe ifu kusuka kumathayela ajwayelekile futhi lihlole kancane njengoba usondela, kodwa ngaphezu kwe-{{ui:Amaphoyinti amaningi kakhulu adwetshiwe}} ngesikhathi esisodwa (amayizigidi ezi-2.5; amakhulu ayinkulungwane ezi-500 efonini). Dweba ngombala wefayela, ukuphakama, isigaba, ukukhanya noma {{ui:Okusele}}. {{ui:Ukukhanya kwe-eye-dome}} kushadela imiphetho ngokujula ukuze isimo sibonakale ngaphandle kwama-normal (kuvalwe efonini); {{ui:Amaphoyinti ancipha ngebanga}} yenza amaphoyinti akude abe mancane namaseduze abe makhulu; {{ui:Usayizi wephoyinti}} usetha usayizi. Ipulani likhombisa ifu njenge-raster ngaphansi komdwebo wakho futhi **lihlola kabusha uma uzoomeka** esiqeshini salo.

## Hlanza ifu

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Hlanza}} dweba i-lasso, ibhokisi noma i-polygon epulanini (noma ku-3D kusuka embonweni ophezulu: ku-3D umkhawulo uthathwa uqonde phansi, ngakho unembe kuphela uma usuka phezulu). Ithebhu ikhombisa ukuthi amaphoyinti angaki angaphakathi **ngaphambi** kokuthi usebenzise: {{ui:Susa lawa maphoyinti}}, {{ui:Shintsha isigaba sawo}}, {{ui:Buyisela amaphoyinti asusiwe}} noma {{ui:Sika kulo mkhawulo}}. {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no-{{ui:Phinda wenze}} bahamba phakathi kwelogi yokuhlela; ifayela ngokwalo alishintshwa ngoke. {{ui:Isihlungi somsindo…}} simaka amaphoyinti azulazulayo njengamaphoyinti aphansi (isigaba 7) noma umsindo ophezulu (isigaba 18).

## Hlukanisa ifu

{{ui:Thola umhlaba}} isebenza ithayela ngethayela futhi imaka amaphoyinti omhlaba (isigaba 2) namaphoyinti aphansi; ukubekezelela kungu-0.15 m, noma 0.08 m uma usebenzisa iswishi yezinga lokuhlola. Ibika izingxenye zamathayela ezingavumelani. {{ui:Hlukanisa izitshalo nezakhiwo}} bese imaka izitshalo eziphansi, eziphakathi neziphezulu kanye nezakhiwo ngokusuka ekuphakameni ngaphezu komhlaba nangokuthi izindawo ezizungezile zilinganiswe kangakanani. Ayikho ukufunda ngomshini, ngakho ishedi elinophahla oluyinqwaba ngaphansi kwezihlahla lingase lingalungi: bheka.

## Umhlaba, amakhonthuwa, nama-breakline

{{ui:Akha umhlaba}} wenza umhlaba okhona kumaphoyinti omhlaba bese uwungeza kuphrojekthi ngendlela efanayo nokungenisa kwe-LAS (isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa). Bese iqhathanisa amaphoyinti nawo: isilinganiso, i-RMS, i-95th percentile, ingxenye ekubekezelelweni, namaphoyinti ayishumi ahluka kakhulu, ngakunye no-{{ui:Yiya lapho}}. Dweba ngo-{{ui:Okusele}} ukubonisa lapho umhlaba namafu kungavumelani khona.

I-**breakline** (i-kerb, umfucuza noma umphetho webhange) ikhethwa kufu: {{ui:Khetha i-breakline kufu}} ku-3D view uthatha iphoyinti eliseduze elidwetshiwe ngokuchofoza ngakunye, futhi {{ui:Khetha i-breakline kupulani}} uthatha iphoyinti lefu eliseduze (umhlaba kuqala) ngaphansi kokuchofoza ngakunye; chofoza kabili noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda umugqa. {{ui:Akha umhlaba}} olandelayo uyawusebenzisa. {{ui:Bonisa amakhonthuwa}} udweba amakhonthuwa phezu kwefu esithombeni esincane futhi asethe isikhala samakhonthuwa sephrojekthi.

## Ama-volume

{{ui:Ivolumu yesitaki…}} ibala ivolumu yomphezulu ngaphezu kwesisekelo ngaphakathi komkhawulo odwetshwe. Dweba umkhawulo emhlabeni olinganayo ozungeze isitaki bese ukhetha isisekelo: iphoyinti eliphansi kakhulu lomkhawulo, indiza, isisekelo esigobile esibushelezi, umphezulu oqhamuka emkhawulweni, noma izinga olibhalayo. Emhlabeni othambekile noma ogobile sebenzisa indiza noma isisekelo esigobile; iphoyinti eliphansi kakhulu lifunda liphakeme kakhulu. Amaphoyinti omkhawulo amile ngaphezu kwabanye (isihlahla esisemugqeni) ayeqwashelwa ngaphandle kwesisekelo. {{ui:Indiza kuya endizeni}} inikeza ukusika nokugcwalisa phakathi kwamafu amabili. Kokubili kungagcinwa njenge-CSV.

## I-orthomosaic

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:I-orthomosaic (isithombe esivela phezulu)}} khetha i-orthomosaic yedrone kanye ne-world file yayo ne-`.prj` uma inazo: i-GeoTIFF, noma i-PNG noma i-JPEG. Iba **ungqimba lwemephu** lwephrojekthi, ngakho ipulani liyibonisa ngaphansi komdwebo wakho, futhi ukubuka kwe-3D (umbala {{ui:Imephu}}) kuyipeyinta emhlabeni owakhiwe, ngakho {{ui:Akha umhlaba}} kuqala. Ithebhu itshela ukuthi isithombe simboza kangakanani amafu, futhi ilumbise uma lingamboni lutho: imbangela evamile uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi oluhlukile, ngakho nika ifayela le-`.prj` kanye nakho. Uma ingekho i-`.prj`, uhlelo lubuza ukuthi amakhoordinethi aphi. Isithombe sigcinwa ngaphakathi kwefayela lephrojekthi; i-GeoTIFF engaphezu kuka-200 MB iyenqatshwa futhi i-mosaic enkulu kakhulu incishiswa ibe ama-megapixel angu-16, ngakho ngokuhlola okukhulu, sika i-mosaic ibe sesayithi kuqala.

## Thumela

{{ui:Thumela}} ibhala ifu elihlanziwe, elihlukaniswe (wonke amaphoyinti, izigaba ezihlanziwe, noma umhlaba kuphela) njengefayela elisha. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Uhlobo lwefayela}} khetha:

- **LAS 1.4 (akucindezelwanga)**: ifomethi yamaphoyinti 6, noma 7 ngombala.
- **LAZ (ucindezelwe)**: amaphoyinti afanayo acindezelwe ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa, ngakho onke amaphoyinti ebuya njengoba ayefana khona. Ibhalwa njengefomethi yamaphoyinti 0, noma 2 ngombala, kufayela le-LAS 1.4. Esiteshini esilingiselwe esingamamitha angu-300 i-LAZ yayicishe ibe yingxenye yesihlanu yosayizi wefayela le-LAS 1.4. Amafomethi e-LAZ 0 no-2 aphatha izigaba ezingu-0 kuya ku-31 kuphela: iphoyinti elinesigaba esiphakeme liyenqatshwa ngomlayezo, ngakho thumela leli fu njenge-LAS 1.4.

I-LAZ ibhalwa ngomcindezeli we-CadCraft, owenziwe ngokusho kwesipesifikheshini se-LAZ esishicilelwe. Sesihlole ukuthi isifundi sethu nabafundi ababili abazimele (i-laz-perf, esetshenziswa uhlelo ukuvula amafayela e-LAZ, ne-laspy ne-lazrs) bafunda wonke amaphoyinti ngokuqondile, nokuthi amafayela e-LAZ enziwe yi-laspy aphuma ebhayithi ngebhayithi efanayo uma sibhala kabusha amaphoyinti awo. Ezinye izinhlelo azikahlolwa, ngakho uma enye ingavuli ifayela, sitshele ukuthi yiphi.

Lapho isiphequluli singabhala ifayela kancane kancane, ukhetha ifayela kuqala futhi ifu elikhulu kakhulu libhalwa kulo ithayela ngethayela esikhundleni sokuqoqwa enkumbulweni; kungenjalo ifayela liqoqwa futhi ilandwe, okuhle kuze kube amamegabyte ambalwa.

## Okungenzi

Ifunda i-LAS ne-LAZ kuphela (i-E57, i-PLY ne-PCD azixhunywanga kule thebhu, nemifula eminingana yendiza eyodwa ayihlanganiswa), ayiphindaphindi uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi lwefu (uhlelo lwephrojekthi nemitha kuthathwa njengoba kunjalo), ayifundi amafayela e-RCP noma e-RCS ka-Autodesk, ayakhi indawo ngezithombe, futhi ayithole izinsika, imiphetho noma amapayipi ngokwayo.
