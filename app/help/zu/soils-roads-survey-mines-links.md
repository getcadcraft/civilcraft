Vula **Ubuso › Okuningi** bese ukhetha ifasitela. Ngalinye ligcina izinto zalo kufayela lweprojekthi yakho, lisebenzisa ibalo elifanayo umsizi alisebenzisayo, futhi lisho lapho inombolo iyisisekelo sokuqala nje. Lezi yizisekeli zokubala: unjiniyela uhlola ngayinye imiphumela.

**Izixhumanisi nemigqa.** Ixhumanisi ngumzila weziqu zihlangana endaweni eyodwa. Nika ngayinye iqu lalo radiyasi lombhede walo uma uthanda, noma khetha *Ukuqondisa kwamaphoyinti amathathu (2:1:2)* ukuze uthole ukubuyela okuhlanganisiwe ngohlobo lapho iloli elijikelezayo lidinga khona. *Izindlela zabahambi* ibeka indlela yabahambi nomgqa wokuma ku-leg ngayinye. Imigqa nezimpawu zihamba umzila. Izinto ezinesethingi *Bonisa ku-plan* ibe yebhusayo zidwetshwa ku-plan, futhi **Londoloza idrowing yomgwaqo (.dxf)** ibhala idrowing efanayo ngezingqimba ezinamagama (`C-ROAD-EDGE`, `C-ROAD-MARK-YELLOW`, `C-ROAD-XWALK`, `C-ROAD-SIGN`, `C-RAIL-TURNOUT`). Izibekelo, ububanzi nezikhodi zezimpawu ziyizibambi: umyalelo wesikhungo sakho uyaphatha.

**Ukulungiswa kwesamukelo.** Faka amaphoyinti nezilinganiso, noma ungenise ifayela lomqoqi wedatha (RW5, GSI, JobXML, CSV). Khetha *Free network: yes* uma kungekho phoyinti elibanjiwe: ukuqala kwesikhundla kuvaliwe ngalokho okudingekayo kancane, ngakho izikhungo zincike kulokho okukhethile kodwa izinsalela, isici sokuguquka nokuhlola kwephutha akuncikile. **Londoloza amaphoyinti alungisiwe (.csv)** ibhala umphumela. Abafundi bebhalwe kusuka ezincazelweni zomphakathi zamafomethi futhi bahlolwa emafayelini esawawenza; bahlu lonke ibhalwe ingasiqondi. *Ama-traverse* avala i-traverse ngomthetho wekhampasi futhi inikeze ukungavali kanye nokunemba njenge-1 : n.

**Imigodi evulekile.** Imigodi yokubhoboza iba yimodeli yebhulokhi nomgodi wokugcina. Setha *Izigaba zemigodi* ngaphezu kwe-0 imigodi eyisihlanganisa ngokuya kwesici senzuzo, nesitshalo nomkhumbi wezimoto ukuze kube nesheduli ngesikhathi nge-cash flow ehlanganisiwe. Yonke intengo, izindleko nethambeka ngokwakho.

**Izixhumanisi zomsakazo.** Ngaphezu komugqa wokubona nokuphepha, ixhumanisi ibika ukuphuma kwesignali ngemiphumela eminingi (isilinganiso sika-Vigants–Barnett, ngaphandle kwemvula). Nika ububanzi besibuko setawu ukuze uthole ukugudluza komoya nomzuzu esiphansi sawo kanye nesici sokuphenduka.

**Ushintsho lomgqa wesitimela.** Khetha umzila nesiteshi bese ushintsho lubekwa kuwo. Yonke imikhawulo iyizibambi uze uyisethe kusukela esilinganisweni sesitimela sakho.

**Umugqa wokukhipha nomgodi ku-3D.** Nika umgodi ububanzi bomgwaqo wokukhipha bese iwindo lomgodi lebeka umgwaqo wokukhipha odongeni ngethambeka lomgwaqo: wehla odongeni lwebhenshi ngaphansi kobude bebhenshi ngaphezu kwethambeka, bese ungena ebhenshini elilandelayo. Isithombe sibonisa wonke imiphetho yebhenshi nomgwaqo phezu kwawo. Okuthi "Umugqa wokukhipha uyalinganisa udonga lomgodi" ukwenza umkhawulo womgodi usebenzise udonga olulingene esikhundleni somgwaqo osebenzisa kuwo wonke amabhenshi. Umgwaqo uyisikisela phezu kwezindonga zebhulokhi ezinamasitebhisi, hhayi ukuklanywa komgwaqo wokukhipha; iwindo liyakutshela uma umgwaqo ungaphinda umgodi ngaphezu kwesihlandla, uwele ebhenshini elisezansi noma ungafaneleki phansi. "Sika umgodi emhlabathini" umba umgodi emhlabathini okhona futhi utshele ukuthi kangakanani; "Bonisa ku-3D view" udweba umhlabathi osikiwe, imiphetho yamabhenshi nomgwaqo ku-3D view (izinketho zemigodi evulekile nemigwaqo yokukhipha ziyashintsha).

**Ukumboza.** Ngaphansi kwe-Radio links, ithebhu ye-Coverage ithatha isiteshi, amandla nokuthola kwayo, ububanzi nosayizi weseli bese yenza ishadi indawo engafinyelelwa ngumhlabathi okhona ku-plan nakwisithombe ongasilondoloza. Iseli ngayinye inokulahlekelwa kwesikhala esivulekile kanye nentaba ebi kakhulu emzileni oqondile (knife-edge diffraction, ITU-R P.526); ayinakho ukuhlukana kwezintaba ezimbili ngaphandle, imvula nemimo yeenta, ngakho ikhanya kancane. Isithandimi sexhumanisi nasesakhiweni sinokuhlolwa kwesisindo: ukucindezeleka nokuphakama kwesisindo esivela emomenteni womoya nesisindo, sishaywa nemvelo esiyinikezayo nezinga lomthwalo, kanye nokugoba kwe-Euler uma unikeza isigaba. Ingokuhlolwa kwezisindo kuphela: akukho zinombolo zekhodi yesakhiwo ezakhiwe ngaphakathi, ngakho ikhodi yakho iyinqumo.

**Amafayela ensimu awakahlolwa kwangempela.** Abafundi be-RW5, GSI, JobXML kanye ne-CSV babhalwa ngokusuka ezincazelweni zomphakathi zamafomethi futhi bahlolwa kuphela emafayelini esawawenza, kuhlanganise nawonkulu alimeleyo ngaphandle kokuthi awabhubhisi. Ifasitela lensimu liyatshela lokho njalo lapho ungenisa. Hlola amaphoyinti nokubheka ngokumelene nombiko womuthi wakho; uma ifayela langempela lingafundeki, sithumele (inothi lokuhlola komphakathi lisesikhungweni docs/community-tests/EG.11.md).
