{{ui:Kumugqa}} (Ukuhlobisa › {{ui:Okuningi}}): chofoza umugqa wesici, noma umugqa noma umbandela wengqimba ye-DWG/DXF engeniswe. Umugqa ovaliwe ungenza ukwakha, ihlaza, ibhedi lezitshalo, ibhedi, ihlaza elimfushane noma iplathifomu; umugqa ovulekile wenza indlela, udonga, ikhanda (amaphoyinti okuqala nokugcina), izinyathelo, irempu, ukuvimba noma umfudlana. Zombili izinhlobo zingenza **feature line**, ngaphandle komugqa wesici ngokwawo.

## Umugqa wesici kusuka emgqeni

Umugqa wesici owenziwe emgqeni uthatha amaphoyinti wawo, uvaliwe uma umugqa wawo wawuvaliwe. Amazinga awo ahlelwa emhlabeni okhona (0 uma kungekho lutho okwamanje), futhi ukusetshenziswa kwawo kungu **Off**: awakhi mhlaba uze ukhethe ukusetshenziswa nokusetha amazinga awo kuma-Properties. Lapho umugqa unyakaza, umugqa wesici onenombolo efanayo yamaphoyinti ugcina ilinga ngalinye oliseti; lapho umugqa engeza noma ulahlekelwa amaphoyinti, amazinga ahlelwa phansi kabusha. Bheka [Imigqa yesici](topic:feature-lines).

Into entsha igcina **ixhumanisi** emgqeni wayo. Lapho umugqa unyakaza (udonga amaphoyinti awo, noma umdwebo uyahanjiswa noma ufundwa kabusha), into ithatha isimo esisha ngokuguqula okufanayo, futhi ngemisebenzi yomhlaba umhlaba ohlongozwayo uyalandela. Properties ichaza ukuthi ixhunyiwe kuphi; {{ui:Hlukanisa ixhumanisi}} iyenza ikhululeke.

Imigqa ihlolwa kuqala: umugqa udinga okungenani amaphoyinti angu-2 (angu-3 kumphetho), angaphezu kwangu-5000 ngokuphezulu, izinombolo zangempela; umphetho kufanele uvalwe futhi ungaqhubeki ngokwawo. Umugqa ohluleka awusetshenziswa: into ihlala njengoba ibe kanjalo futhi ipaneli yeZinkinga ichaza ngani. Uma umugqa ususiwe, into ihlala futhi ipaneli yeZinkinga iyakushoma lokho.

## Ku-SuperCad Studio

Vula indawo nomdwebo ezingeni elifanayo ku-phrojekthi efanayo. Khetha umugqa noma i-polyline eyodwa kumdwebo we-2D, cindezela Ctrl+K bese usebenzisa {{ui:Into yendawo ivela emgqeni womdwebo okhethiwe}}: i-CivilCraft iyabuza ukuthi yenzeni, njengoba kushiwo ngenhla, futhi into ihlala ixhunyiwe kulowo mugqa womdwebo. Uma ushintsha imigqa emdwebweni, Ctrl+K {{ui:Buyekeza indawo kusuka emdwebweni}} yenza izinto zilandele, inyathelo elilodwa lokuhlehlisa ngomugqa ngamunye. Ifayela lezixhumanisi lephrojekthi liyarekhoda ukuthi iyiphi into elandela iyiphi imigqa. Usizo lwe-SuperCad Studio luneSihloko se-Landscape.
