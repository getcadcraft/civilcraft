I-platform yendlu iyipedi (**Ukuklama** › {{ui:Pad}}): ipholigoni eliyisicaba ezingeni elilodwa, eliliwe emhlabeni. Izinto ezine zisiza ezindaweni ezithambekile.

## Izindonga zokubamba njengemiphetho yepedi

Khetha ipedi bese usetha uhlangothi lube {{ui:Vertical (retaining wall)}} esikhundleni sethambeka (**Properties** › {{ui:Sides and retaining walls}}, noma {{ui:Vertical: retaining walls}} zonke izinhlangothi ngesikhathi esisodwa). Ukuphakama kodonga kufundwa emhlabeni ngalo: phezulu emhlabeni, phansi epedini, kubonakala emaphethelweni omabili nasendaweni yalo ephakeme kakhulu epulanini, nesexwayiso uma lide kunomkhawulo owusethile. Izindonga nokuthambeka kungaxutshwa kuhlangana ngakunye.

## Imephu yokusika nokugcwalisa

Khetha {{ui:Cut and fill map}} ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa**. Ipulani iyashadwa ngokujula kokusika noma kokugcwalisa, ngezinyathelo ezilingana (ukusika kushisa, ukugcwalisa kuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, okuseduze nokuthi kulingene kube mhlophe obuthaka) nelejenda, futhi **amazinga aphoyinti** ayaphrintwa epedini: ushintsho lwezinga emaphoyinteni esigceweni, nokusika okujulile kakhulu nokugcwalisa okuphezulu kakhulu kumakwa ngo ▲. Amasele ayefana nalawo imisebenzi yomhlaba esetshenziswa ngayo, ngakho imephu nezinombolo zihlala zivumelana.

Kubonakalo be-3D, khetha **Ukusika nokugcwalisa** kuhlu lwemibala ukuze ufake lemibala efanayo emhlabeni nasemklamweni.

Isibalo sokuvuvuka noma sokuncipha sisemsebenzini wokuhanjiswa kwenhlabathi (**Ukuklama** › {{ui:Mass haul}}); asisetshenziswa kuvolumu zepedi.

## Isigaba esikhululekile

Ngaphansi kwe-**Ubuso** › {{ui:More}}, khetha {{ui:Free section}}, chofoza amaphoyinti omugqa wesigaba noma kuphi (ungagoba) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ingxoxo idweba isigaba: umhlaba ovele ukhona, ukuklama okulinganisiwe (okudashiwe) nokusika nokugcwalisa phakathi kwawo, kushadiwe, nezindawo zawo (ukuklama kubalwa kuphela ngaphakathi kwamapedi namigwaqo). {{ui:Vertical exaggeration}} izenzakalela ukulingana nomdwebo; {{ui:Save as SVG}} kanye ne-{{ui:Save as CSV}} zikunika umdwebo womkhandlu nezinombolo.

## Amarampu namapedi athambekile

Ngaphansi kwe-**Ubuso** › {{ui:More}}, khetha {{ui:Ramp or sloped pad}}, chofoza ipheyinti eliphansi bese eliphezulu, cindezela {{key:Enter}}, bese unikeza ububanzi, izinga elisezingeni eliphansi kanye nokuthambeka. Ipedi iyisigxobo esinamakhona athwala la mazinga (umphetho phakathi kwamakhona amabili uhamba ngokuthambeka okungaguquki), futhi igxuma emhlabeni njenganoma iyiphi ipedi. Amakhona nawo kanoma iyiphi ipedi angaba nezinga lawo, ngakho umgwaqo wokungena, irampu noma iterasi ethambekile kuyipedi enezinga ekhonweni ngalinye.
