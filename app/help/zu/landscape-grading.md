Konke lapha kulinganiswa kube **umphezulu ohlongozwayo** ngokulinganisa okufanayo nopedi namakoridoro. **Umhlaba ovele ukhona** awushintshwa ngokuphelele, futhi i-Earthwork iyawubala wonke. Imenyu {{ui:Grade}} itholakala eqenjini le-Landscape.

## Amapulatifomu

{{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Platform}}, bese uchofoza amakhona bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ipulatifomu iqala ngobuphakathi bomhlaba ngaphansi kwayo; thayipha **Ukuphakama** ukuze uyisethe, noma uyisule ukuze ilandele umhlaba futhi. **Ukwehla** ngo-% no-**Ukwehla kuya** ngamadigri (0 = mpumalanga, 90 = enyakatho) ayithambekisa. Izinhlangothi zayo zilinganiswa kuze kufike emhlabeni ku-**Ukuthambeka kokusika** nako **Ukuthambeka kokugcwalisa** (ngaqondile kuya ku-1).

## Okuthambeka ngohlangothi nezindonga zokubamba

Ngaphansi kwe-**Izinhlangothi nezindonga zokubamba** kuProperties yepedi, iplatifomu noma ibhemu, uhlangothi ngalunye lungalinganiswa ngendlela yalo. Uhlangothi 1 lusuka ekhonweni lokuqala owachofoza kuya kowesibili, uhlangothi 2 kusuka kowesibili kuya kowesithathu, njalo njalo.

- **Zonke izinhlangothi**: **Ethambekile** isebenzisa ukusika nokugcwalisa okungenhla kuzo zonke izinhlangothi; **Ziqondile: izindonga zokubamba** ibeka udonga kuzo zonke izinhlangothi.
- Ngohlangothi ngalunye: **Okuthambeka okungenhla**, **Okuthambeka kwayo** (thayipha ukusika nokugcwalisa kwalolo hlangothi) noma {{ui:Vertical (retaining wall)}}.
- Thayipha ithambeka njenge `3` noma `3:1` (ngaqondile kuya ku-1 ngokuqondile), `33%` noma `18.4°`; likhonjiswa njenge-H:1. **Ukuthambeka kokusika** nethambeka lokugcwalisa lepedi kulandela okufanayo.
- Uhlangothi oqondile alunawo umhlanga: udonga lokubamba lugijima ngomphetho, kusuka epedini kuya phansi emhlabeni ovele ukhona (ekugcwalisweni) noma kusuka emhlabeni kuya phansi epedini (ekusikeni). **Ububanzi bodonga** (0.3 m noma 1 ft ekuqaleni) ngumgama umphetho wepedi okhuphuka ngawo uye emhlabeni ovele ukhona. Ukusika nokugcwalisa akubali umhlanga walolo hlangothi okungasaselo.
- Lapho udonga lufinyelela uhlangothi oluthambekile, ithambeka liwuzungeza ekugcineni kodonga; izindonga ezimbili zihlangana ngemitre.
- Ngaphansi kwezinkambu: ubude bezindonga, indawo ebonakalayo (ukuphakama × ubude), ukuphakama okuphezulu kakhulu nokumaphakathi.

Ipulani idweba ubuso bangaphandle bodonga ngoludo olumfushane olugxuma lapho, nokuphakama kwalo emaphethelweni omabili nakwendawo ephezulu kakhulu (kusendlalelo lezindonga zokubamba, elingena ku-DXF ne-DWG). Umbono we-3D ubonisa izindonga njengamavolumu (Bonisa › Izindonga zokubamba), futhi ukuthumela kwe-GLB, OBJ kanye ne-USD kuzifaka. Imibono yokuhlanganisa emaphethelweni ibonisa isinyathelo.

**Izexwayiso** (ku-Properties nakuPhaneli Yezinkinga): udonga oluphakeme kunoba **Xwayisa ngaphezulu kobude bodonga** (1.0 m noma 3.3 ft ekuqaleni) "kungadingeka udonga oluklanywe unjiniyela noma imvume; hlola imithetho yendawo", futhi, uma uthayipha **Ithambeka elikhulu kakhulu umhlabathi owumile**, noma yiliphi uhlangothi lapho ukusika noma ukugcwalisa kuthambeke kunalelo zinga. Izexwayiso zingezokwazisa: ukulinganisa kusakha. Yonke inguquko ingesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

## Amabhemu

{{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Berm}}: intaba enophezulu wayo kuyi-**Ukuphakama** ngaphezulu komhlaba ophakathi ngaphansi kwayo, izinhlangothi zayo zilinganiswe.

## Imifudlana

{{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Swale}}, bese uchofoza umugqa womfudlana **ngendlela amanzi agijima ngayo** bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Setha **Ukujula**, **Ububanzi bangezansi** (0 ngohlobo lwe-V) kanye **Ithambeka lohlangothi**. Uma ungakho **Ukwehla**, ingaphansi kwayo lilandela umhlaba, lijule ngaphansi kwawo, futhi alikaze lihambe phezulu; thayipha ukwehla ukuze unikeze ukwehla okuzinzile kusuka ekuqaleni kwawo.

## Amazinga aphoyinti nama-contour

1. {{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Grading area}}: chofoza umugqa wangaphandle wendawo ofuna ukuyibumba.
2. {{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Spot elevation}}: chofoza phakathi kwendawo. Thayipha ukuphakama kwayo ku-Properties, noma sebenzisa izinkinobho ▲ ▼.
3. {{ui:Grade}} › {{ui:Edit a contour}}: chofoza i-contour phakathi kwendawo ukuyithatha njengomugqa ongawuguqula.

Khetha iphoyinti noma i-contour bese **uhudula** (noma amaphoyinti ayo) epulanini: umphezulu ulandela lapho uyakhipha, isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Phakathi kwendawo umhlaba wakhiwa kabusha kusuka emphethweni wawo (lapho uhlangana nomhlaba ovele ukhona), amazinga aphoyinti nama-contour ahlelwe.

## Ukusika nokugcwalisa, ngokuphilayo

Izici zakho zokulinganisa ngazinye zibonisa **Cut** kanye **Fill** kanye nakwesayithi yonke, ngendlela yegridi eyisebenzisa i-Earthwork, njengoba uyashintsha. Izinkinga (ngaphandle komphezulu, umugqa ozigqumayo) zidwetshwe lapho.

## Ukuhlolwa kokulinganisa

Ku-**Ukuhlolwa kokulinganisa** ohlangothini lwesokunxele:

- **Ukuhlaziywa kwethambeka** kudweba umphezulu ngombala ngethambeka: kuze kufike kumkhawulo omncane, phakathi, nokuthambeka okudlula umkhawulo oqinile (5 % no-33.3 % ekuqaleni: ejwayelekile ka-1:20 wezindlela ezifinyeleleka, no-1:3 wokugawula utshani). Ilejenda ithi yiziphi izingxenye ezingena ngamunye. Izindawo ezinamathambeka aqinile ziphrintwa nakumashidi.
- **Imicibisholo yokugeleza nokuchitheka kwamanzi** ikhombisa ukuthi amanzi agijima kanjani futhi ibungaze yonke indawo lapho ayoba khona amachibi, ngokujula kwawo; isigaba sisho ukuthi zingaki izindawo, ezijulile kakhulu nokuthi zibamba amanzi amaningi kangakanani.

Ukuhlolwa kufunda umphezulu ohlongozwayo uma kukhona okulinganisiwe, kungenjalo kufunda umhlaba ovele ukhona.
