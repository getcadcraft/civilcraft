Ingxenye ethi **Umtapo** yendawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi iphethe zonke izinto umugudu noma ikhebula okungazisebenzisa. Akukho lutho kuyo oluxhunywe ezweni noma kumkhiqizi; wena wenza izinto osebenza nazo.

## Izinhlobo ezintathu zezinto

| Uhlobo | Lapho ihlala | Ingashintshwa yini? |
|---|---|---|
| **Izibonelo** | Ifakwe ne-CivilCraft, ngezinombolo ezijwayelekile esizisebenzise ngezakhiwo zezinto | Cha: {{ui:Kopisha emtapweni wami}} kuqala |
| **Esiphrojekthini** | Yafika nefayela lephrojekthi owalivula | Cha: {{ui:Kopisha emtapweni wami}} kuqala |
| **Eyami** | Izinto zakho, zigcinwa kulesi sishayeli | Yebo, nezinguquko zigcinwa masinyane |

Yonke iphrojekthi igcina ikhophi yento ngayinye eyisebenzisayo, ngakho iphrojekthi ivuleka kunoma yiliphi ikhompyutha ngocingo nezinsika zayo.

:::warning
Izinto zesibonelo ziyizibonelo nje: hlola zonke izinombolo ngedatha yomthengisi wakho. Amakhebula angaphansi komhlaba awanaso isilinganiso samanje ngenhloso, ngoba sincike ekutheni ikhebula libekwe kanjani.
:::

## Ukwenza into

1. Ku-**Umtapo**, khetha **Uhlobo** (imiqhubi yezinhlangothi, amakhebula angaphansi komhlaba, amasekelo, impahla noma izinhlobo zomsele).
2. Chofoza {{ui:Into entsha}}, noma khetha isibonelo bese uchofoza {{ui:Kopisha emtapweni wami}} noma {{ui:Phinda}}.
3. Gcwalisa izimo zayo. Izimo ezimakwe ngo-`*` yizona ezidingwa okuhlolwa; into engenayo imakwa ngo-`!` futhi ibhalwa ohlwini lwe-**Okuhlolwa** lapho umugudu uyisebenzisa.
4. Nikeza **Isigaba**, ngo-`/` phakathi kwamazinga: `Overhead/LV/Bundled`, `Supports/Concrete/12 m`. Umuthi ulandela izigaba zakho.

Okudingekayo yilokhu kuhlolwa:

| Into | Okudingekayo kokuhlola |
|---|---|
| Umqhubi wezinhlangothi | indawo yesigaba, ububanzi, isisindo ngemitha, amandla okuqhekeka, imodulus yokuguquka (elastic modulus), ukunwebeka ngokushisa |
| Ikhebula elingaphansi komhlaba | izintambo, indawo yesigaba, ivolovolo, ububanzi, ukumelana, isilinganiso samanje |
| Isekelo | ubude bonke, umthwalo ohlongozwayo (uthathwe ephointini ngaphansi kwesiphezulu), ukujula emhlabeni |
| Impahla | uhlobo lwayo; umthwalo olinganisiwe wama-crossarm namaxhumi nezintambo zokubamba; igebe lezigaba nokwehla kwe-crossarm; ubude be-insulator; i-engile yezintambo zokubamba |
| Uhlobo lomsele | ukujula, ububanzi, ukumbozwa kwamakhebula |
