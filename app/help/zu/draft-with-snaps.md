Okusha ku-0.9: uma udweba, ikhesa lifika emaphointini namakhoneni asevele epulanini, futhi ikhona lepedi, lesigaba noma lomugqa wesici lingabhalwa noma likhethwe. Lesi sihloko sidlulisela ngezinsiza ezintathu.

## Okuzokwenza

- Dweba ngokubamba, ukuze umzila, ipedi, isigaba noma umugqa omusha uqale futhi uphele ngqo kokuthile okukhona.
- Setha i-easting ne-northing yekhona ngokuyibhala noma ngokukhetha indawo epulanini.
- Phendulela ithanjenti lomzila ngasezinhlotsheni ezimbili ezikhethiwe.
- Hambisa, kopisha noma uzungeze into yonke.

## Izinyathelo

1. Khetha ithuluzi lokudweba, isibonelo {{ui:Umzila}}, {{ui:Ipedi}}, {{ui:Isigaba}} noma {{ui:Umugqa wesici}}. Hambisa isikhombisi eduze kwephoyinti lokuhlola, i-PI yomzila, ipedi, isigaba noma ikhona lomthamo, isiphetho somugqa wesici noma isakhiwo samanzi emvula. Kuvela uphawu olunephakheji (PI, Ikhona, Isiphetho, Isakhiwo noma Iphoyinti nenombolo yalo) ne-easting ne-northing lapho iyofika khona. Chofoza ukubeka iphoyinti lapho.
2. Ukubeka iphoyinti ngokukhululeka, bamba {{key:Alt}} uma uchofoza. Ukubamba kuyavaleka.
3. Khetha ipedi, isigaba noma umugqa wesici. Ku-**Izici**, ibhlokhi {{ui:Amakhona}} ihlu wonke amakhona anebhokisi le-Easting nebhokisi le-Northing. Bhala izinombolo ezintsha, noma cindezela inkinobho yekhesa elimaphakathi esiceleni sekhona bese uchofoza indawo epulanini. Ikhona lithatha i-easting ne-northing leyo. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukukhansela ukukhetha. Ikhesa lisebenza kuphela epulanini: vala umbono we-3D kuqala.
4. Ukuze uphendulele ithanjenti, khetha umzila. Ku-**Izici**, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ithanjenti ngamaphoyinti amabili}}, khetha ithanjenti, cindezela {{ui:Iphoyinti lokuqala}} bese uchofoza epulanini, cindezela {{ui:Iphoyinti lesibili}} bese uphinda uchofoze, bese ucindezela {{ui:Setha ukuqondisa}}. Ithanjenti liphendukela ukuthi lisuke kwiphoyinti lokuqala liye kowesibili futhi ligcine ubude balo.
5. Ukuze uhambise, ukopishe noma uzungeze, khetha umzila, ipedi, isigaba noma umugqa wesici. Ku-**Izici**, ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Hambisa, kopisha nezungeza}}, bhala {{ui:Hambela empumalanga}} no-{{ui:Hambela enyakatho}} (izinombolo ezinegethivu ziya entshonalanga nasezansi) bese ucindezela {{ui:Hambisa}} noma {{ui:Kopisha}}. Noma cindezela {{ui:Ibanga kusuka kumaphoyinti amabili}} bese uchofoza iphoyinti ozohamba kulo nephoyinti oya kulo.
6. Ukuze uzungeze, bhala ukuphambuka ku-{{ui:Zungeza ngokuphambene newashi}} (amadigri), bese ucindezela {{ui:Zungeza}} noma {{ui:Kopisha uzungezile}}. Izungezela phakathi kwamakhona alo, ngaphandle uma ukhethe iphoyinti lokuzungezela ngalo nge-ikhesa eduze kwe-engeli.

## Amanothi

- Ikhophi ishiya okokuqala lapho kukhona bese ikhethwa ngemva kwalokho.
- Konke ukuhambisa, ukukopisha, ukuzungeza, amakhona akhethiwe nethanjenti elifakiwe kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- Ukubamba kufinyelela ama-pixel angu-10 esikrini kusuka ekhesa. Uphawu lokubamba linikeza indawo kuphela; akukho okushintsha uze uchofoze.
- Ikhesa elifanayo likhona kumigqa ye-PI yomzila. Bona [Imizila](topic:alignment), [Amasigaba](topic:parcels) no-[Imigqa yesici](topic:feature-lines).
