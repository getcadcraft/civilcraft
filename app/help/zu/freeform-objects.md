Isici esisodwa (ibhokisi lezimbali, udonga lwesihlalo, umfanekiso, indunduma yokudlala) singenziwa ku-ModelCraft noma ku-PartCraft bese sibekwa lapha ukuze sihlale kumodeli nakumashejuli afanayo nakho konke okunye.

1. Ukuhlobisa › Okuningi › {{ui:Into ekhululekile…}} bese ukhetha ifayela le-**STL** noma **OBJ** (ngamayunithi ephrojekthi: amafidi kuphrojekthi yamafidi, amamitha kuphrojekthi yamamitha) noma **into yelabhulali** ye-CadCraft (`.json`) enesakhiwo se-mesh.
2. Chofoza lapho okufanele khona.

## Isisekelo sayo

- **Ilandela umhlaba**: iphoyinti layo elisezansi kakhulu lihlala emhlabeni ophansi kakhulu ngaphansi kwesisekelo sayo, ngakho akukho okuntanta. Izakhiwo zilinganisa ukuthi ingakanani yayo ingaphansi komhlaba.
- **Isika umhlaba**: isisekelo sayo sisethwe ekuphakameni futhi umhlaba ngaphakathi kwesisekelo sayo ugredwa kuso ngamathambeka okukhanya kwemini, ubalwe ku-Umsebenzi womhlabathi njengeplatifomu.

Yiphendule nge-**Phendula**, bese uyidonsa epulanini ukuze uyihambise.

## Okubaliwe

Izakhiwo zinikeza **ivolumu** yayo (yesisindo esivaliwe), **indawo yomphezulu**, **indawo ehlala phansi** nokuphakama, ezilinganiswe kusuka ku-mesh. Ukuthunyelwa kwe-IFC kuyibhala nge-**IFC class** oyikhethile nalezi zinani.

:::note
Indawo ehlala phansi yi-convex hull yomumo: isimo esinemphetho ongaphakathi (ibhokisi lezimbali elinesimo se-L) sigredwa njengokungathi sigcwele.
:::
