**Amahora elanga** abala amahora elanga eqondile afinyelela udonga ngalunye lwengqikithi ngayinye yokwakha nomhlaba ozungezile, ngosuku okukhethile noma abalwe ngesizini. Izingqikithi nomhlaba uqobo zikhiqiza umthunzi; akukho okunye okwenza njalo (hhayi izihlahla, hhayi izimbangi ongazidwebanga). Ukukhanya kwesibhakabhaka okungeqondile akubalwa.

## Ngaphambi kokuqala

Indawo yelanga itholakala kububanzi nobude besayithi, obuvela ku-**ohlelo lokuxhumanisa** lwephrojekthi. Iphrojekthi engenalo yenqatshelwa ngomlayezo: yisethe kuqala (**Amakhoordinethi** eqenjini le-GIS). Ububanzi nobude obubhalwa ngesandla bekuyoba ukuqagela, ngakho akukho bhokisi lazo.

## Yibala

Ku-**Izici**, ngaphansi kwe-**Izingqikithi zokwakha**, **Amahora elanga**:

1. Khetha **Usuku**. Iqala ngosuku ilanga elisezantsi kakhulu lapho ukhona (21 Disemba, noma 21 Juni esigabeni esiseningizimu). Ngesizini, khetha **Phezu kwesizini**, khetha **Usuku lokugcina** nokuthi zingaki izinsuku ezihlukaniswa ukuze uthathe isampula.
2. Bhala **Indawo yesikhathi**: igama njenge `America/Mexico_City`, noma ukuhlukaniswa kwe-UTC njenge `-6`. Igama lilandela imithetho yesikhathi sasemini yalowo mkhakha ngalolo suku; ukuhlukaniswa obukubhalayo kusetshenziswa njengoba ubhale, ngakho sebenzisa lokho okusebenza ngalolo suku. Umugqa ongaphansi kwebhokisi ukutshela ukuthi ukuhlukaniswa okutholiwe yikuphi.
3. Khetha njalo nini ukubeka ilanga (njalo imizuzu emi-5, 10, 15 noma 30), bese ucindezela **Bala amahora elanga**. Isizini isebenza ngabasebenzi basemuva; **Misa** iyayiphelisa.

## Okuzothola

- Ithebula ngesakhiwo ngasinye: **udonga olubi kakhulu**, **ilinganiso** kanye **nodonga oluhle kakhulu**, ngamahora ngosuku, nethebula lodonga ngalunye ngokubheka kwalo namahora ngalinye lesitezi.
- Umhlaba ozungeze izingqikithi, udwetshwe epulanini kusukela emnyama (akukho lilanga) kuya ekwasi (okukhulu kakhulu iseli ngayinye ingakuthola), nokhiye. Amaseli angaphansi kwengqikithi ashiywa engenalutho. **Bonisa umhlaba epulanini** uyawucima.
- **Odonga njenge-CSV** no **Umhlaba njenge-CSV** zigcina izinombolo.

Ngesizini izinombolo ziyilinganiso losuku ngalunye phezu kwezinsuku ezithathelwe isampula. Akukho okulondolozwayo kuphrojekthi: umphumela ulapho ukubukwa, nokuphinda kwenze kwenze kabusha.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Udonga ubala isikhathi njengelanga kuphela lapho ibheke ilanga futhi kungekho okuvimbayo. Udonga obheke ngasemuva alutholi lutho.
- Umhlaba uthathelwa isampula ngegridi encika kubukhulu besayithi, nendawo yelanga phakathi kwesinyathelo sesikhathi ngasinye, ngakho amahora alungile cishe ngesinyathelo osikhethile.
- Indawo yelanga iyifomula yezinkanyezi eshicilelwe, elungile cishe ngomzuzu we-arc.
