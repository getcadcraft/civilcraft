**I-CivilCraft ayinayo ikhodi yokuhlela ngaphakathi kwayo.** Ayazi imithetho yanoma yiliphi idolobha futhi ayinalo ilabhulali yayo. Yonke inombolo kulelikhasi yinto oyithayipha, ethathwe ekhodini yakho yomasipala, futhi yonke imiphumela ithi ilinganiswa ngemikhawulo oyithayiphile. Yindlela yokubona ukuthi imikhawulo yesiza ilolekisa kanjani isakhiwo, hhayi isinqumo sokuthi ukuklama kuvunyelwe yini.

## Thayipha imikhawulo

Ku-**Izakhiwo**, ngaphansi kwe-**Izinhlanga zokwakha**, khetha **Iphasela** (noma khetha inhlanga: imikhawulo yephasela layo ibonakala ngaphansi kwayo). Bese ugcwalisa okusebenzayo; shiya ibhokisi lingenalutho uma "akukho mkhawulo":

- **Umugqa wesitaladi**: umugqa wesiza emgqeni wesitaladi. I-**Ukuhlehla kwangaphambili** isebenza kuwo, i-**Ukuhlehla kwasemuva** isebenza komugqa ophambene kakhulu nawo, kanti i-**Ukuhlehla kwasecaleni** isebenza kweminye.
- **Umkhawulo wobude**.
- **Indiza yesibhakabhaka**: iqala ngobude emgqeni wesiza bese iphakama ngesilinganiso osithayipha ngayo yonke i-1 yobude ingena ngaphakathi, ilinganiswa kusukela emgqeni wesitaladi noma kusukela kuwo wonke amagqa esiza (2 kusho 2 ku-1). Ukuphakama okuvunyelwe phezu kwephuzu kuyinto encane kakhulu phakathi kwendiza nomkhawulo wobude.
- **Isilinganiso sendawo yefulo** (indawo yefulo elibanzi yezinhlanga ephasela ihlukaniswa ngendawo yephasela) kanye **Ukumbozwa** (izinyawo zazo zihlukaniswa ngendawo yephasela, zithayipha ngamaphesenti).

## Ikhava yendawo yokwakha

Khetha **Bonisa ikhava epulanini** ukuze uyidwebe endaweni ethi `A-MASS-ENVL`. Ikhava iyisiza esinqunywe kuwo wonke amagqa esiza ngokuhlehla kwawo okuyinhloko, amagqa ahlehliswayo ahlangana emakhoneni anama-mitre. Umgqa wesiza owenziwe ngegobolo uthathwa njengezingcezu ezimfushane ezingaphansi kuka-0.01 kusuka egobolweni. Izinkaba eziphakathi nezinkaba eziphuma kuzo zombili zisebenza. Uma izinhlehlo zigoqa ngokwazo (ukuhlehla okujulile esizeni esincane noma esigobile kakhulu) akukho ikhava, futhi Izakhiwo ziyasho; zama izibalo ezincane.

## Umphumela

Ngaphansi kwamabhokisi, inhlanga ngayinye nomkhawulo ngamunye kuthola umugqa, unophawu lwe-tick noma i-cross:

- **izinhlehlo**: indawo yenhlanga ephumela ngaphandle kwekhava;
- **ubude**: ubude benhlanga uluqhathanisa nobude obuvunyelwe obukhulu kakhulu phezu kwenyawo yayo (iprizim yobude obufanayo ikhawulelwa ngobude obuphansi obuvunyelwe kunoma yiliphi lamakhoni ayo);
- **isilinganiso sendawo yefulo** kanye **nokumbozwa**: zonke izinhlanga ezisephasela ziyangezwa ndawonye.
