Izibalo zesimo sezulu zivela ku-**iphakethi ledatha**, hhayi ku-app. Vula i-{{ui:Ukuguquka kwesimo sezulu}} ku-Izilungiselelo › Ama-add-on, noma layisha ifayela lephakethi lakho ku-bhlokhi {{ui:Isimo sezulu sesikhathi esizayo}}.

:::warning
Iphakethi elifika ne-add-on liyisampula: izindawo ezintathu (i-Denver, i-Miami, i-Johannesburg), imodeli eyodwa yesimo sezulu, neminyaka emihlanu kuzo zonke iminyaka engu-20. Likhona ukuzama amathuluzi, **hhayi ukuklama**. Iphakethi eliphelele lakhiwa nge-`tools/climate/build-pack.mjs` kusukela edatheni le-NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6.
:::

## Lapho kukhona

Umphezulu › Okuningi › Ikheli kuya endaweni. Setha iphoyinti, engeza izingqimba (ingqimba yezikhukhula ye-FEMA inikeza izinga lezikhukhula elisisekelo) bese ucindezela inkinobho efunda indawo. Ngaphansi kwemigqa yendawo kukhona ibhlokhi {{ui:Isimo sezulu sesikhathi esizayo}}: khetha **Isimo** (SSP2-4.5 noma SSP5-8.5) ne-**Unyaka** (2050 = 2041-2060, 2080 = 2071-2090, kokubili kuqhathaniswa no-1995-2014). Indawo yephakethi eseduze kakhulu nephoyinti iyasetshenziswa, noma eyodwa oyikhethile; kude kakhulu nazo zonke izindawo, kusetshenziswa ushintsho lwesilinganiso somhlaba wonke, futhi iphaneli iyatshela lokho.

## Izinga lezikhukhula lokuklama

Izinga lamanje yilo lezikhukhula elisisekelo le-FEMA uma ingqimba yezikhukhula yalo inalo (ngamafiti, NAVD 88), uma kungenjalo uthaye elakho. Khetha uhlobo lwezikhukhula (olwasemanzini, lomfula noma imvula enamandla, noma kokubili), ukujula kwezikhukhula endaweni ngengxenye yemvula, nomkhawulo wokuphakama uma ufuna. {{ui:Phakamisa izinga lezikhukhula}} ibonisa izinga lamanje nelesikhathi esizayo ndawonye, izingxenye zezinga lolwandle nezemvula, nemibono: konke ukukhuphuka kwezinga lolwandle kwengezwa ezingeni lasogwini; ukugeleza komfula kukhuphuka ngokulinganayo nemvula yosuku olumanzi kakhulu, nokujula kulandela ukugeleza ngamandla angu-0.6 (Manning, umsele obanzi); izingxenye ziyangezwa.

:::warning
Ukuhlolwa kokushintsha, hhayi ucwaningo lwezikhukhula. Izinga lezikhukhula lokuklama liwumthetho womphathi wakho noma unjiniyela wakho.
:::

## Ukuchayeka komlilo wehlathi (ukuhlolwa kuphela)

Izitshalo eziseduze, ukuthambeka okuphezulu kuzo nekhaleni kunikeza ibhendi elisukela kulokuphansi kakhulu kuya kokuphezulu kakhulu, okwamanje nakusesikhathi esizayo (okwesikhathi esizayo kuphindaphinda ushintsho lwephakethi lwe-fire-weather index). **Ukuhlolwa kuphela:** isikali sethu esihlobene ukuze kutholakale ukuthi indawo idinga yini ukuhlolwa okufanele komlilo wehlathi; hhayi isilinganiso sekhodi.

## Ukushisa: ifayela lesimo sezulu esizayo

Khetha ifayela le-`.epw` lanamuhla bese uchofoza {{ui:Yenza ifayela lesimo sezulu esizayo}}: liguqulwa ngendlela eshicilelwe yokushintsha nokwelula (Belcher, Hacker and Powell 2005), bese kuqhathaniswa ukushisa (izinga lokushisa eliphakathi nelishisa kakhulu, izinga lokushisa lokuklama, amahora angaphezu kuka-30 °C nangaphezu kuka-35 °C, amazinga osuku). {{ui:Londoloza ifayela lesimo sezulu esizayo}} liligcina ephaneli Yemvelo, ekuhlolweni kwamandla e-BimCraft noma emathuluzini akho.

## Lapho kuvela khona izibalo

NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 (okuphuma kwemodeli ye-CMIP6 enciphisiwe, CC BY 4.0 ngemodeli ngayinye) yezinga lokushisa, umswakama, ukukhanya kwelanga, imvula nesimo somlilo wehlathi; i-IPCC AR6 yesilinganiso somhlaba wonke sokukhuphuka kwezinga lolwandle, ngaphandle uma iphakethi linezibalo zendawo. Iphaneli ibonisa umthombo, ilayisense nosuku lwephakethi.
