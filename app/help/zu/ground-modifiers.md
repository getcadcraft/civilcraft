Umhlaba ohlongozwayo = umhlaba ovele ukhona + izinguquli, ngokulandelana. Umhlaba ovele ukhona awukaze ushintshwe.

## Uhlu

{{ui:Levels}} › {{ui:Ground modifiers…}} ludweba zonke izinto ezakha umhlaba: amapulatifomu, amabhemu, imifudlana, izindawo zokulinganisa, ukufakwa kwamatshe nemizila, izindawo ezitshalwe nezinga eliqediwe, kanye nezitebhisi namarampu lapho i-**Yakha umhlaba** ibhokisi ithikiwe khona (nezinga lawo eliphansi nelaphezulu). Lapho amabili ehlangana, **elilandelayo** liyanqoba. Hudula umugqa, noma sebenzisa izinkinobho zomcibisholo ukushintsha ukuhleleka; umhlaba uyakhiwa kabusha ngokushesha. Umugqa ngamunye ukhombisa ukusika nokugcwalisa kwawo (okwakha emva kwalezo ezingaphezulu), nesamba esingaphansi kohlu.

## Izivivinyo zokuhlangana

IPhaneli Yezinkinga ihlu, ngo-**iya kuyo**:

- udonga lapho uhlangothi oluphezulu lusephezulu kwaphezulu kwalo (umhlaba phezu kodonga), noma uhlangothi oluphansi lubonisa isisekelo salo;
- indawo etshaliwe lapho izinga layo eliqediweliphezulu kunomphetho wepavumende ehlangana nawo, noma ingaphansi kwawo ngaphezu kwe-150 mm ejwayelekile;
- izitebhisi lapho ingaphansi noma ingaphezulu kwazo kungahlanganisi nepavumende abayifikela kuyo;
- izinguquli ezimbili ezihlanganayo (elilandelayo liyanqoba lapho).

Udonga lokubamba lungaba nophezulu walo olandela uhlangothi oluphezulu lomhlaba: ukuphakama kwalo kubalwa kabusha njalo lapho umhlaba ushintsha.

## Udonga lokubamba olwakha umhlaba

Khetha udonga lokubamba bese uyakha ibhokisi le-**Yakha umhlaba**. Ibanga elingaphansi kodonga (elibanzi njengobukhulu bodonga) lingena ohlwini njengesiguquli:

- umphetho ohlangothini **oluphezulu** ubanjwa emhlabeni lapho, isiteshi ngesiteshi, ukuze ophezulu kodonga walandele uhlangothi oluphezulu (nensimbi ephezulu). Thayipha izinga **Phezulu** ukuze ulibambe kulelo zinga ngaphandle kwe-ikhaphu;
- umphetho ohlangothini **oluphansi** ubanjwa ezingeni othayipha lona, **Umhlaba obanjwe ohlangothini oluphansi**, noma, uma lingenalutho, emhlabeni lapho;
- uhlangothi oluphezulu wuhlangothi umhlaba wawo ophakeme phezu kodonga lonke, ngakho lungaguquki ludlule ohlangothini oluphansi ngasemgqeni wodonga;
- umhlaba ngaphesheya kwebanga awushintshwa, nomhlaba ovele ukhona awukaze ushintshwe.

Njengamanye amamodifaya, lihlala ohlwini, futhi imodifaya elilandela lona liyanqoba lapho zihlangana. Udonga okubanjwe kulo izinga ekugcineni lungaphezu kwe-150 mm ejwayelekile kumhlaba ovele ukhona lapho lingena ePhaneli Yezinkinga (no-**iya kuyo**). Udonga oluzimele alwakhi umhlaba. Ibhokisi livaliwe emafayelini avela ezakhiweni zangaphambili, ngakho avulwa njengoba ayenjalo.

## Amazinga aphoyinti nemicibisholo yokuthambeka

{{ui:Spot level}} (ukuchofoza kukodwa) kanye {{ui:Gradient arrow}} (ukuchofoza kabili) ifunda umhlaba njalo lapho idwetshwa: uma uhambisa noma ulinganisa kabusha into, amalebula ayilandela. Ku-{{ui:Properties}} khetha ukuthi ilebula ifunda umhlaba ohlongozwayo noma owovele ukhona; amalebula womhlaba owovele ukhona abonakala ngaphakathi kwamabhrakethi. Imicibisholo ibheke phansi kwentaba futhi ithi `1:20 (5.0 %)`.
