{{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Linganisa}} (futhi kumenyu ye-{{ui:Okuningi}} yesikhala sezintambo zamandla, noma cindezela {{key:M}}) ilinganisa ngaphandle kokungeza lutho kuphrojekthi.

## Esicwini

1. Khetha {{ui:Linganisa}}, bese uchofoza amaphoyinti. Uchofozo oseduze kwephoyinti lokuhlola lithatha indawo yalo nokuphakama kwalo.
2. Umugqa wesimo ukhombisa inani lamaphoyinti nobude obuphelele, ubude nesiqondiso secala lokugcina, ukuthambeka kwalo uma umhlaba waziwa phansi kokubili okuphelo, nobude phansi. Eduze nesikhombi kukhona icala elidwetshwayo kanye nenani elibalekayo.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala futhi ukuze uvale isimo: umugqa wesimo wengeza **indawo** nomjikelezo. Uchofozo olulandelayo luqala ukulinganisa okusha.
4. {{key:Enter}} iqeda ukulinganisa futhi igcina izinombolo zayo emgqeni wesimo; {{key:Backspace}} ibuyisela iphoyinti lokugcina; {{key:Esc}} iyasula, bese {{key:Esc}} futhi ibuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.

Ubude buku-yunithi yephrojekthi, nezindawo ku-yunithi yazo ye-square. Ukuthambeka nobude phansi kusebenzisa umphezulu ohlongozwayo uma ukhona, uma kungenjalo umhlaba okhona; ngaphandle komphezulu ziyashiywa.

## Ku-phrofayili yomugqa wamandla

Kwi-[iphrofayili yomugqa](topic:overhead-line#the-line-profile), {{ui:Linganisa}} ebhalini yephrofayili idinga uchofozo oluwu-2 futhi ifunda umugqa ophakathi, umehluko wobude, ibanga eliqondile kanye nokuthambeka phakathi kwawo. Uchofozo oseduze komhlaba, intambo noma ophezulu wensika luyabambelela kuwo. Chofoza {{ui:Linganisa}} futhi ukuze umise.
