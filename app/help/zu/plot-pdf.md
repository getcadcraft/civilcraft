I-{{ui:PDF}} ishicilela iplani ibe yi-PDF yevekthaza ishidini elilodwa, nebhendi yesihloko, umcibisholo wasenyakatho kanye nebha yesikali. I-PDF yenziwa kusiphequluli futhi ilandelwa; ayidlulisi ku-ingxoxo yokuphrinta yesiphequluli.

## Shicilela

1. Setha iplani ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubonisa**: ubuso obuboniswayo, ukuhlukana kwamakhonthuwa nokuthi yiziphi izinto ezikhona. Ukushicilela kulandela lezi zilungiselelo.
2. Uma ufuna ingxenye yesayithi kuphela, sondeza bese uhambisa iplani kuyo.
3. Chofoza i-{{ui:PDF}} eqenjini le-**Ukuthumela**, noma cindezela i-{{key:Mod+P}}.
   Ingxoxo ethi **Shicilela iplani ibe yi-PDF** iyavuleka.
4. Khetha i-**Indawo**: i-{{ui:Everything}} noma i-{{ui:What is on screen}}.
5. Khetha i-**Iphepha**: Letter, Tabloid, ANSI D, ARCH D, ISO A4, A3 noma A1. Iphrojekthi yamafiti iqala ku-Tabloid, iphrojekthi yamamitha iqala ku-A3. Amashidi ahlala ehlangothini olubanzi.
6. Khetha i-**Isikali**. Uhlu luphawula isikali esikhulu esilingana ngo-"(fits)" futhi siyasikhetha.
   - Amafiti: 1" = 5', 10', 20', 30', 40', 50', 60', 100', 200', 300', 400', 500', 1000'.
   - Amamitha: 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:500, 1:1000, 1:2000, 1:2500, 1:5000, 1:10000.
7. Khetha i-**Inki**: {{ui:Layer colors}} noma {{ui:Black}}.
8. Funda umugqa ongaphansi kokukhethwayo. Unikeza usayizi wendawo ephepheni kanye nosayizi weshidi, isibonelo "Indawo ingu-297 × 222 mm ku-1" = 40' ePhepha elingu-432 × 279 mm (ehlangothini olubanzi)."
9. Chofoza i-{{ui:Download PDF}}.
   Isiphequluli silanda `<name>.pdf`. Ibha yesimo ithi "Ishicilelwe <name>.pdf ku-1" = 40'."

Uma indawo imkhulu kuneshidi ngesikali osikhethile, iplani iyamaphakathi nemiphetho iyanqunyulwa. Khetha isikali esimakwe ngokuthi "(fits)" noma iphepha elikhulu.

## Okusemshidini

- Iplani, iphakathi endaweni yokudweba, njengamavekthaza aqinisekile: amakhonthuwa, amalebula, i-alignment nesiteshi sayo, amakhoridoro, amapadi nemigqa yokukhanya kwemini, amapayipi, amaphoyinti.
- Ububanzi bemigqa ngengqimba: i-alignment, amapadi namapayipi zinzima; onxantathu namakhonthuwa amancane azacile.
- Ibhendi yesihloko ngaphansi kwephepha negama lephrojekthi (**Igama**), isikali, isaziso sokuhlukana kwamakhonthuwa nobuso obuthile (obukhona noma obugredisiwe), kanye nosuku nebuildi ngakwesokudla.
- Umcibisholo wasenyakatho ekhoneni eliphezulu lakwesokudla (inyakatho yeplani ingaphezulu) nebha yesikali ebhendini yesihloko.
- Izendlalelo zemephu ezibonakala ngaphansi kweplani, uma i-**Izendlalelo zemephu ngaphansi kweplani** ikhethiwe: zidwetshwa njengesithombe esisodwa sendawo yonke yokudweba ku-150 dots per inch, ngaphansi komugqa, futhi isazisi semephu singena ebhendini yesihloko. I-CivilCraft ilinda kuze kube ngu-8 amasekhondi izithombe zemephu ezisafika; uma ezinye zisashoda, ibha yesimo iyakutshela.

Umbhalo usebenzisa i-Helvetica ehlanganiswe ku-PDF. Izinhlamvu ezingaphandle kwesethi yaseNtshonalanga Yurophu (Windows-1252) zishicilelwa njengo-"?", futhi umyalezo wesimo uyazibala.

## Okungashicilelwe

- Amabhendi okuphakama.
- Ukubuka kwephrofayili. Ukushicilela amaphrofayili akukatholakali okwamanje.
- Ngaphezu kweshidi elilodwa (ngomzila, sebenzisa [amashidi eplani neprofayili](topic:plan-profile-sheets)), izimbonile, ibhulokhi yesihloko eyenziwe ngokwakho noma amashidi ahlezi ngokuthi ame.

:::note
Ingxoxo ithi izimpondo zohlelo zibonwa esikrinini kuphela. Kulo mbhalo, izimpondo zohlelo ezivulwe ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubonisa** ziyashicilelwa (nokuthunyelwa ku-DXF). Zicime ngaphambi kokushicilela uma ungazifuni.
:::

## Ukuphrinta ephepheni

Vula i-PDF elandiwe kunoma yimuphi umbukeli we-PDF bese uyiphrinta ngo-100% (usayizi wangempela) ukuze isikali sihlale siqinisekile. "Fit to page" iyashintsha isikali.
