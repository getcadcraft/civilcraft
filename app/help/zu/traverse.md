I-traverse iwuchungechunge lwemilenze elinganisiwe: esiteshini ngasinye kukhona i-engle (noma i-bearing) nebanga elilandelayo. Chofoza **Faka i-traverse** ngaphansi kwe-**Traverse** ngakwesokudla uma kungekho okukhethiwe.

## Lapho iqala khona nalapho iphela

1. **Qala ku-survey point**: inombolo yayo. Noma thayipha i-northing ne-easting.
2. **Imilenze njenge**: **Ama-engle akwesokudla nezinkukhula**, noma **Ama-bearing nezinkukhula**.
3. Ngama-engle, tshela lapho aqala khona: **Backsight survey point**, noma **i-azimuth yayo** (kusukela esiteshini sokuqala kuya ku-backsight, ijikeleza ngakwesokudla kusukela enyakatho).
4. **Ukuphela**: **emuva esiqalweni (ilophu)**, noma **ku-survey point** nenombolo yayo (i-traverse evulekile phakathi kwamaphoyinti aziwayo).

## Thayipha imilenze

Umugqa owodwa umugqa, bese ibanga, bese igama lesiteshi elifinyelela kulo uma uthanda:

- Ama-engle akwesokudla, ajikeleza ngakwesokudla esiteshini umugqa oshiya kuso, kusukela esiteshini esingemuva kuya kowelandelayo: `270°00'10" 125.50 B`. `270-00-10`, `270 00 10` kanye no `270.0028` nakho kuyasebenza.
- Ama-bearing: `N 45°30'15" E 125.50 B`, noma i-azimuth njengegama elilodwa: `AZ 45-30-15 125.50 B`.
- Kwilophu elinama-engle, phela ngo-`close` kanye ne-engle esiqalweni futhi, ejikeleza kusuka esiteshini sokugcina kuya kowesibili: `close 270°00'05"`. Yileyo ehlola ama-engle.

Imigqa engafundeki ibhalwa ngezinombolo zayo zomugqa. I-"feet", amakhoma kanye nemigqa yokuphawula enesimo `#` ayamukelekile.

## Funda ukuvalwa

Njengoba uthayipha, ingxoxo ibonisa:

- **Ukungavali kwe-engle**: amagilo ama-engle akhuphuka kangakanani ukuze avale, nesabelo sengxenye ngayinye yengle. Ngaphandle kwe-engle yokuvala, ama-engle awahlolwa, futhi noma yiliphi iphutha kuwo livela ku-ukungavali komugqa.
- **Ukungavali komugqa**: i-traverse iphela kude kangakanani nalapho kufanele iphele khona, nasendleleni.
- **Ukunemba**: ubude be-traverse bohlukaniswe ngokungavali komugqa: 1 : 10,000 kusho i-1 ft kuma-10,000 ft.
- Iziteshi ngemva kokulungiswa, nokuthi ngayinye ihambe kangakanani.

## Ukulungiswa

Kuqala ukungavali kwe-engle kuhlakazwa ngokulinganayo phakathi kwama-engle. Bese ukungavali komugqa kuhlakazwa phezu kwemilenze ngomthetho wekhampasi (Bowditch): isiteshi ngasinye sinyakaza ngokuphambene nokungavali ngokulingana nobude obuhanjwe ukufika kuso, ukuze i-traverse iphele ngqo lapho kufanele khona.

:::note
Umthetho wekhampasi uwukulungisa okujwayelekile, hhayi okwe-least-squares, futhi awulinganisi ama-engle nezinkukhula. Ukuthi ukunemba kwanele yini kuncike ezindleleni zakho nasemithethweni yesifunda sakho.
:::

## Sebenzisa umphumela

- **Engeza njengamaphoyinti okuhlola umhlaba**: iziteshi ezilungisiwe ziba ngamaphoyinti okuhlola umhlaba ngamagama owanikeza (noma izinombolo ezilandelayo ezikhululekile), incazelo `TRAV`, ngaphandle kokuphakama. Isiteshi sokugcina selophu siyisiqalo sayo, ngakho asingezwa kabili. Isinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} uyahlehlisa.
- **Engeza njenge-paseli** (amalophu): iziteshi ezilungisiwe ziba [ipaseli](topic:parcels), namalebula ayo nencazelo yalo esemthethweni.
- **Umbiko (CSV)**: imigqa yokuvalwa, bese kuba isiqondiso, ibanga, izixhumanisi ezingalungisiwe nezilungisiwe kanye nokulungiswa kwesiteshi ngasinye.
