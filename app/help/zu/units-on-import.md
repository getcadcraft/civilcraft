## Okuzokwenziwa

Lethe ifayela kuphrojekthi bese uqinisekisa ukuthi lingena ngosayizi ofanele.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula ifayela njengoba ujwayelekile: {{ui:Vula}}, yiphonsele epulanini, noma umyalo wedwebo le-CAD.
2. Uma ifayela libhala iyunithi yalo, akukho okubuzwayo: ingxoxo yokungenisa iyibonisa njenge-**Njengoba umdwebo ukusho**.
3. Uma kungenjalo, ingxoxo ibuza **Iyunithi ye-** ifayela ngaphambi kokuvulwa kwengxoxo yokungenisa. Khetha ukuthi iyunithi eyodwa efayeleni iyini bese uchofoza {{ui:Sebenzisa lezi zinyunithi}}.
4. {{ui:Khansela}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) ukhipha ifayela. Akukho okufundwa kuphrojekthi.

## Yiziphi amafayela ayakusho iyunithi yawo

- **DWG ne-DXF**: ama-insertion units womdwebo (`$INSUNITS`). Ifayela elingenawo liyabuzwa. Uhlu **Iyunithi zomdwebo** kwingxoxo yokungenisa luqala ngempendulo yakho futhi lusengashintsha.
- **STL ne-OBJ** (izimo zomhlaba ezingahleliwe): azikaze zishilo iyunithi. Uyabuzwa.
- **Izinhlu zamaphoyinti** (CSV, TXT) nezinhlu zokuhlola zenqubekelo: uhlu olushilo iyunithi yalo emigqeni yalo yokuqala luyaguqulwa, futhi umugqa wesimo uyasho lokho. Oluthile olungashongo lutho luyabuzwa lapho ucindezela {{ui:Ngenisa}}, ngaphambi kokungena kwamaphoyinti. Amakholomu e-latitude nelongitude adlula kuhlelo lwe-coordinate kunalokho.
- **Isengezo se-Point clouds**: sibuza lapho wakha ifu lamaphoyinti elingashongo iyunithi, futhi silebhele izinombolo zefu ngeyunithi elithwala lona.
- **IFC ne-LandXML**: zishilo eziyunithi zazo; ziguqulelwa kuyunithi zephrojekthi.
- **LAS, LAZ namagridi wokuphakama**: abekwa ngohlelo lwawo lwe-coordinate; ingxoxo yendawo ibuza iyunithi yokuphakama uma ifayela lingasho.

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- Impendulo ikhunjulwa lelo fayela kulelikhompyutha, kuzo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft. Ngesikhathi esilandelayo lingena akubuzwa.
- Ukubuzwa futhi: ipaleti yomyalo ({{key:Mod+K}}) › **Buza futhi iyunithi yamafayela angeniswayo**.
- Iyunithi uhlu oluyishilo ifundwa emigqeni yalo yokuqala eyisishiyagalombili: umugqa onjengo `units: ft`, noma izihloko zamakholomu aphethe onke iyunithi efanayo phakathi kwemifantu, njengo `Elevation (m)`.
