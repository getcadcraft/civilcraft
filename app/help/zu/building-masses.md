Umthamo wesakhiwo ungumthamo nesisekelo, inani lamaphansi nobude bephansi: umbuzo wasekuqaleni "imalini isakhiwo esingalingana lapha, futhi senzani emhlabathini?" Imithamo ihlala ephlanini ngegama layo, inani lamaphansi nendawo yonke yamaphansi, ingena kuphrinta ye-PDF nasekuthunyelweni kwe-DWG ne-DXF ezingqimbeni `A-MASS` ne-`A-MASS-TEXT`, futhi igcinwa kufayela leprojekthi.

## Dweba umthamo

1. Ku-**Izakhiwo**, ngaphansi kwe-**Imithamo yezakhiwo**, chofoza **Dweba umthamo**. (Itholakala nasemenyini ethi **Okuningi** ngasemuva kwe-**Umphezulu** kwithuluzi.)
2. Chofoza amakhona esisekelo. Ukuchofoza eduze kwekhona lepharseli noma iphoyinti lokuhlola kuthatha indawo yalelo phoyinti ngokuqondile.
3. Chofoza ikhona lokuqala futhi, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}, ukuze uqede. {{key:Backspace}} ubuyisela ikhona lokugcina; {{key:Esc}} uyayeka.

Umthamo omusha unamaphansi amathathu nobude bephansi obungu-12 ft (3.6 m), futhi ungowepharseli lapho phakathi kwawo ukhona.

## Amaphansi, ukuphakama nendawo

Chofoza umthamo ukuze ushintshe **Igama**, **Ukusetshenziswa**, **Amaphansi**, **Ubude bephansi** nethe **Ipharseli**. **Ukuphakama** kuyamaphansi aphindwe ngobude bephansi: bhala ukuphakama bese inani lamaphansi libe yinombolo ephelele eseduze (okungabi ngaphansi kuka-1). **Isisekelo** ne-**Indawo yonke yamaphansi** (isisekelo siphindwe ngamaphansi) kubalwa wena.

## Ngomhlaba othambekile

Umhlaba ongaphansi komthamo ufundwa emphezulwini: umhlaba okhona, noma umhlaba olungisiwe lapho ukhethe **Umhlaba oyakhiwe (olungisiwe)**. **Ngomhlaba othambekile** kusho lapho iphansi lokuqala lishiya khona:

- **Ukulinganisa**: emhlabeni ophakathi ngaphansi kwesisekelo. Uhlangothi oluphansi luyagcwaliswa kuze kufike lapho, luphezulu luyasikwa, bese zisondelana ukuze zilinganiswe.
- **Ipodiyamu**: emhlabeni ophakeme kakhulu. Isisekelo esiqinile sigcwalisa uhlangothi oluphansi.
- **Ukusika**: emhlabeni ophansi kakhulu. Uhlangothi oluphezulu lusikwa lusuke entabeni.

Izakhiwo zibonisa uhla lomhlaba ngaphansi komthamo, **Ileveli yephansi lokuqala**, nevolumu yokugcwalisa nokusika phakathi komhlaba nalelo zinga. Indawo yesisekelo engaphandle komphezulu iyashiywa, futhi Izakhiwo ziyatshela ukuthi ingaki.

## Ku-3D

Imithamo idwetshwa kumbono we-3D wesayithi, ngomugqa ozungeze wonke amaphansi nemigqa egxile ngaphezu kophahla. **Bonisa** unebhokisi lokuhlola lethi **Imithamo yezakhiwo**. Chofoza eyodwa ukuze uyikhethe. Uma umthamo ukhethiwe:

- hudula uphahla lwawo phezulu noma phansi ukuze uyidonse noma uyisunduze iphansi elilodwa ngesikhathi, noma cindezela {{key:PageUp}} ne-{{key:PageDown}};
- lonke ushintsho luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokwenza-kabusha.

Umbono wesitaladi we-**Hamba** nawo ubona imithamo, futhi **Ilanga** lidonsa izithunzi zayo emhlabeni.

## Yithumele

- **Imithamo njenge-IFC** ilondoloza yonke imithamo ibe ngefayela le-IFC4: indawo, isakhiwo esinamazinga, nomthamo njengento yesakhiwo nezinombolo zawo kusethi yezakhiwo `CadCraft_SiteMass`. Uma uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi lusethiwe kuphrojekthi, ifayela libekwa emaphethini futhi linelatitude nelongitude yendawo; uma lingekho, alisho lutho ngokuthi likuphi.
- **Klama ku-BimCraft** ivula i-BimCraft kuthebhu entsha ngemithamo. Lapho, ukukhetha umthamo kubonisa izinombolo zawo kanye **Klama lo mthamo**, okwakha izindonga kuzo zonke izinga nesitezi kanye nephansi ngaphansi kwesakhiwo, kusukela esisekelweni, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokwenza-kabusha. Akukho okulayishwayo: ifayela lidlula phakathi kwamathebhu amabili alesi siphequluli.

Bona futhi **Imikhawulo yokuhlelwa kwezindawo** nethe **Amahora elanga**.
