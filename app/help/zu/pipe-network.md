Iphrojekthi ibamba inethiwekhi eyodwa yamapayipi edonsa ngesisindo, imvula noma imfucuza. Yakhiwe ngezakhiwo (izimbobo, izingenisi) namapayipi aqondile phakathi kwazo. Ukugeleza kuhamba ngendlela odweba ngayo. I-CivilCraft ihlola ipayipi ngalinye nesakhiwo ngasinye ngokuvumelana nemithetho emithathu osetha wena, futhi ingasetha wonke amazinga aphansi kusuka kuleyo mithetho.

## Dweba umugqa

Ungadweba amapayipi ngaphandle komphezulu, kodwa imiphetho, ukumbozwa namazinga azenzakalelayo adinga enye.

1. Cindezela {{key:P}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Amapayipi}} eqenjini le-**Ukuklama**.
   Isiqondiso sithi: "Amapayipi: chofoza izakhiwo kusuka phezulu womugqa uye ezansi (chofoza ekhona ukuze uyihlanganise). Enter iqeda."
2. Chofoza lapho isakhiwo esiphezulu sihlala khona. Isakhiwo siyabekwa.
3. Chofoza ngayinye isakhiwo esilandelayo esiphansi komfula. Ukuze uxhume esakhiweni esikhona, chofoza kuso: ibha yesimo ithi "Joined S3."
4. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
   Amapayipi adwetshwa, amazinga aphansi athathwa kumithetho, futhi ibha yesimo ithi, isibonelo, "3 pipes drawn, 12, inverts set from the rules; every check passes."

Izakhiwo ezintsha zithiwa S1, S2 … nobubanzi obungu-48 in (1200 mm). Amapayipi amasha athiwa P1, P2 … nobubanzi obungu-12 in (300 mm). Ubukhulu bubalwa ngamayintshi kwiphrojekthi yamafiti nangamamillimitha kwiphrojekthi yamamitha.

Qeda ngo-{{key:Enter}}. Ukuchofoza kabili nakho kuyasebenza, kodwa uma uchofoza kabili esakhiweni esikhona ukuze uphelise umugqa lapho, ukuhlanganiswa kuyalahleka: chofoza kanye bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

## Setha imithetho yakho

Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, ipaneli yesokudla (**Iphrojekthi**) inebhlokhi ye-**Pipe network**:

| Isilungiselelo | Inani lokuqala | Okusho |
|---|---|---|
| Uhlobo | {{ui:Imvula}} | {{ui:Imvula}} noma {{ui:Ukudonsa kwemfucuza}}; ibhalwa ku-LandXML |
| Isiyingi esincane | 0.50% | Ipayipi ngalinye kufanele lehle okungenani lokhu |
| Ukumbozwa okuncane | 2 ft (0.6 m) | Ukusuka emhlabeni kuya ophezulu wepayipi, kulo lonke ipayipi |
| Ukwehla ezakhiweni | 0.10 ft (0.03 m) | Iphuma ye-invert kufanele ibe ngaphansi kakhulu kwe-invert ngayinye yokungena |

**Lezi zinani zokuqala ziyizibambi, hhayi isilinganiso.** Setha amanani esidingwa yi-ajensi yakho noma iphrojekthi. Ukumbozwa kulinganiswa kusuka emhlabeni kuya ophezulu (invert kanye nobubanzi bangaphakathi); ubukhulu bodonga abubalwa. Lapho amapedi noma amakhoridoro egredwe, umphezulu ogredwe ngumhlaba.

## Vumela i-CivilCraft isethe ama-inverts

- {{ui:Setha wonke amazinga aphansi ngezimiso}} (ebhlokhini le-**Pipe network**) iyasetha wonke amapayipi.
- {{ui:Setha amazinga aphansi ngezimiso}} (uma ipayipi eyodwa likhethiwe) lisetha leli payipi kuphela.

Ngokusebenza ngasemuva kwesiteshi (downstream), umgijimo ngamunye uqala okungenani kumbozwa esakhiweni sawo esiphezulu, wehla okungenani isiyingi esincane, wehla okungenani ukwehla ngakho konke ukudlula kwesakhiwo ngasinye, futhi ujulile lapho umhlaba ohamba nepayipi ungashiya ukumbozwa okungaphansi kwalokho okuncane. Amazinga aphansi agunyazwa aye ku-0.01 esiphansi, hhayi phezulu. Ibha yesimo ithi "Inverts set: least cover at the top of each run, at least the least slope, at least the drop in each structure." noma iqamba ukuthi yini engenzeka.

## Hlela izakhiwo namapayipi

Chofoza isakhiwo noma ipayipi nge-{{ui:Khetha}} ukuze ulibone epaneli yesokudla.

**Isakhiwo**: **Igama**, **Umphetho**, **Ukujula kwesigxobo**, **Ububanzi**, bese **Umphetho osetshenziswe**, **Isigxobo**, **Easting** no-**Northing**, namapayipi angena nawaphuma ngamazinga awo aphansi.

- Shiya **Umphetho** ungaphenyiwe ukuze uwuthathe emphezulwini; ibhokisi lisho inani lomphezulu ngombala ogrey. Thayipha inani ukuze ulungise umphetho.
- Donsa isibambo sesakhiwo epulanini ukuze usihambise. Amapayipi aso ayalandela; amazinga aphansi ahlala njengoba ayekunjalo, ngakho hlola izinkinga ngemuva.

**Uhlobo**: khetha esinye sezinhlobo zesakhiwo zenethiwekhi (ngezansi), noma akukho lutho ukwenza isakhiwo esijikelezayo sobubanzi bayo.

**Ipayipi**: **Igama**, izakhiwo okuhamba phakathi kwazo, **Ububanzi**, **Upper invert**, **Lower invert**, bese **Ubude**, **Isiyingi** no-**Ukumbozwa okuncane**.

Cindezela {{key:Enter}} emva kwenani ngalinye.

## Funda futhi ulungise izinkinga

Ibhlokhi ye-**Pipe network** ihlu yonke inkinga (kuya kwangu-40); chofoza eyodwa ukukhetha ipayipi noma isakhiwo. Ipayipi noma isakhiwo esikhethiwe sihlu eyaso. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe, kuthi "Wonke amapayipi nezakhiwo adlula imithetho."

| Umlayezo (izibonelo) | Okufanele wenze |
|---|---|
| "P2 runs uphill (-0.40%)." noma "… runs flat …" | Shintsha ama-inverts, noma uwasethe ngemithetho. |
| "P2 at 0.30% is flatter than the minimum 0.50%." | Yehlisa invert esezansi noma phakamisa eyangaphambili. |
| "P3 has 1.40 of cover 35 along it, less than the minimum 2." | Yehlisa ipayipi; setha ama-inverts wayo ngemithetho. |
| "In S3, P4 leaves at 245.20, less than 0.1 below P3 coming in at 245.25." | Yehlisa i-upper invert yepayipi eliphumayo. |
| "P1's crown is above S1's rim." | Ipayipi liphezulu kakhulu kwesakhiwo. |
| "S2 has 2 pipes leaving it; a gravity network should have one way out of each structure." | Susa ipayipi elilodwa eliphumayo, noma uyidwebe kabusha ngendlela efanele. |
| "The pipes form a loop; flow has to end at an outlet." | Umgijimo udwetshwe wabuyela kuwo; susa ipayipi elilodwa. |
| "S5 is off the surface and has no rim elevation." | Thayipha umphetho, noma uhambise isakhiwo phezu komphezulu. |
| "P6 runs partly off the surface; cover there is unknown." | Nweba umphezulu, noma wamukele ukuthi ukumbozwa lapho akwaziwa. |
| "P7 has no inverts yet." | Thayipha wena, noma uwasethe ngemithetho. |

Amapayipi nezakhiwo ezinenkinga nazo zimakwa ngobomvu epulanini.

## Uhlobo lwezakhiwo

Uhlobo lwesakhiwo yisakhiwo esakhelwe ngamapharamitha: umbobo, isingenisi, ibhokisi lokuhlanganisa noma indawo yokuphuma, **ijikelezayo** (ububanzi bangaphakathi) noma **enxeleyo** (ububanzi bangaphakathi nobude), nobukhulu bodonga lwayo, **ipuleti lesisekelo**, **isigxobo** ngaphansi kwepayipi eliphansi kakhulu, kanye **nohlaka nesivalo** (ukuvulwa okujikelezayo noma okunxeleyo, ukuphakama kohlaka). Izinhlobo zingezenethiwekhi, ngaphansi kwe-**Structure types** kuma-Properties yephrojekthi.

1. Chofoza {{ui:Uhlobo lwesakhiwo olusha}}. Ingxoxo idweba isakhiwo epulanini nasesigabeni njengoba uthayipha.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Engeza uhlobo}}.
3. Khetha isakhiwo bese ukhetha uhlobo ngaphansi kwe-**Uhlobo**. Lithatha usayizi wohlobo nesigxobo sayo; isigxobo sayo sisengaguqulwa.

Hlela uhlobo bese zonke izakhiwo zalolo hlobo zilandela usayizi wazo. Susa uhlobo bese izakhiwo zakho zihlala zingezakhiwo ezijikelezayo zobubanzi obufanayo. Ushintsho ngalunye yinyathelo elilodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyalihlehlisa.

Isakhiwo esinxeleyo sidwetshwa epulanini, ku-DXF, DWG ne-PDF, nasekuthunyelweni kwe-3D njengesikwele ngobude bayo ngakwepayipi laphumayo; amapayipi aqala odongeni lwayo. I-LandXML iyibhala njengesakhiwo esinxeleyo (`RectStruct`) nobukhulu bodonga lwayo, futhi ifunda izakhiwo ezinjalo njengezinhlobo.

### Yabelana ngohlobo nge-library

{{ui:Londoloza njengento yomtapo}} ibhala uhlobo njengento yomtapo we-CadCraft (ifayela le-`.json`): isimpawu sayo sepulani, umzimba we-3D ojule ngokujwayelekile, nakho konke usayizi. Layisha emtapweni ukuze wabelane. {{ui:Vula into yomtapo}} ifunda isakhiwo esilondolozwe ngale ndlela sibuyele enethiwekhini, siguqulwe saba amayunithi ephrojekthi. Izinto zomtapo ezingesona izakhiwo ezenziwe ku-CivilCraft ziyenqatshwa, nesizathu sichazwe.

## Susa

Khetha ipayipi bese ucindezela {{key:Delete}} ukulisusa. Ukususa isakhiwo kususa namapayipi axhunyiwe kuso futhi. Ku-Mac, cindezela Fn bese usula, noma chofoza {{ui:Susa}}.

## Akukakwakhiwa

Ukugeleza, usayizi wamapayipi kanye nomugqa wezinga lamanzi ahlanganisiwe kuchazwe ku-[Ukudonsa kwemvula](topic:storm-drainage). Amakhathalogi amapayipi, amapayipi angajikile, amapayipi kwiphrofayili yomugqa, amapayipi ajikelezayo nangaphezu kwenethiwekhi eyodwa ngephrojekthi awatholakali okwamanje.
