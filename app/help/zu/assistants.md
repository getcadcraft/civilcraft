## Okuzofunda

Ukuthi ungamvumela kanjani umsizi we-AI (noma yimuphi umsizi okhuluma i-MCP) asebenze nendawo oyivulile, yini angayenza nayingayenzi, nokuthi ungamyeka kanjani.

|---|---|---|

Isebenza nendawo evulekile. Ku-Studio ingonga futhi ilondoloze ezifolda ozikhethele (ngezansi); ngaphandle kwefolda amafayela ayo ayalandwa njengezinkinobho zawo.

## Vula

Abasizi bafinyelela i-CivilCraft nge-CadCraft Studio ekhompyutheni yakho; isiphequluli esivamile asinayo indlela ngena.

1. Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} bese ukhetha ithebhu ethi **CivilCraft**.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-**Abasizi**, faka itiki ku-**Vumela abasizi be-AI nezikripthi basebenzise i-CivilCraft**.
3. **Funda** ikhethiwe ngawe. Faka itiki **Hlela**, **Londoloza** noma **Thumela** kuphela uma umsizi edinga.
4. Ukuze avumelwe ukulondoloza nokuthumela ku-folda, khetha {{ui:Engeza ifolda…}} ngaphansi kwe-**Amafolda angasetshenziswa** bese ukhetha ifolda kuxoxo lwe-Studio. {{ui:Susa}} ukhipha lokho masinyane; noma iyiphi enye indlela inqatshwa.
5. Xhuma umsizi ku-Studio (i-Studio ikhombisa ikheli nezilungiselelo okufanele uzinamathisele kuwo).

## Lapho umsizi exhunyiwe

Isaziso esiphezulu kophaneli wangakwesokudla sisho ukuthi ngumuphi umsizi oxhunyiwe nokuthi ungenzani. Chofoza {{ui:Nqamula}} ukuze uphelise isikhathi sakhe masinyane; kufanele axhume futhi ukuze aqhubeke.

**Umsebenzi**, ngaphansi kwe-**Abasizi** kuZilungiselelo, ubhala konke ukubizwa umsizi akwenzile, osha kuqala, nokuthi kuphumelele yini.

:::tip
Buza ngamagama alula, isibonelo "yini ukuphakama komhlaba njalo ngamafiti ngomugqa oyi-Main Street?", "ipedi lidinga kangakanani ukumba nokugcwalisa?" noma "engeza indawo yokulawula kulawa makhoordinethi". Umsizi ufunda indawo bese ephendula, noma wenze ushintsho njengesinyathelo esisodwa osingasibuyisela.
:::

## Ngaphandle kokuvula i-CivilCraft

Iseva yamafayela yabasizi ye-CadCraft (`npx @getcadcraft/mcp`) nomugqa womyalo we-`cadcraft` ivula amaphrojekthi alondoloziwe e-CivilCraft (`.civilcraft.json`) namafayela wamaphoyinti ngekhodi yeCivilCraft uqobo, ngaphandle kwewindi: amathuluzi afanayo okufunda, ukungeza amaphoyinti, ukulondoloza iphrojekthi, i-LandXML nendawo ku-3D njenge-OpenUSD. `cadcraft convert site.civilcraft.json site.xml` ibhala i-LandXML kumugqa womyalo. Ukuvula i-LandXML, ukuthunyelwa kwe-DWG namathuluzi wesikhala se-Landscape kusadinga iwindi le-CivilCraft.

## Vala

Susa itiki ku-**Vumela abasizi be-AI nezikripthi basebenzise i-CivilCraft**. Wonke umsizi uyanqanyulwa masinyane, kodwa lesi silungiselelo sigcinwa ukuze sisebenze ngesikhathi esilandelayo.
