## Okuzofunda

Ukukhetha amaphoyinti okuhlola ngokudweba ezungezile, lapho ibhokisi lingathatha amaphoyinti ongawadingi.

## Dweba i-lasso

1. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha}} (noma cindezela {{key:V}}).
2. Bamba {{key:Shift}}, cindezela epulanini bese udonsa **ezungezile** amaphoyinti ofuna, uqale **ngakwesokudla**.
3. Yekela.

Amaphoyinti angaphakathi kohlaka afakwa ekukhetheni. Ukudonsa okuhlala cishe kumugqa oqondile kudweba ibhokisi, njengakuqala; uma sekugoba kude nalowo mugqa (ngaphezu kwamapiksela angu-12 cishe) sibe yi-lasso.

## Iwindi, ukudlula nomgqa

| Udonsa | I-lasso | Ikhetha |
|---|---|---|
| Kwesokudla kuqala | Iwindi (uhlaka oqinile) | Amaphoyinti angaphakathi kwalo |
| Kwesobunxele kuqala | Ukudlula (odashiwe, oluhlaza) | Amaphoyinti angaphakathi kwalo, nalawo uhlaka oluthinta wona |
| Cindezela {{key:Space}} ngenkathi udonsa | Iwindi, bese ukudlula, bese umgqa | Umgqa (indlela edashiwe, emile, ewolintshi, ingavaliwe): amaphoyinti indlela edlula phezu kwawo |

Umugqa wesimo utshela igama le-lasso ngenkathi udonsa. {{key:Esc}} ngenkathi udonsa ikhansela, akukhethi lutho.

:::tip
Umgqa uyindlela esheshayo yokukhetha izithombe ngemuva kwekhanivi noma umfudlana wamanzi: donsa ngawo bese uyekela.
:::
