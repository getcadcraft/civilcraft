Lokhu yikho okushintshile ku-CivilCraft 0.7, uma kuqhathaniswa no-0.6. Izingxenye ezintsha ezinkulu yi-Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi ne-Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle kanye nezinhlanga zezakhiwo.

## Okusha

### Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle

- **Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle endaweni efanayo.** {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle}} yengeza amathuluzi okutshala, okulinganisa, izakhiwo ezinzima nokunisela endaweni osunayo: amaphuzu efuthi efanayo, imiphezulu kanye nefayela lephrojekthi elifanayo. Khetha **Civil** ngaphansi kwe-**Indawo yokusebenza** ukuze ubuyele. Bona [Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle](topic:landscape).
- **Uhlu lwezitshalo.** Cishe izihlahla ezingu-420 ezivamile ezishisayo nezaseMedithera, izihlahla ezincane, imiduli, utshani, izitshalo ezihlala iminyaka, izimbali ezihlala iminyaka, izitshalo ezimbozela umhlabathi kanye nezimbaza, ngobukhulu bazo ekutshaleni nasekuvuthweni, izinga lokubanda, ukukhanya, amanzi kanye nebanga. Sesha ngegama elingoliwe ngesiNgisi, isiSpanish noma isiPutukezi. Ngenisa uhlu lwakho njenge-CSV; lugcinwa neprojekthi. Izinkulumo ezakhelwe ngaphakathi zingamanani ajwayelekile futhi azungeziwe: zihlole nenursery yendawo. Bona [Uhlu lwezitshalo](topic:plant-list).
- **Amapulani okutshala.** {{ui:Isitshalo}} sibeka izitshalo ezingodwa ezihlala phansi futhi zilandela ukulinganisa okuzolandela, {{ui:Ibhedi}} igcwalisa umlinganiselo ngebanga lezitshalo ngenhlanganisela ngamaphesenti, kanti {{ui:Umduli wezihlahla}} usetha imigqa eyodwa noma emibili eduze komugqa. Izitshalo zibhalwa kupulani. Bona [Amapulani okutshala namashidi](topic:planting).
- **Ishidi lezitshalo.** Okuningi › {{ui:Uhlu lwezitshalo…}} ibala izitshalo ngohlobo kanye nobukhulu bokutshala. Lilondoloze njenge-CSV noma nge-XLSX, noma liyithumele ku-TakeoffCraft ukuze ulinde intengo. Bona [Ishidi lezitshalo](topic:planting#the-plant-schedule).
- **Ukulinganisa kwesikhala sangaphandle.** {{ui:Ukulinganisa}} kunezakhiwo (ezilinganayo noma ezehla), amagquma, imifantu kanye neziza zokulinganisa ezinamaphoyinti angaphezulu anikezwa wena nezingqikithi odonsa zona. Ukusika nokugcwalisa kubonakala njengoba usenza. **Ukuhlaziywa kwegradi** kudweba umphezulu ngemikhawulo yakho emibili, kanti **Imicibisholo yokugeleza nokugcwala kwamanzi** ikhombisa lapho amanzi ageleza khona nalapho ayogcwala khona. Konke lokhu kungena emphezulwini ofanayo ohlongozwayo njengamaphedi namakhonco. Bona [Ukulinganisa kwesikhala sangaphandle](topic:landscape-grading).
- **Ukugcwala, imizila, izindonga, imiphetho yomgwaqo, iramu kanye nezinyathelo.** {{ui:Izakhiwo ezinzima zangaphandle}} idweba ukugcwala okunokuwa kwasecaleni noma okuhla kuya emfudlaneni, iphethini kanye namazinga ayo; imizila elandela umhlaba; izindonga zokubamba nezimi ezimile ngokwazo ezinezisekelo ezinezinyathelo; imiphetho yomgwaqo; kanye neramu nezinyathelo ezihlolwa ngemikhawulo oyisethile. Udonga lokubamba olungaphezu kuka-1.2 m (4 ft) luphawulwa ukuze unjiniyela alubheke. Bona [Ukugcwala, izindonga nezinyathelo](topic:hardscape).
- **Isibalo sokunisela.** {{ui:Ukunisela}} kubeka izinhloko, imigudu emincane, umgudu oyinhloko, amavalvu, imigudu yamaconsi kanye nendawo yokuxhuma. {{ui:Hlola uhlelo…}} ithola izindawo ezivela kumapayipi, ibale ukugeleza kwendawo ngayinye, uxinzelelo oludingekayo ezivalvwini zayo nasekuhlinzekweni (Hazen–Williams), ukuna, izikhathi zokusebenza, ukumbozwa kanye nohlu lwezinto. Akuwona ukuqinisekiswa kokuklanywa: umklami wokunisela uhlola yonke imiphumela. Izinhloko ziwuhlobo jikelele, noma zakho ezivela kufayela. Bona [Ukuklanywa kokunisela](topic:irrigation).
- **Amashidi esikhala sangaphandle.** Okuningi › {{ui:Amashidi wesikhala esiluhlaza…}} yenza amapulani okutshala, okulinganisa nokunisela ngezincazelo zawo, kanye neshidi lemininingwane yezigaba zezindonga, ukwakhiwa kokugcwala kanye nezinyathelo, njenge-PDF eyodwa. Bona [Amashidi esikhala sangaphandle](topic:landscape-sheets).
- **Izinto ezikhululekile.** Okuningi › {{ui:Into ekhululekile…}} ibeka imeshi ye-STL, i-OBJ noma into yelabhulali eyenziwe kwenye i-app, njengesitsha sezitshalo noma umbhalo. Ihlala phansi noma ingena emhlabathini, kanti ivolumu, indawo nesiza sayo kuyabalwa. Bona [Izinto ezikhululekile](topic:freeform-objects).
- **I-IFC yesikhala sangaphandle.** Okuningi › {{ui:Thumela i-IFC yesikhala sangaphandle}} ilondoloza izitshalo (njengemvelo ezinezakhiwo zazo), izakhiwo ezinzima nezinto ezikhululekile njenge-IFC 4.3 ukuze kube yimodeli ye-BIM. Bona [IFC](topic:planting#ifc).
- **Kusukela ekuqaleni kuya ekupheleni.** [Klama ubuhlanzi, kusukela ekuqaleni kuya ekupheleni](topic:landscape-walkthrough) ihamba yonke indawo yokusebenza kusayithi lesampula.

### Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi

- **Imigudu ephezulu nezingcingo ezingaphansi komhlaba esayithini lakho.** {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi}}: dweba umugqa ngezinsimbi zawo, izingcingo emifuleni, izinto zokusebenza kanye nezithiyo, endaweni efanayo nasefayeleni lephrojekthi elifanayo. Bona [Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi](topic:power-lines).
- **Imitapo yakho.** Izintambo, izingcingo, izinsimbi, izinto zokusebenza (izingalo ezinamaqoqo, izinsimbi zokunamathela, izifaki, izintambo zokuqinisa, izilawuli zamandla, amabhokisi, izixhumanisi) kanye nezinhlobo zemifulo, ngezigaba ozitaya wena, zabelwana njenge-fayela noma isipredishithi. Akukho kwezakhiwo zezwe noma somkhiqizi osakhelwe ngaphakathi; izibonelo zithi zibonelo. Bona [Umtapo wemigudu yakho kagesi](topic:power-line-library).
- **Ukuhla nokuqina ngaphansi kwezimo zakho zomthwalo.** Ukushisa, umoya, ukhuni nokhuni oluthintwe ngaphansi kohlangothi olulodwa; imikhawulo yokuqina ngesabelo sokuqina ekuphuleni; i-catenary eyiqiniso kuzo zonke izinhlanganiso, kubandakanya nezindawo ezithambekile; amathebula okufaka izintambo. Bona [Ukuhla nokuqina](topic:sag-and-tension).
- **Izinhlolo, zikhona njalo zisha.** Ukusondela phezu komhlaba nezithiyo, ukuhlukana kwezigaba, ukuzungeza kwezilawuli, ukuphakanyiswa, imithwalo yezinsika ngokulinganisa kumtapo wakho, izintambo, izifaki, izisekelo, idatha engekho, izingcingo ezinganxiwe, imithwalo emikhulu kanye nokwehla kwamandla kagesi, okubhalwa kupulani nasemugqeni wephrofayili. Isisiza sokuklama: unjiniyela uyahlola. Bona [Izinhlolo zemigudu kagesi](topic:power-line-checks).
- **Amathebula, amashidi kanye ne-GIS.** Amathebula okufaka izintambo namasupport, izinto ezibalwayo (CSV, Excel, TakeoffCraft), amashidi epulani nephrofayili kanye nombiko wesifundo ku-PDF eyodwa, izendlalelo ze-GIS ezinedatha yayo yonke into. Bona [Amathebula, izinto ezibalwayo, amashidi kanye ne-GIS yemigudu kagesi](topic:power-line-outputs).
- **Emva kokuzama kokuqala.** Iphrofayili yomugqa ilandela umugqa okhethile, inohlu lwe-**Umugqa**, ifunda umhlabathi, ikhondakta kanye nokusondela ngaphansi kwesikhombi, futhi ilinganisa phakathi kwamaphoyinti amabili. Izitshalo nezindonga zesikhala sangaphandle zibalwa njengezithiyo ngaphansi komugqa; izindlela zezingcingo kanye namapayipi zibonakala kuphrofayili yomugqa futhi zihlanganiswa nomthetho wokusondela okhetha wona; **Imithetho…** eduze kwakho konke ukusondela ivula imithetho yakho; kanye namasethi emithetho ane-library alondolozwa njengamaphakheji okwabelwana ngawo ngezwe. Bona [Izinhlolo zemigudu kagesi](topic:power-line-checks) kanye [Amaphakheji](topic:sag-and-tension#packs-rules-and-a-library-for-a-country).
- **Amafomethi wakho amafayela.** Imigudu kagesi igcinwa kwifayela leprojekthi ye-CivilCraft, imitapo ku-`.cclines.json` noma ku-CSV, amasethi emithetho namaphakheji ezifayeleni zawo ze-JSON: amafomethi avulekile umuntu yedwa angawafunda noma awaguqule. Ifayela lesifundo i-`.DON` ingeniso nokuphuma okungaphezulu kwalawo.
- **Amafayela wesifundo (.DON), angakahlolwa.** Gcina umugqa njengefayela lesifundo le-`.DON` ukuze uhlelo lwesinye lwemigudu kagesi lufunde, noma ufunde izinsimbi zalo; yakhiwe ngamagama wensimu wefomethi, kepha akakahlolwa nalolo hlelo. Bona [Amafayela wesifundo](topic:power-line-study-file).

### Ukulinganisa

- **Ithuluzi lokulinganisa.** {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Linganisa}} (noma {{key:M}}) lilinganisela kupulani: ubude nokuqondisa kohlangothi ngalunye, isamba esiqhubekayo, ubude ngaphansi komhlaba kanye nokuthambeka, nendawo nobungqinamba uma ukuvala isimo. Akukho okudwetshwa kuphrojekthi. Bona [Linganisa](topic:measure).

### Izinhlanga zezakhiwo

- **Dweba izinhlanga zezakhiwo.** Kuzo Izakhiwo, ngaphansi kwe-**Izinhlanga zokwakha**, {{ui:Dweba inhlanga}} (futhi {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Izinhlanga}}). Nikeza inhlanga amafulo kanye nobude befulo bese ufunda indawo yayo yonke yefulo elibanzi. Khetha ukuthi ihlala kanjani emhlabeni othambekile (ngezinyathelo, nge-podium noma ngokusikwa) futhi ubone ukugcwaliswa nokusikwa. Izinhlanga zibonakala kupulani, kwi-PDF nakwi-DWG nakwi-DXF ezindaweni zazo ezizimele. Bona [Dweba izinhlanga zezakhiwo kusayithi](topic:building-masses).
- **Phusha futhi uhudule ku-3D.** Kwibonakalo ye-3D, donsa uphahla lwenhlanga ekhethiwe, noma cindezela {{key:PageUp}} no-{{key:PageDown}}, ukuze uyishintshe ngefulo ngalinye, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. **Ilanga** lidala izithunzi zezinhlanga. Bona [Kwibonakalo ye-3D](topic:building-masses#in-the-3d-view).
- **Imikhawulo yokuhlela oyibhalayo.** Ukuhlehla umugqa ngomugqa kusukela emgqeni wesitaladi, umkhawulo wobude, indiza yokuchayeka kwesibhakabhaka, isilinganiso sendawo yefulo kanye nokumbozwa. Ikhava yendawo yokwakha idwetshwa kupulani, futhi inhlanga ngayinye ithola uphawu lokuthi yes noma cha ngomkhawulo ngamunye. I-CivilCraft ayinayo ikhodi yokuhlela ngaphakathi kwayo: yonke inombolo yinto oyibhalayo ekhodini yakho yomasipala. Bona [Hlola izinhlanga ngemikhawulo yokuhlela oyibhalayo](topic:zoning-limits).
- **Amahora elanga.** Khetha usuku, noma isikhathi sonyaka, nesikhathi sezone ngegama noma ngokushintshwa, bese ufunda amahora elanga eliqondile kudonga ngalunye lwenhlanga ngayinye, efulo ngefulo, nasemhlabeni ozungezile, okudwetshwe kupulani. Londoloza izindonga nomhlaba ku-CSV. Iprojekthi idinga uhlelo lokuxhumanisa. Bona [Bala amahora elanga esayithini](topic:sun-hours).
- **Izinhlanga njenge-IFC, nokuya ku-BimCraft.** {{ui:Izinhlanga njenge-IFC}} zilondoloza izinhlanga njenge-IFC4, zibekwe emaphini uma iprojekthi inohlelo lokuxhumanisa. {{ui:Thuthukisa ku-BimCraft}} ivula i-BimCraft ethebhini elisha ngezinhlanga, lapho **Thuthukisa le nhlanga** yakha izindonga kuwo wonke amazinga nophahla kusuka ekugcwaleni komhlaba. Akukho okulayishwa. Bona [Thumela kwadlule](topic:building-masses#send-it-on).

### Izinkomba

- **Ama-clothoid spirals.** Thayipha ubude ku-**Spiral engenayo** nakwi-**Spiral ephumayo** kwethebula le-PI lomjikelo: ithangenti, i-spiral, umjikelo, i-spiral, ithangenti, ngobude obulinganayo noma obahlukile. Ipulani libhala i-TS, SC, CS nq-ST, uhlu lomjikelo lunikeza θs kanye nezibanga zethangenti nezangaphandle, futhi izikhala namaoffset adlula ku-spirals. Bona [Ama-spiral](topic:alignment#spirals).
- **Ama-spiral ku-LandXML.** I-LandXML ibhala futhi ifunde ama-clothoid spiral. Izinkomba ezine-spiral, esaziwa kakade ukuthi i-CivilCraft yayiyishiya ekungeneni, manje ziyavuleka. Bona [Ukushintshanisa kwe-LandXML](topic:landxml).

### Izinkomba zomgwaqo

- **Yakha ngohlangothi ngalunye ngezingxenye.** Vula **Yakha ngohlangothi ngalunye ngezingxenye** ukuze unike ohlangothini ngalunye uhlu lwalo kusukela emgqeni ophakathi uphume: imizila, ishoulder, igutter, i-curb, umgodi wezitshalo, indlela yabahambi ngezinyawo kanye nesiphethu sohlangothi nokusika nokugcwala kwaso. Qala kwi-{{ui:Umgwaqo wasemakhaya}} noma {{ui:Isitaladi sasedolobheni}} bese ukopisha uhlangothi olulodwa kolunye. Imibono yesigaba ebhala i-flowline, i-curb nendlela, futhi umphezulu owenziwe ngegradi unayo i-curb. Bona [Yakha ngohlangothi ngalunye ngezingxenye](topic:corridor#build-each-side-from-parts).
- **Ukuphakama okwedlula ku-spirals.** Emjikelweni onama-spiral, ukuphakama okuqhubekayo kuqhuba phezu kwe-spiral: ukuncipha okulingana ku-TS, ukuphakama okugcwele ku-SC. Bona [Ukuphakama](topic:corridor#superelevation).

### Amafayela wezinto ezintathu

- **Ukuthunyelwa kwe-USD.** Kwibonakalo ye-3D, i-{{ui:USD}} ibhala isayithi njenge-`.usda`, `.usdc` noma `.usdz` ngobukhulu bangempela nangokuxhumanisa kwephrojekthi: imiphezulu, amapayipi kanye nezakhiwo njengemeshi, imigqa, unyawo ngalunye, namaphoyinti okuhlola anezinombolo zawo. Bona [Thumela isayithi le-3D njenge-USD](topic:usd).

### Abasizi be-AI

- **Okuningi kubasizi.** Uma abasizi bevuliwe ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CivilCraft}}, umsizi manje usengafunda amapaseli, ukusika nokugcwala kanye namaphrofayili, angeze amaphoyinti okuhlola, alondoloze iprojekthi, athumele i-LandXML, i-DWG kanye ne-USD, futhi asebenze ezikhwameni ozikhethayo ku-CadCraft Studio. Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala sangaphandle yengeza amathuluzi wokuthola izitshalo, ukubeka izitshalo, amabhedi nemiduli, ukulinganisa, kanye nokuhlola ukunisela. Iseva yamafayela kanye nomugqa womyalo we-`cadcraft` ivula amaprojekthi alondoloziwe ngaphandle kwewindi. Bona [Vumela umsizi we-AI asebenze nesayithi lakho](topic:assistants).

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Amayunithi.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Iyunithi}} inohlu olulodwa ngalunye lobude, indawo, ivolumu, i-engeli nokuthambeka, ngobukhulu obunembile obunikezwa izincazelo zawo (i-foot yesurvey yase-US iyiyunithi yayo). Vala amayunithi ongawasebenzisi ngeke aphume ohlwini lwamayunithi; nama-star eyodwa futhi umsebenzi omusha uqala kuwo. Ifayela ligcina iyunithi yalo njalo. Ku-CivilCraft uhlu lwe-**Iyunithi** lweprojekthi lulandela izinkinobho; i-star yobude be-metric yenza amaprojekthi amasha abe ngamamitha (uma kungenjalo kube amafiti, angamafiti esurvey yase-US), i-star yendawo noma ivolumu ibeka amayunithi endawo nevolumu okubikwa ngawo, kanti ibhokisi lobude lamukela noma iyiphi uphawu lweyunithi, njengo-`3 ch` noma `450 mm`. Bona [Amayunithi, amanani athayiphiwe kanye nohlu lwamayunithi](common:unit-lists).
- **Ukwakheka okuqinile nezinhlaka ezinyakazisekayo.** {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Ubukhulu besakhiwo}} › {{ui:Okuhlangene}} kulungisa okwengeziwe esikrinini (izikrini zokuthinta namafoni zigcina izinkinobho zazo ezinkulu). Iphaneli ngayinye eseceleni inebha yesihloko esincane: yidonsele ukuze uyixhume noma uyikhulule, uyilahle ebhareni yephaneli yomunye ukuze wabelane ngerimu eyodwa njengamathebhu, uyibophe ukuze ishelele ngasemaphethelweni, noma uyifihle bese uyibuyisa emaphethelweni. Bona [Hlela izinhlaka](common:arrange-panels#make-the-layout-denser).
- **Izingxoxo ziyanyakaza.** Donsa ingxoxo ngebha yesihloko iphume endleleni yokudweba; ibuyela lapho ushiye khona. Chofoza kabili ibha yesihloko ukuze ibuyele maphakathi. Bona [Hambisa ingxoxo](common:arrange-panels#move-a-dialog).
- **Izindlela zabasizi be-AI.** Izicelo eziyishumi ezisebenziwe kumsizi wakho (hlela umdwebo, hlela isethi, hlola imidwebo, buza imodeli imibuzo, bala ukuthatha ngokuphelele, zama ukuguqulwa kwengxenye…), ngayinye nezinsiza ezibizwayo namalungelo edingekayo, futhi ngayinye iyaphindwa ngamavivinyo ethu. Izinhlelo azinayo i-AI. Bona [Izindlela zabasizi be-AI](common:assistants-recipes).
- **Izilimi.** **Português (Portugal)** yisha ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} › {{ui:Ulimi}}. Yona, **Português (Brasil)**, **Español (México)** kanye ne-**Bahasa Indonesia** manje sekunawo wonke amagama ezinhlelo, njengamadrafti angakahlolwa yisipikha sendabuko. Izihloko zosizo zisolwimini lwesiNgisi, nezimbalwa ngesiSpanish.
- **I-CadCraft Studio** ichaza ukuthi ukuhlola isibuyekezo kwehlulekile kungani (akukho ifayela lesistimu yakho okwamanje, akukho inethiwekhi, isiginesha engalungile) kunokuthi ibhale "Kwenziwe" nje, futhi ayiphindi ikhadi elithi Okusha elakuvala uze kube khona ukukhishwa okusha.

## Akukatholakali kule nguqulo

- **0.7 ayisayinwanga ikhodi.** Ukusayina kanye nephakheji ye-Microsoft Store kudlulele ku-0.8 ngoba ukuqinisekiswa kwesitifiketi akukafiki. Hlola i-SHA-256 eduze kwefayela ngalinye ku-getcadcraft.com/download. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa umfakeli, khetha {{ui:Ulwazi olwengeziwe}}, bese {{ui:Yiqhube noma kunjalo}}. Ku-Mac, okokuqala, chofoza ngokwesokudla ku-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Vula}}. I-CadCraft Studio ayikatholakali esitolo sezinhlelo, futhi akukakhiwa i-iPad noma i-iPhone.
- Uhlu lwama-engeli nokuthambeka ku-{{ui:Iyunithi}} buyagcinwa, kepha akukho bhokisi elikufundayo okwamanje.
- Izinhloko zokunisela zabakhiqizi: kuphela izinhloko ezijwayelekile kanye nezakho ezivela kufayela. Idatha yabakhiqizi ifika kuphela ngemvume yabo, futhi ayikakanikezwa.
- I-BimCraft ne-ModelCraft ziphethe uhlu lwezitshalo, kodwa i-Beka kusuka emtapweni yazo ayikanikezi izitshalo okwamanje. Izitshalo zifika ku-BimCraft nge-IFC yesikhala sangaphandle.
- Amathuluzi esikhala sangaphandle kubasizi asebenza uma iwindi le-CivilCraft livuliwe; iseva yamafayela nomugqa womyalo awakabe nawo.
- Izinhlanga zezakhiwo: akukho ukukhanya kwendaba noma umoya kohlangothi lwefasadi, akukho isithunzi emithini noma ezinto ezingadwetshwanga njengenhlanga, akukho izimo zophahla, futhi amahora elanga abonakala kupulani, hhayi kwibonakalo ye-3D. Dweba ku-BimCraft yakha izindonga ezilula nefulo, ngaphandle kwezivulo noma uphahla.
- Imigudu kagesi: akukho izinga lezwe elakhelwe ngaphakathi (usetha imithwalo, imikhawulo nokusondela); akukho ukuhamba kwezintambo ngenxa yokushisa, izilinganiso zezingcingo ezingaphansi komhlaba, izifundo zesekhethi emfushane, ukuvikela noma ukuqhuma; akukho mugqa ku-3D noma ku-IFC. Ifayela lesifundo le-`.DON` alikahlolwa nohlelo oluyilifundayo.
- Izinkomba ezingeyona i-clothoid, i-spiral phakathi kwemijikelo emibili, kanye nezinkomba kuzici zomugqa. Izinkomba zihlolwe ngamafomula nangesikimu se-LandXML, kepha akukahlolwa nefayela le-LandXML elinezinkomba eziphuma kwenye iprogramu.
- Izinhlanganisela zezinkomba zomgwaqo ezishintshayo emgwaqeni, izifunda, namagoli wokuthambeka kohlangothi.
- Imigudu yamanzi neminyakazo yoxinzelelo (umsebenzi olandelayo wemisebenzi yomhlaba), ukugeleza okushintshayo ngesikhathi, nezixhumanisi phakathi kwamanethiwekhi amanzi nezamanzi enkuthazo.
- Amagumbi empendulo emigqeni eyedwa okulungiswa kwayo kuyinselele; izinhlobo zemigqa ezajwayelekile zemoto zoyundlu: izimoto ezinhlanu ezidlulayo zibhalwe njengezindawo ezibambayo. Izinkomba zomugqa ezabelwana phakathi kwamaprojekthi zilinde amaprojekthi wethimba.
