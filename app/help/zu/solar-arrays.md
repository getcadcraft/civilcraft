## Okuzofunda

Indlela yokubeka insimu yamatafula elanga endaweni engalingani, nokuthi izinombolo zisho ini. Vula kusuka ku-Umphezulu › Okuningi › Uhlelo lwamatafula elanga.

## Beka amatafula

1. Khetha **Lapho**: isiza noma isisekelo sephrojekthi, noma bhala amakhona endawo. Thikha noma yiziphi izisekelo noma iziza okufanele **zingenalutho** (isakhiwo, ichibi).
2. Bhala **ububanzi nobude** besayithi. Ilanga liyancika kukho; akukho okuqagelwayo. Uma iphrojekthi inohlelo lokuxhumanisa, **Beka ngohlelo lokuxhumanisa** iyazigcwalisa.
3. Setha ukuthambeka, ukubheka (okungenalutho kubheka ekhemeni), ukuthi amamojula angaki enza itafula, namandla nobukhulu bemojula. Izinombolo zemojula ziqala njengamanani ajwayelekile futhi zimakiwe kanjalo; bhala owakho.
4. Cindezela **Beka amatafula**. Iwindi linikeza inani lamatafula namamojula, ubukhulu ku-kWp, nebanga phakathi kwemigqa.

Itafula ngalinye liyikholomu endaweni, libekwe kusukela phambili futhi lihanjelwe emuva. Itafula libekwa kuphela lapho umhlaba ngaphansi kwalo usemkhawulweni wokuthambeka, ngaphakathi kwendawo nakumkhawulo wokusethwa emuva, nalapho amapayili edingekayo engaphakeme kumkhawulo owusethile. Izindawo ezehlulekayo ziyashiywa zingenalutho, newindi litshela ukuthi zingaki futhi kungani.

## Imigqa ibekwe ngokomhlaba

Emhlabathini oshelelayo imigqa imi ngokuhlukana ngomthetho ojwayelekile wokungabi nomthunzi: umthunzi wesigqa ntambama ngosuku lwasebusika olufinyelela kakhulu akufanele ufinyelele umphetho ophansi wesigqa esilandelayo. Ocansini, i-CivilCraft isebenzisa lo mthetho emhlabathini esigqa esilandelayo esimi kuwo ngempela. Lapho umhlaba ukhuphuka emuva kwetafula, imigqa ingema iseduze; lapho wehla khona, ima kude kakhudlwana. Iwindi likhombisa ibanga elisondele kakhulu, elikude kakhulu nelilinganiselwe, eduze kwebanga lasemhlabathini oshelelayo, ukuze ubone ukuthi umhlaba wenze ini.

Ipuleni letafula liphakanyiswa ePiles kuze kube yilapho sonke isampula elingaphansi kwalo lidlula emhlabathini ngekhilensi osuyisethile. Ipayili elide kakhulu libonakala emphumeleni.

## Amandla onyaka

**Amandla onyaka** abala ukukhanya okuvela epuleni elithambekile ngosuku olumelela ngalunye lwenyanga, izinga lokushisa lamaseli, ukulahleka okuthayiphiwe, amatafula ashaya omunye komunye, nezintaba ezivimbela ilanga ngokuthatha ilanga abalifihlayo. Isibhakabhaka siyisibhakabhaka esicacile, ngakho impendulo iyinani eliphezulu; isimo sezulu sangempela sinikeza ngaphansi. Kuwukulinganisa kokuqala kokwakhiwa, hhayi umbiko wesivuno.

## Gcina

**Londoloza kuphrojekthi** igcina okokufaka nezinombolo efayeleni lephrojekthi; amatafula ayabalwa kabusha emhlabathini lapho ifayela livulwa, ngakho umhlaba oshintshiwe uyawashintsha. Abonakala epulanini nasemfanekisweni we-3D (**Bonisa › Amatafula elanga**). Uhlu lwamatafula lulanyelwa njenge-CSV. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa susa noma buyisela uhlelo lwamatafula.

## Akusakhiwe okwamanje

Idatha yesimo sezulu esikhundleni sesibhakabhaka esicacile, amatafula alandela umhlaba ngakwesokudla nakwesokunxele, izilandeli zelanga, kanye nokuklama kukagesi (izintambo, intambo, ama-inverter) ku-CivilCraft. Ipaneli ye-Solar PV ye-BimCraft inezintambo nomugqa-munye wophahla.
