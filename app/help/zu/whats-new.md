Lokhu kushintshile ku-CivilCraft 0.8 (2026-11-01) ne-0.8.1 (2026-11-02), uqhathanisa no-0.7. Izingxenye ezinkulu ezintsha ngamathuluzi ahlola ukugoba kwakho nendawo yakho (imvula, ukuhamba komhlaba, izindlela, amachibi, inqubekelaphambili) kanye namawindi amayisithupha wokubala womhlaba, imigodi, izixhumi nezitimela.

## Okusha

### Hlola ukugoba kwakho

- **Yenza imvula.** {{ui:Make it rain…}} (ngaphansi kwegama elithi {{ui:Surface}}) ilethela isiphepho umhlaba ogobekile. Ubona amanzi egeleza kumapedi, egcwele ezindaweni eziphansi, futhi ephuphuma kumachibi amancane kakhulu. Ukuhamba kuyenziwa ngokunyakaza futhi kunikeza imithamo, ukugeleza okuphezulu kakhulu kanye ne-CSV yachibi ngalinye. Iseli elijwayelekile lincane ukuze ukubuka kokuqala kube msinyane; iseli elincane kakhulu liyahamba kancane. Amachibi aphuphumayo aphinde avele kuphaneli Yezinkinga.
- **Isilinganisi somhlabathi.** {{ui:Earthwork balancer…}} siphakamisa noma sehlise ipedi kuze ukukhipha kulingane nokugcwalisa, futhi sibonise ithebhula lokuthutha phakathi kwazo.
- **Indlela engcono kakhulu.** {{ui:Best route…}}: khetha isiqalo nesiphetho, bese i-CivilCraft ithola indlela ehamba kancane kakhulu phezu komhlaba ehlala ngaphakathi kwemikhawulo yakho yokwehla nokugoba, bese yenza umzila ngayo.
- **Amachibi okulawula.** {{ui:Detention ponds…}} ibala isitezi nokugcinwa kusuka emhlabeni wakho, ithatha izinto zokuphuma (orifice, weir, V-notch, spillway), idlulisa isiphepho ngechibi bese ihlola ukuthi amanzi agcinwe kangakanani, isikhathi sokukhipha nesikhala sokuphepha.
- **Inqubekelaphambili kusuka ekuhloleni.** {{ui:Progress from surveys…}} iqhathanisa imiphezulu ye-drone noma yokuskena esukela kuzinsuku ezahlukene nomhlaba njengoba ubukeka ngaleso sikhathi nokuklama: iphesenti eliqedile ngomthamo, izindawo ezimbiwe kakhulu nezigcwele kakhulu, izitokisi, nokuthi umsebenzi uzophela nini ngale ndlela (kuchazwa njengokulinganisa).

### Azi indawo

- **Ikheli kuya esizeni.** {{ui:Address to site…}} ihlanganisa idatha evulekile yepaseli, isakhiwo, ukuhlelwa kwezindawo, inhlabathi, izikhukhula nomhlaba kuseyidi enye. Ungagcina indawo kwifayela lephrojekthi: ikheli olibhalile, umthombo ngamunye ngosuku owawutholwe ngalo nelayisensi yawo, nokuthi kubuyile yini. Vula kabusha noma yabelana ngephrojekthi, indawo isalapho, akukho okuphinde kutholwe. Ungayilondoloza futhi njengefayela lendawo noma uyithumele ku-BimCraft. Bheka [Ikheli kuya esizeni](topic:address-to-site).

### Umhlaba, imigwaqo, ukuhlola, izimayini, izixhumi nezitimela

- **Izinhlabathi namathambeka.** {{ui:Soils and slopes…}} idweba iphrofayili yenhlabathi phakathi kwemigodi oyibhalayo. Indlela kaBishop bese isesha umjikelo obalulekile emhlabeni wakho, kunomugqa wamanzi womhlaba, umthwalo oengeziwe nesibalo sokunyakaza komhlaba; ukuthamba kwesisekelo kuthola ukuthamba okuhlanganisiwe, ukuthamba okuqondile nesikhathi.
- **Izixhumanisi nokumaka.** {{ui:Junctions and striping…}} ihlanganisa imilenze ngamajika amakhona (iradiyasi eyodwa ngomlenze, noma amajika amaphuzu amathathu), ibanga lokubona ngesivinini sokuklama, ukuhlola kwamanxantathu okubona, izindlela zabahambi ngezinyawo, imigqa yokuma, izimaka nezimpawu nohlu lwezimpawu. Zidwetshwe epulanini futhi zilondolozwe njenge-.dxf nemigqa egatshiwe.
- **Ukulungisa ukuhlola.** {{ui:Survey adjustment…}} isebenzisa ukulungisa kwezikwele ezincane kakhulu komgwaqo noma inethiwekhi: amakhoordinethi alungisiwe, ama-ellipse amaphutha, isivivinyo se-chi-squared kanye nophawu esitheni somlinganiso ongalingani. Ukuhamba kwenethiwekhi okungavaliwe nokuvala umgwaqo ngomthetho wekhampasi kanye ne-RW5, amafayela asimini e-GSI, JobXML noma CSV ayafakiwe, namaphoyinti alungisiwe agciniwa njenge-CSV.
- **Imigodi evulekile.** {{ui:Open pits…}} iguqula amasampula okubhoboza ibe yimodeli yebhulokhi nemgodi wokugcina ngaphakathi kwe-engeli yakho yokuthamba, ngesilinganiso sendle, ithebhula ngebhentshi kanye nejika lamabanga nemithamo. Imigodi eyingxube ngokusho kokuphindaphinda kwemali nohlelo lokukhiqiza nomnotho wemali ngokudonsela phansi nawo ukhona.
- **Izixhumi zomsakazo.** {{ui:Radio links…}} ihlola umugqa wokubona phezu komhlaba wakho nokugoba komhlaba, i-Fresnel zone yokuqala, ibhajethi yesixhumanisi, isikhala sokufiphala nokuphazamiseka kwemisindo, bese inikeza ukuphakama kwemasti okuzohlanza indlela. Iphinde yenze ibhajethi yokulahlekelwa kwefibha, izinani lendlela nomoya ophephela ethawini.
- **Amaswichi esitimela.** {{ui:Rail turnouts…}} yenza iswichi ngenombolo yayo ye-frog, izixhumi phakathi kwemigqa ehlanganayo kanye nesikali sesakhiwo esithambekiswe yi-cant futhi sandiswe ejikeni. Iswichi ingahlala esiteshini somzila.

Bheka [Izinhlabathi, izixhumanisi, ukulungisa ukuhlola, imigodi, izixhumi zomsakazo namaswichi](topic:soils-roads-survey-mines-links).

## Kuso sonke isicelo

- **Izilimi.** Izilimi ezingama-54, eziyishumi zazo zintsha. Izilimi ezingeyona isiNgisi ngimizamo yomshini kuze kufundwe yindoni yomuntu. Izihloko zosizo sisaningi kakhulu ngesiNgisi.
- **Imigqa yebha yamathuluzi.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Toolbar rows}}: Wrap (njengangaphambili), imigqa emi-2 noma umugqa ongu-1. I-Ribbon ye-CivilCraft ibonisa ithuluzi elisetshenziswa kakhulu likhulu, amalikanye amancane, nokunye kusemuva kwesimpandu segama leqembu.
- **Amadivayisi okufaka.** I-trackpad, ipeni, imbewu ye-3D, isilawuli ne-MIDI kungashayela izinhlelo.
- **Izinkinga.** Iphaneli Yezinkinga exwayisa kuzo zonke izinhlelo, ngezheke ezifunda amaphakheji wemithetho.
- **Ukuphila.** Ukubuka ukuthi ubani usiphi kuseshini ye-Live.
- **Ama-add-on.** Uhlu lwama-add-on, anama-add-on okukhanyisa.
- **Xhuma i-AI yakho.** Noma isisekeli esixhumile singashayela amathuluzi e-CivilCraft. Sifika nenothi yokuthi ukuxhuma ngenkinobho eyodwa ku-CadCraft Studio nomxhumi ogcinwe kuseva akukalungi. Bheka [Vumela umsizi we-AI asebenze nendawo yakho](topic:assistants).

## Kulungiswe ku-0.8.1

- **Isigaba se-DWG classes.** Sasifundwa njengohlobo olungalungile (GitHub cadcraft#5). Kulungiswe ku-CivilCraft kanye ne-CadCraft, BimCraft, ModelCraft kanye ne-TakeoffCraft.
- **Ukushintsha ulimi kuso sonke isikhathi sokusebenza.** I-CadCraft Studio namaphakheji ahlanganisiwe angashintsha ulimi kuso sonke isikhathi sokusebenza. Ngaphambili bekuyisiNgisi kuphela. Akukahlolwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo: sitshele uma ulimi lungashintshi.

## Akukafiki kulenguqulo

- **Lezi yizisekeli zokubala.** Yonke imiphumela yamawindi amasha idinga ukuhlolwa yinjiniyela. Amandla enhlabathi aqala ngamanani ajwayelekile yeqembu lenhlabathi, nawindi ayakusho lokho.
- **Yenza imvula.** Ukuhamba esampuleni lendawo kungathatha ngaphezu kwemizuzu emibili kusiphequluli ngeseli elincane kakhulu. Ihlolwe ngezimo ezenziwe ngesandla nendawo ye-V, kodwa ayikahlolwa endaweni yangempela eyakhiwe.
- **Ikheli kuya esizeni.** Ifunda amafayela edatha evulekile oyiletha, futhi yahlolwa kumafayela ethu kuphela. Izihlahla azisona omunye umthombo. Ukuthatha imithombo ngokwakho kulindele isimemezelo sobumfihlo.
- **Inqubekelaphambili kusuka ekuhloleni.** Ihlolwe ngokuhlola okwenziwe ngokokuzenzela kuphela. Inqubekelaphambili ezinkombeni namamakhi (MarkupCraft) nokuskena kuya kumodeli (BimCraft) akwakhiwa.
- **Izinhlabathi namathambeka.** Imijikelo yomphezulu kuphela: akukho ukusesha ngesakhiwo esiyisikwele noma esiyibhulokhi, futhi akukho umugqa wokuqhekeka kokuthwala.
- **Izixhumanisi.** Umzila ngamunye oyodwa wephalishi, akukho imizila yamalori okuthuthelwa, futhi azikangeni ku-corridor.
- **Amafayela asensimini.** Abafundi bakhiwe ngezincazelo zefomethi zomphakathi futhi bahlolwe ngamafayela esiwenzile. Kudingeka amafayela angempela ezinsimbi.
- **Izixhumi nezitimela.** Izixhumi zomsakazo: ukulahlekelwa kwesikhala nemisindo kuphela (akukho ukuncipha ngemvula noma amaphethini wehanti), futhi akukho sitendi sezitimela: yonke imikhawulo yesitimela iyisibambi, ngezinombolo zokuqala ezingahlolwanga yinjiniyela yezitimela.
- **Amawindi wezinhlelo ze-Windows nemac.** I-0.8 ayikakhishwa ngesiginelo sekhodi. I-Windows ingase ixwayise, futhi i-macOS icela ukuchofoza nge-ngakwesokudla bese i-Open.
