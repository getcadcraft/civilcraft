Uma usuke wasebenzisa i-Civil 3D, usuyazi imibono ye-CivilCraft: amaphoyinti namaqembu amaphoyinti, imiphezulu ye-TIN enemigqa yokwephula nemingcele, imizila nge-PI, amaprofayili nge-PVI, izakhiwo ezigoqelwa zibe ngamakhoridoro, ukugrada kuze kufike ku-daylight, amapayipi adonsa ngamandla adonsayo, i-LandXML. Okuhlukile yilokho okuzungezile: akukho fayela lokudweba, akukho umugqa womyalo (ngaphandle kokuthi ukhethe isitayela semenyu ethi {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}}), akukho izitayela, akukho i-Prospector, futhi akukho amawindi ezilungiselelo. I-CivilCraft ingumsebenzi omncane, ogxile esiphequluleni, futhi kule bhilidi wenza ingxenye encane kuphela yalokho iphakheji egcwele yobunjiniyela engakwenza. Lelikhasi lihambisa lokho owakwaziyo lapho okukhona.

:::tip
Uma uthanda i-ribbon ye-Civil 3D, khetha isitayela semenyu ethi {{ui:Ibha ye-Civil}} ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:CivilCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}: amathebhu Ikhaya, Imiphezulu, Shintsha, Indawo, Ukubuka nowe-Okuphumayo, inkinobho ephakathi ishelelisa, futhi {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho ephakathi ijikeleza umbono we-3D. Uma uthayipha imiyalo, {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}} yengeza umugqa womyalo (ALIGNMENT, PAD, PROFILE, ZOOM …). Bona [Imenyu, okhiye nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).
:::

## Okuhlukile okuhlupha abantu

**Akukho umugqa womyalo** ngaphandle kokuthi ukhethe isitayela semenyu ethi {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}}. Amathuluzi ayizinkinobho zethuluzi nezinhlamvu ezimbalwa: {{key:A}} umzila, {{key:G}} ipedi, {{key:P}} amapayipi, {{key:B}} umugqa wokwephula, {{key:O}} umngcele, {{key:R}} iphrofayili, {{key:I}} engeza ama-PVI, {{key:V}} khetha, {{key:F}} lingana. Ezinye izitayela zemenyu, ukuthayipha igama lomyalo akwenzi lutho. Bona [Izinkinobho nemouse](topic:keyboard).

**Enter iyaqeda; ukuchofoza kabili kuyingozi.** Chofoza iphoyinti lokugcina kanye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Kule bhilidi, ukuchofoza kabili nakho kuyaqeda, kodwa kukhipha iphoyinti owalichofoze kabili. Ukuchofoza ngakwesokudla akuqedi noma kuphinde; kushelelisa kuphela uma udonsa ngakho.

**Konke kuyashintsha njalo.** Shintsha iqembu lamaphoyinti, iradiyasi, i-PVI noma ukuthambeka kwepedi, bese umphezulu, amakhonthuwa, ikhoridoro, imigqa ye-daylight namavolumu ayabuyekezwa ngokushesha. Akukho okwakhiwa kabusha futhi akukho okuphelelwa yisikhathi.

**Akukho okuyisinto yokudweba.** Iplani kuyimbono yokwakhiwa, hhayi umdwebo onganhlelwa ngemiyalo ye-CAD. Awukwazi ukungeza imigqa noma umbhalo wakho, ukushintsha umbala wesendlalelo, noma ukuhlephula okunye. Thumela ku-DXF uqede umdwebo kuhlelo lwe-CAD.

**Akukho izitayela.** Imibala, ubukhulu bemigqa, usayizi wamalebula namagama ezingqimba akulungisiwe (bona [Izingqimba zeplani](topic:plan-layers)). I-Paper ne-Model kwi-app bar ziyashintsha phakathi kwesikrini esikhanyayo nesimnyama.

**Okukodwa kwakho konke.** Umphezulu womhlaba okhona owodwa, umphumela wokugrada owodwa, inethiwekhi yamapayipi eyodwa ngephrojekthi ngayinye. Imizila namapedi amaningi kuyalungile.

**Amayunithi ngamalebula kuphela.** Ukukhetha {{ui:Amafidi ye-US}} noma {{ui:Amamitha}} akuguquli lutho. Akukho isikali sokudweba. Uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi lungakhethwa: setha ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Amakhoordinethi}} futhi akukho okuhamba, kusho nje ukuthi izinombolo zisho ukuthini (bona [Setha uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi lweprojekthi](topic:coordinate-systems)). Ama-feet athathwa njengamafidi ezilinganiso ze-US lapho ebhalwa ku-LandXML.

**Amaqembu emigqa yokwephula ahlanganiswa ngokulandelana kwenombolo yephoyinti.** Iqembu elisethelwe ku-{{ui:Umugqa wokuphula}} libe umugqa owodwa kuwo wonke amaphoyinti awo, ngokulandelana kwenombolo. Amakhodi afanayo (imigqa yokugeleza emibili ekhodwe ngokuthi "FL") yenza umugqa owodwa ozigxizayo; yidwebe ngesandla.

**Izisiteshi zibukeka zifanayo, kodwa amamitha ayehluka.** Ama-feet asebenzisa izisiteshi ze-100 ft (12+34.56); amamitha asebenzisa izisiteshi ze-1000 m (1+234.567).

**Ukususa ku-Mac.** Inkinobho ebhalwe delete ku-Mac iyi-{{key:Backspace}} kusiphequluli. Sebenzisa Fn ne-delete, noma inkinobho ethi {{ui:Susa}}, ukuze ususe okukhethiwe.

**Amafayela ayalandwa.** {{ui:Londoloza}} ilanda ifayela le-`.civilcraft.json` njalo; akukho "londoloza phezu kwefayela elivuliwe". Ikhophi yokubuyisela komsebenzi ongakalondolozwa igcinwa kuleli siphequluli ([Buyisela umsebenzi ongakalondolozwa](common:recover)). Ukuvula ifayela lamaphoyinti kuyashintsha amaphoyinti ngaphandle kokubuza. Bona [Amafayela ongawavula, ongawalondoloza nongawathumela](topic:files).

**Imithetho ingeyakho.** Imithetho yenethiwekhi yamapayipi nokuhlanganiswa kwekhoridoro kuqala ngamanani abambe indawo angewona umgomo. Setha okwakho ngaphambi kokuthembela noma yikuphi ukuhlola.

**Amavolumu asekelwe egridini.** Ukumba nokugcwalisa kuvela egridini elinde phakathi kwemiphezulu emibili, ngaphakathi kwemigqa ye-daylight, ngaphandle kokuvuvukala noma ukuncipha. Aseduze, kodwa awafani, namavolumu we-TIN-kuya-ku-TIN.

## Okungakakwakhiwa

Izipirali ezingesona ezeclothoid, izinhlelo ezishintsha emgwaqweni, amapedi athambekile, ukukhuthazwa nokugcinwa kwe-inlet, amakhathalogi ezingxenye, amapayipi kwiprofayili, izakhiwo zamapharseli nokuhlelwa kwezindawo, ukulinganisa kwe-least-squares nezinhlelo zedatha zokuhlola, ukuvula i-DWG, nokunye. Uhlu oluphelele, nokuthi yenzeni kwasekuqaleni namuhla, lutholakala ku-[Akukakwakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).
