{{ui:Amazinga}} › {{ui:Umugqa wesigaba}}: chofoza indawo yokuqala nendawo yokugcina. Izigaba zibizwa ngokuthi A, B, C… (zithiye igama kuProperties). Isigaba sidweba, eduze komugqa waso:

- umhlaba okhona (odashiwe) nomhlaba owakhiwe;
- izindonga nezisekelo zazo, lapho umugqa udlula kuzo;
- ukufaka ipavumenti nezakhiwo zendlela, kanye nodaka lwezindawo ezitshaliwe nodaka lwangaphezulu (ngemva kokusika), olusongela ngaphansi komphezulu owuqedile;
- izinyathelo ngokuphakama kwazo;
- izitshalo ezingaphakathi kwebanga elibekwe emugqeni, njengezithombe ezimnyama ngobukhulu obugcwele.

Izici zibeka leli banga nokukhulisa okuqondile. Engxoxweni yamashidi, **Izigaba** zizibeka emashidini azo, kuze kube ngu-amane ngeshidi, ngalinye ngesikali esijwayelekile esilingana.

## Isethi yokushicilela

{{ui:Isethi yokushicilela (.zip)}} engxoxweni yamashidi yenza, ku-.zip owodwa:

- i-PDF eyodwa evula **uhlu lwamashidi** bese kulandela wonke amashidi esikhala sangaphandle, enombolo L-1, L-2… (ukutshala, ukulinganisa umhlaba, ukunisela, imininingwane, izigaba, amashidi okusebenza);
- i-DWG ngeshidi ngalinye lepulani enezingqimba zaleso sheet (amagama `L-*` ayagcinwa), kanye ne-DWG ngomdwebo ngamunye wesigaba.

Umugqa ngamunye wesigaba epulanini utshela ukuthi usemashidini aphi (`A / L-5`); inombolo ibalwa kusuka esethini njalo, ngakho ukungeza, ukususa noma ukuqamba kabusha izigaba kuyizibalela kabusha.
