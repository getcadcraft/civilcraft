Faka indawo yokupaka endaweni yepaseli, ipad noma umugqa ovaliwe wesici, bese uthola izibalo, ukulinganisa nokugcoba ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

## Yihlele

1. Khetha ipaseli, i-pad noma umugqa ovaliwe wesici epulanini.
2. Vula **Surface, More, Parking lot...** (**Indawo, Okwengeziwe, Indawo yokupaka...**).
3. Khetha imithetho, i-engeli yezikhala (90, 75, 60, 45, 30 wamadigri noma parallel) nezindlela ezinhlangothi eyodwa noma ezimbili. Usayizi wezikhala, wezindlela neziqhingi uqala kwithebula futhi ngayinye ingabhalwa phezu kwawo.
4. Chofoza **Hlela**.

Imigqa ihamba ecaleni komphetho wesakhiwo onikeza izikhala eziningi kakhulu (noma ku-engeli oyithayipha). Isikhala sibalwa kuphela uma sisendaweni yonke ngaphakathi komgqa. Lapho kusele indawo yomugqa owodwa nendlela, kuyengezwa ibhendi eyodwa. Iwindi libhala izikhala, izikhala zabakhubazekile nezimoto ezinkulu, iziqhingi, izimbobo nendawo ngesikhala ngasinye, futhi ipulani libonisa izikhala, izikhala zabakhubazekile ngezindlela zokufinyelela ezibhalwe ngemigqa, iziqhingi, imigqa emaphakathi yezindlela nezimbobo ezigabeni zazo (C-PARK-...), ukuze zifike ku-PDF, DXF ne-DWG.

## Izikhala zabakhubazekile

Ngemithetho yase-United States inani lilandela ithebula 208.2 le-2010 ADA Standards lezikhala ezinikeziwe (eyodwa kwezingu-6, ezungezwa phezulu, ibe yisikhala se-ivan), zibekwe eduze kwekhona lokuqala lomgqa wangaphandle (indawo yokungena), ngayinye nendlela yokufinyelela ephelele; ezimbili zabelana ngenye. Isikhala esincane kunobubanzi bokufinyelela, noma ukwehla okudlula u-1:48 ezikhaleni zabakhubazekile, kuboniswa njengenkinga. **Lezi zinombolo zathayiwa kusuka kumgomo njengoba zikhunjuzwa futhi azihlolwanga ngokushicilelwe kumgomo.** Usayizi ojwayelekile wesikhala nendlela uvela ezinhlanganweni zokuklama izindawo zokupaka nawo awuhlolwanga. Hlola bobabili, nomthetho wakho wasendaweni, ngaphambi kokuthembela kuwo. Khetha **Custom** ukuze ufake inani lakho lezikhala zabakhubazekile.

## Ukwehla kumbobo, ukusika nokugcwalisa

Ngaphandle kokuthi uyivale, indawo iba yindawo yokulinganisa ku-Landscape workspace. Izimbobo zibekwa emigqeni emaphakathi yezindlela cishe njalo ngamamitha angu-100 ft (30 m); indawo iphakama kusuka kwisimbobo ngasinye ngokwehla okunikeza (1.5 amaphesenti ukuqala), ngakho wonke amaphoyinti ehla esimbobeni sawo esiseduze. Izinga likhethwa ukuze ukusika nokugcwalisa kwendawo kulingane emhlabeni okhona. Umhlaba okhona yindawo ekhona yeprojekthi, engavela kumaphoyinti okuhlola, ku-LandXML, ku-lidar noma kufu lamaphoyinti lokuskena elishintshwe laba yindawo, noma kumakhonturu. Iwindi libonisa ukulinganisa kokusika nokugcwalisa; amavolumu eprojekthi anikeza inani eliqondile emva kwalokho. Indawo yokulinganisa igcina umphetho wayo emhlabeni okhona, ngakho lapho indawo entsha ihlukile emhlabeni emphethweni kuvela isinyathelo, newindi lisho ukuthi likhulu kangakanani.

## Ukugcoba nokuhlanyeza

Umgqa wonke uthola ukugcoba ngamatshe (ipatheni yomugqa ongashintshwa ngaphansi kwe-Landscape, Hardscape), futhi isiqhingi ngasinye sithola ibhedi yesihlahla esincane futhi, uma sibanzi ngokwanele, nesihlahla, esithathwa ohlwini lwezitshalo. Konke lapho kuyinto evamile yomsebenzi we-Landscape: yihlele, noma khetha **Hlela futhi** ukuze uthole okwenziwe yindawo ngasikhathi sinye. Ukususa indawo kususa lokho eyakwenza.

## Kumsizi

Indawo efanayo ingacelwa nge-MCP: `parking_layout` ibonisa ukuthi izoba njani, `parking_build` ibhala, `parking_remove` isusa indawo. Cela ngegama lepaseli noma uthumele umgqa wangaphandle.

## Okungakwenziwe

Akuhlolwa ukuthi izindlela zifinyelela endleleni yini. Ukugcoba kumboza yonke indawo yangaphandle, ngakho indawo yakhe ifaka neziqhingi. Izinketho zokugcoba ezivela ku-Umtapo nohlobo lwezikhala oluvela kuMtapo azenziwe; ipatheni yokugcoba iyiHardscape. Imithetho yamanye amazwe ayikakwakhiwa.
