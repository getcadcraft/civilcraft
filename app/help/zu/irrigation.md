## Hlela uhlelo

1. Ngaphansi kwesigaba se**Ukunisela** ohlangothini lwesokunxele, khetha i-**Head**, i-**Arc** nokuthi i-arc iqala kuphi, nosayizi wamapayipi amancane nomgogodla.
2. {{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Point of connection}}: chofoza lapho amanzi okuhlinzeka ehlanganyela khona. Setha its **ingcindezi**, **ukugeleza okutholakalayo** kanye nokulahleka kwemitha nokubuyela emuva ku-Properties.
3. {{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Valve}}: chofoza lapho ivalvu ngayinye yesifunda ifika khona. Ivalvu iqala isifunda.
4. {{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Head}}: chofoza ukubeka amakhanda; ukuchofoza ngakunye kubeka elilodwa.
5. {{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Lateral pipe}}: chofoza kusuka kuvalvu udlule emakhanda awo bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. {{ui:Mainline}}: kusuka endaweni yokuxhuma kuya kuvalvu ngalinye.

Izinhlangothi zamapayipi zibamba ngokuzenzakalela emakhanEni, emavalvini, emthonjeni nasezinhlangothini zamanye amapayipi, ngakho uhlelo luyahlangana. Isifunda yiyo yonke engafinyeleleka kusuka kuvalvu wayo ngamapayipi amancane; umgogodla uthwala isifunda esisodwa ngesikhathi.

## Amathonsi

{{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Drip line}}: chofoza ngasemgqeni, uqale lapho uthola khona (esiphelweni sepayipi elincane noma ivalvu). Setha **ukugeleza kwe-emitter** kanye **nebanga** ku-Properties; ukugeleza kwawo kuwukugeleza kwawo wonke ama-emitter aphezu kwawo.

## Ukuhlola

{{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Check the system…}} ludweba zonke izifunda:

| Ikholomu | Ibalwa kanjani |
|---|---|
| Ukugeleza | Ukugeleza kwamakhanda (nemigqa yamathonsi) kuhlanganisiwe |
| Esivalvini | Ingcindezi yokusebenza yekhanda elibi kakhulu kanye nokulahlekelwa kwamapayipi kuwo (Hazen–Williams, ububanzi bangaphakathi bepayipi, ukushintsha kokuphakama) kanye nokulahlekelwa ngevalvu |
| Ekuhlinzekeni | Okufanayo kanye nokulahlekelwa komgogodla othwala lesi sifunda, nokulahlekelwa kwemitha nokubuyela emuva |
| Imvula | Ukugeleza kwamakhanda phezu kwendawo ayinisela ngobude bokuhlukana kwekhanda nekhanda |
| Amaminithi ngeviki | Ukusebenzisa isilungiselelo **Namanzi ngeviki** |
| Izinkinga | Ijubane eliphezulu kunomkhawulo, ikhanda elidlula uhla lwengcindezi yalo, izimvubelo namakhanda ezinzulu esivalvini esisodwa, ingcindezi noma ukugeleza okungenele ekuhlinzekeni, amakhanda angafinyeleli esifundeni, izigoqo |

Khetha ivalvu ukuze ubone izibalo zesifunda salo nekhanda elibi kakhulu elijikelezwe epulanini. Uhlu lwezinto lubala amakhanda ngohlobo nangearc, amapayipi ngosayizi, amavalvu, umugqa wamathonsi kanye nama-emitter, kanye nezitheshi zomlawuli. **Londoloza njenge-CSV** igcina kokubili.

Izilungiselelo esigabeni se**Ukunisela**: **Hazen–Williams C** (150 wamapayipi amasha epulasitiki), **ukugeleza okusheshe kakhulu** kupayipi (1.5 m/s), **ukulahlekelwa ngevalvu**, kanye **namanzi ngeviki**. Ziboniswa njengezilungiselelo ezijwayelekile ezimakiwe.

## Ukumbozwa

{{ui:Irrigation}} › {{ui:Show coverage}} itshela ukuthi amakhanda ashiya kangakanani indawo eyomile, ukuthi amakhanda amabili noma ngaphezulu anisela kangakanani, nokuthi yiziphi amakhanda angafinyeleli omakhelwane (ibanga lekhanda nekhanda).

## Amakhanda

Amakhanda ahlolwe **ngokujwayelekile** izinhlobo ezakhiwe yi-CadCraft ngamazinga emvula okwakha (izifafazi ezingashintshi, izinozzle ezijikelezayo, ama-rotor egiya, ama-bubbler, izifafazi ezincane, ama-emitter), hhayi imikhiqizo yanoma yimuphi umenzi. **Engeza amakhanda kusuka kufayela…** ifunda amakhanda akho (uhlu lwe-JSON olunomkhathizo wekhanda ngalinye, ukugeleza noma izinga lemvula, nohla lwengcindezi); agcinwa neprojekthi. Idatha eshicilelwe yomenzi ingangeniswa kuphela ngemvume eqoshiwe yalowo mkhiqizi.

Ngaphansi kwamakhanda ajwayelekile kukhona uhlu **amakhanda ajwayelekile** (amagama awo aphela nge-"typical values"): usayizi wamanozzle wezifafazi ezingashintshi, ama-rotor ajikelezayo namagiya, izifafazi ezincane, ama-bubbler namaemitter, anomsakazo, ukugeleza nohla lwengcindezi. Akhiwe yi-CadCraft ngezibalo ezishicilelwe (i-orifice equation yokugeleza, ukukalwa kokusakazeka komsindo kwe-jet ngomsakazo) kanye namazinga okugeleza ajwayelekile, kusuka emithonjeni evulekile kuphela; ayikho itafula lomkhiqizi elikopishiwe. Khetha elilodwa bese isikhethi sikhombisa isisekelo nelayisense yalo. Iphrojekthi esebenzisa elilodwa igcina ikhanda nomthombo walo kufayela leprojekthi. Zilungele ukuhlela kuphela: hlola idatha yekhanda langempela yomenzi ngaphambi kokwakha.
