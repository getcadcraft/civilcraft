Vula i-{{ui:Settings}} nge-gear esinqeni se-app noma nge-{{key:Mod+,}}. Ithebhu yokuqala, {{ui:CivilCraft}}, ibamba izilungiselelo ezingeza le app; i-{{ui:General}} ibamba ezabelwana ngazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft (itimu, ibha yamathuluzi, amapaneli). Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft zikhona kuphela kule app: uzishintsha lapha, futhi lo mbhali-ncwadi uyazikhumbula. I-{{ui:Setha kabusha izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft}} ibuyisela zonke izilungiselelo kokuqala kwazo.

## Imigqa yamaphoyinti

**P, N, E, Z, D** (okokuqala) noma **P, E, N, Z, D**: ipoyinti, i-northing, i-easting, ukuphakama, incazelo, noma i-easting ngaphambi kwe-northing. Isetshenziswa ezindaweni ezintathu:

- **Kopisha**: imigqa i-CivilCraft ebeka kwi-clipboard yesistimu ye-spreadsheet.
- **Namathisela**: imigqa enamathiselwe ku-spreadsheet noma kumbhalo ongenaso umugqa wesihloko.
- **Ngenisa**: ukubheka kwe-**Ngenisa amaphoyinti** lapho ifayela linganawo umugqa wesihloko (ifayela elinamakholomu amane lifundwa njengePENZ, amathathu njengENZ). Bona [Amafomethi amafayela amaphoyinti](topic:point-file-formats).

Umugqa wesihloko othi amakholomu agama ngawo uyaphumelela njalo, njalo ukukhetha kwohlu lwe-**Amakholomu** kwingxoxo yokungenisa.

## Izinombolo zamaphoyinti akapasteka

- **Gcina izinombolo ezikhululekile** (okokuqala): iphoyinti elikapasteka ligcina inombolo yalo uma kungekho phoyinti elinayo; kuphela amaphoyinti ayaphikisana athola izinombolo ezintsha.
- **Izinombolo ezintsha njalo**: ilelo phoyinti elikapasteka lithola inombolo entsha, nakuba lingena kwalo ikhululekile.

**Izinombolo ezintsha ziqala ku-**: yishiye ingenalutho ukuze ziqhubeke kusukela kwenye ngaphezulu kwenombolo ephezulu esetshenzisiwe. Bhala inombolo, isibonelo 5000, ukuze amaphoyinti amasha athole izinombolo kusukela lapho; izinombolo esezisetshenzisiwe ziyaphuthelwa. Amaphoyinti amabili akhe ahlala ekabelwa inombolo efanayo kunoma iyiphi indlela. Bona [Khetha, kopisha futhi namathisele amaphoyinti okuhlola](topic:copy-paste-points#point-numbers-never-clash).

## Amalebula amaphoyinti

Okubonwa yilebula yephoyinti uma i-{{ui:Point labels}} ivuliwe ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa**: **Inombolo**, **Inombolo nokuphakama**, noma **Inombolo, ukuphakama nencazelo** (okokuqala). Ama-export e-DXF, DWG no-PDF abonisa amalebula afanayo nakusikrini.

## Isikhombi sekhona

Okubekwa ekhoneni le-plan nakumbono we-3D: **Ikhyubu yokubuka** (okokuqala), **Isithonjana sendikimba** noma **Akukho**.

- **Ikhyubu yokubuka** (phezulu kwesokudla). Ku-plan ibonisa phezulu, nendandatho yekhampasi: N kuyinyoka yegridi (phezulu kwe-plan). Chofoza noma iyiphi enye imiphetho, izinhlangothi noma amagumbi ekhyubu, noma uyidonse, futhi i-[umbono we-3D](topic:site-3d) uyavulwa ubheka kulowo mkhakha. I-{{ui:Home}} ku-plan cube ilinganisa i-plan.
- Ku-3D ikhyubu iyajikeleza nombono: chofoza uhlangothi, umphetho noma igumbi ukubheka kulowo mkhakha, donsa ikhyubu ukuze uzungeze, donsa indandatho ye-kompasi ukuze ujikeze ngokuya ngokuthi kuphezulu, chofoza uhlamvu ukubheka kulolo hlangothi, futhi usebenzise i-{{ui:Home}} ukuze uthole umbono we-iso wonke isayithi. Lapho ubheka ngqo uhlangothi, imicibisholo emincane ihamba iye kolandelayo nemicibisholo egoqekile ijikeza umbono ngekota.
- **Isithonjana sendikimba** (phansi kwesokunxele): uphawu olumfushane lwe-X (mpumalanga) ne-Y (ukuya enyakatho) ku-plan, u-X, u-Y no-Z (phezulu) ku-3D.

## Ukukhuliswa okuthikameziwe kwe-3D

Ukuthi umbono we-3D unwebe ukuphakama kangakanani: **1×**, **2×** (okokuqala), **3×**, **5×** noma **10×**. Uhlu olusebhakeni lombono we-3D ngokufanayo nalesi setting, ngakho ukushintsha kokubili kushintsha lokho. Amavolumu, amaphrofayili nama-export e-3D ahlala ngesilinganiso sangempela.

## Ubude nobubanzi

Indlela isibonisi se-status bar ngayo ukuphakama kwephoyinti nobubanzi lapho iprojekthi inohlelo lokuqondanisa: **amadigri, amaminithi namasekhondi** (okokuqala, isibonelo `30°18′00.00″ N`) noma **amadigri ayinkomba** (`30.300000° N`). Bona [Setha uhlelo lokuqondanisa lweprojekthi](topic:coordinate-systems#latitude-and-longitude-in-the-status-bar).

## Amamephu azivela ku-inthanethi

**Akuvuliwe** (okokuqala) noma **Kuvuliwe**. Lapho kuvuliwe, i-CivilCraft icela amaseva amamephu ongawengeza izithombe zendawo ebonakala esikrinini. Idrowing yakho ihlala kulele kulkhompyutha; iseva ibona indawo nekheli lakho le-inthanethi. Lapho kuvaliwe, akukho seva yamamephu ecelwa lutho, ngisho nalapho iprojekthi oyivulayo iqamba eyodwa; isithombe esinokuhlanganiswa kwesithombe kusuka kufayela sisebenza noma kanjani. Bona [Bonisa izendlalelo zamamephu ngaphansi kwe-plan](topic:map-layers).

## Abasizi

**Vumela abasizi be-AI nezikripthi ukusebenzisa i-CivilCraft** (kucishe ku-off ekuqaleni) ivumela umsizi ongomunye wakho ukufinyelela isayithi evulekile nge-CadCraft Studio: **Funda** ichaza isayithi futhi inikeze amaphoyinti, amaphezulu okuphakama, izinkomba, amaphrofayili, amavolumu nezinqumo; **Hlela** ivumela ukuthi akhethe futhi angeze amaphoyinti okuhlola; **Londoloza** no **Export** ivumela ukuthi alondoloze iprojekthi noma abhale i-LandXML noma i-DWG. **Amafolda angasebenzisa** (ku-Studio) yindawo lapho angalondoloza khona futhi aphume khona. **Umsebenzi** ulandisa izingcingo zakhe. Bona [Vumela umsizi we-AI asebenze nesayithi lakho](topic:assistants).
