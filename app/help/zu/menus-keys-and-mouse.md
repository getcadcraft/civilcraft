Imiyalo ye-CivilCraft ifana noma ufinyelela kanjani. **Isitayela somenyu** sinquma kuphela lapho zihlala khona esikrinini, amagama azo lapho, nokuthi yiziphi izinkinobho ezizisebenzisayo, ukuze ugcine izinkambiso zakho. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:CivilCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}; ushintsho luyashesha, futhi leli browser liyakukhumbula.

:::

## Yenze eyakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Okhiye nemenyu}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi losesho liyasiza). {{ui:Engeza ukhiye}} bese ucindezela ukhiye, noma okhiye ababili ngokulandelana ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Uma kukhona ibha, {{ui:Lapho ihlala khona}} ihambisa umyalo kwelinye ithebhu nendawo, kumenyu ye-{{ui:Ifayela}}, noma ku-{{ui:Okhiye kuphela}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Imouse}}, setha ivili, izinkinobho ezishelelisa icwecwe, nokuthi inkinobho ngayinye yenzani ku-3D.
5. Yinike igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela sakwangaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho (njenge-"Ibha yami ye-Civil"); eyakwangaphakathi ayishintshi ngokuphelele. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili, noma owandulela ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili ukuze uthayipheke, ubonakala ngombala obomvu futhi kufanele ulungiswe ngaphambi kokulondoloza.

## Imouse

- **Ivili lemouse**: {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphakathi}} (okokuqala) noma {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphandle}}, nokuthi kusheshe kangakanani, esicwini nasezingeni le-3D. Ukuhlwithwa kwe-trackpad kusondeza njalo ngendlela eminwe yakho ehamba ngayo.
- **Shelelisa icwecwe nge-**: inkinobho ephakathi, inkinobho yesokunene, noma zombili. {{key:Space}} nokudonsa nakho kuyashelelisa. Inkinobho yesokunxele ikhetha futhi idwebe njalo.
- **Donsa ku-3D**: {{ui:Okwesibuko ngasinye}} kugcina izinkinobho ze-CivilCraft (esokunxele iyazungeza, esokunene iyashelelisa). Noma setha inkinobho ngayinye ukuthi izungeze, ishelelise, isondeze noma ingenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho iguqula ukuzungeza nokushelelisa. Ukuchofoza ngaphandle kokudonsa kusakhetha.

## Thatha nawe

{{ui:Thumela}} igcina isitayela esisebenzayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa…}} lengeza elinye, kwelinye ikhompyutha noma kumsebenzisi. Ifayela elingeniswe lingakhomba kuphela imiyalo ye-CivilCraft; okunye kukho kushiywe futhi kubalwe kumugqa wesimo. {{ui:Susa lesi sitayela}} isusa eyakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi browser. {{ui:Sebenzisa leli sitayela kuzo zonke izinhlelo}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi esitayela yenza zonke izinhlelo zivule ngesitayela esihambisanayo (ithuluzi lazo, ibha noma Ukudweba kwakudala), nezinguquko ozenzile kwinhlelo ngayinye. I-CivilCraft ayinasitayela sebha yemenyu: uma ibha yemenyu yenye insiza ikhethelwa zonke izinhlelo, i-CivilCraft igcina ithuluzi layo.
:::
