Isigaba se-**Izitshalo** ekhoneni elingakwesobunxele kubhalwe zonke izitshalo ongazibeka. Chofoza enye ukubona usayizi wayo futhi uyenze isitshalo esisetshenziswa yi-**Isitshalo**, i-**Ibhedi** ne-**Umduli wezihlahla**.

## Sesha nohlunge

Thayipha ku-**Sesha**: noma yiliphi igama legama lesitshalo kunoma yiluphi ulimi (isiNgisi, isiSpanish, isiPortuguese), igama lalo lesayensi, uhlobo lwalo noma igama ezincwadini zalo. Yonke imigqa oyithayiphayo kumele ihambisane, ngakho i-`quercus evergreen` ithola ama-oki aluhlaza njalo. Izinamathiselo azibalulekile.

Nciphisa uhlu nge-**Uhlobo**, **Ukukhanya**, **Amanzi** kanye ne-**Izone yokuqina** (izone ye-USDA esayithi yakho ikuyo: isitshalo sifakwa uma izone isendaweni yaso).

## Okuthwelwe yisitshalo

| Inkambu | Incazelo |
|---|---|
| Uhlobo | Isihlahla, isihlahlana, umduli, utshani (kuhlanganise nezithanga zotshani), isitshalo esihlala iminyaka, isitshalo esimbozayo noma isitshalo esikhwela |
| Ekuvuthweni | Ubude nesisabalalo sesitshalo esikhulile esitshalwe ensimini noma emgwaqeni, ngamamitha |
| Ekutshalweni | Usayizi esitshalwa ngawo, nesitsha sakhe |
| Ukuhlukana | Isikhungo nesikhungo ebhedini noma ngomduli |
| Izone yokuqina | Izone ze-USDA esivame ukuqina kuzo |
| Ukukhanya, amanzi, inhlabathi | Okudingayo |
| Amanothi | Ukusetshenziswa nezexwayiso, njengezingxenye ezinobuthi noma isitshalo esisabalalayo |

:::note
Uhlu lwangaphakathi lwabhalelwa i-CadCraft ngolwazi lwezolimo jikelele: usayizi ovamile, ujikelezwe. Akuyona ikhathalogi yanoma yisiphi isitshalishi. Hlola usayizi nokuqina nesitshalishi sendawo. Izitshalo ezaziwa kakhulu njengezinamandla okusabalala ze-Iberia nezingxenye eziningi zomhlaba oshisayo zishiywe ngamabomu, futhi ezimbalwa ezisabalalayo kwezinye izindawo zinanothi.
:::

## Izitshalo zakho

- I-**Engeza kuhlu lwalo projekthi** ikopisha isitshalo kuhlu lwalo projekthi, lapho sigcinwa khona neprojekthi. Isitshalo kuhlu lwakho sithatha indawo yesakhiwe ngaphakathi esinegama elifanayo, ngakho ungashintsha usayizi waso ngesifunda sakho.
- I-**Ngenisa uhlu lwezitshalo…** ifunda i-CSV (noma ifayela lezitshalo). I-CSV idinga ikholomu enegama lesayensi futhi, uma kungenzeka, uhlobo, ubude nesisabalalo; amagama amakholomu ngezwi lakho ayamukeleka (Latin, Igama elivamile, Ubude, Ukusabalala, Ububanzi, Izone). Kufundwa okuhlukaniswa ngamaphuzu-nkama, izikhala noma amathebhu, kanye nezinkama zamadesimali. Usayizi wokutshala, ukuhlukana nezitsha ezingenalutho zithola izilinganiso ezifanele kuhlobo.
- I-**Thumela njenge-CSV** igcina uhlu lwakho (noma, uma lungekho, izitshalo ezisekhonjiswe ukusesha) ukuze uvule kuspredshithi.

Ifayela lohlu lwezitshalo lifundwa njengalo nanoma yiliphi elivela ngaphandle: konke engekeikusebenzise kuyasuswa futhi kubalwe.

## Izitshalo kwezinye izinhlelo

Uhlu lwezitshalo ngumtapo oyodwa wazo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft: i-BimCraft ne-ModelCraft zinohlu olufanayo, kodwa i-Bekela okuvela emtapweni yazo ayikanikezi izitshalo okwamanje. Njengamanje izitshalo zifika emodelini ye-BIM ngeIFC yesikhala sangaphandle se-CivilCraft (isitshalo ngasinye singu-`IfcGeographicElement`). Bheka [Ukutshala](topic:planting#ifc).
