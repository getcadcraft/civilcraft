Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi ibeka imigudu yephezulu namakhebula angaphansi komhlaba esayithini le-CivilCraft osinaso: amaphoyinti ofanayo esayithi, umhlaba ofanayo, ifayela lephrojekthi elifanayo.

:::warning
Isisetshenziswa sokuklama. Indawo yokusebenza ibala ukuhla, ukuqina, amagebe nemithwalo ngokwezimo **zakho** zomthwalo nemikhawulo yakho; akukho mgomo wezwe odidiwe ngaphakathi. Unjiniyela uhlola yonke imiphumela ngomgomo osebenzayo ngaphambi kokuthi kwakhiwe lutho.
:::

## Vula

Khetha {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza yemigudu kagesi}}, noma **Power lines** ngaphansi kwe-Workspace. Ithulusi bese libonisa iqembu le-**Power lines**; amaqembu e-Design ne-GIS ayasuka ecaleni. Khetha **Civil** ngaphansi kwe-Workspace ukubuyela. I-CivilCraft ikhumbula indawo yokusebenza oyisebenzise ekugcineni kulesi sishayeli.

Iphaneli eseceleni kwesobunxele ithola izigaba ezimbili phezulu:

- **Power lines**: izimo zakho zomthwalo nemikhawulo, imigudu nezinkebhe zephrojekthi, kanye ne-**Okuhlolwa**, kubuyekezwa njalo. Chofoza ukuhlola ukuze ukhethe okuphathelene nakho.
- **Umtapo**: izinto zakho zocingo, amakhebula, amasekelo, impahla nezinhlobo zomsele. Bona [Umtapo wakho wemigudu kagesi](topic:power-line-library).
