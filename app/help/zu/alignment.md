Umzila ku-CivilCraft uyiketanga yamathangenti edlula emaphoyinteni wokuhlangana (PI), nevoluthi lesiyingi kunoma yiliphi PI elingaphakathi elineradiyasi. Uphawulwa isiteshi ukusuka esiteshini sokuqala osikhethayo. Iphrojekthi ingabamba imizila eminingi.

## Dweba umzila

1. Cindezela {{key:A}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Umzila}} ku-qembu **Idizayini**.
   Isiphakamiso sithi "Umzila: chofoza ama-PI ngokulandelana. Enter noma ukuchofoza kabili kuqeda, Backspace isusa okokugcina, Esc ikhansela."
2. Chofoza i-PI yokuqala (ukuqala komzila).
3. Chofoza yonke i-PI elandelayo ngokulandelana. Umugqa obomvu ulandela ukhesha futhi ubonise ubude nokuqondisa kwethangenti oliyidwayo, isibonelo "212.40 ft  N 63°12'05" E".
4. Chofoza i-PI yokugcina (sekupheleni komzila).
5. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
   Umzila udwetshiwe unezinombolo zesiteshi futhi ukhethiwe. Ibha yesimo ithi, isibonelo, "Umzila 1: 386.36 ft, amavoluthi aneradiyasi ye-100 ft lapho ehlangana khona. Hlela imisindo ku-Izakhiwo; Iphrofayili (R) ukuklama igradi yawo."

Ngenkathi udweba: {{key:Backspace}} isusa i-PI yokugcina, {{key:Esc}} ikhansela, futhi ukudonsa endaweni engenalutho kushelelisa ngaphandle kokungeza i-PI.

:::warning
Qeda ngo-{{key:Enter}}. Ukuchofoza kabili nakho kuyaqeda, kodwa kule bhilidi kukhipha i-PI owayichofoze kabili. Chofoza i-PI yokugcina kanye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
:::

Yonke i-PI ephakathi ithola ivoluthi elingu-100 ft (30 m kuphrojekthi yamamitha), noma iradiyasi enkulu ephelele esingafaka ohhafini wethangenti ngalinye uma i-100 ft ingafaki. Ama-PI abukhali kakhulu noma adlula eqondile awatholi voluthi.

## Setha iradiyasi namakhoordinethi

Uma umzila ukhethiwe, uphaneli wangakwesokudla ubonisa **Umzila**:

1. **Igama** nethe **Isiteshi sokuqala**. Bhala isiteshi njengo `10+00` (ama-feet) noma `0+500` (amamitha), noma njengenombolo evamile. Bona [Izinyunithi, iziteshi nokubhalwa kwezinombolo](topic:units-and-stations).
2. **Ubude** nethe **Isiteshi sokugcina** kubalwa wena.
3. Ithebula le-**PIs** libonisa i-**Easting** ne-**Northing** yePI ngayinye, ne-**Iradiyasi** yePI ngayinye ephakathi. Bhala inani elisha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
   - Iradiyasi engu-0 yenza indawo yokuguquka engenayo ivoluthi.
   - I-PI yokuqala nelokugcina azisoze zibe neradiyasi.
   - Uphawu olumincane x ekugcineni komugqa luyasusa leyo PI (umzila ugcina okungenani amabili).
4. Funda uhlu **Amavoluthi**: kuwo wonke amavoluthi, izisiteshi zawo ze-PC ne-PT, iradiyasi R, ukuphambuka Δ nesiqondiso (kwesokunxele noma kwesokudla), ubude L, ithangenti T, ukuphuma E kanye ne-chord.
5. Umugqa ngaphansi kwamavoluthi unikeza ukuqondisa kwethangenti lokuqala.

## Uma ivoluthi lingafaki

Uma amavoluthi edinga ithangenti elengeziwe kunalelo elikhona phakathi kwama-PI awo, umzila awunayo isixazululo: awudwetshwa ngokulungile size uwulungise, futhi awufakwa ekuthunyelweni kwe-LandXML. Ibhokisi elibomvu lichaza ngani ngezinombolo, futhi linikeze lokho okuzolingana:

- "Amavoluthi ku-PI 2 ne-PI 3 adinga ama-240.00 ft ethangenti phakathi kwawo (130.00 + 110.00), kodwa ama-PI akhe ama-180.00 ft." Noma, uma kuphela enye inevoluthi, "Ivoluthi ku-PI 2 idinga ama-240.00 ft ethangenti ngasosi ku-PI 1 …".
- Inkinobho ngayinye ye-PI isetha **iradiyasi enkulu esingafaka** lapho, elinye ivoluthi lisagcinwa njengoba lisanjalo (kufanele futhi lifake ithangenti lesibili le-PI). Lapho ama-PI omabili enamavoluthi, **Bobabili** unciphisa amarediyasi womabili ngokulinganayo ukuze ahlangane.
- Uma ivoluthi elilodwa lide kunethangenti, ibhokisi lithi akukho voluthi kwelinye PI elingafaka ngenkathi lisakhulu kangaka.
- I-PI ebuyela emuva ngqo ayikwazi ukuthatha ivoluthi; ibhokisi linikeza i-**Akukho voluthi ku-PI n**.

Inkinobho ngayinye yisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} ubuyisela amarediyasi. Amarediyasi anikezwayo ayancishwa aye ku-0.01 ukuze ahlale ekhona.

### Iradiyasi encane yokuklama

Bhala **Iradiyasi encane yokuklama** (njengokuthi kusukela esivinini sakho sokuklama) ukuze kuhlolwe wonke amavoluthi ngayo; shiya ingenalutho uma ingekho. Ivoluthi elingaphansi kwalo libonisa inkinobho ye-**iradiyasi encane ehlangabezana nokuncane**, uma leyo radiyasi ingafaka. Uma ingafaki, ibhokisi liyatshela lokho futhi linikeze iradiyasi enkulu amathangenti avumela, ukuze wazi ukuthi kudingeka ukuhambisa i-PI noma wehlise ukuncane.

## Hambisa ama-PI kwiplani

1. Uma i-{{ui:Khetha}} isebenza, chofoza umzila ukuze uwukhethe. Izibambi ezisikwele zivela emaphoyinteni wawo.
2. Donsa isibambi. Umzila, izisiteshi zawo, iphrofayili nekhoridoro kulandela.
3. Khipha. Ukuhamba kuba yisinyathelo esisodwa sokwenza-kabusha.

Ukungeza i-PI phakathi kwamabili akhona akutholakali okwamanje: susa umzila bese uwudweba futhi, noma bhala amakhoordinethi amasha wama-PI osenawo.

## Funda isiteshi nokuphambuka

Hambisa isikhombisi phezu kweplani. Ibha yesimo ibonisa umzila osondele kakhulu (noma okhethiwe) ngesiteshi nokuphambuka, **L** noma **R** ukubheka phambili kwesiteshi, isibonelo "Umzila 1: Isiteshi 2+15.30, ukuphambuka 18.42 R". Ngaphesheya kwesiphetho ngasinye yengeza "(ngaphesheya kwesiphetho)".

## Okudwetshiwe

Ephlanini, ku-DXF nasePDF: umugqa ophakathi (amathangenti njengemigqa, amavoluthi njengamagomo angempela), uphawu lwe-50 ft (20 m) ngalo lonke ibanga, nophawu olude olunelebula yesiteshi ngalo lonke ibanga le-100, nophawu lwe-PC ne-PT olubhalwe ngezisiteshi zawo.

## Isivinini sokuklama nesigaba

Ngaphansi kwe-Izakhiwo yomzila, i-**Isivinini sokuklama** (km/h noma mph) ne-**Isigaba sokuklama** kugcinwa nomzila, kulondolozwe kuphrojekthi futhi kubhalwe ku-IFC (Pset_RoadDesignCriteriaCommon). I-CivilCraft ayinatafula exhumanisa isivinini neradiyasi encane, ngakho akukho okuhlolwayo ngazo: setha **Iradiyasi encane** ngokwesilinganiso sakho sokuklama.

## Izipirali

Ivoluthi lingaba nesipirali sekhlothoyidi ngapha nangapha: ithangenti, ispiral, ivoluthi, ispiral, ithangenti. Ku-PI table, bhala ubude ku-**Ispiral ekungeneni** ne-**Ispiral ekuphumeni** kwe-PI eneradiyasi. I-PI ihlala lapho ikhona; ivoluthi liyasondela ukuze kwenziwe indawo, ngakho ibanga lethangenti likhula ngokwe-offset yespiral. Lezi zinde zingahluka, futhi i-PI enobude obubodwa ithola ispiral kuleyo ndlela kuphela.

Uhlu lwamavoluthi bese lufundeka i-TS, SC, CS ne-ST (ithangenti kuya kuspiral, ispiral kuya kuvoluthi, ivoluthi kuya kuspiral, ispiral kuya kuthangenti) ne-engeli yespiral θs, izibonelo zethangenti nokuphuma ngaphandle. Izisiteshi zihamba ngamaspiral njengakho konke. Izipiral ezindawonye zigoba ngaphezu kwevoluthi zenqatshwa, kuchazwe i-PI; ispiral ende kunethangenti elivumelekile iyinkinga njengakunoma yiliphi ivoluthi. Ukuphakanyiswa kwegoba kusebenzisa ispiral njengendawo yokuphuma (bona [Ikhoridoro](topic:corridor)).
