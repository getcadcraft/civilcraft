Umbono we-3D ubonisa isayithi njengemodeli eyodwa: umhlabathi okhona nobuso okuklanywe, izinkomba ezidonswa phezu kwabo, imigqa yokuhlukanisa nomphetho wobuso, umzila ngamunye phezu kwephrofayili yawo yokuklama nezimpawu zesiteshi, imigqa yesici yekhoridoro nomgwaqo ngokwawo (imigwaqo, amahlombe, amagutters, amakhabe, amabhande nezindlela zabahambi ngemibala yazo), amapadi namagumbi okukhanya, inethiwekhi yamapayipi namapayipi nezakhiwo ezinomkhawulo wangempela, futhi ku-workspace yezitshalo kukhona izitshalo, ukugcwala, izindlela, izindonga, amakhabe nezinyathelo. Ifunda iphrojekthi efanayo nakwi-plan, ngakho konke ukuhlela kubonakala kokubili.

## Vula umbono we-3D

1. Vula amaphoyinti okuhlola (noma ifayela le-LandXML noma leprojekthi) ukuze kube nobuso. Izitshalo, ukugcwala noma izinyathelo kuzodwa nazo ziyanele ukuvula.
2. Cindezela {{key:D}}, noma chofoza {{ui:3D}} eqenjini le-{{ui:View}} ebhande lamathuluzi.
   Umbono we-3D uvula phezu kwe-plan, ubheka isayithi lonke usuka eningizimu-mpumalanga. Okokuqala kuthatha isikhathi esithile ukulayisha.
3. Cindezela {{key:D}} futhi, noma chofoza {{ui:3D}} futhi, ukubuyela ku-plan. Ukukhetha ithuluzi lokudweba njenge-{{ui:Alignment}} noma {{ui:Pad}} nakho kubuyisela ku-plan, njengoba ukudweba kwenzeka lapho.

## Hamba ungazungezile

| Ukuze | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Zungeza | Donsa ngenkinobho yokhlakaza noma enkatheni |
| Shelelisa | Donsa ngenkinobho yokudla, noma {{key:Shift}} udonse |
| Sondeza | Jikeleza iwili; isondeza ngokuya kumcibisholo |
| Zungeza kupadi yokuthinta | Swayipha ngeminwe emibili ({{key:Shift}} nokuswayipha kushelelisa) |
| Sondeza kupadi yokuthinta | Cindezela ngeminwe emibili (pinch) |
| Ukuthinta | Umunwe owodwa uzungeza; iminwe emibili ihlanganisa ukusondeza nokuhamba ukushelelisa |
| Izinkinobho (umbono unegeyi) | {{key:Left}} {{key:Right}} {{key:Up}} {{key:Down}} zungeza, nge-{{key:Shift}} zishelelisa; {{key:Plus}} no {{key:Minus}} zisondeza |

Ibha ephezulu komsizi inezinye:

- Uhlu lombono: **Iso**, **Top**, **Bottom**, **Front** (ubheka enyakatho), **Back**, **Left**, **Right**.
- {{ui:Perspective}}: ucindezelwe kubonakala umbono wejuqu; chofoza ukuze uthole iprojekshini eqondile (akukho ukufinyeleleka kwesikhala).
- {{ui:Extents}}: ibonisa konke futhi. {{key:F}} yenza okufanayo ngenkathi umbono we-3D uvulekile.

## Ikhyubu yokubuka

Ikhyubu yokubuka esekhoneni elingenhla kwesokudla iyajikeleza nombono. Chofoza uhlangothi, umphetho noma igumbi ukubheka isayithi kulowo mkhakha (ikhamera iyaphenduka ngokuthambile uma uhlelo lwakho lucela ukunciphisa ukunyakaza). Donsa ikhyubu ukuze uzungeze. Indandatho yekhampasi ngaphansi kwayo ibonisa enyakatho (enyakatho yegridi, +Y): donsa indandatho ukuze ujikeze isayithi ngokuya kwendiza, noma chofoza N, E, S noma W ukubheka kulolo hlangothi. I-{{ui:Home}}, indlu encane esekhoneni elingenhla kwesobunxele, ibuyela kumbono we-iso wesayithi lonke. Ngekhibhodi: {{key:Tab}} ukuya kukhyubu, imicibisholo ikhetha uhlangothi, umphetho noma igumbi, {{key:Enter}} ibheka kulowo mkhakha.

Ku-plan ikhyubu efanayo ibonisa phezulu. Chofoza noma iyiphi enye imiphetho, izinhlangothi noma amagumbi (noma uyidonse) ukuvula umbono we-3D ubheka kulowo mkhakha: lokhu kuyindlela esheshayo yokungena ku-3D ngokuya kwesiqondiso esithile. Ukushintsha ikhyubu ibe yisithonjana sendikimba esincane, noma ukuyifihla, bona **Isikhombi sekhona** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings).

## Khulisa ukuphakama

Amasayithi ngokuvamile abanzi kakhulu kunokuphakama kwawo, ngakho ukuphakama kwenwetshwa nge-**2×** ekuqaleni. Khetha **1×**, **2×**, **3×**, **5×** noma **10×** kuhlu olusebhakeni, noma ngaphansi kwe-**Ukukhuliswa okuthikameziwe kwe-3D** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings) (zombili ngukusetha okufanayo). Umbono kuphela uyashintsha: amavolumu, amaphrofayili nama-export ahlala ngesilinganiso sangempela.

## Ukudweba izindawo

- **Amabhendi ophakeme**: amabhendi namabala afanayo nawe-elevation bands ye-plan (inani lamabhendi lisethwa ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa** kwipaneli yesobunxele). Umphetho ngamunye webhendi usikwa ngokunembile ekuphakameni kwawo.
- **Umbala owodwa**: umhlabathi okhona nobuso okuklanywe ngamunye ngombala ovamile.
- **Izendlalelo zamamephu**: izendlalelo zamamephu ezibonakala ngaphansi kwe-plan (isithombe esinokuhlanganiswa kwesithombe, amathayili amamephu noma isevisi yamamephu; bona [Izendlalelo zamamephu](topic:map-layers)) zibekwe phezu kwezindawo njengeshidi, ngakho isithombe se-aerial sibonakala ezintabeni. Izithombe ezisenza ziyagcwala njengoba zifika. Uma kungekho sendlalelo wamamephu obonakalayo, izindawo zigcina amabhendi wazo ophakeme futhi umbono uyatshela lokho. Isithombe sesikrini kuphela: ama-export e-GLB ne-OBJ awasithwali.

Lapho zombili izindawo zibonakala, umhlabathi okhona uyabonakala ngaphansi kobuso okuklanywe, ngakho ungabona ukusika (umhlabathi ophakeme kwesakhiwo) nokugcwalisa.

## Khetha okubonakalayo

Chofoza **{{ui:Show}}** ebhalayo bese ukhetha noma ususa:

| Into | Yini |
|---|---|
| Umhlabathi okhona | Ubuso obakhiwe ngamaphoyinti okuhlola (noma kufundwa ku-LandXML) |
| Ubuso okuklanywe | Umhlabathi okhona nekhoridoro namapadi aklanywe kuwo (kuphela lapho kukhona) |
| Imiphetho yenxantathu | Yonke imiphetho ye-TIN, njengemigqa |
| Izinkomba | Izinkomba ezincane nezinkulu zawo wonke ubuso obonisiwe, ngesilinganiso se-plan |
| Umphetho wobuso | Umphetho ongaphandle kwawo wonke ubuso obonisiwe, ekuphakameni kwawo |
| Imigqa yokuhlukanisa | Yonke imigqa yokuhlukanisa, ekuphakameni kwayo |
| Imizila nezimpawu zesiteshi | Umzila ngamunye ephrofayilini yawo yokuklama (emhlabathini lapho kungekho phrofayili), namabhendi wamasikhathi ayi-50 ft noma ayi-20 m |
| Imigqa yesici sekhoridoro | Umphetho wendlela, ihlombe nokukhanya ngakozombili amahlangothi |
| Imigwaqo | Umgwaqo ngamunye ekhoridoro uwakha, kusuka ekwakhiweni kwawo: izingxenye zomgwaqo, amahlombe, amagutters, amakhabe, amabhande nezindlela zabahambi, ngayinye ngombala wayo, phezu kwesimo esiklanywe |
| Izitshalo | Yonke isitshalo, nezitshalo ezibhedini nasezindongeni zezitshalo, njengezimo ezilula ezilinganiswa uhlu lwezitshalo: isihlahla siyinkanda nesikhutha esiyindilinga (ikhoni sezihlahla eziwusuthu, uhlamvu esigxeni eside sepalmu), izihlahla ezincane, izitshalo zezindonga, ama-perennial nezimbali zomhlaba ziyizimpushana, izinhlamvu ziyizinhlamvu ezincane, izitshalo zokukhwela ziyizimbazo ezincane. Zimi phezu komhlaba ogxilile (noma umhlabathi okhona) ngosayizi okhombisa i-plan: okukhulile noma lapho zitshalwa |
| Izinto eziqinile | Ukugcwala nezindlela ngezinhlamvu zazo, izindonga, izinkinobho, izinyathelo, namabhoshi amasikhathi ahlala ngamasiteshi ahambisanayo |
| Amapadi nokukhanya | Umugqa wepadi ngamunye ekuphakameni kwawo nomugqa wokukhanya wawo |
| Amapayipi nezakhiwo | Amapayipi njengamapayipi ayindilinga phakathi kwawo wokwehla, izakhiwo kusuka emphethweni kuya kusump |
| Amaphoyinti okuhlola | Ichashazi kuphoyinti ngalinye elinokuphakama (kuvaliwe ekuqaleni) |
| Izendlalelo ze-GIS | Izendlalelo ze-GIS ezibonakala ku-plan, ngemibala yazo: imigqa nemiphetho ngokuphakama kwazo, ezinye ezibekwe emhlabathini okhona (zilandela kahle); amaphoyinti njengemisarha emincane. Izimo ezingenakuphakama ngaphandle kobuso azishiywe, futhi umbono uyatshela ukuthi zingaki |
| Amalebula | Amagama nezinani ezibheka wena njalo: igama lomzila ngamunye esiqalweni nezimpawu zawo zesiteshi njalo ngama-100 ft (noma ama-100 m), ukuphakama kwephrofayili ngayinye ye-major contour, amapadi, imigqa yesici nezakhiwo ngamagama, futhi uma **Amaphoyinti okuhlola** ethikiwe inombolo nokuphakama kwephoyinti ngalinye |

Amalebula ahlala ngosayizi ofanayo futhi ema ngqo noma ujikeza noma usondeza, futhi intaba ekuphambi kwelebula iyayifihla. Lapho ahlanganyela khona, okubalulekile kakhulu kuyabonakala futhi okunye kulinda uze usondeze: imizila kuqala, bese izakhiwo, amapadi, imigqa yesici, izimpawu zesiteshi, izingoqo namaphoyinti, eziseduze kuqala. Ayakhombisa esikrinini kuphela; ama-export nezinhlelo azishiyi.

Izinketho zakho, ukukhuliswa kanye nombala kukhunjulwa kulesi sibhamuza.

Umgwaqo wakhiwa phezu komhlabathi okhona. Lapho kukhona umzila nekhoridoro kodwa kungekho amaphoyinti okuhlola noma ubuso okwamanje, umgwaqo udwetshwa phezu komhlabathi wesikhathi esithile: ekuphakameni okuphakathi kwephrofayili yawo yokuklama, noma ulingene ku-elevation 0 uma kungekho phrofayili nhlobo. Isexwayiso ngaphansi kombono we-3D nasezakhiweni zekhoridoro siyatshela lokho. Kuyisithombe kuphela: akukho mavolumu noma izigaba ezivela kuso. Vula amaphoyinti okuhlola noma ubuso bese umgwaqo ushintshela emhlabathini wangempela ngokwawo.

Ukukhuliswa okuthikameziwe kwelula umhlabathi, hhayi izitshalo: zimi emhlabathini okhulisiwe ngobude bazo bangempela nangokusabalala kwazo, ngakho isihlahla sibukeka njengesihlahla noma kunoma yiliphi ukuseta. Izitshalo ziyizimo ezilula, hhayi amamodeli aqoshiwe: izinkulungwane zazo zihlala zishesha ukujikeleza nokusondeza.

## Amapayipi nezakhiwo

- Ipayipi lidwetshwa ngaphansi lalo ngaphakathi kwawo wokwehla owusethile nophezulu lwalo (i-crown) ngaphakathi ububanzi obubodwa obungaphakathi obuphezulu kakhulu. Ububanzi bubhalwa ngamainchi kuprojekthi yesinamba yamafidi nangamamillimitha kuprojekthi yemitha, njengakwipaneli yesokudla.
- Isakhiwo sihamba kusuka emphethweni waso (sibhalwe, noma kusuka phezulu) siye ku-sump yaso (ukwehla okuphansi kakhulu kuwe ngokujula kwe-sump). Ngaphandle kwanoma yikuphi ukwehla, sidwetshwa sijule ngama-5 ft (1.5 m).
- Amapayipi nezakhiwo ezinenkinga (bona ukuhlolwa kwenethiwekhi kwipaneli yesokudla) zidwetshwa ngokubomvu.
- Ipayipi elingenakwehla alidwetshwa; inothi ngaphansi komzila liyakutshela kangaki.

## Khetha ku-3D

Chofoza umzila (noma umgwaqo wawo), ipadi, umgqa wesici, ipayipi, isakhiwo, iphoyinti lokuhlola, isitshalo, uhlu lokugwaza noma enye into eqinile. Iyakhethwa njengoba wayichofoza kwi-plan: izakhiwo zayo zivula kwipaneli yesokudla, lapho ungayihlela khona, futhi idwetshwa ngombala wokukhetha phezulu kwakho konke ku-3D. Chofoza umugqa wokuhlukanisa ukuze ubone ukuthi yiliphi: uyakhanyiswa, futhi ibha yesimo ibiza igama lawo (imigqa yokuhlukanisa anazo izakhiwo zawo).

Chofoza umhlabathi ukuze ufunde i-easting, i-northing nokuphakama lapho kwiqoqo.

## Hamba ngomzila

Chofoza **Shayela** ebhalayo ye-3D bese ukhetha umzila. Umbono uhamba uye esihlalweni somshayeli: ama-3.5 ft (1.07 m) ngaphezulu komgwaqo, phezu kwephrofayili yawo yokuklama (emhlabathini okhona ngomugqa omaphakathi uma ingakabi nayo), ubheka phambili kuwo.

- **Dlala** noma {{key:Space}} ishayela ngejubane olikhethayo (10 kuya ku-65 mph, noma 15 kuya ku-100 km/h); {{key:Space}} futhi iyamisa. Iyama ekugcineni; **Dlala** ibuye iqale kusukela ekuqaleni.
- Isiphethu sivela kunoma yisiphi isiteshi; {{key:Left}} no-{{key:Right}} zinyathela ama-10 ft (5 m) emuva nasekuhambeni.
- Ukudonsa esikrinini kumisa futhi kukuvumela ubheke ngaphandle.
- **Misa** noma {{key:Esc}} ibuyisela umbono lapho bekunjalo khona.

Ukukhulisa kwe-vertical kusebenza nasekushayeleni: sethe ku-1× ukubona umgwaqo njengoba uzoba njalo.

## Ukukhanya kwelanga

Vula **Ilanga** ebhalayo ye-3D bese uthikha **Ukukhanya kelanga**. Nika usuku, isikhathi sewashi nesikhathi sakho sesifunda (ukubalwa kusuka ku-UTC: ekwehlukweni kwesikhathi sasehlobo), kanye nobude nobubanzi besayithi. Uma iprojekthi inohlelo lokuqondanisa, zigcwaliswa kusuka kulo.

Isayithi-ke likhanyiswa ngokusuka lapho ilanga likhona, nezithunzi ezinqwabeni, ekhoridoreni nasemapadini; imenyu ibonisa i-azimuth yelanga nokuthi liphakame kangakanani, noma ukuthi lingaphansi kwesikhathi sokukhanya. Izindawo zivela ezibalweni zelanga zase-NOAA. Izithunzi ziwela ngendlela efanele kunoma yisiphi ukukhuliswa, kodwa ziyayeka emphethweni wesayithi: izintaba nezakhiwo ngaphesheya kwawo azikhiqizi zithunzi.

## Export umbono we-3D

Chofoza {{ui:GLB}} noma {{ui:OBJ}} ebhalayo. Okubonwa umbono kugcinwa, ngesilinganiso sangempela (akukho ukukhuliswa), emitheni, nge-Y ephezulu. Imininingwane: [Export isayithi nge-3D](topic:export-3d).
