Imigudu yamanzi iyinxa yoxinzelelo yesayithi: amapayipi agcwele njalo, amanzi ashayelwa phambili yisitobhi, ithangi noma iphampu. I-CivilCraft ibala ikhanda nocindezelo kuwo wonke amaxhumanisi nokugeleza kuwo wonke amapayipi, kuhlanganise nama-network amalophu lapho amanzi enendlela ngaphezu kweyodwa. Isesilabhorethri ngenkathi ihlolwa.

**Isilinganiso esisodwa kuphela.** Yonke inombolo idinga ukuhlolwa yinjiniyela ehlelelwe: impendulo ilungile ngangokuthi izidingo, amanani okuphakama, usayizi wamapayipi nezinanguluka ozinikeze zona, futhi imodeli engazinzile ayazi lutho ngokushaqeka kwamanzi, ikhwalithi yamanzi noma ukuziphatha kwangempela kwephampu yomlilo.

## Dweba inethiwekhi

1. Ngaphansi kwe-**Surface** › {{ui:Okuningi}}, khetha {{ui:Imigudu yamanzi…}}. Iwindi lihlala livulekile ngenkathi usebenza epulanini.
2. Khetha {{ui:Isitobhi}} bese uchofoza ipulani lapho kukhona khona ukuhlinzeka. Ikhanda lakhe liqala ngo-100 ft (30 m) ngaphezulu komhlaba; liguqule ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Ixhumanisi}} bese uchofoza lapho amanzi edonswa khona noma lapho amapayipi ehlangana khona. Ixhumanisi lithatha ukuphakama kwalo komhlaba; thayipha okuhlukile ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}} ukuze ukhiphe.
4. Khetha {{ui:Ipayipi}}, chofoza inodi, bese inodi elihlangana nalo. Ithuluzi lihlala livulekile ipayipi elilandelayo. {{ui:Iphampu}} ne-{{ui:Ivalvu yokwehlisa uxinzelelo}} ziyadwetshwa ngendlela efanayo, kusukela enodeni yokusonga (noma eyasemthonjeni) kuya enodeni yokuhambisa (noma eyasezansi). {{ui:Ithangi}} libekwa njengexhumanisi.
5. Khetha ixhumanisi bese uthayipha {{ui:Ukudingeka}} lalo (gpm, noma L/s kuphrojekthi yamamitha). Khetha ipayipi bese usetha {{ui:Idayamitha}} lalo (in, noma mm) futhi, uma uyazi, i-Hazen–Williams C yalo.

Inguquko ngayinye iyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. {{key:Delete}} ususa inodi namapayipi aphela kulo.

## Okusho ingxenye ngayinye

- **Ipayipi.** Ubude bulinganiswa ngomugqa epulanini ngaphandle uma uthayipha obuthile. C ingukuqina kwe-Hazen–Williams (130 ukuqala; shintsha isilinganiso sokuzenzakalela ewindini). I-minor loss coefficient K yengeza ukulahleka kwamajika nezixhumi. Phawula **Ivalvu yokuhlola** ephayipini okungamele lidlulise amanzi emuva; ipayipi **Ivaliwe** alithwali lutho.
- **Isitobhi.** Umthombo wekhanda elingashintshi, izinga lamanzi alibambayo ngaphezu kwe-datum. Liyanikela noma lithathe noma yini inethiwekhi edinga.
- **Ithangi.** Izinga elishintsha ngosuku. Ezimpendulweni ezizinzile liyumthombo ngezinga lalo lamanje.
- **Iphampu.** Indawo eyodwa yokuklama (ukugeleza nekhanda) inikeza ijika elijwayelekile ngayo: ikhanda ekugelezeni kwe-zero liyi-133 % yekhanda lokuklama, nekhanda liyi-zero ngokugeleza kabili kokuklama. Iphampu ayikaze ishayele amanzi emuva.
- **Ivalvu yokwehlisa uxinzelelo.** Ibamba uxinzelelo osuwusethile ngaphansi kwesikhathi ukuhlinzeka kuphakeme. Lapho ukuhlinzeka kuphansi ivuleke futhi ayibambi lutho; uma amanzi ezogijima emuva iyavaleka.

## Funda imiphumela

Iwindi libonisa ikhanda nocindezelo kwinodi ngayinye nokugeleza, ijubane nokulahleka kwekhanda kwexhumanisi ngalinye ngamamitha ayi-1,000 ft (noma ngama-km), namaxhumanisi ayikhala ngemibala epulanini: okuluhlaza kuphakathi kwoxinzelelo oluphansi nolukhulu owuthayiphile, okubomvu kuphansi, okuphuzi kuphezulu. Imicibisho yokugeleza ibonisa ukuqondisa. Khetha inodi noma ipayipi ukuze ufunde izinombolo zalo ku-{{ui:Izakhiwo}}.

Ngaphansi kwamathebula kukhona uhlu lwento okufanele ukubheka: uxinzelelo oluphansi noma olukhulu, ipayipi elisheshe kunomkhawulo wakho, ixhumanisi elihlukaniswe kuwo wonke amathuluzi, noma i-solver engazinzanga. Imiphumela efanayo ikwiphaneli ye-Problems. Setha imikhawulo ewindini (20 psi, 100 psi kanye ne-10 ft/s ukuqala; 140 kPa, 700 kPa kanye ne-3 m/s kuphrojekthi yamamitha).

## Ukugeleza komlilo

{{ui:Ukugeleza komlilo…}} idweba ukugeleza kwexhumanisi elilodwa bese ibonisa uxinzelelo lapho nocindezelo oluphansi kakhulu noma kuphi kunethiwekhi. Iphinde ithole ukugeleza okutholakalayo kuleloxhumanisi, kuyilapho inodi elibuthakathaka kunethiwekhi ligcina uxinzelelo olusele osuwunikeze. Ezinye izidingo ziyahlala njengoba zinjalo.

## Usuku lokudingeka

{{ui:Usuku lokudingeka…}} isebenzisa inethiwekhi ihora ngehora. Nikeza ipatheni yokudingeka ezilungiselelweni zefayela (uhlu lwezinani eziphindaphindwayo); amathangi agcwala futhi akhiphe ngokugeleza okungenayo nokuphumayo, nephampu ingalandela ipatheni yokuvula/yokuvala. Ithangi elifinyelela ezingeni lalo eliphezulu noma eliphansi kakhulu liqanjwa ohlwini: i-CivilCraft ayivaleli ukungena noma ukuphuma kwalo ngawe, ngakho lufunde njengokuthi "lelithangi lincane kakhulu noma iphampu likhulu kakhulu".

## EPANET text

{{ui:Londoloza njenge EPANET text (.inp)}} ibhala inethiwekhi ngefomethi yombhalo ocacile abahlelo bamanzi abaningi abayifundayo, futhi {{ui:Vula i-EPANET text (.inp)…}} ifunda enye. Kuphela okumodelwa yi-CivilCraft kuyawela: amaxhumanisi, izitobhi, amathangi, amapayipi, amaphampu anejika lekhanda, amavalvu okwehlisa uxinzelelo, amapatheni okudingeka nezikhathi. Ezinye izinhlobo zamavalvu, amaphampu anikezwa ngamandla, nezinye izifomula zokulahleka ziyabizwa ziyashiywa ngaphandle. Ifayela kumele lisebenzise iyunithi efanayo neprojekthi.

## Ama-hydrant, amavalvu nezixhumi

Khetha ixhumanisi bese uphawula **Hydrant** ukuze ubeke enye lapho; khetha umgudu bese ucindezela **Engeza emaphakathi** ukuze ubeke ivalvu, ivalvu yomoya, i-blow-off noma imitha kuwo. Ayizinto zenethiwekhi: ziyahamba nayo, futhi isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa siyazisusa.

Isakhiwo siphinde sisho nezixhumi, futhi i-CivilCraft iyazidla ngaphandle kokucelwa: i-**tee** lapho imigudu emithathu ihlangana, i-**cross** lapho imine, i-**bend** (nengalo yayo) lapho imigudu ephendukela ngama-5° noma ngaphezulu, i-**reducer** lapho izinga ezimbili zihlangana, nethoshi (**cap**) esiphethweni esivalekile. {{ui:Uhlu lokufakwa (CSV)}} ibala lona nemigudu ngosayizi. Lokhu yisichibiyelo esibonisa ukuthi isakhiwo sisho ini, ukuthathwa kokukhipha okufanele ukuqhathaniswa nemininingwane yakho, hhayi uhlu lokuoda kukho.

Imigudu ibekwa emhlabeni ngaphansi kokumbozwa (4 ft noma 1.2 m) ukuze ikwazi ukudwetshwa nokwenziwa imodeli nge-3D; izinkinga ezingenhla azikaze zincike kukho.

## Epulanini nasemafayeleni

Ipulani, i-PDF, i-DWG noma i-DXF zithwala imigudu ku-**C-WATR-MAIN** nosayizi wayo ku-**C-WATR-TEXT**, ama-hydrant ku-**C-WATR-HYDR**, amavalvu ku-**C-WATR-VALV** nezixhumi ku-**C-WATR-FITT**. I-LandXML ephumayo ibhala inethiwekhi yamapayipi amanzi (isakhiwo ngasinye senodi, ipayipi ngalinye lomgudu, ama-hydrant aqanjwe); i-LandXML engenayo iyifunda ibuyele njengemigudu yamanzi, ngaphandle kwezidingo, amaphampu namavalvu, ngoba i-LandXML ayiwathwali. Ukuthunyelwa kwe-IFC kubhala imigudu njengezingxenye zamapayipi, ama-hydrant, amavalvu nezixhumi ohlelweni olulodwa lwamanzi. Lawa mafayela ahlolwe ngomfundi nababhali bethu; wawavivinye kwezinye izinhlelo zakho bese usitshela ukuthi athini.

## Okungakabi khona

Ikhwalithi namazinga amanzi, izilawuli nemithetho ngaphezu kwepatheni yokuvula/yokuvala yephampu, ezinye izinhlobo zamavalvu, ukudingeka ngoxinzelelo, amapayipi anobubanzi obushintshayo, nokugeleza kwemigudu yamanzi angcolile ngesikhathi.
