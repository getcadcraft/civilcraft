Amafayela e-DXF awaphathi ububanzi bemigqa; zisethe ngengqimba kuhlelo lwakho lwe-CAD.

## Usayizi wamalebula

Amalebula alinganiswa ephepheni: amalebula amakhonthuwa angu-2.0 mm, amalebula esiteshi angu-1.8 mm, amalebula e-PC ne-PT kanye namalebula amapayipi nezakhiwo angu-1.6 mm, amalebula amaphoyinti angu-1.5 mm. Amalebula amapayipi anikeza igama, ububanzi nokuthambeka; amalebula ezakhiwo anikeza igama nomphetho. Esikrinini ahlala esayizi efanayo nxa usondeza. Ku-DXF, akalinganiselwa isikali esishiwo kumyalezo wokuthumela; bona [Thumela iplani ibe yi-DXF](topic:dxf-export).

## Akukho okubonisiwe kunoma yiluphi ungqimba

Amabhendi wokuphakama adwetshwa ngaphansi komugqa esikrinini kuphela. Ukubuka kwephrofayili akuyona ingxenye yeplani.
