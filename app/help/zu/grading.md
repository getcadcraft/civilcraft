Ipedi iyindawo eyicwebe ekuphakameni okukodwa. Kusukela emphethweni walo, i-CivilCraft iyilinganisa iye ngaphandle ngethambeka lakho lokusika lapho umhlaba uphakeme kunepedi, nethambeka lakho lokugcwalisa lapho uphansi, kuze ithambeka lihlangane nomhlaba okhona. Lowo mugqa wokuhlangana uyimugqa wokukhanya kwemini. Ipedi namathambeka alo kuthatha indawo yomhlaba okhona ngaphakathi komugqa wokukhanya kwemini ukuze kwakhe umphezulu oqhutshiwe.

## Dweba ipedi

Udinga umphezulu womhlaba okhona kuqala.

1. Cindezela {{key:G}}, noma uchofoze {{ui:Ipedi}} eqenjini le-**Ukuklama**.
   Isiphakamiso sithi "Ipedi: chofoza amakhoni alo. Enter noma ukuchofoza kabili kuqeda, Backspace ususa okokugcina, Esc ukhansela."
2. Chofoza amakhoni ephedi ngokulandelana, noma ngasiphi isiqondiso. Umbandela oluhlaza uyilandela inkomba.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ngemva kwekhona lokugcina (okungenani amathathu).
   Ipedi idwetshwa futhi ikhethiwe. Iqala ekuphakameni komhlaba ephakathi kwalo, lijikelezwe ku-0.1, ngethambeka lokusika elingu-2 : 1 nethambeka lokugcwalisa elingu-3 : 1. Ibha yesimo ithi, isibonelo, "Ipedi 1 ekuphakameni 254.3 (umhlaba ephakathi kwalo), ukusika 2:1, ukugcwalisa 3:1. Shintsha kuZakhiwo."

:::warning
Qeda ngo-{{key:Enter}}. Ukuchofoza kabili nakho kuyaqeda, kodwa kuleli bhilidi kususa ikhona olichofoze kabili. Chofoza ikhona lokugcina kanye, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
:::

Umbandela wephedi akumele uwelane wodwa ("Umbandela wephedi uwelana wona.").

## Setha ukuphakama namathambeka

Lapho ipedi likhethiwe, iphaneli esobunxeni ikhombisa **Ipedi**:

1. **Igama**.
2. **Ukuphakama**: ukuphakama okucwebe kwephedi.
3. **Ithambeka lokusika** nethambeka **lokugcwalisa**, njenge-H:V (2 kusho 2 : 1). Okubili kumele kube ngaphezu kuka-0.
4. **Indawo yephedi** nezinkonzo **Amakhoni** zibonakala njengesikhombi.

Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ngemva kwenani ngalinye. Umugqa wokukhanya kwemini, umphezulu oqhutshiwe nevolumu kubuyekezwa ngokushesha.

Ngaphansi kwezinsimu, umugqa ufunda isibonelo "Umugqa wokukhanya kwemini: imigqa yokuthambeka engu-48 (engu-20 ekusikeni, engu-28 ekugcwalisweni), ekude kakhulu 31.20 ft ngaphandle." Iphlani likhombisa umugqa wokukhanya kwemini nemigqa yokuthambeka ngombala we-magenta.

## Indlela okutholakala ngayo ukukhanya kwemini

- Imigqa yokuthambeka iphuma kowonke umphetho ngokuqondile kuwo, cishe njalo ngamashumi amabili kwezingxenye zosayizi wephedi.
- Emakhoneni angaphandle ziyasakazeka njalo ngama-10°, ukuze ithambeka ligoqe ikhona.
- Emakhoneni angaphakathi umugqa owodwa uhamba ngomugqa wokuhlukanisa ngamabili, lapho amathambeka emiphetho emibili ahlangana khona.
- Umugqa ngamunye ukhuphuka ngethambeka lokusika noma wehle ngethambeka lokugcwalisa kuze kufinyelele emhlabeni.

## Ukuthambeka ngohlangothi ngalunye nodonga oluxhasayo

Ngaphansi kwe-**Izinhlangothi nezindonga zokuxhasa** ku-Izakhiwo zephedi, lethafomu noma lebhemu, uhlangothi ngalunye lungalinganiswa ngendlela yalo. Uhlangothi 1 uhamba kusukela ekhoneni lokuqala owachofoza kuya kowesibili, uhlangothi 2 kusukela kowesibili kuya kowesithathu, njalo.

- **Zonke izinhlangothi**: **Okuthambekile** kusebenzisa amathambeka okusika nokugcwalisa angenhla kuwo wonke amahlangothi; **Okuqondile: izindonga zokuxhasa** kubeka udonga ohlangothini ngalunye.
- Ngohlangothi ngalunye: **Amathambeka angenhla**, **Ithambeka lalo** (bhala ithambeka lokusika nelokugcwalisa lalolo hlangothi) noma **Okuqondile (udonga oluxhasayo)**.
- Bhala ithambeka njenge-`3` noma `3:1` (okuvundlile kwe-1 okuqondile), `33%` noma `18.4°`; kubonakala njenge-H:1. **Ithambeka lokusika** nethambeka **lokugcwalisa** lephedi ngokwalo kuthatha okufanayo.
- Uhlangothi oluqondile alunalo ukuphakanyiswa komhlaba: udonga oluxhasayo luhamba emphethweni, usuka ephedini lehle liye emhlabeni okhona (ekugcwalisweni) noma usuka emhlabeni uhle uye ephedini (ekusikeni). **Ubukhulu bodonga** (0.3 m noma 1 ft ekuqaleni) yindlela umhlaba ophinde ngayo emphethweni wephedi uye emhlabeni okhona. Amavolumu okusika nokugcwalisa ayayeka ukuphakanyiswa uhlangothi olungasenakho.
- Lapho udonga luhlangana nohlangothi olunethambeka, ithambekaligoqa ekugcineni kodonga; izindonga ezimbili zihlangana ngokhoneni olunamaphuzu (mitre).
- Ngaphansi kwezinsimu: ubude bezindonga, ubuso obubonakalayo (ukuphakama × ubude), ukuphakama okuphezulu nokuphakama okuphakathi.

Iphlani lidweba ubuso bongaphandle bodonga ngoludwe olufushane olunqamula kubo nokuphakama kwalo emaphethelweni omabili nasekuphakameni kwalo okuphezulu (ezingqimbeni zezindonga zokuxhasa, engena nakuDXF ne-DWG). Umbonakalo we-3D ubonisa izindonga njengezinto eziqinile (Bonisa › Izindonga zokuxhasa), futhi ukuthunyelwa kwe-GLB, OBJ ne-USD kuzifaka. Izigaba ezidlula emphethweni zibonisa isinyathelo.

**Izexwayiso** (ku-Izakhiwo, nakwiphaneli Yezinkinga): udonga oludlula **Xwayisa phezu kobude bodonga okungu** (1.0 m noma 3.3 ft ekuqaleni) "lungadinga udonga oluklanywe ngobunjiniyela noma imvume; hlola imithetho yendawo", futhi, uma ubhala **Ithambeka eliphezulu inhlabathi ezinze kulo**, noma iyiphi uhlangothi lapho ithambeka lokusika noma lokugcwalisa liqine kunaleso. Ziyizexwayiso nje: ukulinganisa kuyakhiwa. Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa se-{{key:Mod+Z}}.

## Hambisa amakhoni

Khetha ipedi, bese udonsa isibambo esisikwele ekhoneni. Ukudonsa ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ukungeza noma ukususa ikhona akukatholakali: susa ipedi bese uyidweba kabusha.

Ipedi enenkinga liyashiywa emphezulwini oqhutshiwe nasemavolumini kuze kulungiswe.

## Bona umphumela

1. Ngaphansi kwe-**Bonisa**, setha **Umphezulu** ku-{{ui:Okuhlongozwayo (okuqhutshiwe)}}.
   Ama-contour manje aqondile ngaphezulu kwephedi futhi alandela amathambeka aye emugqeni wokukhanya kwemini.
2. Hambisa inkomba phezu kwephedi: ibha yesimo ibonisa ukuphakama kwe-**Umhlaba** nokwe-**Oqhutshiwe**.
3. Funda ukusika nokugcwalisa ngaphansi kwe-**Ukumba umhlaba**; bona [Amavolumu okumba umhlaba](topic:volumes).

Amaphedi alinganiswa ngemva kwamakhorido, ngodaba lapho adwetshwa ngalo. Ipedi elilandelayo lithatha indawo yanoma yini edlule ngaphakathi komugqa walo wokukhanya kwemini.
