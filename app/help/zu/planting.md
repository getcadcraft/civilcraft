## Beka izitshalo

1. Khetha isitshalo esigabeni se-**Izitshalo** ngakwesobunxele.
2. Chofoza i-{{ui:Plant}}, bese uchofoza eplanini: ukuchofoza ngakunye kubeka isitshalo esisodwa. Cindezela i-{{key:Esc}} ukuze uyeke.

Upawu wesitshalo udwetshwa ngesisabalalo sawo ekuvuthweni; i-More › {{ui:Show at planting size}} idweba wonke amasitshalo ngosayizi esitshalwa ngawo. Izihlahla zihlala emhlabeni ngaphansi kwazo: uma usuqeda ukugreda isayithi kamuva, ziyalandela. Ukuze ubopha isitshalo ezingeni lokuphakama, sithayiphe ku-**Ukuphakama** ku-Izakhiwo.

## Amabhedi okutshala

1. Khetha isitshalo esiyinhloko, chofoza i-{{ui:Bed}} bese uchofoza amakhona webhedi. Cindezela i-{{key:Enter}} (noma chofoza ikhona lokuqala futhi) ukuze uyivale.
2. Ibhedi igcwala ngalesi sitshalo ngokuhlukana kwaso, ngephethini ye-**ethrayangula** (evamile: isitshalo ngasinye sisesikhashana esifanayo nezinye eziyisithupha), ngesigamu sokuhlukana siphakathi emphethweni.
3. Ku-Izakhiwo, i-**Engeza isitshalo** ixuba isitshalo esikhethwe ngakwesobunxele. Setha i-**Isabelo %** sesitshalo ngasinye: ibhedi ithatha kanye leyo nani ngalinye, lisatshalaliswe kuyo. I-**Xuba futhi** ishintsha lapho zihlala khona.

Uma i-**Ukuhlukana** ingenalutho kusetshenziswa ukuhlukana kwezitshalo ngokwazo (kusindwa ngezabelo zazo); thayipha okuthile ukusebenzisa kukho kuzo zonke izitshalo. I-**Ephethini** ingaba esikwele, futhi i-**Imigqa ku-** ishintsha izimpande zemigqa.

## Imiduli

Khetha isitshalo somduli, chofoza i-{{ui:Hedge}}, chofoza ngomduli bese ucindezela i-{{key:Enter}}. Izitshalo zihamba ngomugqa ngokuhlukana kwazo, zimiswe phakathi ukuze zombili izigcawu zibe nomkhawu ofanayo. Umduli ungaba **imigqa emibili, ehlukaniswe**, ngebanga elisethiwe.

## Amalebula

Izitshalo ezingodwa zilebulwa ngesethingi lelebula (`{name}` ngokuzenzakalelayo); thayipha ilebula yakho sitshalo esisodwa ku-Izakhiwo. Amabhedi alebulwa ngenani lezitshalo ngasinye, imiduli ngayo. I-More › {{ui:Plant labels}} ivula futhi icime amalebula. Izinkambu zelebula: `{name}`, `{botanical}`, `{size}` (isitsha), `{qty}`, `{height}`, `{spread}`, `{container}`.

## Uhlu lwezitshalo

I-More › {{ui:Plant schedule…}} ibala zonke izitshalo ngohlobo, kusukela ezitshalweni ezingodwa, emabhedeni nasemiduleni ndawonye, nosayizi wazo wokutshala. Gcina njenge-**CSV** noma **XLSX**, noma **Thumela ku-TakeoffCraft**: ifayela lezinani i-TakeoffCraft elivulayo ukuze ibize izitshalo (nezinani zesakhiwo nokunisela ecaleni kwazo).

## IFC

I-More › {{ui:Export landscape IFC}} igcina i-landscape njengefayela le-IFC (IFC 4.3) imodeli ye-BIM engalibamba: isitshalo ngasinye sibe yi-`IfcGeographicElement` yohlobo le-VEGETATION, esinohlobo lwaso ngohlobo lwaso kanye nezakhiwo zesitshalo (igama lesayensi, usayizi, ukuqina, amanzi, ukukhanya), ibhedi nomduli ngayinye kube yinto eyodwa enenani lazo, esayithini. I-BimCraft iyavula ecaleni kwesakhiwo.
