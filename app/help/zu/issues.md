Inkinga yinothi ephiniwe endaweni ethile yomdwebo wakho we-CivilCraft, inesihloko, isimo namazwana. Yisebenzise ukutshela abasebenzi endaweni noma enye yohlelo ukuthi yini okufanele babheke, nokufunda impendulo zabo.

## Engeza inkinga

1. Vula **Ubuso, Okuningi, Izinkinga (BCF)...**
2. Thayipha isihloko nokuthi yini engalungile.
3. Khetha lapho okuzophinwa khona: phakathi kombono wephulani, iphoyinti lobuso eliphakathi, isiteshi se-alignment ekhethiwe (thayipha isiteshi nokubekwa eceleni, kwesokudla kuyinhle), noma i-pipe, isakhiwo noma umugqa wesici okhethiwe.
4. Cindezela **Engeza inkinga**. Ipini libonakala ephulanini njengedayimane elincane nenombolo yalo.

Chofoza isihloko senkinga ukuze uyisondeze, shintsha isimo sayo oluhlwini (Vuliwe, Iyasebenza, Ixazululiwe, Ivaliwe) bese wengeza amazwana. Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, nezinkinga zilondolozwa kufayela leprojekthi.

## Ukuyishintshanisa njenge-BCF

- **Thumela i-BCF** ibhala ifayela le-BCF 2.1 linesihloko esisodwa senkinga ngayinye: isihloko, isimo, amazwana, nombono owodwa onekhamera yephulani ethe xaxa nepini.
- **Ngenisa i-BCF** ifunda ifayela le-BCF (amafayela e-2.0 ne-3.0 ayafundwa uma efana ne-2.1). Isihloko esinesifanekiso se-guid esisunayo sibuyekeza leyo nkinga (isimo, amazwana amasha) esikhundleni sokwengeza enye.
- **Kusukela ezinkingeni** lenza inkinga kuwo wonke amagatsha avulekile apaneli Yezinkinga akhomba lapho akhona.

I-BCF ithwala amamitha ngokuhlelwa kwezixhumanisi zalolu hlelo, ngaphandle kokushintsha. Enye inhlelo kumele ibe kuhlelo lwezixhumanisi olufanayo nefayela lakho le-LandXML ukuze amaphini afike endaweni efanele. Into ephini elikhomba kuyo (i-alignment, isiteshi, ukubekwa eceleni, i-pipe) ihamba njengamalebula aqala nge-"cc:", ayafundwa yi-CivilCraft.

## Okungahlolwanga

Amaphini nemibono yokubuka ihlolwe kuphela ngokubhala ifayela nokulifunda kabusha ku-CivilCraft. Akukavulwa noma yiluphi uhlelo lwe-BIM, futhi ukuthi "view to world scale" ku-BCF isho ubude obugcwele noma obuhhafu bombono kuyahlukahluka phakathi kwezinhlelo: i-CivilCraft ibhala ubude obugcwele.
