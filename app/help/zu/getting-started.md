I-CivilCraft iyi-app yokuklama kwemiklamo yezwe nendawo yomndeni we-CadCraft. Ithatha amaphoyinti okuhlola bese iwaguqula ibe umphezulu (i-TIN) newa-contour, bese ikuvumela ukuthi ubeke imizila ngamaphrofayili okuklama, ukhiphe umgwaqo olula wekoridoro, ulinganise amapedi ngokuhlobana nokukhanya kwemini, uhlole inethiwekhi yamapayipi ayisisindo ngokwemithetho yakho, futhi ubone imithamo yokukhipha nokugcwalisa. Ifunda futhi ibhala i-LandXML, ithumela ipulani ibe yi-DXF futhi iyiphrinta ibe yi-PDF.

Uma ukhe wasebenzisa i-Civil 3D, imibono izokwazisa: amaphoyinti, amaqembu wamaphoyinti, imigqa yokwephula, imizila ngama-PI, amaphrofayili ngama-PVI, amakoridoro, ukulinganisa umhlaba, inethiwekhi yamapayipi. Indlela oyisebenzisa ihlukile, futhi incane kakhulu: akukho umugqa wemiyalo, akukho izitayela, akukho ifayela lomdwebo. Funda [Ukuvela kwezinye izinhlelo zezokwakha](topic:coming-from) kanye kanye ngaphambi komsebenzi wakho wokuqala wangempela.

## Okudingekayo

- Isiphequluli sedeskithophu esisha: Chrome, Edge, Firefox noma Safari. Bheka [Iziphequluli](common:browsers).
- Imbewu noma i-trackpad nekhibhodi, noma iskrini esithintekayo: ukudweba kunezinkinobho zesikrini {{ui:Finish}}, {{ui:Undo point}} kanye {{ui:Cancel}} (bheka [Imbewu, i-trackpad nokuthinta](topic:keyboard#mouse-trackpad-and-touch)).
- Ipulani idwetshwa ngo-2D ngecanvas yesiphequluli. Ukubuka kwe-[3D](topic:site-3d) kusebenzisa ukuhlinzeka kwezithombe kwesiphequluli (WebGL); uma kungaqali, ibha yesimo iyasho ukuthi kungani, futhi ipulani isasebenza.
- Akukho akhawunti futhi akukho ukulayisha. Amafayela avulwa kukhompyutha yakho futhi alondolozwa njengokulandwa. Bheka [Amafayela akho](common:your-files).

:::note
I-CivilCraft isebenza njengamanje njengesakhiwo sesiphequluli sokuqala esisadalwa. Izakhiwo zedeskithophu azikatholakali, nokulondoloza ku-inthanethi akukatholakali. Konke oyenzayo sekuhlala kukhompyutha yakho uze ulondoloze noma uthumele.
:::

## Vula i-CivilCraft

1. Vula i-CivilCraft esiphequluleni sakho.
2. Linda kuze iskrini sigcwale: ibha yamathuluzi phezulu, iphaneli ngakwesobunxele, ipulani phakathi, nephaneli **Izakhiwo** ngakwesokudla.
3. Ipulani elingenalutho libonisa ibhokisi elithi **Qala ngamaphoyinti okuhlola**. Lidwe izinyathelo zokuqala futhi linesixhumanisi, {{ui:Open the sample site}}.

Ukuze usebenzise i-CivilCraft ngaphandle kokuxhumeka kamuva, chofoza {{ui:Save for offline}} embhalweni wohlelo beseulinda {{ui:Offline ready}}. Ifaka lo siza, indawo yesampula, namalabhulali e-3D ne-GIS. Bheka [Ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline). Cindezela {{key:F1}} noma chofoza {{ui:Help}} noma nini ukuze uvule lo siza ngasemuva kwe-app.

## Imizuzu yakho eyishumi yokuqala: indawo yesampula

Indawo yesampula ngumhlola omncane ngamafidi e-US: amaphoyinti angu-276 ngamakhoordinethi e-state plane, umgogodla, umjula onomugqa wokugeleza kanye nezinhlangothi ezimbili zebhange. Ifika ne-app, ngakho ungalandela ngaphandle kwefayela lakho.

1. Chofoza {{ui:Open the sample site}} kwibhokisi epulanini.
   Ingxoxo **Ngenisa amaphoyinti** iyavuleka. Ithi "amaphoyinti angu-276 afundwe njenge-PNEZD (kusuka emugqeni wekhanda), ahlukaniswe ngamakhefu." futhi ibonisa imigqa yokuqala.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Import}}.
   Ipulani ligcwala ngamaphoyinti, ama-contour nomphetho womphezulu. Ibha yesimo ngaphansi ithi amaphoyinti angeniswe kanye nokuthi umphezulu unonxantathu malini (546).
3. Bheka ngaphansi kwe-**Amaphoyinti** ophaneli yesobunxele. Amaphoyinti ahlelwe ngegama lokuqala lencazelo yawo: CP, FL, GRD, RIDGE, TBN, TBS kanye ne-TREE.
4. Ekholomini ethi **Umphezulu** wale tebhula, setha i-**FL**, i-**RIDGE**, i-**TBN** ne-**TBS** ibe {{ui:Breakline}}.
   Umphezulu wakhiwa kabusha ngokushesha. Izinto ze-**Umphezulu** manje zithi "Imigqa yokwephula: 4", futhi ama-contour agoba kabukhali emugqeni wokugeleza nasegogodleni, esikhundleni sokuwagwaza.
5. Hambisa isikhombisi phezu kwepulani. Ibha yesimo ibonisa ukuma nokuthula ngaphansi kwesikhombisi kanye nokuphakama komhlaba lapho.
6. Ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubonisa**, setha {{ui:Contours every}} ibe 0.5 ft bese uvula {{ui:Elevation bands}}. Kuvela ibhande lemibala nencazelo ngaphansi kwamabhokisi okuhlola. Vala amabhande futhi uma usuqedile.

Manje unomphezulu womhlaba okhona. Okulandelayo, umgwaqo.

## Hlela umgwaqo omfushane

1. Cindezela {{key:A}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Alignment}} eqenjini elithi **Ukuklama**).
2. Chofoza amaphoyinti amathathu endaweni yonke, eyodwa emva kwenye. Umugqa obomvu ulandela isikhombisi futhi ubonise ubude nensondo yethanjana elilandelayo.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uqede.
   Umzila uvela ngokubalwa kwesiteshi. Iphaneli yesokudla ibonisa **Umzila**: ubude bawo, ithebhula ye-PI kanye nejika ku-PI ephakathi (ibanga lika-100 ft, noma elincane uma i-100 ft lingalingani).
4. Kuphaneli yesokudla, ngaphansi kwe-**Iphrofayili yokuklama**, chofoza {{ui:Start from the ground}}.
   Ibuka lephrofayili livula ngaphansi kwepulani nomhlaba okhona kanye nebanga eliqondile lokuklama kusuka ekugcineni kuya ekugcineni.
5. Cindezela {{key:I}} ukuze uvule {{ui:Add PVIs}}, bese uchofoza kanye eduze kwephakathi kwibuka lephrofayili. I-PVI iyavela.
6. Ethebhulini ye-PVI ngakwesokudla, bhala ubude bejika (isibonelo 100) ebhokisini le-**Curve L** ye-PVI ephakathi bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Iphrofayili manje inejika elime, nenani lalo le-K livezwe.
7. Ngaphansi kwe-**Ikoridoro**, khwezela **Hambisa umkhakha ngomugqa wephrofayili wokuklama**.
   Umgwaqo ugxilwe emhlabeni: imizila, izincele namathambeka asethelwa kumhlaba. I-**Umsebenzi womhlaba** ibonisa ukukhipha nokugcwalisa.

Ungakhathazeki ngezinani eziphelele. Zonke izinombolo zingashintshwa ephaneli yesokudla, futhi {{key:Mod+Z}} ihlehlisa ushintsho lokugcina.

## Londoloza umsebenzi wakho

1. Thayipha igama ebhokisini elithi **Igama** phezulu kwephaneli yesobunxele.
2. Cindezela {{key:Mod+S}} (noma chofoza {{ui:Save}}).
   Isiphequluli silanda ifayela elaqanjwa ngegama lendawo, eliphela nge-`.civilcraft.json`. Lingaphatha amaphoyinti, izilungiselelo zomphezulu, imizila, amaphrofayili, amakoridoro, amapedi namapayipi.
3. Ukuze uqhubeke kamuva, chofoza {{ui:Open}} (noma cindezela {{key:Mod+O}}) bese ukhetha leli fayela, noma liyeke epulanini.

:::warning
Akukho ukulondoloza okuzenzakalelayo. Uma uvala ithebhu ngezinguquko ezingalondolozwanga, isiphequluli sikubuza ukuthi ufuna ukuhamba; uma uhamba, izinguquko ziyanyamalala. Londoloza kaningi.
:::
