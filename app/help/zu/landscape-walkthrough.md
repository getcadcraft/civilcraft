Ukuklama kwesikhala sangaphandle kuyazungeza: ukuhlola, uhlaka, imigqa, umhlaba, izindawo, amazinga, ukutshala, izinhlu, amashidi, bese kuyabuya futhi. Lezi yizinyathelo ezilishumi, nokuthi yini ekhona ngayinye. Lapho isinyathelo sinokuntuleka, sikhonjwa ku-**Akukakhiwa okwamanje** ekugcineni, hhayi sifihlwe kuso.

Ungalandela esayithini yesampula, noma komdwebo wakho wokuhlola. Uma usebenzisa owakho, qala ngesinyathelo 1.

## 1. Letha ukuhlola

{{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Imigqa evela kumdwebo…}}, khetha umdwebo wokuhlola (DWG 2000–2018 noma DXF). Hlola iyunithi, thatha amakhonthuwa, ama-polyline a-3D nomngcele womphezulu, namaphoyinti okuhlola njengamabhlokhi amaphoyinti. Isigaba **Amazinga nokuhlola kokuhlola** sifunda amazinga abhalwe njengombhalo noma njengesici bese sidwebela phansi izinto ezibonakala zingalungile (iyunithi, uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi, ukuba kude neziro, amakhonthuwa alingana, amazinga angajwayelekile, amaphoyinti aphindwe kabili) ngaphambi kokungeza noma yini. Bona [Hlola umdwebo wokuhlola lapho ungenisa](topic:survey-check) nale [Vula i-DWG noma i-DXF njengemigqa](topic:open-dwg-dxf). Setha uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi nge-{{ui:Uhlelo lwamakhoordinethi…}}.

## 2. Amaskesi ngesandla

Ishidi lephepha, njengesurvey endala noma ikhophi enemakhiye, lifika nge-{{ui:Skena…}} (Qala kuskena): libekwa ngesikali noma emakhoordinethini aziwayo ngaphansi kwepulani, futhi lingalandelelwa libe imigqa. I-ModelCraft ifunda isithombe seskesi sesandla (Ifayela › Ngenisa isithombe seskesi…). CivilCraft ngokwayo akunalo ithuluzi leskesi sesandla: beka ishidi ngaphansi kwepulani bese udweba phezu kwalo.

## 3. Eskesi kuya emigqeni

Dweba imigqa yezici namaphethelo phezu kokuhlola ku-CivilCraft, noma udwebe kumdwebo wa-2D. Isikhala sangaphandle › Okuningi › {{ui:Kumugqa}} iguqula umugqa ube yi-paving, indawo etshaliwe, udonga, izinyathelo, umugqa wesici nokunye, ibambelelwe: uhambise umugqa, into iyalandela. Bona [Izinto ezivela emigqeni](topic:objects-from-lines). Ku-SuperCad Studio, vula isayithi, umdwebo nemodeli yesakhiwo kwiprojekthi eyodwa: indawo yokusebenza ye-**Isikhala sangaphandle** inawo wonke amathuluzi awo, nomugqa okhethwe kumdwebo wa-2D ungaba yinto yesikhala sangaphandle elandela wona (Ctrl+K, {{ui:Into yesikhala sangaphandle evela kumugqa womdwebo okhethiwe}}).

## 4. Umhlaba owuhlelwe

Vula {{ui:Umphezulu}} › {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala}}. Yakha umhlaba owuhlelwe nge-{{ui:Ipulatifomu}}, {{ui:Ibheme}}, {{ui:Umfudlana wamanzi}} nezindawo zokulinganisa ngamazinga amaphoyinti namakhonthuwa; ukufaka ipavumenti, imizila, izindonga, amaramphu nezinyathelo ongezayo kuyalinganiswa ngaphakathi komphezulu ohlelwe ofanayo. Umhlaba okhona awushintshi ngakanye. {{ui:Amazinga}} › {{ui:Izinguquli zomhlaba…}} ibala konke okwakha umhlaba ngolandelano olulodwa, ngokusika nokugcwalisa ngakunye. Bona [Ukulinganisa umhlaba](topic:landscape-grading) nale [Izinguquli zomhlaba namazinga aphilayo](topic:ground-modifiers).

## 5. Ukufaka ipavumenti nezindawo ezitshaliwe

{{ui:Izakhiwo ezinzima zangaphandle}} › {{ui:Ukufaka ipavumenti}}, {{ui:Indlela}}, {{ui:Udonga}}, {{ui:Umphetho wendlela}}, {{ui:Iramphu}} nezinyathelo {{ui:Izinyathelo}}. {{ui:Izindawo}} udweba ilawu, ibhedi, ingxube yotshani noma umhlabathi ombozayo ngokwakhiwa kwayo; izenza zodwa zisike ezungeze ukufaka ipavumenti, izindonga nezinyathelo. {{ui:Ibhedi}} igcwalisa indawo ngebanga lezitshalo, ngomxube wezitshalo. Bona [Izakhiwo ezinzima zangaphandle](topic:hardscape), [Izindawo ezitshaliwe](topic:planted-areas) nale [Ukutshala](topic:planting).

## 6. Amazinga, amathambeka nokuhlangana

Ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuhlola kokulinganisa umhlaba**, thika **Ukuhlola kwethambeka** kanye **Imicibisholo yokugeleza nokuchitheka kwamanzi**. Uma amanzi echitheka echibini eliseduze nethala, engeza {{ui:Umfudlana wamanzi}} ezungeze lona. Izici zikutshela ukuthi izinyathelo zidinga amazinga angaki nokuthi ayalingana yini, nokuthi yiphi indlela edlula umkhawulo wethambeka layo. Iphaneli yezinkinga ibala lapho izinto zihlangana kabi: udonga oludlula phezulu, ibhedi ephezu kwepavumenti, izinyathelo ezingafinyeleli ekuphumuleni kwazo. {{ui:Izinga lephoyinti}} no {{ui:Umcibisholo wokuthambeka}} babhala umhlaba futhi bazibuyekeze.

## 7. Ukutshala

Ku-**Izitshalo**, sesha uhlu lwezitshalo (izinhlobo ezimbalwa ezingamakhulu, noma uhlu lwakho), khetha esisodwa, bese {{ui:Isitshalo}}, {{ui:Ibhedi}} noma {{ui:Umduli wezihlahla}}. Setha usayizi wesitshalo namalebula kusuka ku-More. More › {{ui:Ithebula lezitshalo}} ihlela amarekhodi akho: izinhlobo ezilinywayo, usayizi abawunikezayo, ukuminyana, amanothi okucaciswa. Irekhodi ngalinye lingaphatha uphawu lwakho nemodeli yakho ye-3D (OBJ, STL noma GLB), futhi More › {{ui:Izithombe zezitshalo}} udweba izitshalo ngokulula kakhulu ezikalini ezinemininingwane emincane. Bona [Uhlu lwezitshalo](topic:plant-list), [Amarekhodi ezitshalo](topic:plant-records) nale [Izithombe zezitshalo](topic:plant-graphics).

## 8. Izinani nezinhlu

More › {{ui:Amashidi okusebenza}}: izinhlu zezitshalo nezakhiwo zangaphandle, nezakho, zihlala zixhunyiwe ezintweni; zigcine njenge-CSV noma njenge-XLSX noma uzibeke emashidini (bona [Amashidi okusebenza](topic:worksheets)). More › {{ui:Uhlu lwezitshalo…}} ithumela izinani ku-TakeoffCraft. Izinani zezakhiwo zangaphandle ezinzima zisezici zepavumenti, zodonga nezinyathelo. Ukunisela kunolwazi lwayo lwezinto ezidingekayo: {{ui:Ukunisela}} › **Hlola uhlelo…**. Bona [Ukunisela](topic:irrigation).

## 9. Imidwebo namashidi

More › {{ui:Amashidi wesikhala esiluhlaza…}}: amapulani okutshala, okulinganisa umhlaba nokunisela, nemininingwane. {{ui:Amazinga}} › {{ui:Umugqa wesigaba}} udweba izigaba ezidlula emhlabeni, ezindongeni, ezakhiweni zokwakha, ezinyathelweni nasezitshalweni. Bona [Amashidi wesikhala esiluhlaza](topic:landscape-sheets) nale [Izigaba nesethi yokushicilela](topic:landscape-sections).

## 10. Shicilela

{{ui:Isethi yokushicilela (.zip)}} engxoxweni yamashidi yenza i-PDF eyodwa enohlu lwamashidi kanye ne-DWG ngeshidi ngalinye. More › {{ui:Thumela i-IFC yesikhala sangaphandle}} inikeza i-BimCraft izitshalo nezakhiwo ezinzima zangaphandle. Londoloza iprojekthi: konke kusefayeleli elilodwa.

## Shintsha into eyodwa: isinyathelo

Inhloso yokugcina konke kwiprojekthi eyodwa ukuthi ushintsho luyadlulela. Zama lokhu ngezinyathelo phakathi kwamathala amabili:

1. Dweba umugqa wezinyathelo njengomugqa wesici (noma umugqa emdwebweni), bese Isikhala sangaphandle › Okuningi › {{ui:Kumugqa}} › {{ui:Izinyathelo}}. Ku-Izici zezinyathelo thikha **Kubumba umhlaba**, bese usetha amazinga aphansi nephezulu kumathala amabili.
2. Dweba ibhedi etshaliwe eduze nangaphansi kwezinyathelo nge-{{ui:Izindawo}}, nepavumenti emathaleni. Ibhedi liyasika lizungeze izinyathelo nepavumenti, futhi uhlu lwezitshalo lubala okutshaliwe kuphela.
3. Khuphula izinga eliphezulu lezinyathelo ku-Izici. Umhlaba owuhlelwe, amazinga amaphoyinti, imicibisholo yethambeka nokusika nokugcwalisa kuyashintsha ngasikhathi sinye. Uma izinyathelo sekungahlangani nethala eliphezulu, iphaneli yezinkinga iyakutshela ukuthi kanjani.
4. Hambisa umugqa wezinyathelo. Izinyathelo zilandela umugqa wazo ngesinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa; ibhedi iyasika futhi ibalwe kabusha, namashidi wokusebenza nezinhlu zikhombisa izinombolo ezintsha.
5. Cindezela Ctrl+Z kanye: umugqa, izinyathelo, umhlaba nebhedi kubuyela ndawonye.

Bona [Izinguquli zomhlaba namazinga aphilayo](topic:ground-modifiers), [Izindawo ezitshaliwe](topic:planted-areas) nale [Amashidi okusebenza](topic:worksheets).

## Akukakhiwa okwamanje

Lezi zicelwa kodwa azikho kulokhu kwakhiwo:

- Izindonga ezakha umhlaba (zibika lapho zihlangana khona nalapho uphezulu wazo ungalandela khona).
- Ukuhlela uhlu lwakho lwezitshalo lomtapo ku-app (hlela uhlu lweprojekthi bese uligcina njengefayela lezitshalo).
- Izakhiwo nemibala yamamodeli akho e-GLB ezitshalo (isimo siyafundwa; kusetshenziswa umbala wezitshalo).

Bona [Indawo yokusebenza yesikhala](topic:landscape) ngawo wonke amathuluzi.
