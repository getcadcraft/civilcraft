I-{{ui:Areas}} kwiqembu le-Landscape idweba **ithanga lotshani**, **ibhedi etshaliwe**, **idlelo** noma **isitshalo esimbozayo**: chofoza amakhona alo bese ucindezela i-{{key:Enter}}. Ibhedi noma isitshalo esimbozayo siqala sigcwele isitshalo esikhethwe esigabeni se-Izitshalo; ithanga lotshani alinazo izitshalo.

## Okuyibambayo

- **Ukwakheka**: ubujulile benhlabathi yomhlaba engaphezulu, ubujulile bogqoko, imembrane yokuvimbela okhula, kanye nomphetho (insimbi, izinkuni, izitsha, ukhonkolo, iplastiki noma akukho). Izinzulu zejwayelekile zigcwaliswe kusengaphambili; zisete ezakho.
- **Ingxube**: izinhlobo (nenhlobo elilinywayo nosayizi) kanye nezabelo, zigcwaliswe njengebhedi ngokuhlukana noma ukuminyana.
- **Izinga eliqediwe**: lishiye lingenalutho bese indawo ilandela umhlaba; uyinike izinga bese indawo ibe yimodifayi yomhlaba (bheka [Izimodifayi zomhlaba nezinga](topic:ground-modifiers)).
- **Ukusika ezungeze ukugcwaliswa, izindonga nezinyathelo** (kuvulekile ngokuzenzakalelayo): lapho ukugcwaliswa, indlela, udonga, umphetho wendlela, ukwehla noma izinyathelo kudlula kuleyo ndawo, lowo mhlaba uyashiywa. Asikho isitshalo esibekwa ngaphansi kwazo noma ngaphakathi kwesigamu sokuhlukana kumphetho wazo.

## Izinani

Izakhiwo zibonisa **indawo esele** (emva kokusika, ilinganiswa ngokunembile), umjikelezo, **ubude womphetho** (kuphela ngomphetho wendawo ngokwayo, hhayi lapho ukugcwaliswa kuwusika khona), amavolumu enhlabathi yomhlaba engaphezulu nogqoko, indawo yemembrane kanye nenani lezitshalo ngomugqa ngamunye wengxube. Uhlu lwezitshalo lubala izitshalo zendawo; ifayela lezinani le-TakeoffCraft ithola indawo, inhlabathi yomhlaba engaphezulu, ugqoko, imembrane kanye nomphetho; i-IFC yesikhala sangaphandle ibhala i-IfcGeographicElement enesethi yezinani.
