Dweba izinsiza ezingaphansi komhlaba zesayithi njengenethiwekhi eyodwa ngensiza ngayinye, hlola lapho zidlula khona, bese uzithumela njengezingqimba zomdwebo nasku-IFC.

## Yenza imigudu

Vula **Ubuso, Okunye, Amanethiwekhi wezinsiza...**

- **Yenza umgudu ngokukhethiwe** iguqula umugqa okhethiwe, umugqa wephrofayili noma ipayipi ibe umgudu wensiza oyikhethile. Bese ugcwalisa impahla, usayizi (mm), ukumbozwa (ukujula kuya phezulu kwepayipi noma kwekhebula) kanye nomnikazi kuthebula.
- **Funda idatha ye-OpenStreetMap** ifunda ifayela le-Overpass JSON owe wayilondoloza (umbuzo onomugqa `out geom;`). Iphrojekthi idinga uhlelo lokuhlanganisa kuqala, ngoba izindawo ze-OpenStreetMap ziwukuthi ububanzi nobude. Imipayipi (ngokwempahla), amakhebula kagesi nokuxhumana angaphansi komhlaba, ihayidrendi, izimbobo zokungena kanye nemifudlana yemvula iyafundwa; okunye konke kubalwa njengokushiyiwe futhi kuchazwa emyalezweni. Imigqa ephezulu ishiywa.
- **Engeza imephu ye-OpenStreetMap** yengeza imephu ye-OpenStreetMap ngaphansi kwepulani. Idwetshwa kuphela uma izingqimba zemephu zevela ku-inthanethi zivulekile ku-Izilungiselelo.

## Ukuhlola nokuphumayo

- **Ukudlula**: imigudu yezinsiza ezahlukene edlula ngaphezulu kwepulani iyalulwa, futhi kubonakala ukuphakama okusele phakathi kwazo lapho kukhona indawo yomhlaba; okuncane kuwukubamba indawo oyisethayo, hhayi inani lomgomo.
- **Umsele**: umgudu ngamunye uthola umsele (usayizi wepayipi kanye nokukhululeka ngakolunye nakolunye uhlangothi, ukuthambeka kohlangothi, indlela yokubeka) nevolumu. Inani eliphelele libonakala ewindini nasku-CSV.
- **Umdwebo**: imigudu namaphoyinti asezingqimbeni zensiza (isibonelo U-WATR-RUN, U-POWR-RUN), ngakho afika ku-PDF, DXF kanye ne-DWG.
- **IFC**: imigudu ingena ekuthunyelweni kwe-IFC njengezingxenye zamapayipi nezingxenye zamakhebula, kanye namahayidrendi, amavalvu kanye nezimbobo, isistimu eyodwa ngensiza ngayinye, ku-uhlelo lokuhlanganisa lwephrojekthi.

## Idatha ye-OpenStreetMap

Idatha ye-OpenStreetMap ilungelo lokushicilela labanikezeli be-OpenStreetMap futhi inelayisense ye-ODbL. Okokubonga kwengezwa emdwebeni, ku-CSV nasku-IFC. Uma wabelana ngefayela eliqukethe idatha ye-OpenStreetMap, gcina okokubonga futhi ufunde imigomo yokwabelana ngendlela efanayo yelayisense.

## Okungakenziwe

Imigudu aikadwetshwa ngephoyinti ngephoyinti kupulani ngethuluzi okwamanje, futhi ukudlula akudwetshwa kumbono wephrofayili okwamanje (ukubalwa sekwakhiwe). Ukujula, usayizi kanye nokukhululeka kuqala njengezibambi: sebenzisa izinombolo zomgomo wakho.
