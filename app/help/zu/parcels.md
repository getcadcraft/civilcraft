Amapaseli ahlala epulanini nesikhombisi nobude ngakunye komugqa oqondile (igoqo likhombisa iradiyasi, ubude nengeli esemaphakathi) negama lawo nendawo yawo phakathi. Aya kwi-PDF plot nakuma-export e-DWG ne-DXF, ezendaweni `V-PROP` (imigqa) ne-`V-PROP-TEXT` (amalebula). Konke kutholakala ngaphansi kwe-**Amapaseli** kwipaneli yesokudla lapho lutho olukhethiwe.

## Dweba ipaseli

1. Chofoza **Dweba ipaseli**.
2. Chofoza amakhona, uqale ngendawo yokuqala. Ukuchofoza eduze kwephoyinti lesamukelo kuthatha indawo eqondile yalelo phoyinti.
3. Chofoza ikhona lokuqala futhi, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}, ukuze uqede. {{key:Backspace}} ibuyisela ikhona lokugcina emuva; {{key:Esc}} ilahla amakhona esewenziwe kuze kube manje; cindezela futhi ukuze uphume kwithuluzi.

## Faka izinkulumo edidini

1. Chofoza **Faka izinkulumo zedidi**.
2. Tshela lapho izinkulumo ziqala: inombolo yephoyinti lesamukelo, noma i-northing ne-easting.
3. Thayipha noma unamathisele izinkulumo, eyodwa ngomugqa:
   - umugqa: isikhombisi nobude, njengo `N 45°30'15" E 125.50`. `N45-30-15E`, `N 45 30 15 E`, `N 45.5 E` nama-azimuth (`AZ 45°30'15"`) nawo ayasebenza, futhi "thence", amakhoma nokuthi "feet" ayanganakwa.
   - igoqo: `C R 300 L 52.36 right` (iradiyasi nobude bomkhondo) noma `C R 300 D 10°00'00" left` (iradiyasi ne-engeli esemaphakathi). Igoqo lilandela umugqa owandulele; uma lingalandeli, engeza isikhombisi sayo se-chord: `C R 300 L 52.36 right CB N 50 00 00 E`.
   - `#` iqala umugqa wokuphawula.
4. Njengoba uthayipha, ingxoxo ibonisa ubude bezinkulumo, i-**misclosure** (ukuthi zikude kangakanani nokuqala futhi ziya ngasiphi isiqondiso), i-**precision** (ubude bahlukaniswe nge-misclosure, 1 : 10,000 njalo njalo) kanye nendawo. Imigqa ebingafundeki iyadwetshwa namanombolo emigqa yayo.
5. **Engeza ipaseli**. Uma izinkulumo zingavali, ipaseli ivalwa ngomugqa oqondile ngaphezu kwesikhala: hlola izinkulumo nedidi. Ukuze usabalalise iphutha lohambo lwasensimini ezigabeni zalo, lilungise kuqala.

## Amagoqo

Ku-**Properties** yepaseli, igoqo ngalinye linayo **Iradiyasi**. Thayipha eyodwa ukwenza leligoqo libe igoqo elidlula kulawo makhona amabili afanayo (iradiyasi encane kunengxenye yesibili yomgwaqo phakathi kwamakhona ayikwazi ukufinyelela, ngakho iyenqatshwa), bese ukhetha **Right** noma **Left** ku-**Curves**: igoqo elingakwesokudla linesikhungo salo ngakwesokudla kwendlela ehamba ngayo, njengasedidini. Sula iradiyasi ukwenza kube qondile futhi. Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyabuyisela.

## Incazelo yomthetho

Ku-Properties yepaseli kuphela incazelo yayo yomthetho, ebhalwe kusuka kumdwebo:

- "Kuqala ku-" **Indawo yokuqala** oyithayipha (isibonelo "ikhona elingasenyakatho-mpumalanga le-Lot 4"), uma kungenjalo inombolo yenyakatho nokuya empumalanga yekhona lokuqala, noma iphoyinti lesamukelo ayeqala kulo izinkulumo.
- "thence" ngakunye kwezigaba: isikhombisi nobude, noma igoqo nobude balo bomkhondo, ingeli esemaphakathi, nesikhombisi nobude be-chord.
- "Iqukethe" indawo yayo ngezinyawo ezisikwele namaekhari (amamitha ayisikwele namahektha kuphrojekthi yamamitha).

**Kopisha** ikopisha ebhodini lekhophi; **Landa njengombhalo** iyilondoloza njengefayela le-`.txt`.

:::warning
Incazelo yomthetho iyidrafu evela kumdwebo, hhayi isilinganiso sezindawo. Umhloli wezindawo onelayisensi kudingeka ayihlole ngorekhodi bese ayisayina ngaphambi kokuthi noma ngubani ayithembele.
:::

Chofoza ngaphakathi kwepaseli epulanini ukuze uyikhethe; {{key:Delete}} iyisusa. Amapaseli alondolozwa kufayela leprojekthi.
