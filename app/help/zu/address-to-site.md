## Yini ekwenzayo

Umphezulu › Okuningi › Ikheli kuya endaweni iphendula, ngephoyinti: ukuthi liphi ipharseli, izakhiwo ezizungezile, ukuhlelwa kwendawo, inhlabathi nendawo yezikhukhula. Idatha ivulekile: amafayela owengeza (GeoJSON, shapefile, KML, GeoPackage) nezinkinobho, uma uvula okwengeziwe, ezicela isevisi evulekile eyodwa ngayinye.

## Ukusebenza

1. Bhala ikheli bese ucindezela inkinobho yokusesha ikheli (okwengeziwe "Ukusesha ikheli"), noma bhala ububanzi nobude.
2. Engeza izingqimba: amafayela ngokuthi "Engeza amafayela", noma ezinye izinkinobho zemithombo (indawo yezikhukhula, izakhiwo, inhlabathi, ukuphakama komhlaba, amapharseli ezifundazwe). Isevisi icelwa kuphela uma ucindezela inkinobho yayo; akukho okulethwa ekuqaleni.
3. Cindezela inkinobho yokusebenza ukuze uthole impendulo ngamagama, nefayela le-CSV.
4. "Londoloza indawo njengefayela" noma "Thumela ku-BimCraft njengezincomo" inikeza izinhlangano ku-BimCraft noma ku-ModelCraft; zona ziyazibhala bese zengeza kuphela lokho okuthi ufake itiki kukho.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ukusesha ikheli kudlula ku-getcadcraft.com, okucela i-Nominatim ye-OpenStreetMap ngawe (isicelo esisodwa ngesekhondi okungenani; izimpendulo zigcinwa izinsuku ezingu-90, ukuze ikheli elifanayo lingacelwa kabili; kugcinwa i-hash yekheli kuphela, hhayi ubani owecele). Ezinye izinkinobho zihamba ngqo kusuka kusiphequluli sakho kuya kwisevisi yazo.
- Ilayisense ngayinye inelayisense yayo (i-OpenStreetMap ingaphansi kwe-ODbL: gcina umugqa wokuncoma).
- Izingqimba zinhle njengedatha evulekile endaweni yakho. Hlola okubalulekile ngokuhlola umhlaba (survey).
