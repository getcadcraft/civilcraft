Amaphoyinti okuhlola angakopishwa futhi anamathiselwe ngaphakathi kweprojekthi, phakathi kweprojekthi ezimbili kumathebhu amabili esiphequluli, nokuya nokusuka kuspredishithi. Yonke inguquko yisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, futhi indawo yakhiwa kabusha ngokushesha kumaphoyinti amasha.

## Khetha amaphoyinti

Nge-{{ui:Khetha}} ({{key:V}}):

- **Chofoza** iphoyinti ukuze uliKhethe (lona kuphela). Izinombolo zalo zibonakala ngaphansi kwe-**Izici**.
- {{key:Shift}} kanye **nokuchofoza** kungeza iphoyinti ekukhethweni, noma kulisuse.
- {{key:Shift}} kanye **nokudonsa** kudweba ibhokisi: amaphoyinti angaphakathi kwalo engezwa ekukhethweni. Uma udonsa uzungeze amaphoyinti, kuba yi-lasso; bona [Ukukhetha nge-lasso kumaphoyinti okuhlola](topic:lasso-selection). Ukudonsa ngaphandle kwe-{{key:Shift}} kuhambisa ipulani, njengoba kujwayelekile.
- {{key:Mod+A}} (noma {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Khetha wonke amaphoyinti}}) ikhetha wonke amaphoyinti.
- {{key:Esc}} (noma {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Khetha lutho}}) isula ukukhetha.

Amaphoyinti akhethiwe anesiyingi. Uma kukhethwe okungaphezu kwelodwa, **Izici** zibonisa ukuthi zingaki, izinombolo zazo, ububanzi bokuphakama bazo namaqembu azo.

## Kopisha, sika nokususa

- {{ui:Kopisha}} noma {{key:Mod+C}} zikopisha amaphoyinti akhethiwe. I-CivilCraft igcina ikhophi, futhi iyibeka nakubhodi lokukopisha lesistimu njengemigqa yamaphoyinti, ukuze uyinamathisele ngqo kuspredishithi noma kufayela lombhalo.
- {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Sika}} noma {{key:Mod+X}} ziyawakopisha bese ziyawasusa, ngesinyathelo esisodwa.
- {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Susa}} noma {{key:Delete}} ziyawasusa. {{key:Mod+Z}} iyawabuyisa.

Imigqa imi ngale ndlela: iphoyinti, i-northing, i-easting, ukuphakama, incazelo (P,N,E,Z,D), ihlukaniswe ngamakhoma, namadesimali amathathu. Ukuze i-easting ibe phambili kwe-northing, setha **Imigqa yamaphoyinti** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings). Incazelo okuthi isipredishithi siyibhekisele njengefomula (eqala nge-=, +, - noma @) ithola i-apostrophe phambili, ukuze ihlale ingumbhalo.

## Namathisela

1. Cindezela {{key:Mod+V}} noma uchofoze {{ui:Namathisela}}. Amaphoyinti alandela isikhombisi njengemifantu emincane, nomugqa oqhekeke ubuyela lapho aqhamuka khona.
2. Chofoza lapho ayokuya khona. Phakathi kwamaphoyinti akopishiwe kufika endaweni ochofoze kuyo.
3. Noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuwabeka emakhoordinethini awo, noma {{key:Esc}} ukukhansela.

{{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}} noma {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} unamathisela emakhoordinethini amaphoyinti ngokushesha. Amaphoyinti anamathisiwe akhethwa ngemva kwalokho, ukuze ukwazi ukuwakopisha, ukuwahambisa noma ukuwasusa futhi.

:::note
Ukuchofoza {{ui:Namathisela}} kwibha yamathuluzi kufunda ibhodi lokukopisha lesistimu. Okokuqala, isiphequluli singabuza ukuthi i-CivilCraft ingayibona yini ibhodi lokukopisha. Uma unqaba, inkinobho yebha yamathuluzi inamathisela ikhophi yayo ye-CivilCraft kuphela; {{key:Mod+V}} isahlala inamathisela imigqa yaspredishithi ngaphandle kokubuza.
:::

## Izinombolo zamaphoyinti azihlangani

Amaphoyinti amabili akalokothi abe nenombolo efanayo. Uma unamathisela:

- Iphoyinti elinamathisiwe ligcina inombolo yalo uma kungekho phoyinti eprojekthini elinayo. Ngakho ngemva kwe-**Sika**, amaphoyinti abuyela namathisela ngezinombolo zawo, namaphoyinti akopishwe kwenye iprojekthi agcina zona lapho zikhululekile.
- Iphoyinti elinamathisiwe enombolo yalo esele ithathiwe lithola inombolo entsha ephelele: eyodwa ngaphezu kwenombolo ephakeme kakhulu esetshenziswayo, ikhuphuka.
- Ibha yesimo itshela okwenzekile, isibonelo "1 kugcine inombolo yalo; 9 bathole izinombolo ezintsha: 10–18 baba 277–285".

Ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings) ungakhetha **Izinombolo ezintsha njalo**, kanye nenombolo lapho izinombolo ezintsha ziqala khona, isibonelo 5000 wamaphoyinti edizayini.

:::warning
Amaqembu amaphoyinti akha indawo njenge-**umugqa wokwephula** ahlanganisa amaphoyinti ngokulandelana kwezinombolo. Amakhophi amaphoyinti omugqa wokwephula anamathisiwe (isibonelo amaphoyinti e-FL) athola izinombolo ezintsha, ezinkulu, bese ejoyina ekugcineni komugqa wokwephula, kungenzeka ngokugxuma okukhulu. Hlola ipulani ngemva kokunamathisela amaphoyinti anjalo, noma setha iqembu lawo libe ku-{{ui:Amaphoyinti}}.
:::

## Phinda

{{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Phinda}} noma {{key:Mod+D}} yenza ikhophi yamaphoyinti akhethiwe elandela isikhombisi: chofoza lapho iyokuya khona. Amakhophi ngaso sonke isikhathi athola izinombolo ezintsha, kanti ibhodi lokukopisha le-CivilCraft lishiyeka njengoba besinjalo.

## Kusuka kuspredishithi noma kufayela lombhalo

Khetha imigqa yamaphoyinti kuspredishithi (iphoyinti, i-northing, i-easting, ukuphakama, incazelo) bese uyakopisha. Ku-CivilCraft cindezela {{key:Mod+V}} bese uchofoza lapho ayokuya khona, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} ukuwabeka emakhoordinethini awo, okuvamise ukuba yikho okudingekayo kudatha yangempela yokuhlola.

Imigqa ifundwa njengefayela lamaphoyinti ([ifayela lamaphoyinti](topic:point-file-formats)) ngokuqondile: amakoma, amathebhu noma izikhala, umugqa wekhanda uma ukhona. Ngaphandle komugqa wekhanda, ukulandelana kwamakholomu kuvela ku-**Imigqa yamaphoyinti** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-CivilCraft](topic:settings).

Uma kokubili kukhona, i-CivilCraft inquma ngokusha: imigqa esibhodini lokukopisha lesistimu ehlukile kokukopishwa kokugcina kwe-CivilCraft ikopishwe kamuva, ngakho iyanamathiselwa. Umbhalo ongaqukethi maphoyinti uyanganakwa, kanti kunamathiselwa ikhophi ye-CivilCraft.

## Phakathi kweprojekthi namathebhu

Ikhophi igcinwa kulesi siphequluli, ngakho ithebhu yesibili ye-CivilCraft ingayinamathisela, ngisho nangemva kokuvalwa kwethebhu yokuqala. Amaphoyinti akopishwe kweprojekthi yamamitha futhi anamathiselwe kweprojekthi yamafidi agcina izinombolo zawo njengoba zinjalo: akukho okuguqulelwayo, nebha yesimo iyasho lokho.

## Okungakopishwa

Amaphoyinti okuhlola kuphela akopishwa okwamanje. Imizila, amapedi, amanethiwekhi amapayipi, imigqa yokwephula nomngcele akukakopishwa noma unamathiselwe. Ukunamathisela akukwazi ukuhambisa amaphoyinti akhona: sebenzisa {{ui:Sika}} bese unamathisela, noma hambisa iphoyinti elilodwa ngesikhathi ([Hlela iphoyinti lokuhlola](topic:edit-points)).
