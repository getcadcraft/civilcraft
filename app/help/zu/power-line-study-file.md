Ezinye izinhlelo zomugudu wephezulu zigcina ucwaningo kufayela le-`.DON` (umbhalo we-XML). I-CivilCraft ingabhala umugudu owodwa njengalelo fayela futhi ifunde amasekelo kwelinye.

:::warning
**Akuhlolwanga.** Sakha lokhu kusuka ohlwini lwamagama amasimu wefomethi ayabelana ngawo umsebenzisi, hhayi kusuka kufayela langempela, futhi asikavuli amafayela ethu kuhlelo olufunda. Vula kulo ubheke yonke inani ngaphambi kokuthi ulithembele. Uma ungabelana nefayela le-`.DON` (ngemininingwane yeklayenti esusiwe), kusisiza ukuthi sikulungise lokhu.
:::

## Londoloza ifayela locwaningo

1. Nikeza umugudu ucingo futhi uqinisekise ukuthi unemiphumela (akukho "akukaqhutshwa" ku-**Izakhiwo**).
2. {{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Londoloza ifayela locwaningo (.DON)…}}, khetha umugudu, engeze incazelo nenombolo yomsebenzi.
3. {{ui:Londoloza ifayela locwaningo}}.

Ifayela linamasekelo womugudu (izixhumanisi, umhlaba, amabanga, izindima, ingalo ewelayo, isekelo eligcinwe noma eliphakanyisiwe, imithwalo ngesimo ngasinye, isisekelo), isigaba esisodwa ngasinye phakathi kwezindawo ezifile (ucingo, ivolovolo, indawo yokulinganisa, izinhlu, ukuqina nezinkawu ze-catenary, ukuqina okukhulu kakhulu ngesimo ngasinye) namathebula okufaka umugqa. Ubude buba ngamamitha, namandla angama-daN; umbhalo ubhalwa nge-Latin-1, ngakho uhlamvu olungaphandle kwalo lube `?`.

## Funda ifayela locwaningo

{{ui:Okuningi}} › {{ui:Vula ifayela locwaningo (.DON)…}} ifunda amasekelo ibe umugudu omusha. Ucingo, amasekelo nengalo ewelayo kuhlanganiswa nomtapo wakho ngegama; okungatholakali kushiywa ukuthi ukhethe ku-**Izakhiwo**.
