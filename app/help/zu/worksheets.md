{{ui:Amashidi okusebenza}} (Indawo yesikhala sangaphandle › Okuningi) ivula amashidi okusebenza alolu hlelo. Okokuqala, i-**uhlu lwezitshalo** nethebula le-**izakhiwo ezinzima nezindawo** kwenziwa ngawe. Ishidi lokusebenza lisetshenziswa kusuka ezintweni njalo lapho libonisiwe, lilotshiwe noma libekwe ishidi: shintsha ibhedi bese izinombolo zayo zishintsha kuwo wonke amawebhusayithi.

- **Imigqa evela ku**: izitshalo (umugqa owodwa ngohlobo, ngocwecwe nangobukhulu), izindawo ezitshaliwe, ukugcwala, izindonga, izinyathelo, abalungisi bomhlaba (ukusika nokugcwalisa) noma zonke izinhlanganisela.
- **Amakholomu**: insimu (`qty`, `area`, `botanical`, noma igama phakathi kwamabhulaketi njengo `[Net area]`) noma ifomula: `+ - * / ^ %`, `&` ukuhlanganisa umbhalo, `= <> < <= > >=`, kanye ne-`round`, `ceil`, `floor`, `abs`, `sqrt`, `min`, `max`, `if`, `and`, `or`, `not`, `concat`, `upper`, `lower`, `len`, `text`. Ifomula engenakubalwa ibonisa `#ERR` (noma `#DIV/0`) kuseli layo. Amafomula afundwa yisihlaziyi sikaCadCraft; akukho okuqhutshwa njengekhodi.
- **Isamba** ngekholomu ngayinye (isamba, inani, isilinganiso, esincane kakhulu, esikhulu kakhulu), **Hlanganisa ngokuthi** (izingqikithi ngeqembu ngalinye) kanye **Imigqa kuphela lapho**.
- {{ui:Londoloza njenge-CSV}}, {{ui:Londoloza nge-XLSX}}, kanye **Beka lo shidi okusebenza emashidini** (Amashidi › Amashidi okusebenza kudweba njengethebula, phezu kwamashidi amaningi njengoba kudingeka).

Izimpawu zezitshalo nezindawo zifunda amagama afanayo ensimu (`{botanical}`, `{cultivar}`, `{size}`, `{qty}`).
