Uma i-pad noma ikhoridoro selilinganisiwe ngaphandle kwezinkinga, i-CivilCraft iqhathanisa ingaphezulu elihlongozwayo nomhlaba wangempela bese ibika ukusika nokugcwalisa. Izinombolo zibuyekezwa ngalo lonke ushintsho.

## Lapho ufunda khona

- **Umsebenzi womhlaba** ngakwesokudla ngaphandle kokukhethwa (ukubuka **kwePhrojekthi**) uma kukhona okungenani i-pad eyodwa noma ikhoridoro eyodwa.
- **Umsebenzi womhlaba** ngakwesokudla lapho i-pad ikhethiwe (amanani afanayo kuyo yonke iphrojekthi).
- Umugqa ngaphansi kwezilungiselelo zekhoridoro, nokusika nokugcwalisa kwalelo khoridoro.

Ibhulokhi **Umsebenzi womhlaba** ibonisa:

- **Ukusika**, **Ukugcwalisa** kanye **Okuseleyo** (nakho kulandelwa "ukugcwalisa" noma "ukusika", kuye ngokuthi yiyiphi enkulu)
- umugqa ngamunye uma kukhona okungaphezu kwento eyodwa, isibonelo "Pad 1: ukusika 120.4 cu yd, ukugcwalisa 310.2 cu yd"
- umugqa wendlela: "Indlela yegridi: ingaphezulu lomhlaba nengaphezulu elilinganisiwe kusampuliwe njalo ngo-0.5 ft ngaphakathi kwemigqa yemini (amaseli angama-92,891). Akukho ukuvuvuka noma ukuncipha okusetshenziswe."

Amavolumu asezindaweni zamayadi ayiqhubu kuphrojekthi yamafidi namamitha ayiqhubu kuphrojekthi yamamitha.

## Thumela ku-TakeoffCraft

Ukuze unikeze intengo womsebenzi womhlaba, khetha {{ui:Thumela ku-TakeoffCraft}} ezansi kwebhulokhi **Umsebenzi womhlaba**. I-CivilCraft igcina ifayela lezinani (`<site>.quantities.json`) linezinani ezimbili: ukusika nokugcwalisa, ngamayadi ayiqhubu (iphrojekthi yamafidi) noma ngamamitha ayiqhubu (iphrojekthi yamamitha), izinombolo ezifanayo nebhulokhi, kuze kube ngamakhulu. Vula kwi-TakeoffCraft nge-**Vula ukubalwa**, noma uyilahle lapho: ziba yizinto ezimbili ongazinikeza intengo (ezinye ([Ukusika nokugcwalisa okuvela ku-CivilCraft](app:takeoffcraft/earthwork#cut-and-fill-from-civilcraft))). Thumela futhi ngemva kokushintsha bese uvula ifayela elisha: izinto ezifanayo zithola izinani elisha.

Kuthunyelwa izinani eliphelele kuphela, ukuze kungabalwa lutho kabili; inothi lefayela libala ukusika nokugcwalisa kwe-pad nekhoridoro ngayinye.

## Indlela ezibalwa ngayo

I-CivilCraft isebenzisa **indlela yegridi**:

1. Igridi yamaseli ayisikwele ibekwa phezu kwendawo ngaphakathi kwemigqa yemini ngayinye.
2. Esikhungweni sanoma yiliphi iseli, zombili izingaphezulu ziyasampulwa.
3. Iseli ngalinye libalwa njengeprizimu: umehluko wokuphakama ophindaphindwe ngendawo yeseli. Lapho ingaphezulu elihlongozwayo lisephansi, kuyasikwa; liphakeme, kuyagcwaliswa.
4. Amaseli ayengezwa.

Usayizi weseli ukhethelwa wena ukuze indawo ye-rectangle eduze namazinga aphakathi kwamaseli angu-250,000, ilungiswe ibe inani elihle (1, 2 noma 5 amandla ayi-10). Awukakwazi ukukhetha wena. Umugqa wendlela ukutshela usayizi kanye nenani lamaseli. Amaseli amancane alungile kakhudlwana emaphethelweni; kumaseli aphelele phakathi kwezindiza eziyisicaba umphumela uqinile.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Kubalwa kuphela indawo ngaphakathi kwemigqa yemini. Umhlaba ngaphandle kwayo awushintshi.
- I-pad noma ikhoridoro enenkinga ayilinganiswa ngaphakathi futhi aliengezi lutho. Uma ibhulokhi isithi "Azikho izivolumu okwamanje: i-pad idinga ukulinganiswa ngaphandle kwezinkinga.", bheka ibhokisi elibomvu elingaphezulu kwayo.
- Lapho izinto zihlangana, eyamuva ishintshe eyangaphambili ngaphakathi kwemigqa yayo yemini, futhi ukuhlangana kubalwa kanye.
- Akukho inani lokuvuvuka noma ukuncipha elisetshenzisiwe. Phindaphinda imiphumela ngokwakho uma udinga amavolumu ebhange, ahlakazekile noma aqiniswe.
- Ukukhishwa kwesoyili yokuqala, ukujula kwendlela yokuhamba ngezinyawo nomgqa wangaphansi, namavolumu e-TIN-to-TIN prism akukatholakali okwamanje.

## Ukuhlola umphumela

- Vula **{{ui:Okuhlongozwayo (okuqhutshiwe)}}** ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubonisa** bese ubheka amakhonthuwa ngaphakathi kwemigqa yemini.
- Hambisa umkhomba phezu kwendawo elinganisiwe: i-status bar ibonisa **Umhlaba** no-**Okulinganisiwe**. Umehluko uwujulile kokusika noma kokugcwalisa kulelo sendaweni.
- Ekhoridoreni, ifestile yephrofayili ibonisa ukusika noma ukugcwalisa emugqeni ophakathi kusiteshi ngasinye.
