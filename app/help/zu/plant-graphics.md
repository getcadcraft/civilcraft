## Izimpawu zeplani ngesikali

Ummango › {{ui:More}} › {{ui:Plant graphics}} usetha ukuthi izitshalo zidwetshwa kanjani eplanini. I-**Okuzenzakalelayo, ngesikali** ikhetha elinye kwezinga lamathathu kusukela kusikali sokudweba:

- **Isiyingi esilula** (esinobubanzi kunga-1:500): isiyingi esisabalele kwesitshalo.
- **Umphetho nesikhungo** (kusukela nga-1:500 kuya ku-1:200): isiyingi nomnqamulelo omncane wesikhungo.
- **Upawu oneminingi** (oluhle kunga-1:200): upawu wesitshalo wirekhodi uma unawo, uma kungenjalo upawu ohlanganisiwe.

Shintsha izikali ezimbili nge-**Ilula kusukela ku-1:** ne-**Umphetho kusukela ku-1:**, noma khetha izinga elilodwa lazo zonke izikali. Ingxoxo yamashidi ngayinye enesethi yayo ye-**Ukubonakala kwesitshalo eplanini**, ngakho iplani yesayithi ka-1:500 neplani yokutshala ka-1:100 kungahluka. Izilungiselelo zigcinwa kanye neprojekthi.

## Upawu wakho

Khetha isitshalo esigabeni se-Izitshalo bese ucindezela i-{{ui:Own symbol…}}. Ifunda i-SVG, noma ibhulokhi yomdwebo we-DXF noma we-DWG (uma umdwebo unamabhulokhi amaningi, uyakhetha eyodwa). Upawu uyalingana nesisabalalo sesitshalo futhi udwetshwa ezingeni elineminingi. Ugcinwa kuphrojekthi, ngakho ifayela lihamba nayo.

- I-SVG ifundwa ngemigqa yayo, imigqa exhunyiwe, izikhala ezine, izikwele, izindilinga, ama-ellipse kanye nezindlela. Izinhlelo, izitayela, izixhumanisi nezithombe kuyo akuke kubhekwe. Ukuguqulwa akusetshenziswa: uyatshelwa lapho ifayela lineziguquli, ngakho zilinganise kuhlelo lwakho lokudweba kuqala.
- Imikhawulo: 512 KB ye-SVG kanye nezimo ezingu-2000. Ifayela elikhudlwana liyenqatshwa nesizathu.

## Isitshalo sakho se-3D

I-{{ui:Own 3D model…}} kulolu rekhodi olufanayo ifunda ifayela le-**OBJ**, **STL** noma **GLB**. Bese, ku-Plant graphics, setha i-**Izitshalo ku-3D** ibe ngu-**Imodeli yalo lonke irekhodi**: yonke isitshalo salolo rekhodi sikhombisa imodeli yakho, silinganiswe ngobude nangesisabalalo sayo. Amarekhodi angenayo imodeli yawo agcina izimo ezakhelwe ngaphakathi. Khetha i-**Izinkulu ezilula** ukuze ubone izindawo ezinkulu ngokushesha.

I-GLB ifundwa ngesimo sayo kuphela:

- Konke imodeli eyidingayo kufanele kube phakathi kwefayela le-.glb. Imodeli ekhomba kwamanye amafayela (i-.bin ehlukile, noma izithombe ngekheli) iyenqatshwa: akukho okulandwayo.
- Ijiyometri eshiyekile noma epakishiwe (i-Draco, i-meshopt, izindawo ezilinganisiwe) ayifundwa. Hlela ngaphandle kokucinywa.
- Izindwangu nemibala efakwe kwifayela azisetshenziswa: imodeli idwetshwa ngombala wesitshalo ngokwaso. Izindwangu zisahlolwa, futhi indwangu ephezu kwamapikseli angu-4096 ngakolunye uhlangothi iyenqatshwa.
- Imikhawulo: ifayela elingu-32 MB, izinxantathu ezingu-50 000 (kubalwa yonke ikhophi yengxenye ebekwe ngaphezu kowodwa), izithombe ezingu-64 kanye nezithombe ezingu-16 MB.

Yilelo nalelo khawulo lenqaba ifayela ngesizathu; akukho okufundwe ngesigamu kugcinwa. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa sinika irekhodi upawu noma imodeli yalo, futhi i-{{ui:Remove own symbol}} noma i-{{ui:Remove own 3D model}} iyakususa futhi.
