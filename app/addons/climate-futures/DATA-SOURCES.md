## The bundled pack is a SAMPLE

Three places (Denver, Miami, Johannesburg), **one model (MRI-ESM2-0)**, **five years** spread over each 20-year period, scenarios SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5, horizons 2050 (2041-2060) and 2080 (2071-2090). One model is not an ensemble and five years are not a climate: the figures are real model output but carry a lot of sampling noise. The pack says `"sample": true` and the panel says so in bold. **Not for design.**

## The full pack (for the owner to run)

`node tools/climate/build-pack.mjs --out pack.json` with the config's nine models and 20 years (about 9 × 5 × 20 × 7 = 6,300 small requests per place, ~8 s each on the server, cached and resumable in `tmp/climate-cache`). Add places to `tools/climate/pack-config.json` (a grid of cities, or the places your users work in). Then copy it to `tools/climate/packs/` and run `node tools/climate/sync.mjs --to bimcraft,civilcraft`.
