Reads: a walk file (.fieldwalk.json, format cadcraft-fieldwalk), GPX 1.0 and 1.1 (waypoints, routes and tracks), records as CSV, and a template pack (.json, format cadcraft-land-templates). Parcels and GIS layers are read from the drawing.

Writes: a walk as .fieldwalk.json, GPX, CSV and GeoJSON; the records, the land by designation, the check report and a proposal's schedule as CSV; a filled paper as a page to print (HTML) and as text (Markdown).

Records, walks and proposals are saved inside the CivilCraft project file.
