1. In CivilCraft, open Settings, then Add-ons, and switch on Land records and field walk.
2. Open the Land records tab on the right.
3. To sketch a plot: open the drawing on a phone, press Start a walk, and press Mark corner at each corner.
4. To check a parcel map: open Records, type or import what the register says, then run the check under Drawing against record.
5. To prepare an application: work out the change under Change parcels, keep it as a proposal, then fill a paper under Documents.
