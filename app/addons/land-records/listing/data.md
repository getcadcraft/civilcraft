The add-on ships one template pack of plain outlines (an application, a statement of parcel change, a draft amendment of designation, a schedule of parcels and a notice). They belong to no authority and say so on the page.

It ships no register data, no zoning rules and no lot sizes. Records are what you type or read from your own CSV file or GIS layer. Tolerances and lot rules are yours.

The device's position is read by the page only while the walk screen is open. Nothing is sent to CadCraft or anywhere else. A walk, the records and the proposals are kept in the drawing's own file. Holders' names are personal data: handle the file accordingly.

No place's rules are in this add-on. They come as side add-ons (Land rules: …), each made from a research file that records, for every figure, the document, the clause and the words read. The notes on the plan and the buildings are the drawing's own (its issues and building masses); nothing is copied out of the drawing.
