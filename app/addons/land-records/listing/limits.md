This add-on is a design aid, not an engineering approval, and not a survey.

A field walk is not a survey. A phone or a watch is rarely closer than a few meters and can be much worse under trees, in narrow streets and beside buildings. Do not set out, fence, buy, sell or file a boundary from a walk. Only a licensed surveyor and the land registry fix a boundary.

The accuracy shown is the device's own figure. What it means differs between phones, and it is not a guarantee.

The watch button works by showing the walk as "what is playing" on the phone. Whether a given watch passes its buttons to the phone's browser has not been tried by us on a real watch. The screen button and a Bluetooth clicker do not depend on it.

A walk on a drawing with no coordinate system is placed beside the drawing, north up, not where it really is.

The check compares the drawing with the records. It does not say which is right. It reports corners that nearly meet a neighbor; it does not measure the area of a gap.

Joining parcels needs a shared side. Parcels that meet at a corner as well as along a side, or that would enclose a gap, are refused. Cuts are straight lines.

The buildings counted on a lot are the drawing's building masses: a footprint, a number of floors and a floor height. Coverage and floor area ratio are plain quotients of those; your code's own definition of floor area or coverage may differ.

A footprint read from an IFC model is traced on a grid, so it is true to about the grid size along its edge (5 cm unless the model is very large; the size is shown). Ground a building encloses counts as footprint, so a courtyard closed on every side is counted too. Openings are not cut. Every storey in the model counts as a floor, a basement included. A model with no map position is placed by numbers you type.

The plan for marking up is a plain sheet: lot lines, building outlines, lot names and numbered note marks. Letters a standard PDF font lacks lose their marks (ğ prints as g). Coming back, a note is recognized by its words and its place on the sheet; a note that was moved far on the sheet comes back as a new note. A BCF topic with no place on the sheet is left out.

A place's rules come as a separate add-on and are a reading aid. Each figure was read in a published text on the date the pack states and may have changed since. Width is measured on the street line named in a lot's zoning and depth from that line; the place's own definition governs. What cannot be compared is said, not passed. A worksheet is not the authority's form.

The papers are outlines. They are not legal documents and not any authority's form. Use your authority's own form and procedure.
