It is a design aid, not an approval, and the numbers are not a certificate: the registered professional decides what the ground, the contours and the volumes are worth. It has been tested on a made-up 300 m site and on made-up files only; no real drone survey has been tried yet, so the settings are tuned on that and may need changing for your site. It does not draw point-by-point selections in 3D exactly (an outline in 3D is a vertical prism; use the plan or the top view when it has to be exact). It does not read E57, PLY, PCD, PTX or XYZ, or several files of one flight as one cloud, reproject a cloud, write LAZ point formats 1 and 3 or 6 to 10 (the LAZ it writes is format 0, or 2 with colour, classes 0 to 31), or read Autodesk's RCP and RCS files. It does not make surfaces from photographs, colour a cloud from photos, or find poles, kerbs and pipes. A browser without folder access keeps clouds in its own storage, whose size the browser limits.
