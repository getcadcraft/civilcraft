Made from research done on 2026-11-21, kept in the source as tools/landrecords/research/us-ca-la.json and us-ca-la-code.json: for every figure the document, the section and the words read. No figure was typed from memory.

The Municipal Code's own site refuses automated reading. Chapter 1 (Sections 12.03 to 12.12, 12.21 and 17.05) was therefore read in Internet Archive copies of the official code pages, captured between August 2024 and November 2026; each source states its capture date, and an amendment made after a capture would not show. Chapter 1A was read on the city's own zoning site. All 44 figures first read in City Planning's summary (CP-7150) matched the code and are now carried from the code.

Not read: Sections 17.50 to 17.60, the small lot exceptions of 12.22 C, overlays and specific plans, fees. The code prints no minimum lot depth in any section read. One figure of the Chapter 1A hillside table is misprinted in the source and was left out.
