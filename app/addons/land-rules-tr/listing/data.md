Article 6 applies where the plan itself says nothing: the plan and its notes come first. Nine figures are printed as a yard distance plus a figure; the yard distances are the plan's and are not in this pack, so those figures are only a floor. One of them (block ends, up to four storeys) is printed without the plus sign and is read here like its neighbours.

The sizes outside planned areas were read on the same day in the Unplanned Areas Zoning Regulation (Plansız Alanlar İmar Yönetmeliği) and Law 5403. The agricultural figures are the law's floors: the Ministry may set larger ones, and the district-by-district table of the law's annex was not transcribed. A provincial council may set other sizes for a village.
