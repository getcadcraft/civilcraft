The rules of one place for the Land records add-on: the smallest parcel frontage and depth the Planned Areas Zoning Regulation (Planlı Alanlar İmar Yönetmeliği, Article 6) prints for subdivision where the plan says nothing, and the figures the Planning Law (no. 3194) and its regulations state about subdivision (ifraz), consolidation (tevhit), land readjustment under Article 18 and plan amendments.

Switch it on, choose it under Place rules in the Land records panel, and your lots and proposals are compared with the printed figures.
