1. Switch the add-on on in Settings, Add-ons, then open the Weather files tab.
2. If the model has a site with a latitude and longitude, the nearest stations are listed. If not, type the latitude and longitude.
3. Pick a station and read its design temperatures and degree-days.
4. In BimCraft, press Use in Heating and cooling loads, then open the Daylight tab and press Work out the loads.
5. Press Explore the year to see the station's hours as a 3D surface and other charts.
6. In BimCraft, for a year's run in the EnergyPlus check, press Use the year in the EnergyPlus check.
