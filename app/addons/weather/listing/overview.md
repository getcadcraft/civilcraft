Weather files gives a model the weather of the place it stands. It reads the model's latitude and longitude, lists the nearest weather stations, and shows each one's winter and summer design temperatures and its heating and cooling degree-days. In BimCraft, one press puts those figures into Heating and cooling loads, so the outside temperatures and degree-days are a station's, not something typed from memory. Another press fetches the station's hourly year, an EnergyPlus weather file (.epw), and hands it to the EnergyPlus check or saves it to your computer.

Explore the year opens the station's hours as a 3D surface, a flat map, profiles, a wind rose, a histogram and a monthly table, for every quantity the weather file carries.

In CivilCraft you type the latitude and longitude and use the stations, the year viewer and the .epw file; the hand-offs to Heating and cooling loads and the EnergyPlus check are BimCraft's.

The weather is data, not part of the app. The add-on holds a small list of stations with their figures; a station's year (about 1.5 MB) is fetched only when you ask for the hours. You can also open a weather file of your own and get the same figures from it.
