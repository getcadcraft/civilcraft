It is a design aid, not an engineering approval (a planning aid): the results are not a certificate, and an engineer decides what they are worth. It models low-speed geometry only: no dynamics, braking, speed limits, driver behaviour, or tyre scrub. Every shipped vehicle figure is a placeholder marked unverified; check them against the vehicle or table you design to. It has been tested against closed-form geometry and made-up sites, not on a real road, airport or tram design, and it has not been compared with any other program's output. Clearance is checked in plan only, with no height or vertical clearance. A roundabout also needs sight distance and entry deflection checks, which this does not do; the movements are smoothed paths, not a driver's line. Aircraft are checked on the ground at low speed, with the wing, tail and engine points you give; rail follows the track line rigidly and checks the body ends only, not a vehicle gauge against structures. Parking checks one hardest bay, with a smoothed turn from the aisle. It does not read other CAD formats for vehicles or import swept path files from other programs.
