Swept path shows where a vehicle goes and what it sweeps. Pick a road vehicle, an aircraft or a light rail car, give it a path from an alignment or a line in your CivilCraft project, and see the ground its body covers, the tracks of its wheels, wing tips or bogies, and every place it comes within a margin of a kerb, a building or any other line. Conflicts go into the Problems panel, and the result is drawn into the project as ordinary lines (one undo step) and saved as a CSV report.

It also draws a roundabout from a circle, a lane and its legs and runs every entry and exit of a chosen vehicle through it, and lays out parking bays inside a closed line, counts them and checks how a vehicle pulls into the hardest bay with its neighbours occupied. It uses CivilCraft's own swept path solver for road vehicles, so a road run here is the same number you get from the Swept path button. Your project stays on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere. It is a planning aid: the engineer decides.
