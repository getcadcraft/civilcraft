The hydraulics are CivilCraft's Water mains solver (Hazen-Williams head loss and the global gradient method). Fire flow is a bisection on the draw at a hydrant until the lowest pressure that matters reaches the residual. An outage closes the pipe, or every pipe of a valved section, and solves again. Water quality walks plug-flow segments through each pipe in small time steps, mixes completely at junctions and in tanks, and uses first-order decay with a bulk rate and a wall rate (2 times the wall coefficient over the diameter). Demands go to the nearest junction, or are shared by inverse distance. All of it is our own code from published methods. From 0.11: control rules and a stepped run cut the step to the moment a tank meets a level rule; a flow-control valve holds its flow, a pressure-sustaining valve is found by bisection on the flow that holds its upstream pressure; pump energy is flow times head over efficiency, integrated over the steps; pressure-dependent demand uses the Wagner curve and is solved by an accelerated fixed point around the same steady solver; calibration is damped least squares (Levenberg-Marquardt) on the logarithm of each roughness; sizing and rehabilitation are greedy marginal analysis (the change that helps most per dollar, then take back what was not needed). All of it is our own code from published methods.
