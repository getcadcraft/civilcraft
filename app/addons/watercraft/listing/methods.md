The hydraulics are CivilCraft's Water mains solver (Hazen-Williams head loss and the global gradient method). Fire flow is a bisection on the draw at a hydrant until the lowest pressure that matters reaches the residual. An outage closes the pipe, or every pipe of a valved section, and solves again. Water quality walks plug-flow segments through each pipe in small time steps, mixes completely at junctions and in tanks, and uses first-order decay with a bulk rate and a wall rate (2 times the wall coefficient over the diameter). Demands go to the nearest junction, or are shared by inverse distance. All of it is our own code from published methods.
