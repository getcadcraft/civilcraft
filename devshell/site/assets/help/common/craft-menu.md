The buttons that belong to the site rather than to your drawing sit in one menu. Click the app mark and name at the very top left, or press {{key:Alt+O}}.

## What the menu holds

- {{ui:Settings}} ({{key:Mod+,}}), {{ui:Full screen}}, {{ui:Help}} ({{key:F1}}) and {{ui:About}}.
- {{ui:Save for offline}}, when the app can be kept on this computer.
- {{ui:Present}}: hides the interface so only the drawing shows. See [Present a drawing](common:present).
- {{ui:Visit GetCadCraft.com}}.
- A row of two icons for the theme: the sun is the light theme, the moon is the dark theme.
- {{ui:Account}} with {{ui:Sign out}} under it, or {{ui:Sign in}} when you are not signed in.

## Pin what you use often

Each option has a small dot at its right. Click the dot to pin that option as a button in the top row, and click it again to unpin it. Up to six options can be pinned. A number on a pinned button, or on the app mark, is something waiting for you.

## Add-ons and Find

{{ui:Add-ons}} in the top row lists the add-ons and panels; a tick means one is open, and picking it opens or closes it. At the right of the top row, the search box finds commands and help topics.

## Good to know

- The menu is part of the Drafting layout ({{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Drafting}}). The other layouts keep these buttons in the app bar. See [The Drafting layout](common:layout-drafting).
- This is in the lab (the version for testing) and is not released yet.
