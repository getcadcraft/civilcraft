The apps contain no AI. The Ask box sends a question to the assistant you connected, which answers by using the app's own tools. This page covers what is around that box. It is in the lab builds.

## Open it from Help

1. Press {{key:F1}} or choose {{ui:Help}}.
2. Choose {{ui:Ask the assistant}} at the top of the Help panel. The Ask box opens.
3. Type your question and choose {{ui:Ask}}.

With a question goes a short note of where you are: the file's name, how much is selected, the screen layout, the tool in use and the project stage. It never holds text from the file.

## Why did this happen?

1. In the Ask box choose {{ui:Why did this happen?}}.
2. Read the list. It is the last steps in this window, oldest first: the commands you started, each change to the model by its name in the undo list, steps you took back or put back, and what an assistant did or was refused.
3. With an assistant connected, choose {{ui:Ask the assistant to explain}} and it explains from those same steps.

The list works with no assistant connected. It is a record of steps, not a judgement.

## Report a problem with the steps

1. In the Ask box, type what went wrong and choose {{ui:Report a problem}}. The app's own About box opens with your words in it.
2. Leave {{ui:Add the steps I took before it happened}} ticked. Open {{ui:See the steps}} to read them first.
3. Only if you want to, tick the model excerpt. It holds what the app counts and how much is selected: no names, no emails, no folders and never the file itself. Open {{ui:See the excerpt}} to read it first.
4. Add your name and email and choose {{ui:Send report}}. The report goes to our Submit a problem page, and the box gives you the link to follow it.

An assistant can write the draft for you. It can only put the draft in this box: sending is always yours.

## What an assistant is sent

Assistants get model data only. People's names (author, drawn by, last saved by), contact details, account and licence details and your user name in a folder path are held back before anything leaves the app.

1. Choose {{ui:Activity}} in the bar at the top.
2. Set the list to {{ui:Everything}}.
3. Under each line read **Sent back**: the size of the answer and the names of its fields. **Held back** lists each value that was kept, with the reason.

The log never keeps the answer itself.

## Project stage

1. Choose {{ui:Drawing details}} in the bar at the top.
2. Pick the {{ui:Project stage}}: Brief, Concept, Design development, Submission, Working drawings, Tender or Close-out.
3. Choose {{ui:Save details}}. The stage is kept in the file with the other details.

An assistant reads the stage so its help fits where the project is.

## Show me how, with no assistant

The teacher works from the app's own Help. It needs no assistant and sends nothing anywhere.

1. In the Ask box type what you want to do, for example "how do I draw a wall".
2. Choose {{ui:Show me how}}. The Help topics that answer it are listed. The tool you are using and the project stage move the best fit to the top.
3. Choose a topic. A step card opens with the topic's first step, and a ring marks the real button when the layout you use has one.
4. Do the step, then choose {{ui:Next}}. {{ui:Back}} goes to the step before. Nothing is pressed for you.

A topic with no numbered steps says so: open it in Help instead.

## Rules every assistant follows

- **Optional.** Everything works without an assistant and offline.
- **The app calculates.** An assistant explains, arranges and checks, and never writes a figure. Under each answer the box says whether every figure in it is in a result the app returned, and lists the ones it cannot find. A figure on that list is not to be trusted until the app has calculated it.
- **Trust but verify.** What an assistant makes is marked unverified until you check it. Estimates and audits an assistant hands back say "Not signed off by a person" until you sign them off. Open the list under {{ui:Rules every assistant follows}} in the Ask box.
- **Model data only.** Your personal and account details are never sent.

## Sign off an estimate or an audit

1. Choose {{ui:Drawing details}} in the bar at the top.
2. Under {{ui:Sign-off}}, check your name, add a note if you like and choose {{ui:Sign off}} beside {{ui:Estimate}} or {{ui:Audit}}.
3. Who signed and when are kept in the file with the other details. If the drawing is saved after you sign, the panel says so: sign off again.

Only you can sign off. No assistant can do it, or take your sign-off back. {{ui:Take my sign-off back}} removes it.

## Step by step on your screen

An assistant, or a lesson, can show a task one step at a time. A small card says what to do, and a ring marks the real button on your screen. Nothing is pressed for you. Close the card with its {{ui:Close the step}} button.

:::note
An assistant answers better when it knows this version of the app. The knowledge pack holds the manuals, the step-by-step help, every command and the skills as plain text, with a version that matches the app. The Ask box says so when a connected pack is for another version.
:::
