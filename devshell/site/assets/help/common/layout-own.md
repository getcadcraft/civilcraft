Besides the three built-in layouts you can arrange one of your own, and save your whole setup under a name so you can switch back to it in one step.

## Arrange your own layout

1. Open {{ui:Settings}} (the gear, or {{key:Mod+,}}), choose {{ui:Global}}, then {{ui:Layout}}.
2. Choose {{ui:Arrange your own layout…}}. The dialog starts from the layout you use now.
3. Give it a name, then choose:
   - **Over the drawing:** the app's toolbar, a ribbon or neither; a title bar; an info box and which controls it holds.
   - **Beside the drawing:** the toolbox on the left, on the right or not at all, with or without {{ui:My tools}}; which panel holds the palettes, which one is on top (the project browser or Properties, for example), and which are tabs under it.
   - **The drawing area:** view tabs, a quick bar and a view bar with the controls you tick, the edit palette, a status bar.
   - **Look:** size, button outlines and tab style.
4. Choose {{ui:Use this layout}}. The app switches to it at once, and every app that has layouts uses it too.

Only what this app has is offered. Your layout is listed under {{ui:From add-ons and files}}; {{ui:Export this layout}} saves it as a file you can give a colleague, and the remove button takes it away. The panels it fills still dock, float and move to their own windows, as in [Arrange the panels](common:arrange-panels).

## Save your setup as a work environment

A work environment is your whole setup for every app in this browser: the layout, the menu style and your keys, the toolbar style, the panels and their sizes, and {{ui:My tools}}. The theme and the language are not part of it.

- **Save:** type a name under {{ui:Work environments}} and choose {{ui:Save my setup}}. Saving under a name that exists replaces that setup.
- **Switch:** pick a setup in the list and choose {{ui:Switch to it}}. The app reloads in that setup; if you have unsaved work, the app asks first, as it does on any reload.
- **Share:** {{ui:Export}} saves the picked setup as a `.ccenv.json` file; {{ui:Import a setup…}} adds one from a file. A file only ever sets these choices, nothing else.
- **Remove:** pick a setup and choose {{ui:Remove}}.

You can keep up to twelve setups.

To try the built-in arrangements first, see [the toolbox and info box layout](common:layout-toolbox) and [the ribbon and project browser layout](common:layout-ribbon).
