## What an add-on is

An add-on adds features (a new tool or panel) or data (regulation packs, materials, trade packs) to an app. You add it once in the {{ui:Library}} and it is on your account list, so it follows you to every edition: the browser, Studio, the portable build and the apps on a tablet.

## Your list

Open {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Add-ons}} › {{ui:Your add-ons}}.

- **Sign in** connects this app to your account. Signed out, the app keeps a list for this device only, and offers to merge it into your account when you sign in. In Studio the sign-in shows a short code to approve in your browser.
- **Remove** takes an add-on off the list. It is refused while another add-on you keep needs it, and the screen names that add-on. When you remove something, anything that was brought in only for it is taken off too, and the screen says which.
- **Check for updates** lists every change together. Updates inside the same major version arrive by themselves. A new major version, a changed permission, a pinned version or a lab version always asks you first, and an update is all or nothing: if one file fails its check, nothing changes.
- **Keep a version** pins it. **Follow newest** unpins it. **Try lab versions** moves one add-on to the lab channel.
- **Roll back** returns to the version you had.

## Needs

An add-on can need other add-ons or a shared engine. Add brings them along and tells you what it also adds, how big it is and which permissions it asks for. Engines are shared: one copy serves everything that needs it, and a copy nothing needs is freed after 30 days.

## Files that use add-ons

A drawing remembers the add-ons whose data it holds. Open it on a computer that does not have them and the app says "This file uses ..." and offers to add them. Until you do, the data stays in the file untouched.

## Offline

{{ui:Download everything for offline}} fetches every add-on on your list. Once fetched, Studio and the portable build work with no connection. Each download is checked against its checksum and its signature before it is used.

## iPad and iPhone

Add-ons that are only data download as usual. Code add-ons made by CadCraft are already inside the app and are switched on from your list. Code add-ons from outside makers are not offered on the iPad and iPhone apps; the browser app (Add to Home Screen) has them.
