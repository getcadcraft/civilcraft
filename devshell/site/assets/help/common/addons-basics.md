## What an add-on is

An add-on adds features (a new tool or panel) or data (regulation packs, materials, trade packs) to an app. You add it once in the {{ui:Library}} and it is on your account list, so it follows you to every edition: the browser, Studio, the portable build and the apps on a tablet.

## Your list

Open {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Add-ons}} › {{ui:Your add-ons}}.

- **Sign in** connects this app to your account. Signed out, the app keeps a list for this device only, and offers to merge it into your account when you sign in. In Studio the sign-in shows a short code to approve in your browser.
- **Remove** takes an add-on off the list. It is refused while another add-on you keep needs it, and the screen names that add-on. When you remove something, anything that was brought in only for it is taken off too, and the screen says which.
- **Check for updates** lists every change together. Updates inside the same major version arrive by themselves. A new major version, a changed permission, a pinned version or a lab version always asks you first, and an update is all or nothing: if one file fails its check, nothing changes.
- **Keep a version** pins it. **Follow newest** unpins it. **Try lab versions** moves one add-on to the lab channel.
- **Roll back** returns to the version you had.

## Needs

An add-on can need other add-ons or a shared engine. Add brings them along and tells you what it also adds, how big it is and which permissions it asks for. Engines are shared: one copy serves everything that needs it, and a copy nothing needs is freed after 30 days.

## Files that use add-ons

A drawing remembers the add-ons whose data it holds. Open it on a computer that does not have them and the app says "This file uses ..." and offers to add them. Until you do, the data stays in the file untouched.

## Skill packs and agents

The add-on directory at {{ui:Add-ons}} on getcadcraft.com also lists skill packs and agents for your own AI assistant. They are text only: a skill pack is instructions your assistant reads, an agent is a listing that describes a program someone wrote that uses the apps' tools. We host, run and vouch for none of an outside maker's code, and they have no price here. Open them with the {{ui:Skill packs}} and {{ui:Agents}} filters.

To offer one, a verified member or lab tester uses {{ui:Send a skill pack}} or {{ui:Send an agent listing}} in the directory once submissions are open. A person on our team reads every submission before it is listed, and the same review decides it as for any add-on.

## Offline

{{ui:Download everything for offline}} fetches every add-on on your list. Once fetched, Studio and the portable build work with no connection. Each download is checked against its checksum and its signature before it is used.

In a browser, the website does the same for your whole list in one go: open **Your add-ons** on getcadcraft.com and choose **Make my add-ons available offline** under **Offline on this device**, in the browser you will take with you. The check below it says how many are ready, how much is stored and when, and names anything missing or out of date. Add-ons that come inside an app are ready once that app is saved for offline ({{ui:Save for offline}} in the app bar). Browsers can clear stored files when space runs low, so look at that check before you go.

With no account, the Library keeps a list for this browser ("Your add-ons in this browser") with the same button; **Use offline** on an add-on's page adds it there and fetches it at once. Free add-ons only. Sign up and the list moves onto your account.

## Add from a file

On an add-on's page, **Download as a file** saves its signed package (a `.ccaddon.json` file). In the app, {{ui:Add from a file}} under {{ui:Your add-ons}} takes that file with no connection needed, or drop the file onto the app's window. The app checks the signature first and refuses a file that was changed, is not signed by getcadcraft.com, or is made for another app. Reload the app to use it.

## Paid add-ons offline

A paid add-on keeps working offline through its licence on the device: a signed entitlement that lasts up to 30 days from the last time the app (or the Your add-ons page) was online, and never past the end of what you bought. Going online again renews it. A paid package file only installs on a device that holds a licence for it.

## iPad and iPhone

Add-ons that are only data download as usual. Code add-ons made by CadCraft are already inside the app and are switched on from your list. Code add-ons from outside makers are not offered on the iPad and iPhone apps; the browser app (Add to Home Screen) has them.
