**Iribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi** yenye yezakhiwo ezintathu zendawo yokusebenza zohlelo. Iribhoni yamathebhu iyagijima phezulu, i-Properties nesiphequluli sephrojekthi zabelana ngephaneli elingakwesobunxele, wonke umbono ovulekile uthola ithebhu phezu komdwebo, futhi ibha yesimo ihlala ngaphansi. Amathuluzi ngawohlelo lwawo: konke okungenziwa esakhiweni sakudala kusebenza nalapha, futhi umdwebo wakho, isitayela semenyu nokhiye bakho bahlala njengoba bunjalo.

## Khetha yona

1. Vula {{ui:Settings}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Khetha ithebhu {{ui:Global}}, bese uya ku-{{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}}.
3. Khetha Iribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi.

Isakhiwo siyashintsha ngokushesha. Ukukhetha kuyodwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo kulesi siphequluli: ngakho ngalesi sikhathi izinhlelo zonke ziyavula kuso, futhi sikhona ngaphambi kokuthi ikhasi lidwetshwe, ngakho awuyikubona isakhiwo sakudala kuqala.

## Okuphi

- **Ibha yesihloko:** igama lohlelo, izinkinobho zokufinyelela okusheshayo (vula, londoloza, hlehlisa, phinda wenze), igama lefayela elivuliwe, {{ui:Okwenziwa yithuluzi ngalapha}} (uhlu lwawo wonke amathuluzi nebhokisi lokusesha, luchaza ukuthi yiliphi elilungele manje nokuthi kungani amanye engalungile), isikrini esigcwele, {{ui:Usizo}} kanye {{ui:Settings}}. Kulesi sakhiwo ibha yesihloko ithatha indawo yebha yohlelo.
- **Iribhoni:** amathuluzi ohlelo emathebhini, ithebhu ngalinye linamaqembu. {{ui:Ifayela}} ngakwesobunxele ivula, ilondoloza nokunye. Ezinye izinhlelo zengeza ithebhu yazo lapho usebenza kumodi noma endaweni yokusebenza ethile; ikhanya ngenkathi isebenza futhi inyamalala emva kwalokho.
- **Ibha yezinketho:** ngaphansi kweribhoni, okudingwa yithuluzi elisebenzayo kuwe, noma okukhethiwe.
- **Iphaneli elingakwesobunxele:** {{ui:Properties}} phezulu, nangaphansi kwalo ama-palette (isiphequluli sephrojekthi nokuqukethwe kwephaneli ngokwalo) njengamathebhu. Hudula umugqa phakathi kwawo ukuze wabelane ngendawo.
- **Amathebhu okubuka:** ithebhu eyodwa ngombono ngamunye ovuliwe (ishidi, ipulani, isigcawu…). Vala elilodwa ngenkinobho yalo yokuvala; ifayela lihlala livuliwe.
- **Ibha yokubuka nebha yesimo:** ngaphansi komdwebo, izilawuli zombono ngokwawo (isikali, ukusondeza); ngaphansi, imilayezo yohlelo.

Ithuluzi elingasebenzi manje liba mnyama, nethuluzi lalo lisho ukuthi kungani (njengesibonelo "Vula ifayela kuqala", noma ukuthi uhlelo alinalelo thuluzi okwamanje). Chofoza kulo, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} kulo, ukuze ufunde isizathu ebhokisini.

## Sebenzisa iribhoni ngekhibhodi

Uma ithebhu yeribhoni igxilile, {{key:Left}} kanye {{key:Right}} zihambisa phakathi kwamathebhu, futhi {{key:Home}} kanye {{key:End}} ziya kokuqala nokokugcina. {{key:Esc}} iqeda ithuluzi elisebenzayo kuqala, njengakwisakhiwo sakudala, nazo zonke izinqamulelo kanye {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (izimodi zesikrini) zisebenza njengakuqala.

## Goqa iribhoni noma uyinike iwindi layo

- Chofoza kabili ithebhu yeribhoni ukuze ugoqe iribhoni ibe ngamathebhu alo; ukuchofoza ithebhu bese kubonisa amathuluzi alo kuze kube usuhamba. Chofoza kabili futhi ukuze uyigcine ivulekile. Inkinobho {{ui:Hambisa iribhoni}} ekugcineni kwesokudla seribhoni nayo inezinketho {{ui:Goqa iribhoni}} kanye {{ui:Gcina iribhoni ivulekile}}. Uhlelo ngalunye liyakukhumbula.
- {{ui:Hambisa iribhoni}} › {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} ibeka iribhoni kwiwindi layo, njengesibonelo esikrinini sesibili. Okhiye acindezelwa lapho bafinyelela kuhlelo. {{ui:Buyisela iribhoni}}, noma ukuvala leli windi, iyibuyisela ekhaya.

Izilungiselelo webha yamathuluzi zisebenza eribhoni: {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Ithuluzi}} › {{ui:Izithonjana namagama}}, {{ui:Izithonjana kuphela}} (iribhoni ngalokho ibonisa imigqa emi-2 yezithonjana ezincane ngeqembu ngalinye) noma {{ui:Amagama kuphela}}, kanye {{ui:Bonisa amagama amaqembu (Ifayela, Dweba, …)}}.

## Amaphaneli akho asalele wena

Amaphaneli angakwesobunxele nangakwesokudla agcina konke okuchazwe ku-[Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels): ukufaka, ukuntanta, ukubopha, ukufihla, amathebhu, {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} kanye {{ui:Setha kabusha isakhiwo}}.

## Buyela esakhiweni sakudala

Vula {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}} bese ukhetha {{ui:I-CadCraft yasendulo}}. Yonke ibha yamathuluzi, ithebhu nephaneli kubuyela lapho kwakukhona khona.

:::note
Efonini, noma kwiwindi elingu-760 pixels ububanzi noma elincane, uhlelo lusebenzisa isakhiwo sefoni noma yini oyikhethile. Iwindi elibanzi lisibuyisela isakhiwo sakho.
:::

Ukuze uzame enye indlela yokuhlela, bona [isakhiwo sebhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi](common:layout-toolbox).
