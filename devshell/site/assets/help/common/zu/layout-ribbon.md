**Iribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi** ngomunye wezakhiwo ezintathu zendawo yokusebenza yohlelo. Iribhoni yamathebhu igijima phezulu, i-Properties nesiphequluli sephrojekthi zabelana ngephaneli lesobunxele, yonke ivibha evulwayo ithola ithebhu phezu komdwebo, futhi ibha yesimo ihlala ngaphansi. Amathuluzi ngawohlelo lwawo: konke ongakwenza esakhiweni sakudala kusebenza nalapha, futhi umdwebo wakho, isitayela semenyu nezinkinobho zakho ziyahlala njengoba zinjalo.

## Yikhethe

1. Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Khetha ithebhu ye-{{ui:Jikelele}}, bese uya ku-{{ui:Isakhiwo}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Iribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi}}.

Isakhiwo sishintsha ngokushesha. Ukukhetha kuyodwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo kulo isiphequluli: ngayinye ivula kuso kusukela lapho, futhi sisendaweni ngaphambi kokuthi ikhasi lidwebe, ngakho awuwaboni ngaso isakhiwo sakudala.

## Okukuphi

- **Ibha yesihloko:** igama lohlelo, izinkinobho zokufinyelela ngokushesha (vula, londoloza, hlehlisa, phinda wenze), igama lefayela elivuliwe, {{ui:Okwenziwa yithuluzi ngalapha}} (uhlu lwawo wonke amathuluzi nebhokisi lokusesha, lukhombisa ukuthi yiliphi elilungele manje nokuthi kungani amanye engalungile), isikrini esigcwele, {{ui:Usizo}} kanye {{ui:Izilungiselelo}}. Kuleli sakhiwo ibha yesihloko ithatha indawo yebha yohlelo.
- **Iribhoni:** amathuluzi ohlelo emathebhwini, ngamaqembu ngaphakathi kwawo. {{ui:Ifayela}} ngakwesobunxele livula, lilondoloze, njalo njalo. Amanye amahlelo engeza ithebhu yawo lapho usebenza kuthi ethile imodi noma indawo yokusebenza; ikhanya lapho isebenza, bese iyanyamalala.
- **Ibha yezinketho:** ngaphansi kweribhoni, into ethuluzi elisebenzayo elidinga kuwe, noma okukhethiwe.
- **Iphaneli lesobunxele:** i-{{ui:Properties}} phezulu, nangaphansi kwayo amaphaleti (isiphequluli sephrojekthi nokuqukethwe kwephaneli ngalinye) njengamathebhu. Donsa umugqa ophakathi kwawo ukuze wabelane ngendawo.
- **Amathebhu okubuka:** ithebhu eyodwa ngombono ngamunye ovuliwe (ishidi, ipulani, indawo ebonakalayo…). Vala eyodwa ngenkinobho yayo yokuvala; ifayela lihlala livulekile.
- **Ibha yokubuka nebha yesimo:** ngaphansi komdwebo, izilawuli zombono wawo (isikali, isilinganiso sokuzoom); ngaphansi kakhulu, imilayezo yohlelo.

Ithuluzi elingasebenzi manje liyabonakala limnyama, nethiphu yalo ichaza ukuthi kungani (isibonelo "Vula ifayela kuqala", noma ukuthi uhlelo alikwazi okwamanje ukuthola leyo thuluzi). Chofoza kulo, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} kulo, ukuze ufunde isizathu ebhokisini.

## Sebenzisa iribhoni ngekhibhodi

Uma ithebhu yeribhoni igxilile, {{key:Left}} no-{{key:Right}} bahambisa phakathi kwamathebhu, no-{{key:Home}} no-{{key:End}} bafike owokuqala nowokugcina. {{key:Esc}} iqeda ithuluzi elisebenzayo kuqala, njengakusakhiwo sakudala, futhi zonke izinqamuleli kanye no-{{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (izimodi zesikrini) zisebenza njengakuqala.

## Goqa iribhoni noma uyinike iwindi layo

- Chofoza kabili ithebhu yeribhoni ukuze uyigoqe ibe amathebhu awo; ukuchofoza ithebhu bese kubonisa amathuluzi ayo kuze kube yilapho uhamba. Chofoza kabili futhi ukuze uyigcine ivulekile. Inkinobho ye-{{ui:Hambisa iribhoni}} ekugcineni kwesokudla kweribhoni nayo inezinketho ze-{{ui:Goqa iribhoni}} ne-{{ui:Gcina iribhoni ivulekile}}. Uhlelo ngalunye luyikhumbula.
- {{ui:Hambisa iribhoni}} › {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} ibeka iribhoni eWindini layo, isibonelo esikrinini sesibili. Izinkinobho ocindezela zona kuleyo ndawo ziyafinyelela kuhlelo. {{ui:Yibuyisele iribhoni}}, noma ukuvala leyo windi, iyibuyisela ekhaya.

Izilungiselelo zebha yamathuluzi zisebenza eribhoneni: {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Ibha yamathuluzi}} › {{ui:Izithonjana namagama}}, {{ui:Izithonjana kuphela}} (iribhoni bese ibonisa imigqa emibili yezithonjana ezincane ngeqembu ngalinye) noma {{ui:Amagama kuphela}}, kanye {{ui:Bonisa amagama amaqembu (Ifayela, Dweba, …)}}.

## Amaphaneli akho asahlala ngokwakho

Amaphaneli esobunxele nesokudla agcina konke okuchaziwe kuthi [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels): ukufaka, ukuntanta, ukubopha, ukufihla, amathebhu, {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} kanye {{ui:Setha kabusha isakhiwo}}.

## Buyela esakhiweni sakudala

Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Isakhiwo}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} bese ukhetha {{ui:CadCraft yasendulo}}. Yonke ibha yamathuluzi, ithebhu nephaneli kubuyela lapho kwakukhona khona.

:::note
Efonini, noma ewindini elingu-760 pixels noma elincane kunalokho, uhlelo lusebenzisa isakhiwo sefoni nxa kungakhethwa lutho. Iwindi elibanzi libuyisa isakhiwo sakho.
:::

Ukuze uzame enye indlela yokuhlela, bheka [isakhiwo sebhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi](common:layout-toolbox).

Ukuze ushintshe lokho esakhiwo esikubonisayo, bheka [Shintsha isakhiwo esikrinini](common:layout-edit); ukuze uthole ukuhlelwa okumnyama, okunebha yemenyu neribhoni, bheka [isakhiwo sakwa-Uhlaka](common:layout-drafting).
