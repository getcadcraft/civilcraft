**Ibhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi** yenye yezakhiwo ezintathu zendawo yokusebenza zohlelo. Amathuluzi omdwebo ami ngekholomu ngakwesobunxele, ibhokisi lolwazi phezulu libonisa okudingwa ithuluzi elisebenzayo noma okukhethiwe, wonke umbono unethebhu, futhi ibha esheshayo ngaphansi komdwebo ibamba izinketho zansuku zonke ezimbalwa. Amathuluzi ngawohlelo lwawo: konke okungenziwa esakhiweni sakudala kusebenza nalapha, futhi umdwebo wakho, isitayela semenyu nokhiye bakho bahlala njengoba bunjalo.

## Khetha yona

1. Vula {{ui:Settings}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Khetha ithebhu {{ui:Global}}, bese uya ku-{{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}}.
3. Khetha Ibhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi.

Isakhiwo siyashintsha ngokushesha. Ukukhetha kuyodwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo kulesi siphequluli: ngakho ngalesi sikhathi izinhlelo zonke ziyavula kuso, futhi sikhona ngaphambi kokuthi ikhasi lidwetshwe, ngakho awuyikubona isakhiwo sakudala kuqala.

## Okuphi

- **Ibha yamathuluzi:** ibha yamathuluzi yohlelo ihlala phezulu endaweni yomdwebo, namamenyu ayo.
- **Ibhokisi lolwazi:** ngaphansi kwebha yamathuluzi. Ithuluzi lisebenza libonisa ithuluzi nokudingayo. Uma ukhetha into libonisa izilungiselelo zayo eziyinhloko (kudonga: uhlobo lwayo, ukujula, ubude, umugqa wendawo nokuphakama), ungazishintsha lapho: ukushintsha lapho kufana nokushintsha ku-Properties, futhi {{key:Mod+Z}} iyakuhlehlisa. Uma kungekho okukhethiwe kuthi "Akukho okukhethiwe". Uhlelo ngalunye lungeza amabhokisi awo lapha, njengongqimba noma izinga lamanje, noma idokhumenti evuliwe.
- **Ibhokisi lamathuluzi:** ikholomu ngakwesobunxele, amathuluzi ohlelo emaqenjini. Chofoza igama leqembu ukuze uligoqe noma uliphinde livule. Ithuluzi elisebenzayo liyakhanya.
- **Amathuluzi ami:** iqembu lokuqala lebhokisi lamathuluzi, izikhala eziyisishiyagalombili ezingenalutho zamathuluzi osebenzisa kakhulu. Chofoza isikhala esingenalutho ukuze ukhethe noma yiliphi ithuluzi noma umyalo wohlelo; noma chofoza ngesokudla ithuluzi ebhokisini lamathuluzi bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza kumathuluzi ami}}; noma hudula ithuluzi libe esikhaleni. Chofoza ngesokudla isikhala ukuze usishintshe noma usiphenye. Izikhala zakho zigcinwa ngohlelo ngalunye kulesi siphequluli.
- **Umqondisi:** iphaneli elingakwesokudla. Isiphequluli sephrojekthi sohlelo siphezulu (amapulani, izigaba esinqunyiwe, ukubukeka kohlangothi, imibono ye-3D, uhlu, amashidi, njengoba uhlelo lunawo): chofoza owodwa ukuze uwuvule. Ngaphansi kwawo, {{ui:Properties}} nethebhu nokuqukethwe okunye kwephaneli elingakwesokudla.
- **Amathebhu okubuka:** ithebhu ngalo lonke umbono wefayela elivuliwe (ishidi, ipulani, isigcawu…), phezu komdwebo.
- **Ibha esheshayo:** ngaphansi komdwebo, izilungiselelo zombono nezinketho zansuku zonke ohlelo olunazo, njengesikali, izinga lemininingwane, ububanzi bombono, ukulinganisa, ipulani, ukuhlukanisa noma i-3D, izibambi nezinyunithi.
- **Ipaleti yokuhlela:** chofoza into emdwebweni bese kuvela ipaleti elincane eduze kwesikhombi nemiyalo yohlelo yokuhlela yalo, njengokuhambisa, ukukopisha, ukujikisa, ukubonisa ngasemuva, ukuphinda nokususa, kanye {{ui:Properties}}. {{key:Esc}}, ivili le-mouse, ukuchofoza okunye noma inkinobho yalo yokuvala ilifihla.

Ithuluzi elingasebenzi manje liba mnyama, nethuluzi lalo lisho ukuthi kungani. Chofoza kulo, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} kulo, ukuze ufunde isizathu ebhokisini.

{{key:Esc}} iqeda ithuluzi elisebenzayo kuqala, njengakwisakhiwo sakudala, nazo zonke izinqamulelo kanye {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (izimodi zesikrini) zisebenza njengakuqala.

## Nika ibhokisi lamathuluzi iwindi layo

Inkinobho {{ui:Hambisa ibhokisi lamathuluzi}} ephezulu kwebhokisi lamathuluzi inezinketho {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}}: ibhokisi lamathuluzi livula kwiwindi elincane lalo elingabekwa eceleni kohlelo noma esikrinini sesibili. Okhiye acindezelwa lapho bafinyelela kuhlelo. {{ui:Buyisela ibhokisi lamathuluzi}}, noma ukuvala leli windi, iyibuyisela ekhaya.

Izilungiselelo zebha yamathuluzi zisebenza nakubhokisi lamathuluzi: {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Ithuluzi}} › {{ui:Izithonjana namagama}}, {{ui:Izithonjana kuphela}} noma {{ui:Amagama kuphela}}, kanye {{ui:Bonisa amagama amaqembu (Ifayela, Dweba, …)}}.

## Amaphaneli akho asalele wena

Amaphaneli angakwesobunxele nangakwesokudla agcina konke okuchazwe ku-[Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels): ukufaka, ukuntanta, ukubopha, ukufihla, amathebhu, {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} kanye {{ui:Setha kabusha isakhiwo}}.

## Buyela esakhiweni sakudala

Vula {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}} bese ukhetha {{ui:I-CadCraft yasendulo}}. Yonke ibha yamathuluzi, ithebhu nephaneli kubuyela lapho kwakukhona khona.

:::note
Efonini, noma kwiwindi elingu-760 pixels ububanzi noma elincane, uhlelo lusebenzisa isakhiwo sefoni noma yini oyikhethile. Iwindi elibanzi lisibuyisela isakhiwo sakho.
:::

Ukuze uzame enye indlela yokuhlela, bona [isakhiwo seribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi](common:layout-ribbon).
