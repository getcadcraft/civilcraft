**Ibhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi** ngomunye wezakhiwo ezintathu zendawo yokusebenza yohlelo. Amathuluzi omdwebo ami ngekholomu ngakwesobunxele, ibhokisi lolwazi libonisa phezu kwesikrini into ethuluzi elisebenzayo elidinga yona noma eyikhethile, kube nethebhu ngombono ngamunye, futhi ibha esheshayo ngaphansi komdwebo ibamba izinketho ezimbalwa zansuku zonke. Amathuluzi ngawohlelo lwawo: konke ongakwenza esakhiweni sakudala kusebenza nalapha, futhi umdwebo wakho, isitayela semenyu nezinkinobho zakho ziyahlala njengoba zinjalo.

## Yikhethe

1. Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Khetha ithebhu ye-{{ui:Jikelele}}, bese uya ku-{{ui:Isakhiwo}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Ibhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi}}.

Isakhiwo sishintsha ngokushesha. Ukukhetha kuyodwa kuzo zonke izinhlelo kulo isiphequluli: ngayinye ivula kuso kusukela lapho, futhi sisendaweni ngaphambi kokuthi ikhasi lidwebe, ngakho awuwaboni ngaso isakhiwo sakudala.

## Okukuphi

- **Ibha yamathuluzi:** ibha yohlelo yayo ihlala phezulu kwendawo yomdwebo, nemenyu yayo.
- **Ibhokisi lolwazi:** ngaphansi kwebha yamathuluzi. Ithuluzi lisasebenza, libonisa ithuluzi nalokho olukudingayo. Lapho ukhetha into, libonisa izilungiselelo zayo eziyinhloko (ngodonga: uhlobo lwalo, ukuthi lujulile kangakanani, ubude, umugqa wendawo nobude), futhi ungazishintsha lapho: ukushintsha lapho kufana nalokho okukuphrophathi, bese {{key:Mod+Z}} kukubuyisela. Uma ungakukhethi lutho litshela ukuthi "Akukhethiwe lutho". Uhlelo ngalunye lwengeza amabhokisi awo lapha, njengesendlalelo noma inheli esebenzayo, noma incwadi evuliwe.
- **Ibhokisi lamathuluzi:** ikholomu ngakwesobunxele, amathuluzi ohlelo emaqenjini. Chofoza igama lweqembu ukuze uligoqe noma uliphinde uliveze. Ithuluzi elisebenzayo liyakhanya.
- **Amathuluzi ami:** iqembu lokuqala lebhokisi lamathuluzi, amasilothi amathathu ayishumi nesishiyagalombili azingile ngamathuluzi osebenzisa kakhulu. Chofoza isilothi esingenalutho ukuze ukhethe noma yiliphi ithuluzi noma umyalo wohlelo; noma chofoza ngakwesokudla ithuluzi ebhokisini lamathuluzi bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza kumathuluzi ami}}; noma donsa ithuluzi uliyise esilothini. Chofoza ngakwesokudla isilothi ukuze ulishintshe noma ulikhiphe. Amasilothi akho agcinwa ngohlelo ngalunye kulo isiphequluli.
- **Inavigeyitha:** iphaneli ngakwesokudla. Ishadi lephrojekthi lohlelo lisezansi (amapulani, amasigaba, izinkanyezi, imibono ye-3D, amashadi okuhlela, amashidi, njengoba uhlelo lunawo): chofoza olunye ukuze uvule. Ngaphansi kwalo, i-{{ui:Properties}} nethebhu nokuqukethwe okunye kwephaneli lesokudla.
- **Amathebhu okubuka:** ithebhu yombono ngamunye wefayela elivuliwe (ishidi, ipulani, isimo…), phezu komdwebo.
- **Ibha esheshayo:** ngaphansi komdwebo, izilungiselelo zombono nezinketho zansuku zonke ohlelo olinazo, njengesikali, izinga lemininingwane, uhla lombono, fit, plan, split noma 3D, izinamathela nezinyunithi.
- **Iphaleti lokuhlela:** chofoza into emdwebweni bese kuvela iphaleti elincane ngasesikhombisi nemiyalo yokuhlela yohlelo yayo, njengokuhambisa, ukukopisha, ukujikisa, ukufaka isibonakalo esigxilile, ukuphinda, noma ukususa, kanye ne-{{ui:Properties}}. {{key:Esc}}, inkinobho yevili le-mouse, ukuchofoza okunye noma inkinobho yokuvala kuyishiya.

Ithuluzi elingasebenzi manje liyabonakala limnyama, nethiphu yalo ichaza ukuthi kungani. Chofoza kulo, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} kulo, ukuze ufunde isizathu ebhokisini.

{{key:Esc}} iqeda ithuluzi elisebenzayo kuqala, njengakusakhiwo sakudala, futhi zonke izinqamuleli kanye no-{{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (izimodi zesikrini) zisebenza njengakuqala.

## Nika ibhokisi lamathuluzi iwindi lalo

Inkinobho ye-{{ui:Hambisa ibhokisi lamathuluzi}} phezulu kwebhokisi lamathuluzi inezinketho ze-{{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}}: ibhokisi lamathuluzi livula kwiwindi elincane lalo, lingabekwa eceleni kohlelo noma esikrinini sesibili. Izinkinobho ocindezela zona kulowo windi ziyafinyelela kuhlelo. {{ui:Yibuyisele ibhokisi lamathuluzi}}, noma ukuvala leyo windi, iyibuyisela ekhaya.

Izilungiselelo zebha yamathuluzi zisebenza nakubhokisi lamathuluzi: {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Ibha yamathuluzi}} › {{ui:Izithonjana namagama}}, {{ui:Izithonjana kuphela}} noma {{ui:Amagama kuphela}}, kanye {{ui:Bonisa amagama amaqembu (Ifayela, Dweba, …)}}.

## Amaphaneli akho asahlala ngokwakho

Amaphaneli esobunxele nesokudla agcina konke okuchaziwe kuthi [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels): ukufaka, ukuntanta, ukubopha, ukufihla, amathebhu, {{ui:Hambisa uye kwewindi elisha}} kanye {{ui:Setha kabusha isakhiwo}}.

## Buyela esakhiweni sakudala

Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Jikelele}} › {{ui:Isakhiwo}} › {{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} bese ukhetha {{ui:CadCraft yasendulo}}. Yonke ibha yamathuluzi, ithebhu nephaneli kubuyela lapho kwakukhona khona.

:::note
Efonini, noma ewindini elingu-760 pixels noma elincane kunalokho, uhlelo lusebenzisa isakhiwo sefoni nxa kungakhethwa lutho. Iwindi elibanzi libuyisa isakhiwo sakho.
:::

Ukuze uzame enye indlela yokuhlela, bheka [isakhiwo seribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi](common:layout-ribbon).

Ukuze ushintshe lokho esakhiweni esikubonisayo, bheka [Shintsha isakhiwo esikrinini](common:layout-edit); ukuze uthole ukuhlelwa okumnyama, okunebha yemenyu neribhoni, bheka [isakhiwo sakwa-Uhlaka](common:layout-drafting).
