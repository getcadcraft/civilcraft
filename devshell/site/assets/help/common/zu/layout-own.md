Ngaphandle kwezakhiwo ezintathu ezakhelwe ngaphakathi, ungahlela esakho, bese ulondoloza sonke isethi yakho ngegama ukuze ubuyele kuyo ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

## Hlela isakhiwo sakho

1. Vula {{ui:Settings}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}), khetha {{ui:Global}}, bese {{ui:Layout}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Arrange your own layout…}}. Ingxoxo iqala kusakhiwo osisebenzisa manje.
3. Nikeza igama, bese ukhetha:
   - **Phezu komdwebo:** ibha yamathuluzi yohlelo, iribhoni noma akukho; ibha yesihloko; ibhokisi lolwazi nokuthi lubamba izilawuli ezimaphi.
   - **Eceleni komdwebo:** ibhokisi lamathuluzi ngakwesobunxele, ngakwesokudla noma akukho nhlobo, nanoma ngaphandle kwe-{{ui:My tools}}; iphaneli elibamba amapaleti, elingaphezulu (isiphequluli sephrojekthi noma Properties, njengesibonelo), namathebhu aphansi kwalo.
   - **Indawo yomdwebo:** amathebhu okubuka, ibha esheshayo nebha yokubuka enezilawuli ozikhethile, ipaleti yokuhlela, ibha yesimo.
   - **Ukubukeka:** usayizi, izingcezu zezinkinobho nesitayela sethebhu.
4. Khetha {{ui:Use this layout}}. Uhlelo lushintshela kuso ngokushesha, futhi zonke izinhlelo ezinezakhiwo nazoziyasisebenzisa.

Kukhethwa kuphela lokho uhlelo lolu olunakho. Isakhiwo sakho sibhalwe ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:From add-ons and files}}; {{ui:Export this layout}} siyilondoloza njengefayela ongalinika umsebenzi wakho, futhi inkinobho yokususa iyasisusa. Amaphaneli esigcwalisa asafakwa, ayintante futhi ahanjiswe kumawindi awo, njengoba kuchazwe ku-[Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels).

## Londoloza isethi yakho njengendawo yokusebenza

Indawo yokusebenza yisethi yakho yonke yazo zonke izinhlelo kulesi siphequluli: isakhiwo, isitayela semenyu nokhiye bakho, isitayela sebha yamathuluzi, amaphaneli nosayizi wawo, kanye {{ui:My tools}}. Itimu nolimi akuyona ingxenye yakho.

- **Londoloza:** bhala igama ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Izimo zokusebenza}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Save my setup}}. Ukulondoloza ngegama elivele likhona kushintsha leso sethi.
- **Shintsha:** khetha isethi oluhlwini bese ukhetha {{ui:Switch to it}}. Uhlelo luyalayisha kabusha kuleyo sethi; uma unomsebenzi ongalondolozwanga, uhlelo buyisa kuqala, njengoba lenza kunoma yiliphi ukulayisha kabusha.
- **Yabelana:** {{ui:Export}} ilondoloza isethi okhethiwe njengefayela `.ccenv.json`; {{ui:Import a setup…}} yengeza isethi esuka kufayela. Ifayela libeka lezi zinketho kuphela, akukho okunye.
- **Susa:** khetha isethi bese ukhetha {{ui:Remove}}.

Ungagcina kuze kube amasethi ayishumi nambili.

Ukuze uzame okwakhelwe ngaphakathi kuqala, bona [isakhiwo sebhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi](common:layout-toolbox) kanye [isakhiwo seribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi](common:layout-ribbon).
