Ngaphandle kwezakhiwo ezintathu ezakhelwe ngaphakathi, ungahlela esakho, futhi ulondoloze wonke amasethi akho ngegama ukuze ubuyele kuwo ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

## Hlela isakhiwo sakho

1. Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} (igiya, noma {{key:Mod+,}}), khetha {{ui:Jikelele}}, bese {{ui:Isakhiwo}}.
2. Khetha {{ui:Hlela ukulungiswa kwakho…}}. Ingxoxo iqala ngesakhiwo osisebenzisa manje.
3. Nikeza igama, bese ukhetha:
   - **Phezu komdwebo:** ithuluzi lohlelo, iribhoni noma akukho lutho; ibha yesihloko; ibhokisi lolwazi nezilawuli okuzibambayo.
   - **Ngasohlangothini lomdwebo:** ibhokisi lamathuluzi ngakwesobunxele, ngakwesokudla noma ngeke libe khona, nangaphandle kwe-{{ui:Amathuluzi ami}}; iphaneli elibamba amaphaleti, elisezansi kwayo yonke (isiphequluli sephrojekthi noma {{ui:Properties}}, isibonelo), nalawo angamathebhu ngaphansi kwalo.
   - **Indawo yomdwebo:** amathebhu okubuka, ibha esheshayo nebha yokubuka enezilawuli oziklikile, iphaleti lokuhlela, ibha yesimo.
   - **Ukubukeka:** usayizi, imiphetho yezinkinobho nesitayela sethebhu.
4. Khetha {{ui:Sebenzisa lolu lungiselelo}}. Uhlelo lushintshela kulo ngokushesha, futhi wonke amahlelo anezakhiwo nawo ayasisebenzisa.

Kunikezwa kuphela lokho uhlelo olunakho. Isakhiwo sakho sidweshulwa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Kusuka kuzengezo nasemafayelini}}; {{ui:Thumela lolu lungiselelo}} silondoloza njengefayela onganikeza ngalo umngane wakho wokusebenza, futhi inkinobho yokususa iyasisusa. Amaphaneli alo agcwalisayo asafakwa, ayantanta futhi ahambe awale ewindini awo, njengakuthi [Hlela amaphaneli](common:arrange-panels).

## Londoloza isethi yakho njengendawo yokusebenza

Indawo yokusebenza iyisethi yakho yonke yazo zonke izinhlelo kulo isiphequluli: isakhiwo, isitayela semenyu nezinkinobho zakho, isitayela sebha yamathuluzi, amaphaneli nosayizi wawo, kanye ne-{{ui:Amathuluzi ami}}. Itimu nolimi akuyona ingxenye yayo.

- **Londoloza:** bhala igama ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Indawo yokusebenza}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza isethi yami}}. Ukulondoloza ngegama elikhona kushintsha leso sethi.
- **Shintsha:** khetha isethi ohlwini bese ukhetha {{ui:Shintshela kuyo}}. Uhlelo luyalayisha kabusha kuleyo sethi; uma unomsebenzi ongalondolozwanga, uhlelo luyababuza kuqala, njengakunoma yiliphi ilayisha kabusha.
- **Yabelana:** {{ui:Thumela}} ilondoloza isethi okhethiwe njengefayela le-`.ccenv.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa isethi…}} yengeza enye esuka efayelini. Ifayela libeka lezi zinketho kuphela, akukho okunye.
- **Susa:** khetha isethi bese ukhetha {{ui:Susa}}.

Ungagcina kuze kube amasethi ayishumi nambili.

Ukuze ushintshe isakhiwo ngaphandle kokuvula ingxoxo (donsa ibhulokhi, uchofoze ngakwesokudla ethulusi), bheka [Shintsha isakhiwo esikrinini](common:layout-edit). Ukuze uzame okuhlelwe ngaphambili kuqala, bheka [isakhiwo sebhokisi lamathuluzi nebhokisi lolwazi](common:layout-toolbox), [isakhiwo seribhoni nesiphequluli sephrojekthi](common:layout-ribbon) kanye no-[isakhiwo sakwa-Uhlaka](common:layout-drafting).
