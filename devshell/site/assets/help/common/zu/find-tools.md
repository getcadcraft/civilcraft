Uhlelo ngalunye linebhokisi lokusesha lamathuluzi alo, izilungiselelo zalo kanye nalolu sizo. Akudingeki wazi ukuthi ithuluzi likuphi ebhareni yamathuluzi.

## Thola bese usebenzisa

1. Cindezela {{key:Mod+K}}, noma khetha {{ui:Thola}} ebhareni ephezulu.
2. Bhala izinhlamvu ezimbalwa ozifunayo: "fillet", "panel text", "units". Wonke amagama obhalayo kumele afane, ngakho engeza igama lesibili ukuze unciphise uhlu.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze usebenzise okokuqala okufanele, noma uhambe ngezinkinobho zomcibisholo kuqala. {{key:Esc}} ivala ibhokisi.

Ithuluzi lisebenza sengathi ulichofoze ebhareni yamathuluzi. Isilungiselelo sivula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} kulelo silungiselelo. Isihloko sosizo sivula lapha, ephaneli losizo.

## Okufanele wazi

- Okusetshenziswe ekugcineni kuvela kuqala ngesikhathi esilandelayo, ngakho ithuluzi osebenzisa kaningi lilele ukucindezela okukodwa kukhiye.
- Amathuluzi angasebenzi manje, njengasemva kokuvulwa kwefayela, ayalwa kodwa abonakala emnyama.
- Amathuluzi ezengeziwe nawo akuhlu, uma sekuvuliwe okwengeziwe.
