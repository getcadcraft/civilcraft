Every CadCraft app and the website share one theme: light or dark. Change it in one app and the others and the site follow.

## Switch it in the top bar

At the right of the top bar there are two small buttons:

- the **sun** is the {{ui:Light theme}}: a light page with dark lines;
- the **moon** is the {{ui:Dark theme}}: a dark page with light lines.

The one in use is lit. Hover over a button to see its name. On a phone the two buttons are in the top bar's {{ui:More}} menu.

Where the sun and the moon are not there yet, the same two buttons carry the words {{ui:Paper}} (light) and {{ui:Model}} (dark). They do the same thing.

## Choose it in Settings

{{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Theme}} offers the light theme (the sun), the dark theme (the moon) and a third choice that matches your system. With that one the apps follow your computer's light or dark setting and change when it changes.

## Good to know

- The theme is kept in this browser. It is not part of a [work environment](common:layout-own) and it does not change your drawing.
- The drawing's own spaces are a different thing: in CadCraft, model space and paper space (the {{ui:Model}} tab and the sheet tabs) are where you draw and where you lay out sheets. The theme only changes the colours of the interface.
- Logos and icons follow the theme by themselves.
