The interface follows the size of your window. You do not have to set anything.

## Three steps

- **Compact** on a small laptop screen (a window under 1600 pixels wide or 900 high), so the drawing gets the room.
- **Normal** on a regular monitor.
- **Comfortable** on a very wide, large screen (3000 pixels wide and 1200 high or more), where targets and type grow a little.

The step is chosen again when you resize the window or move it to another screen. It is separate from the Comfortable / Compact choice in the panels (see [Arrange the panels](common:arrange-panels)). A touch screen is never made compact.

## Panels and bars

Side panels keep a width that fits the step and the window, so a panel is never a third of a small screen and never a sliver on an ultrawide. A bar of tools never wraps onto a second line: the tools that do not fit fold into a {{ui:⋯}} menu at its end, and come back when the window is wider.

## Good to know

This is in the lab (the version for testing) and is not released yet. There is no setting to pick a step yourself, and no presentation mode, yet.
