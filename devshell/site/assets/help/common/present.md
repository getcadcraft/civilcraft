When you show a drawing to someone across a table or on a shared screen, the interface gets in the way. Present hides it and leaves the drawing.

## Start presenting

Open the CadCraft menu (click the app mark and name at the top left, or press {{key:Alt+O}}) and pick {{ui:Present}}. To have it one click away, pin it to the top row with the dot beside it (see [The CadCraft menu](common:craft-menu)).

The title bar, menu bar, ribbon, palettes, status bar and the marks over the drawing are hidden. The view cube stays, so you can still turn a 3D view.

## Stop presenting

Press {{key:Esc}}, or click {{ui:Exit presentation}} at the top right corner. Everything comes back where it was: nothing was closed and nothing in the drawing changed.

## Good to know

- Present is in the Drafting layout, where the CadCraft menu is.
- This is in the lab (the version for testing) and is not released yet.
