In a layout with a ribbon, tool rows or a toolbox, any group of tools can leave its bar and become a palette at the side, as a column of tools.

## The grip and the close button

Every bar that can move has the same head: a grip {{ui:⠿}} at the far left and a close button {{ui:×}} at the far right. Click the grip for its menu: **Open in a window**, the bar's own choices (such as **Collapse the ribbon**) and **Hide**. You can also right-click the head.

Each group of tools has a small grip at the left of its name.

## Dock a group as a palette

1. Drag the group's grip. Outlined areas appear: the left panel, the right panel, and the drawing's left and right columns.
2. Drop the group on one of them. Its tools now stand in a column there, under a head with the group's name.

Or click the grip and pick **Dock to** and a place.

Two groups in the same place become tabs. The {{ui:⌃}} button folds the palette to its head and opens it again.

## Put it back

Click the palette's {{ui:×}}, pick **Put it back** in its grip menu, or drag its grip onto the bar it came from. The group goes back to its place in the ribbon, the tool rows or the toolbox.

Where you docked each group is remembered for this app and this layout.

## Arrange mode

While you arrange the layout (Settings › Layout › Arrange), the places where a group can dock are outlined. Drag a group's grip onto one: it becomes a palette there, and a second group dropped on the same place joins it as a tab.

## Float a group over the drawing

Pick **Float on the drawing** in a group's grip menu, or drag the grip onto the drawing where no place is outlined. The palette floats there; drag its grip to move it.

## Pin

**Pin** in a grip menu keeps that bar or palette where it is: it cannot be dragged, closed or hidden, and a pinned ribbon stays unfolded. **Unpin** gives it all back.

## Close a group

Each ribbon group has a {{ui:×}} at the right of its name, and every group's grip menu has **Close**. The group leaves its bar; the bar's own grip menu lists it as **Show … again**.

## Your windows, next time

A bar you moved to its own window remembers where that window was, and on which screen, for this app and layout. When you open the app again, a note offers **Restore my windows**: one click opens them where they were. A browser may open only one window per click; if one stays closed, click **Restore my windows** again. **Keep them here** forgets them.
