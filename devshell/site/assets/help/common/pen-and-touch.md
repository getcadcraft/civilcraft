## What you will learn

How the app works with a pen on a tablet or a touch-screen laptop, and the settings that change it. They are in {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Pen and touch}}, which shows on a touch screen, or once you have used a pen.

## A pen is noticed the first time

Nothing changes until you touch the drawing with a pen. From then on the app remembers, on this device, that you draw with one, and the choices below start to apply. Before that, your fingers draw and select as usual.

## Your palm is ignored

With {{ui:Ignore my palm while I use a pen}} on (it starts on), a touch on the drawing is ignored while the pen touches the screen, for a moment after you lift it or while it hovers just above the screen, and whenever the contact is as big as a palm. Rest your hand on the screen and draw.

If a quick finger right after the pen does nothing, wait a moment: that moment is the palm rule at work.

## Let the pen draw and your fingers move the view

Under {{ui:Fingers on the drawing, once you use a pen here}}:

- {{ui:Draw and select}} (the start): a finger does what the pen does.
- {{ui:Only move the view}}: one finger on the drawing does nothing, so a stray touch never draws or picks. Two fingers still move and zoom the view as usual.

The toolbars, panels and dialogs work with your fingers either way.

## Undo with a tap

With {{ui:Tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo}} on (it starts on), tap the drawing with two fingers together to undo your last step, and with three to redo it. It works with or without a pen. Moving the fingers is a pan or a zoom, never a tap.

## The pen's buttons

- {{ui:Pen side button}}: {{ui:What the app does (a right click)}} at the start, or {{ui:Undo}} or {{ui:Redo}}.
- {{ui:Pen eraser end}} (on pens that have one): {{ui:What the app does}} at the start, or {{ui:Undo}} or {{ui:Redo}}. In ModelCraft, what the app does is to erase while the eraser end touches.

## These are the device's settings

The pen and touch choices are kept for this device and apply in every app on it, so you set them once. {{ui:Reset the layout}} and resetting an app's settings leave them as they are.

## Not built yet

Pen pressure and tilt don't change line weights yet, and no app has a handwriting or sketch-to-shape tool. The app reads the pen's pressure, tilt and hover, so these can come later per app.
