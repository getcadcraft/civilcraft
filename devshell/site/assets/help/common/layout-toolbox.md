**Toolbox and info box** is one of the app's three workspace layouts. The drawing tools stand in a column on the left, an info box across the top shows what the running tool needs or what you have selected, every view has a tab, and a quick bar under the drawing holds a few everyday options. The tools are the app's own: everything you can do in the classic layout works here too, and your drawing, your menu style and your keys stay as they are.

## Pick it

1. Open {{ui:Settings}} (the gear, or {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Choose the {{ui:Global}} tab, then go to {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}}.
3. Choose {{ui:Toolbox and info box}}.

The layout changes at once. The choice is one for every app in this browser: each app opens in it from then on, and it is in place before the page draws, so you never see the classic layout first.

## What is where

- **Toolbar:** the app's own toolbar stays at the top of the drawing area, with its menus.
- **Info box:** under the toolbar. While a tool runs it shows the tool and what it asks for. When you pick something it shows its main settings (for a wall: its type, thickness, length, location line and height), and you can change them right there: a change there is the same as in Properties, and {{key:Mod+Z}} takes it back. With nothing picked it says "Nothing selected". Each app adds its own boxes here, such as the current layer or level, or the open document.
- **Toolbox:** the column on the left, the app's tools in groups. Click a group's name to fold it or open it again. The tool that is running is lit.
- **My tools:** the first group of the toolbox, eight empty slots for the tools you use most. Click an empty slot to choose any tool or command of the app; or right-click a tool in the toolbox and choose {{ui:Add to My tools}}; or drag a tool onto a slot. Right-click a slot to change it or empty it. Your slots are kept for each app in this browser.
- **Navigator:** the right panel. The app's project browser is on top (plans, sections, elevations, 3D views, schedules, sheets, as the app has them): click one to open it. Under it, {{ui:Properties}} and a tab with the right panel's other contents.
- **View tabs:** a tab for every view of the open file (a sheet, a plan, a scene…), over the drawing.
- **Quick bar:** under the drawing, the view settings and everyday options the app has, such as the scale, the detail level, the view range, fit, plan, split or 3D, snaps and units.
- **Edit palette:** click something in the drawing and a small palette comes by the pointer with the app's edit commands for it, such as move, copy, rotate, mirror, duplicate or delete, and {{ui:Properties}}. {{key:Esc}}, the mouse wheel, another click or its close button put it away.

A tool that can't run right now is greyed, and its tooltip says why. Click it, or press {{key:Enter}} on it, to read the reason in a box.

{{key:Esc}} ends the running tool first, as in the classic layout, and every shortcut and {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (screen modes) work as before.

## Give the toolbox its own window

The {{ui:Move the toolbox}} button at the top of the toolbox has {{ui:Move to a new window}}: the toolbox opens in a narrow window of its own that you can put beside the app or on a second monitor. Keys pressed there reach the app. {{ui:Put the toolbox back}}, or closing that window, brings it home.

The toolbar settings apply to the toolbox too: {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Toolbar}} › {{ui:Icons and labels}}, {{ui:Icons only}} or {{ui:Labels only}}, and {{ui:Show group names (File, Draw, …)}}.

## Your panels stay yours

The left and right panels keep everything described in [Arrange the panels](common:arrange-panels): dock, float, pin, hide, tabs, {{ui:Move to a new window}} and {{ui:Reset layout}}.

## Go back to the classic layout

Open {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}} and choose {{ui:CadCraft classic}}. Every toolbar, tab and panel goes back where it was.

:::note
On a phone, or in a window 760 pixels wide or narrower, the app uses its phone layout whatever you picked. A wider window brings your layout back.
:::

To try another arrangement, see [the ribbon and project browser layout](common:layout-ribbon).
