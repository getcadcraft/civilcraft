Every app carries its own help. It is part of the app, so it opens with no connection and always describes the version you are running.

## Open the help

- Press {{key:F1}}, or choose {{ui:Help}} in the bar at the top of the app.
- Press {{key:Esc}} or choose {{ui:Close}} to put it away. Your drawing stays as it was.

:::mac
On most Mac keyboards the top row controls brightness and sound. Press {{key:F1}} together with the **fn** key (or the globe key), or use the {{ui:Help}} button. See [Keys on a Mac](common:keys#function-keys-on-a-mac).
:::

## Find a topic

- Type in the search box at the top of the help. Results update as you type and include other programs' command names, so typing what you would have typed elsewhere usually finds the right page.
- Topics are grouped the same way in every app: getting started, walkthroughs, coming from other software, reference, and troubleshooting.
- Links inside a topic open the next topic in the same panel. {{ui:Back}} returns to where you were.

## Steps for your computer

Some steps differ between Mac, Windows and Linux, between browsers, and between being online and offline. The help works out which applies to you and shows those steps. The line at the top of a topic says what it chose, for example "Showing steps for Mac, Chrome or Edge, online".

- Choose {{ui:Change}} on that line to pick another computer or browser, for example to help a colleague.
- Choose {{ui:Show all}} to see every version of every step, each labeled.

Keyboard shortcuts are written the way your keyboard labels them: {{key:Mod+S}} reads as ⌘S on a Mac and Ctrl+S on Windows and Linux.

## The same help on the website

The website shows the same topics at getcadcraft.com/tutorials, with every platform's steps. In the app, {{ui:Open on the website}} at the bottom of a topic opens that page when you are online.
