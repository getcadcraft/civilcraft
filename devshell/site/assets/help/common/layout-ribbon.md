**Ribbon and project browser** is one of the app's three workspace layouts. A ribbon of tabs runs across the top, Properties and a project browser share the left panel, every view you open gets a tab over the drawing, and a status bar sits at the bottom. The tools are the app's own: everything you can do in the classic layout works here too, and your drawing, your menu style and your keys stay as they are.

## Pick it

1. Open {{ui:Settings}} (the gear, or {{key:Mod+,}}).
2. Choose the {{ui:Global}} tab, then go to {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}}.
3. Choose {{ui:Ribbon and project browser}}.

The layout changes at once. The choice is one for every app in this browser: each app opens in it from then on, and it is in place before the page draws, so you never see the classic layout first.

## What is where

- **Title bar:** the app's name, quick access buttons (open, save, undo, redo), the name of the open file, {{ui:What each tool does here}} (a list of every tool with a search box, saying which are ready now and why the others are not), full screen, {{ui:Help}} and {{ui:Settings}}. In this layout the title bar takes the place of the app bar.
- **Ribbon:** the app's tools in tabs, each tab in groups. {{ui:File}} at the left opens, saves and the rest. Some apps add a tab of their own while you work in a particular mode or workspace; it is lit while it applies and goes away after.
- **Options bar:** under the ribbon, what the running tool needs from you, or what is selected.
- **Left panel:** {{ui:Properties}} above, and below it the palettes (the project browser and the panel's own content) as tabs. Drag the line between them to share the space.
- **View tabs:** one tab per view you opened (a sheet, a plan, a scene…). Close one with its close button; the file stays open.
- **View bar and status bar:** under the drawing, the view's own controls (scale, zoom); at the bottom, the app's messages.

A tool that can't run right now is greyed, and its tooltip says why (for example "Open a file first", or that the app does not have that tool yet). Click it, or press {{key:Enter}} on it, to read the reason in a box.

## Use the ribbon from the keyboard

With a ribbon tab focused, {{key:Left}} and {{key:Right}} move between tabs and {{key:Home}} and {{key:End}} go to the first and the last. {{key:Esc}} ends the running tool first, as in the classic layout, and every shortcut and {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (screen modes) work as before.

## Fold the ribbon or give it its own window

- Double-click a ribbon tab to fold the ribbon to its tabs; a click on a tab then shows its tools until you move on. Double-click again to keep it open. The {{ui:Move the ribbon}} button at the ribbon's right end has {{ui:Collapse the ribbon}} and {{ui:Keep the ribbon open}} too. Each app remembers it.
- {{ui:Move the ribbon}} › {{ui:Move to a new window}} puts the ribbon in a window of its own, for example on a second monitor. Keys pressed there reach the app. {{ui:Put the ribbon back}}, or closing that window, brings it home.

The toolbar settings apply to the ribbon: {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Toolbar}} › {{ui:Icons and labels}}, {{ui:Icons only}} (the ribbon then shows two rows of small icons per group) or {{ui:Labels only}}, and {{ui:Show group names (File, Draw, …)}}.

## Your panels stay yours

The left and right panels keep everything described in [Arrange the panels](common:arrange-panels): dock, float, pin, hide, tabs, {{ui:Move to a new window}} and {{ui:Reset layout}}.

## Go back to the classic layout

Open {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Global}} › {{ui:Layout}} › {{ui:Workspace}} and choose {{ui:CadCraft classic}}. Every toolbar, tab and panel goes back where it was.

:::note
On a phone, or in a window 760 pixels wide or narrower, the app uses its phone layout whatever you picked. A wider window brings your layout back.
:::

To try another arrangement, see [the toolbox and info box layout](common:layout-toolbox).
